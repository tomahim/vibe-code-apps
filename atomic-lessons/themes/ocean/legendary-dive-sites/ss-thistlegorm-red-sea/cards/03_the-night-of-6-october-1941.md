title: "The night of 6 October 1941"

German intelligence suspected that a troop carrier was in the area, and two Heinkel He 111 bombers were sent from Crete to find it. They did not find a troop ship, but one of the aircraft spotted the ships waiting at the anchorage. The crew chose the largest vessel and dropped two heavy bombs on the Thistlegorm, at around 1:30 in the morning of 6 October 1941.

Both bombs struck hold 4, near the stern, and the explosion of ammunition stored there sank the ship. Nine men died: four sailors and five naval gunners. Survivors were picked up by the cruiser HMS Carlisle, which had been moored in the same anchorage. Captain William Ellis received the OBE, and crewman Angus McLeay was awarded the George Medal for saving another sailor.
