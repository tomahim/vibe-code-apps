SS Thistlegorm was a British cargo steamship built by J.L. Thompson and Sons in Sunderland, in the north-east of England. She was launched on 9 April 1940 and completed in June of that year. At about 126.5 m long, she was an ordinary working freighter, not a warship.

Because Britain was at war, she was a defensively equipped merchant ship. That meant she carried guns, including a gun on her stern and an anti-aircraft gun, and naval personnel joined her crew to use them. Her first three voyages were routine: steel rails and aircraft parts from the United States, grain from Argentina, and rum from the West Indies. Her fourth voyage would be her last.
