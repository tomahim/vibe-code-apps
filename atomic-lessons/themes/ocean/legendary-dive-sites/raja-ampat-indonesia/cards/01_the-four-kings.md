Raja Ampat is an archipelago off the north-west tip of the Bird's Head Peninsula on the island of New Guinea, in Southwest Papua province, Indonesia. It has over 1,500 small islands, cays and shoals around four main islands: Misool, Salawati, Batanta and Waigeo, plus the smaller island of Kofiau.

The name means four kings. Local mythology tells of a woman who found seven eggs, and three of them hatched into kings who ruled the islands, with a fourth settling at Waigama. The islands lie just south of the equator, so the water is warm all year, about 28 to 30 degrees Celsius in the north and a little cooler, 26 to 28 degrees, around Misool in the south.
