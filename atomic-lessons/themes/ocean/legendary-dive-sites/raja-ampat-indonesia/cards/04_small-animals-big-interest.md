Raja Ampat is known to divers for the variety of small animals as well as large ones. The islands' waters contain camouflaged pygmy seahorses and tiny cleaner shrimps, and also manta rays, whale sharks and other large species. Rare marine mammals such as dugongs are also found in the region.

Diving here is often about close observation. A guide can point out a tiny pygmy seahorse that blends into its surroundings, and many divers spend a dive looking closely at a single patch of reef. It is a very different experience from a shark dive, and it shows that a legendary site can be legendary for patient looking as much as for big animals.
