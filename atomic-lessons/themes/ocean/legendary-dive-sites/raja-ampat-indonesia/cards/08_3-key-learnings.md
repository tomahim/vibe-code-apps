**1. A huge archipelago.** Raja Ampat has over 1,500 small islands around Misool, Salawati, Batanta and Waigeo, just south of the equator.

**2. Record diversity.** It holds more than 600 hard coral species, about 75% of the world's known species, and more than 1,700 reef fish.

**3. Protection is shared.** Villagers protect turtle nests, a marine park was designated in 2009, and illegal mining is a growing threat.
