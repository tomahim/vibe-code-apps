The first Europeans to record the islands were Portuguese navigators led by Jorge de Menezes in 1526. Later English explorer William Dampier gave his name to Dampier Strait, between Batanta and Waigeo. But the islands were home to people long before.

Today local communities take part in conservation. In the north-east of Waigeo villagers protect nests and move eggs of leatherback, olive ridley and hawksbill turtles, with support from the local government and non-governmental groups. The Raja Ampat Marine Recreation Park was designated in 2009 and is made up of four marine areas, including waters around northern Salawati, Batanta and south-western Waigeo, Mayalibit Bay, the waters south-east of Misool, and the Sembilan Islands. Protection works best when the people who live nearby are partners.
