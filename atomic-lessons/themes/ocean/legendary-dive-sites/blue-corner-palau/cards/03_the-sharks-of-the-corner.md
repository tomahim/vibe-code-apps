Blue Corner is known for grey reef sharks, which are often seen patrolling the reef wall in numbers, along with big schools of black snappers, barracuda and jacks. Sleeping whitetip reef sharks can be seen on the sand in some areas.

A scientific assessment of the Ngemelis area for important shark and ray areas lists it as home to threatened species such as the whitetip reef shark, and as a feeding area and a reproductive area for grey reef sharks. In other words, the sharks are not simply passing through. They use the reef for feeding and breeding, which is why protecting the reef and protecting the sharks go together.
