Blue Corner is a section of Palau's barrier reef in the south-east of Koror, close to Ngerukewid and German Channel. Its triangular shape, with stepped walls on the Pacific Ocean sides, resembles a submerged peninsula pointing out into the open sea.

In the northern part of Blue Corner there is a large underwater cavern called Blue Holes. The wider area is a vertical reef wall off Ngemelis Island, which also includes dive sites called Big Drop Off, New Drop Off and Turtle Wall. The reef there is covered with small gorgonian sea fans and lush purple soft corals. Because it is so varied, Blue Corner is often described as the most requested dive in Palau.
