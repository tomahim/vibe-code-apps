The French ocean explorer Jacques Cousteau helped make Sipadan famous. In his 1989 film Borneo: The Ghost of the Sea Turtle, he said he had seen other places like Sipadan 45 years earlier, but that they no longer existed, and that at Sipadan he had found an untouched piece of art.

That remark explains why conservation became so important. A site can be loved to death: more visitors bring more boats, more waste and more damage to coral. Sabah Parks responded with rules, including daily permit limits, dive marshals to monitor diving activity, and a one-month closure in November 2023 to let the ecosystem recover from human disturbance. Fame brings attention, and attention has to be managed.
