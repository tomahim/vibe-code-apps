Sipadan sits in the centre of the Indo-Pacific basin, a region that holds some of the richest marine habitats in the world. More than 400 species of fish and hundreds of coral species have been classified in the waters around the island.

That diversity includes animals that stay close to the reef and others that only pass through. Frequently seen are green and hawksbill turtles, which mate and nest at Sipadan, enormous schools of big-eye trevally and bumphead parrotfish, and barracuda that gather in tornado-like formations. Manta rays, eagle rays, scalloped hammerhead sharks and whale sharks also visit. Mixing resident reef species with open-ocean visitors is what makes a wall-and-current site like this so special.
