title: "From bird sanctuary to marine park"
archived: false
---
Sipadan was protected on land before it was protected in the sea. It was declared a bird sanctuary in 1933 by the colonial government of North Borneo and re-gazetted by the Malaysian government in 1963. Diving became popular in the 1980s, and resorts opened on the island.

The pressure from tourism grew, and in 2004 Sabah Parks declared the island a marine park and closed the six resort facilities on the island because of their effects on the ecosystem. Resorts moved to nearby islands. In 2015 the protected area was expanded by about 6,846 hectares of surrounding coral reef. Today the park is managed by Sabah Parks, and visiting requires a permit.
