Beneath the island there is a limestone cave called the turtle tomb. It is a labyrinth of tunnels and chambers inside the column of the island, and it contains many skeletal remains of turtles.

The explanation is simple but sad. Turtles that enter the tunnels can become lost, and because they breathe air they drown before they find the way back to the surface. The cave is a natural trap, not a nesting place. It is also a good example of the difference between what exists and what is safe to visit. The turtle tomb is a famous feature in books and documentaries, but it is a dangerous overhead cave, and it is a place for expert teams, not for a recreational dive.
