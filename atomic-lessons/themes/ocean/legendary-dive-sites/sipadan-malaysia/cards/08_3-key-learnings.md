**1. A coral island on a volcano.** Sipadan is Malaysia's only oceanic island, built by corals on an extinct volcanic cone rising about 600 m.

**2. Walls and currents bring life.** More than 400 fish species, nesting turtles and tornado-like barracuda schools gather at the drop-off.

**3. Fame needed rules.** Resorts were closed in 2004, permits and marshals followed, and the island was awarded to Malaysia by the ICJ in 2002.
