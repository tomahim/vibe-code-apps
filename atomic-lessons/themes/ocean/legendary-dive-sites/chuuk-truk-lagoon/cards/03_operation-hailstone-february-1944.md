---
Operation Hailstone was a large United States Navy air and surface attack on Truk Lagoon, beginning on 17 February 1944. Carriers of Task Force 58 launched a fighter sweep about 90 minutes before daybreak. Japanese aircraft were caught partly unprepared, and many merchant ships were at anchor with little protection.

The Japanese had seen reconnaissance planes the week before and moved their larger warships, including battleships, heavy cruisers and carriers, to Palau. What remained was smaller warships and many merchant ships. The attack sank 12 smaller Japanese warships and 32 merchant ships, destroyed large numbers of aircraft, and burned about 17,000 tons of stored fuel. Shipping losses totalled almost 200,000 tons.
