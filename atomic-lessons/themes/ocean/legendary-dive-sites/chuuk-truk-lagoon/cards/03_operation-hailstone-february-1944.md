title: "Operation Hailstone, February 1944"

---
Operation Hailstone was a large United States Navy air and surface attack on Truk Lagoon, beginning on 17 February 1944. Japanese aircraft were caught partly unprepared, and many merchant ships were at anchor with little protection.

The Japanese had seen reconnaissance planes the week before and moved their larger warships, including battleships, heavy cruisers and carriers, to Palau. What remained was smaller warships and many merchant ships. The attack sank about 40 Japanese ships and destroyed hundreds of aircraft. Shipping losses totalled almost 200,000 tons.

The raid shows how a base can become a trap. A lagoon that protected ships from the sea could not protect them from the air. Many of the ships that lie there today were not warships at all but cargo vessels, which is why the wrecks hold trucks, supplies and everyday objects as well as weapons.
