Chuuk's wrecks are also war graves, because many people died in the attack. Visitors are expected to treat them with respect.

There is also an environmental concern. Reports in 2011 compared the possible release of oil from rusting hulls to a major spill. Many ships may have had little fuel in their tanks, given the state of the Japanese war effort in 1944, so researchers have been surveying the wrecks together with Japanese colleagues to estimate how much oil remains. Weno, the state capital and largest city of the Federated States of Micronesia, depends heavily on this tourism. Steel does not last forever, so the same ships that tell a story about 1944 may need active management by the people of Chuuk.
