**1. A natural fortress.** A 225 km reef encloses a huge lagoon that became Japan's main South Pacific naval base.

**2. Hailstone sank a supply fleet.** On 17 February 1944 US carrier aircraft sank 12 smaller warships and 32 merchant ships.

**3. The wrecks are archive, reef and grave.** They hold cargo records and coral life, and they need respect and careful management.
