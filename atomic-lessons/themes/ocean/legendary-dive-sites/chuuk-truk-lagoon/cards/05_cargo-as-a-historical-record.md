The wrecks include merchant ships, tankers, ammunition carriers and warships, and each shows part of the Japanese supply system. Details are documented in the National Park Service booklet on Truk Lagoon, which lists 52 ships known to have been sunk inside the lagoon.

The Aikoku Maru, an ammunition ship, was hit by a torpedo and the explosion of ammunition near the bow caused an enormous blast. The San Francisco Maru, a 117 m freighter built in 1919, arrived at Truk on 5 February 1944, only days before the raid. Each wreck is a record of how a particular ship was used, and of the final days of the base. Archaeologists value them because they are time capsules from a specific date.
