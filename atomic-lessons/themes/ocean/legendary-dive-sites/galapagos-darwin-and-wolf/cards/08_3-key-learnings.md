**1. Currents make the reserve rich.** Different ocean currents meet around the Galápagos, supporting animals from tiny fish to whale sharks.

**2. Landmarks erode.** Darwin's Arch, 43 m high, collapsed on 17 May 2021 through natural erosion, leaving the Pillars of Evolution.

**3. Read any site the same way.** Ask what the place is made of, what moves through it and who protects it.
