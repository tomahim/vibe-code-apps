On 17 May 2021 Darwin's Arch collapsed into the sea. Ecuador's Ministry of Environment and Water said the event was a consequence of natural erosion. The arch was made of natural stone that was once part of Darwin Island. The collapse was witnessed by divers aboard the Galapagos Aggressor III.

What remains are two columns of rock. Locals in the tourism and diving industry have nicknamed them the Pillars of Evolution, a name that nods to Darwin. The collapse is a good example of geology in action: waves and weather slowly wore away the rock, and one day the arch gave way. It is a reminder that natural landmarks change.
