Getting data from the bottom is difficult. Scientific diving here needs cave training and mixed gases, because the work takes place far deeper than ordinary recreational limits.

This is a good example of why some legendary sites are mainly for science and for highly trained specialists. The hole is deep, the walls overhang, and the lower caves are an overhead environment, where a diver cannot simply swim straight to the surface. A video of the Blue Hole looks simple, but visiting its depths is a job for experienced technical teams, not a casual outing.

This is why the Blue Hole is a place for specialists. A visit for a photograph and a research dive are very different jobs, and the second needs careful planning, extra gas supplies and a team that knows the limits.
