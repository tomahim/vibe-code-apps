The Great Blue Hole is a large marine sinkhole near the centre of Lighthouse Reef, a small atoll about 70 km from the Belize mainland and Belize City. Seen from above it is almost a perfect circle, 318 m across and 124 m deep, with a surface area of about 70,650 square metres.

The colour is the first clue to what it is. The reef flat around it is shallow and pale, so the water looks turquoise. Inside the circle the bottom drops away so far that the water looks deep blue, which is where the name comes from. It is a hole in the reef, not a bay or a lagoon, and its walls are vertical limestone.
