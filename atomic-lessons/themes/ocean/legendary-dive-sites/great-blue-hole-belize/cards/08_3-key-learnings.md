**1. It was once dry land.** The hole is a flooded karst cave whose stalactites grew in air during ice-age low sea levels.

**2. It records Earth's history.** Ledges, stalactites and their tilt show sea-level change and ground movement.

**3. The life is at the rim.** Deep down a hydrogen sulfide layer leaves the water without oxygen, while the shallow edge holds fish and reef sharks.
