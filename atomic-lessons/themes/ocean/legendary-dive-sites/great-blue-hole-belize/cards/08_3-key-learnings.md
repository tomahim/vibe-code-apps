**1. It was once dry land.** The hole is a flooded karst cave whose stalactites grew in air during ice-age low sea levels.

**2. It records Earth's history.** Ledges at about 21, 49 and 91 m, dated stalactites and a 5 degree tilt show sea-level change and ground movement.

**3. The life is at the rim.** Below about 91 m a hydrogen sulfide layer leaves the water without oxygen, while the shallow edge holds fish and reef sharks.
