title: "Life in nearly sterile water"

---
It may be surprising, but Silfra has very little life. The extremely cold water supports only a few species. An amphipod called Crymostygius thingvallensis lives only in Þingvallavatn lake and the fissures around it. Arctic char visit the fissure in the mating season of August and September.

The only fish that live in Silfra all year are dwarf char, a subspecies of Arctic char, and they stay in the deeper, darker corners, so divers rarely see them. The site is therefore a place to learn about geology, not about wildlife. The contrast with a tropical reef is the point: here, the big attraction is the rock and the clarity of the water, rather than animals.
