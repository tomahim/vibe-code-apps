The dive site is divided into named sections. Silfra Hall is a series of cave systems under the surface, with a maximum depth of about 45 m. Silfra Cathedral is the long fissure with views from end to end. Silfra Lagoon is the third main section. The deepest part of the fissure reaches about 63 m, but that depth is seldom visited because it needs technical diving skills.

Between the cave area and the main crack there is a narrow tunnel nicknamed the toilet. It is the only opening for the water to pass through, so it creates a strong current. This is why the site is run by guides in a controlled way, and why a famous, apparently gentle place still demands training.
