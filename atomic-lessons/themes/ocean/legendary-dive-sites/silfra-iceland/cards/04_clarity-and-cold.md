The visibility in Silfra is famously long, often described as among the best in the world, because the water has been filtered through lava for decades and carries almost no particles or algae. The Cathedral section is a fissure about 100 m long where the far end can be seen from the other.

The same source that makes it clear makes it cold. The water comes from glacial meltwater and is very cold, which is why it is a dive for people who have trained for cold water. Because few animals can live in such cold, clear water, there is little to see except the rock, and the water itself.
