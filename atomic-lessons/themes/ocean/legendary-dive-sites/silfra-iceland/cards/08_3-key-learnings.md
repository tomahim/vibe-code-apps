**1. It is a plate boundary.** Silfra lies on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, where North America and Eurasia move apart by about 2 cm a year.

**2. The water is old glacier melt.** Meltwater from Langjökull filters through lava for decades and emerges exceptionally clear.

**3. Geology, not wildlife, is the attraction.** Cold water holds little life, and the site is set in Þingvellir, where Iceland's Althing began in 930.
