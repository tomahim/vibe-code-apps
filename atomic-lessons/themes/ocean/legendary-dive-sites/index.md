Ten dive sites that every ocean lover has heard of, explained through their geology, wildlife, wrecks and history. Built from official park and heritage sites (Belize Audubon Society, Thingvellir National Park, Sabah Parks, the US National Park Service, the United Nations) and encyclopedia entries checked against them. This is background reading about places, not diver training: visiting any of these sites safely needs proper certification, local guides and current rules.
