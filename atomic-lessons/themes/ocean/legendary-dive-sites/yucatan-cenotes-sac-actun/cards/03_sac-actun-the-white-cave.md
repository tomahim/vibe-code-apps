Sac Actun is a Yucatec Maya name meaning white cave. The Sistema Sac Actun is an underwater cave system along the Caribbean coast of the Yucatán, with passages to the north and west of Tulum. Through years of exploration, teams connected separate caves into one system.

In 2018 the Gran Maya Aquifer project, led by cave diver Robbie Schmittner, found a connection between Sac Actun and the Dos Ojos system. That discovery made Sac Actun the longest known underwater cave system at that time. As of January 2023 it is the second longest, after the Ox Bel Ha system, which is about 435.8 km long, and Sac Actun's explored length is about 259.5 km.
