title: "Sac Actun, the white cave"

Sac Actun is a Yucatec Maya name meaning white cave. The Sistema Sac Actun is an underwater cave system along the Caribbean coast of the Yucatán, with passages to the north and west of Tulum. Through years of exploration, teams connected separate caves into one system.

In 2018 the Gran Maya Aquifer project, led by cave diver Robbie Schmittner, found a connection between Sac Actun and the Dos Ojos system. That discovery made Sac Actun the longest known underwater cave system at that time. The Quintana Roo Speleological Survey list of February 2025 ranks Ox Bel Ha first, at about 497 km, and Sac Actun second, at about 380 km. Guinness World Records gave Sac Actun 371.958 km in July 2019, so the figures keep growing as divers map more passages.
