A cenote is a sinkhole that opens into one of these flooded cave systems. It forms when the roof of a cavity collapses or opens to the surface, exposing the water below. Some cenotes are open pools in the sunlight, and others are caverns with stalactites and beams of light.

Divers use cenotes as entrances to the cave systems. Exploration of the Sac Actun system started from Gran Cenote, near the town of Tulum, in the state of Quintana Roo. Wildlife is rare in freshwater cenotes like Dos Ojos, apart from some small crustaceans and fish that have adapted to the cave environment, so the main attraction is the geology, the clear water and the light.
