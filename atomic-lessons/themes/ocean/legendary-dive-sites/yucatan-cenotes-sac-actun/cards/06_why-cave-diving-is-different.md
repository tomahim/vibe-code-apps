Cave diving is not just deeper cave exploring. It takes place in an overhead environment, which means a diver cannot swim straight to the surface if something goes wrong. The caves are dark and the passages can be narrow. For this reason cave diving has its own training, equipment and rules, separate from open water diving.

Sac Actun's own history shows the risks. The people who explore and map these systems are trained specialists who use guide lines and careful planning. The famous cenotes visited by many people are the shallow, lit areas near the entrances, and those are very different from the deep cave systems explored by teams.
