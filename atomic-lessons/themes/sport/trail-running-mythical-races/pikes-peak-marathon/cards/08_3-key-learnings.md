**1. A doctor started it.** Dr. Arne Suominen challenged smokers to race him up and down the peak in 1956.

**2. Women made history here.** Arlene Pieper became the first woman to officially finish a marathon in the United States in 1959.

**3. Two races, one mountain.** The Ascent goes up only; the Marathon adds the descent, and Matt Carpenter's 1993 times set the benchmark.
