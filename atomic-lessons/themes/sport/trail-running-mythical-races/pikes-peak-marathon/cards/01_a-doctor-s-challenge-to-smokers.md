The Pikes Peak Marathon was officially founded in 1956, although people had wanted to climb Pikes Peak long before that. The man behind the modern races was Dr. Arne Suominen, described in the race history as a "militant tobacco-hating physician" from Delray Beach, Florida. He wrote a letter to the Colorado Springs Chamber of Commerce and challenged cigarette smokers to race him up and down the mountain.

The point of the challenge was easy to understand: if smoking was harmless, smokers should be able to keep up with a non-smoker on a big mountain. The challenge turned into an annual event, and the race that began as one doctor's dare is still run today from the town of Manitou Springs, Colorado.
