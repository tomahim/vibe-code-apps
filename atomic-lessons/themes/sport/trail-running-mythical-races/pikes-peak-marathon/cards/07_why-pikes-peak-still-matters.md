Pikes Peak is one of the oldest marathons in the United States, and it helped establish mountain running as a sport. A single letter from a doctor in 1956 started a tradition that has lasted for generations, with runners from Colorado Springs, Mexico City, New Mexico and far beyond appearing in the old result sheets.

The race also shows how a mountain race builds a story from many small moments. A failed expedition by Zebulon Pike gives the mountain its name. A disqualified runner in 1958 becomes a record-setting finisher in 1959. A runner with no feet in 1972 makes the race a story about human will. Each lesson in this path will show that the best races are built from people and place, not only from distance.
