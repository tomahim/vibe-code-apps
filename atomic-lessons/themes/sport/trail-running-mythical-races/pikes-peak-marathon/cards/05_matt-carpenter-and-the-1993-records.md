title: "Matt Carpenter and the 1993 records"
archived: false
---
If one name is tied to Pikes Peak, it is Matt Carpenter of Colorado Springs. On Sunday, August 22, 1993, he won the Pikes Peak Marathon in 3 hours 16 minutes 39 seconds, according to the official results archive. His split on the way up was 2:01:06 and his split down was 1:15:33.

The 2:01:06 Ascent time stood as the fastest ever recorded for about thirty years, until Switzerland's Remi Bonnet climbed in 2:00:20 at the 2023 Pikes Peak Ascent. Carpenter went on to win the race many times, including an unusual double of the Ascent and the Marathon on consecutive days in 2001. His 1993 run set the standard that mountain runners around the world measured themselves against.
