**1. Since 1986.** The AC100 runs between Wrightwood and Altadena in the San Gabriel Mountains.

**2. The length changed.** The finish moved from the Rose Bowl, and the course went from 101.9 to 100.2 miles in 1992.

**3. Fire shapes it.** Recent fires forced out-and-back courses and a 2026 Altadena start.
