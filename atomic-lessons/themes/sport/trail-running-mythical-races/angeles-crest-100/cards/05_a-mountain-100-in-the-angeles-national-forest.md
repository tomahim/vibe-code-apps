The course runs through the San Gabriel Mountains, with climbs such as Mt. Baden-Powell that the official course page highlights in its elevation profile and 3D map. Runners deal with exposed ridges, remote canyons and big temperature differences between day and night.

Organisers publish an estimated elevation profile each year, because changes to the course alter the total climbing. That is why the AC100 is better understood as a course with an official history of changes than as one fixed set of numbers. The 2025 3D terrain map is based on the course GPX file and starts zoomed in on the climb of Mt. Baden-Powell.
