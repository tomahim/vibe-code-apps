Fire has changed the race in recent years. The official history says the 2025 race returned to the "Out-and-Back" course first used in 2022 because of extensive fire damage in both the opening and final sections of the traditional route. The Eaton Fire closed trails near Altadena, and the Bridge Fire affected the Acorn Trail and Blue Ridge near Wrightwood.

The out-and-back route added elevation and technical challenge. Organisers prefer the traditional point-to-point course, but safety and access come first. It is a reminder that mountain races depend on land that can be closed by weather, fire and recovery work.
