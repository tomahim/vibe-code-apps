Many famous races say they never change, but the AC100 openly keeps a record of changes. The page is titled Course History and Changes and covers the move of finish lines, fire reroutes and the 2026 Altadena start.

That honesty is part of the legend. A mountain race is a conversation between runners and a landscape that can change. The race's message is that the challenge is meant to stay the same even when the map does not. The next lesson looks at a race with an even more unusual design philosophy. Reading the full page is a good way to see how a race and a landscape change together over decades.
