The 36th Angeles Crest 100 was scheduled for October 3, 2026, the first October race since 2008. Organisers expected cooler temperatures to help runner performance and finisher rates.

Because of storm damage to Highway 2 and the impact of recent fires, the 2026 race used a modified loop. For the first time in race history it started and finished at Loma Alta Park in Altadena. The route passed through places like Clear Creek, Mt. Josephine, Red Box, Newcomb, Shortcut and Chilao, turned around near Mt. Hillyer, and returned through Chantry, Mt. Wilson and the front range trails. The organisers said they remain committed to returning to the traditional route when access and safety allow.
