The first Angeles Crest 100 was held in September 1986. According to the official course history, race management has worked to preserve the original point-to-point route from Wrightwood to Altadena, in the San Gabriel Mountains near Los Angeles, while adapting to the realities of fire damage, trail closures and wilderness protections.

A point-to-point race is different from a loop. Runners move from one town to another, which gives the day a feeling of travel. Over the years the course has seen temporary reroutes, but the official line is that the spirit and the challenge of the AC100 remain unchanged. Keeping the route as close to the original as possible is a way of respecting the people who first ran it.
