The race book describes how the finish changed over time. The original finish line was at the Rose Bowl, and in 1992 it was replaced by Johnson Field. This shortened the course from 101.9 miles to 100.2 miles. The current finish at Loma Alta Park was adopted in 1998.

These details matter because many people assume a 100-mile course is exactly 100 miles from the beginning. The AC100 shows how organisers adjust finish lines for practical reasons such as space, access and safety. The official course length is 100.2 miles, not exactly 100. Runners who compare times across years should remember that the finish has moved, even if the start town is the same.
