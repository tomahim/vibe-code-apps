title: "Why the AC100 matters"

The AC100 is one of the classic United States 100-milers and a clear example of a mountain trail race. It began just three years after Leadville and a few years after Western States became an independent race.

It also tells a modern story. Wildfires and storms are changing how races are planned, and the AC100 shows how organisers can respond without losing their identity. The commitment to return to the traditional route when access and safety allow shows how much the point-to-point history means to the community. For runners looking at the history of 100-milers, it is a useful example of a race that evolved without losing its character.
