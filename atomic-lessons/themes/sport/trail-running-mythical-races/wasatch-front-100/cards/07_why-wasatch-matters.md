Wasatch shows how trail racing can start with almost nothing. There was no website, no registration and no marked course in 1980, only a map and a few stubborn friends. That makes its history a good contrast to the large organised races you will meet later in this path.

It is also a story about community. Race director Richard Barnum-Reece, route mapper Steve Baugh, Evelyn Baugh at the wheel of the aid vehicle and Laurie Staton at the front all contributed. Decades later, the race still attracts runners who love long, high, difficult courses and the comradeship that comes with them.
