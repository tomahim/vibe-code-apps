Steve Baugh used Forest Service 1:24,000 quadrangle maps to highlight a point-to-point course. It combined cross-country route finding, existing trails, dirt roads and paved roads. The route often crossed private land, and no attempt was made to ask landowners for permission.

There was no course marking and no written directions. At a spaghetti dinner on the Friday night, the runners reviewed the maps and agreed that one of them would lead navigation for each section, so they could stay together. Evelyn Baugh, Steve's wife, was the only mobile aid station, driving the family's old car to meet runners wherever the roads allowed.
