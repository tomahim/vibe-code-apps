The official history by Dana Miller says that Wasatch, and trail ultrarunning in general, changed considerably over 40 years. In 2021, 165 of 298 starters finished, compared with two of five in 1980. That is a huge contrast with the early years when the finish was uncertain.

What did not change, in Miller's words, was Wasatch's "mind-boggling difficulty", the beauty of the course and the comradeship between runners, volunteers and organisers. The race still runs through the Wasatch Mountains with a large amount of climbing, and the history has been kept alive through annual race articles and personal stories. The numbers show that the sport has become safer, better organised and better understood, while the course remains a serious mountain test.
