Richard Barnum-Reece conceived the first Wasatch Front 100 Mile Endurance Run sometime in the summer of 1980. He was already known in Utah for two early trail races, the Flight of the Eagle and the Park City 40-Miler. He told Steve Baugh about the idea of a hundred-mile trail race in the Wasatch Mountains, and Baugh agreed to map a route between a chosen start and finish.

The first race was set for September 27 and 28, 1980. According to the official history there was little publicity, and no one remembers registering or paying an entry fee. News travelled inside the small Utah trail ultrarunning community, and five experienced runners decided to try it.
