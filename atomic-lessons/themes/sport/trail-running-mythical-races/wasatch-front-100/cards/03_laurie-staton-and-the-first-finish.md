The five original entrants were Richard Barnum-Reece, Steve Baugh, Laurie Staton, Greg Rollins and Jan Cheney. Laurie Staton was a woman in a field of men, but the history says that thought had not even entered her head, even though only a handful of women had finished a 100-miler before 1980.

During the race Greg Rollins fell behind at one point, and she realised she was leading when a store clerk at Brighton asked who was ahead. In the end Staton and Rollins arrived together near Sundance. Richard Barnum-Reece declared the first Wasatch Front 100 finished after 35 hours, 1 minute and 21 seconds. Two of the five starters completed the course.
