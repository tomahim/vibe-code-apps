**1. It began with five.** Five runners tried the first Wasatch 100 in September 1980.

**2. It was almost unmarked.** Steve Baugh drew the route on maps, and Evelyn Baugh was the only aid station.

**3. It grew steadily.** The first race had two finishers of five, and in 2021 165 of 298 finished.
