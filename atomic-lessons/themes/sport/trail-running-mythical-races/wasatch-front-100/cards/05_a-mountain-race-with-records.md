Over the years the Wasatch course became a place for records and legends. The history lists course records falling in 1985, when Dana Miller shattered the course record, and again in 1986 and 1990. Miller later wrote the history and finished 20 times himself.

The race also became part of the "Grand Slam" of ultrarunning. The yearly reports mention Grand Slammers, runners who finish several of the classic 100-mile races in one summer, and note two more in 1987 and eleven finishing in 1989. Wasatch is seen as one of the hardest of those races, which is why the title mind-boggling difficulty appears in the history.
