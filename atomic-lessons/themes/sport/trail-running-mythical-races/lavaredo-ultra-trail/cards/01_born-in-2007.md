The Lavaredo Ultra Trail was born in 2007. The official UTMB page calls it the dream of all trail runners, held in one of the most evocative landscapes of the UNESCO World Heritage Dolomites, around Cortina d'Ampezzo. That town hosted the 2026 Winter Olympics.

The iRunFar profile of race director Cristina Murgia explains that her business partner Simone Brogioni had just launched an innovative endurance event in Italy, the Lavaredo Ultra Trail, now a 120-kilometre ultramarathon through the Dolomites. It started in 2007, two years before the Dolomites were inducted as a UNESCO World Heritage site.

According to iRunFar, the Lavaredo Ultra Trail started in 2007, and UNESCO inducted the Dolomites as a World Heritage site in 2009.
