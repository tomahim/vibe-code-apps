title: "Why Lavaredo matters"
archived: false
---
Lavaredo matters because it shows how scenery can be the core of a race. Its history is shorter than Western States or Pikes Peak, only since 2007, but its location gives it instant recognition. The Three Peaks of Lavaredo are among the best known rock formations in the Alps.

It is also an example of the modern path of trail racing: an independent start, rapid growth, and then integration into a world series. For runners, it is a dream because of the combination of technical trails, high mountain views and a well-organised event in the Dolomites. That combination has made it one of the Italian events that runners put on their wish list.
