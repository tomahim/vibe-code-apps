The Dolomites protect an area of about 550 square miles across nine mountain ranges and five Italian provinces in the northeast corner of the country. From the air they look like an archipelago, a scattered collection of spires, needles and pinnacles.

The iRunFar profile calls the landscape awe-inspiring and geologically unique. UNESCO inducted it as a World Heritage site in 2009. For a trail race, this means a course in a protected mountain area with famous rock walls and a postcard view at nearly every turn. Those facts explain why the race organisers talk about the landscape first and the distance second. The pale rock walls change colour through the day, so the same course looks different at dawn and at dusk, and runners who start in the afternoon cross the high sections in the dark.
