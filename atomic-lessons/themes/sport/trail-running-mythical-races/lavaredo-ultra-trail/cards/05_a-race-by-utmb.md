The race's current name is La Sportiva Lavaredo Ultra Trail by UTMB. The by UTMB label shows that it belongs to the UTMB World Series, the circuit of more than 70 events that grew out of the Mont Blanc race you saw earlier.

Being part of the series gives runners qualification points and gives the race access to a common technology and communication platform. It also shows how an independent Italian race, created in 2007, joined a global network without losing its local roots in Cortina and the Dolomites. For a runner, the label means a familiar rules framework and an international standard of organisation. It also means the results count toward qualification for the finals in Chamonix, so a good run at Lavaredo can open a path to the original race.
