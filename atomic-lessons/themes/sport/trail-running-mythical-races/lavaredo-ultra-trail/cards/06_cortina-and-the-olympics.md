Cortina d'Ampezzo is a historic mountain town, and the official page notes that it hosted the 2026 Winter Olympics. The same landscapes that attract winter sports fans in the cold months attract trail runners in June.

This overlap between winter and summer sport is common in the Alps. Mountain towns develop infrastructure for one season and find that it also supports another. Lavaredo grew in that environment, with roads, mountain huts and local support that make a big event possible. The dates, the town and the peaks together make the race easy to place on a map, and a trip to Cortina in late June combines hiking, scenery and racing for visitors who do not run at all. For the town it also fills hotels at the start of the summer season.
