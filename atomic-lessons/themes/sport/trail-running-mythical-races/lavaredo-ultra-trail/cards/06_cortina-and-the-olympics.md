Cortina d'Ampezzo is a historic mountain town, and the official page notes that it hosted the 2026 Winter Olympics. The same landscapes that attract winter sports fans in the cold months attract trail runners in June.

This overlap between winter and summer sport is common in the Alps. Cortina is a mountain town where one place serves two seasons.

According to the Cortina tourism page, the race is held every year in late June around the Tre Cime di Lavaredo. The official site lists the 120K flagship with an elevation gain of 5,800 metres, and the race is part of the UTMB World Series.
