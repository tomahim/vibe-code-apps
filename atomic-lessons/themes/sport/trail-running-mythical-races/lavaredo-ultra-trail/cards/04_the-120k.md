The flagship race is the Lavaredo 120K, which the official site lists with an elevation gain of 5,800 metres. It is the most important of five race distances offered by the event, and it is awarded 4 Running Stones for qualification in the UTMB system.

The event also offers an 80K and shorter races, with the shortest listed at 300 metres of positive gain. The number of distances shows the same approach as other race families in this path: a flagship ultra surrounded by shorter races that let more people take part. The dates in 2027 range from June 23 to 26.
