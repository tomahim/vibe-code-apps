According to the Cortina d'Ampezzo tourism page, the event is held every year in late June in the Dolomites surrounding Cortina d'Ampezzo and the Tre Cime di Lavaredo. It takes in the most spectacular peaks of the area, such as the Three Peaks of Lavaredo, Cristallo, Tofane, Sorapis and Croda da Lago.

Since 2007 the event has attracted thousands of international athletes and offers technical high mountain trails. It is part of the UTMB World Series circuit, which confirms its place among the world's major trail races. This is a good example of a race that is defined by its mountains.
