Twenty-one races that shaped trail running, from Pikes Peak in 1956 to Mt.FUJI 100 and the SaintéLyon. Built from official race histories, [Ultrarunning History](https://ultrarunninghistory.com/), [iRunFar](https://www.irunfar.com/), the [Golden Trail World Series](https://goldentrailseries.com/) and the [UTMB World Series](https://utmb.world/). The Marathon des Sables is a desert stage race, included for its place in ultrarunning history.
