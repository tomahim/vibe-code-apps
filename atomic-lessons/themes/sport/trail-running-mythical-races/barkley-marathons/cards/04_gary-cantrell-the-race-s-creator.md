In 1978 Gary Cantrell was an accounting student at Middle Tennessee State University and an experienced marathon runner with eight finishes. In 1979 he and his friends, the "Horse Mountain Runners", created the Strolling Jim 40-mile run in Wartrace, Tennessee, named after a famous horse. Cantrell is the man who created the Barkley Marathons in 1986.

In 1981 he organised "The Idiot's Run" in Shelbyville, 76 miles with 37 significant hills. He explained the idea of his races: the objective is not so much to see who finishes first as to see who survives for the longest distance. He later extended the distance of that run to 115 to 120 miles in 1984.
