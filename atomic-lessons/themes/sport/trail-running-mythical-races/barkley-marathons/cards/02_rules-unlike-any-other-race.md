The entrance fee includes bringing a license plate from your home state or country. Runners receive the course directions the day before the race, and they are not told exactly when the race will start. They only get a one-hour warning when a conch is blown.

To prove they ran the course correctly, books are placed at various points. Runners must tear out the page matching their bib number from each book. If they lose a page or miss a book, they are out. In contrast to most ultras, the course is designed to minimize the number of finishers. The system is simple but unforgiving, and it keeps the race honest without any electronic timing at all.
