title: "Why the Barkley is a legend"

---
The Barkley is a legend because it reverses the usual idea of a race. Most events try to help runners finish, with marked courses, aid stations and public information. The Barkley hides information, uses books and a conch, and is built to limit finishers.

For runners, the appeal is the challenge and the mystery. For history, the race is an example of how a place's stories can be turned into a sporting event. The Ultrarunning History article is the best source because the Barkley has no official website. You can read it free online, along with the podcast version. It is the opposite of a race that tries to make the finish easy.
