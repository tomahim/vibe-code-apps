Cantrell and his friend Karl Henn had hiked in Frozen Head State Park many times during the 1970s and became interested in it. Nearby was Brushy Mountain State Prison, famous because of the 1977 escape.

The Cumberland Mountains, the history notes, were made rugged by erosion that cut deep gorges, with crags, bluffs and caves. In the late 1800s Tennessee leased convicts to work coal mines, and in 1896 the state started mining on land that included part of the future Barkley course. The prison and mines gave the place a history that is part of the race's story. The article spends many paragraphs on this background because, in its view, the history of the land is the history of the race.
