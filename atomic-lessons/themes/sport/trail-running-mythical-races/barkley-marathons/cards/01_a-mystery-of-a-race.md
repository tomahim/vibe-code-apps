The Barkley Marathons is described by Ultrarunning History as perhaps the most difficult ultramarathon trail race in the world, with a historic low finish rate. It is held in and near Frozen Head State Park in Tennessee over a distance of more than 100 miles.

The event is full of mystery. It has no official website, it is unclear how to enter, and no course map or entrants list is published online. It is not a spectator event. For the 2018 race, 1,300 runners applied and only 40 were selected. Those who want to enter must submit an essay, which is part of the legend.
