The Mont-Blanc Marathon kept adding races. The Vertical Kilometer was created in 2011, an 80 km race in 2013, and the Duo Etoile and the Young Race in 2015. Today the event brings together more than 10,000 runners each year across eight races in the Mont-Blanc massif: the 90 km, the 42 km, the 23 km, the 10 km, the Vertical Kilometer, the Duo Etoile, the Young Race and the Mini-Cross.

The 80 km race of 2013 is now listed as the 90 km, which shows that distances evolve over time. The race family gives different levels of runners a way into the mountains, from children to ultra runners.
