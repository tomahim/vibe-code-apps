The race is organised by the Club des Sports de Chamonix, an association founded in 1905 under the law of 1901. It has 25 sports sections and 4,000 members. Besides the Mont-Blanc Marathon, the club organises two other international events: the Kandahar Ski World Cup and the Climbing World Cup.

The club's missions include promoting access to sport for the entire population of the Chamonix valley, especially young people, and helping competitors reach the highest level. The programme includes the Young Race and the Mini-Cross. The marathon is a community event as well as a competition.

The official history describes the organiser as an association founded under the law of 1901, with 25 sports sections and 4,000 members.
