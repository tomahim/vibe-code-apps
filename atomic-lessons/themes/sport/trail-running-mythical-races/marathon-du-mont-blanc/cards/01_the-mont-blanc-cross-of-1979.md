The race began on July 1, 1979, as the Mont-Blanc Cross-Country Race. According to the official history, it was launched at the initiative of the French Alpine Club of Chamonix, then chaired by Georges Costaz and Christian Roussel, both keen runners.

For this first event, 450 participants ran on the trails of the valley and finished at Planpraz. Over the next 24 years the Mont-Blanc Cross grew in popularity, attracting nearly 1,000 participants each year. That is a long time before the days of trail-running boom, and it shows that Chamonix had a mountain running tradition well before UTMB appeared in 2003.
