title: "Mandatory gear and respect"
archived: false
---
Runners must carry mandatory equipment: a working cell phone, at least 1 litre of water, a waterproof jacket with a hood that fits properly, a survival blanket, a cup, a whistle and some cash. No race bibs are issued to runners who do not have their mandatory gear and identification documents.

The organisers also warn that anyone who shows disrespect towards volunteers or the organising committee responsible for enforcing time limits can be permanently banned from all Mont-Blanc Marathon events. Gear and respect are two sides of the same idea: the mountain is serious, and a race depends on volunteers who protect runners.
