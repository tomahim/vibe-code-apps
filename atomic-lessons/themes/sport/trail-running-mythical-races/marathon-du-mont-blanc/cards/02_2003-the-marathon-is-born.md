In 2003 the race took a new direction and became the Mont-Blanc Marathon. The iconic 23 km distance was joined by the 42 km race that people still know today. Next came a 10 km race, which was more accessible to the general public.

From that moment registrations increased so fast that organisers had to limit the number of runners who could enter the Cross and the Marathon, starting in 2010. It is interesting that the same year 2003 saw the first UTMB in the same valley. Chamonix offered a short mountain marathon on one side and an ultra around the massif on the other.
