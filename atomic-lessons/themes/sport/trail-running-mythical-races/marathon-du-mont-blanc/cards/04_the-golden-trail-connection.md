In 2018 the event took on a new dimension when Salomon created the Golden Trail World Series. The 42 km Mont-Blanc Marathon was part of the series through 2024, according to the official history.

The Golden Trail World Series is a circuit of races for fast mountain runners, and the Mont-Blanc Marathon provided a classic alpine setting, just as Zegama and Sierre-Zinal did. Being part of the series brought international attention to a race that had started in 1979 with 450 participants. It also shows how races can be linked together as a circuit while keeping their own character. The circuit's races are chosen for their scenery and for the level of the runners who line up.
