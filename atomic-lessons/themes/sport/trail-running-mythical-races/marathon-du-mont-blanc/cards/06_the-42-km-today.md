The official page for the 42 km sets a maximum race time of 10 hours, counted from the start of each wave. There are time cuts along the way: at the Tour aid station the start time of the last wave plus 2 hours 45, at Vallorcine plus 5 hours 30, and at La Flegere plus 8 hours 30. Runners who arrive after a cut-off are disqualified, and the lower right corner of their bib is cut off by the station chief.

The organisers also reserve the right to stop the race or change the time limits in case of bad weather, for safety reasons. In the mountains, safety rules are part of the course.
