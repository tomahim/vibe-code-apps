title: "Why the Tor matters"

---
Tor des Geants matters because it changed what an ultra can be. Instead of a 100-mile race in one day and night, it is a week-long journey through a whole region. The idea of going non-stop, with no compulsory stages, creates a different kind of challenge.

It also keeps a local identity in its name, from the valley's patois, and in its support from municipalities. It shows how far the sport has gone from Gordy Ainsleigh's single day on the Western States Trail in 1974.

The first edition in 2010 had 179 finishers out of about 300 runners over 330 km and more than 24,000 metres of climbing, which shows how new the idea was.
