title: "Why the Tor matters"
archived: false
---
Tor des Geants matters because it changed what an ultra can be. Instead of a 100-mile race in one day and night, it is a week-long journey through a whole region. The idea of going non-stop, with no compulsory stages, creates a different kind of challenge.

It also keeps a local identity in its name, from the valley's patois, and in its support from municipalities. For many runners, finishing the Tor des Geants is the most demanding event of their lives. It shows how far the sport has gone from Gordy Ainsleigh's single day on the Western States Trail in 1974.
