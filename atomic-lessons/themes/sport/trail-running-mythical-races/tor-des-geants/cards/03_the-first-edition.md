The first Tor des Geants took place in September 2010. The DUV statistics list it as the 1st Tor des Geants, 330 km Endurance Trail della Valle d'Aosta, from September 12 to 18, 2010, with 179 finishers (164 men and 15 women). A blog about the early years says 179 of 300 runners reached the finish line in Courmayeur after 330 km and over 24,000 metres of elevation gain.

That is a high finish rate for such a hard race, about 60 percent. Later editions grew and brought more international attention. The race starts and finishes in Courmayeur, a town at the foot of Mont Blanc.
