The official site presents the race as more than a sporting event. It is also a way to promote tourism in the Valle d'Aosta, through the municipalities the race passes through. The 2010 press kit lists 34 municipalities involved in the first edition.

The event therefore resembles Leadville and Zegama, where a race supports a community. Local villages and valleys welcome runners during the week. That community support is a large part of the character of the race. Every municipality along the route plays a part in welcoming runners, which turns the race into a regional celebration instead of a single event in one town.
