The course is 330 km long with about 24,000 metres of elevation gain, according to the first edition reports and later descriptions. The race follows the Alta Via 2 route of the region and goes through Gran Paradiso Natural Park and Mont Avic Regional Park. The Wikipedia article gives the minimum altitude as 300 m and the highest point as 3,300 m.

There are no compulsory stages. According to the official page, runners must complete the course in the shortest time, making their own decisions on when and how long to stop for rest and refreshment. That makes sleep strategy part of the race.
