TOR330 is part of the TORX family of events, a group of races united by the same experience and values. The official page says finishing a TORX eXperience race means having the numbers to try the TOR330.

This is similar to how UTMB uses qualifying points, and it reflects a trend toward helping runners build up experience before taking on the hardest events. The 150-hour time limit is also a reminder that this is not just a race but an expedition, where runners sleep in life bases and manage weather, altitude and fatigue over almost a week. The approach also gives newcomers a clearer way to prepare for the toughest event in the family.
