The official page for the 2026 race says the TOR330 would take place from September 13 to 19, 2026, starting from Courmayeur in two groups on Sunday, September 13 at 10:00 and 12:00. The finish must be reached within a maximum time of 150 hours, by 6 pm on Saturday, September 19. The awards ceremony was set for Sunday, September 20.

Registration follows a system called TORX eXperience. Finishers of races in the TORX system receive points, called PAX, whatever their ranking position, and these help them to try to finish the TOR330. That is another way the race tries to make sure that participants are prepared.
