Tor des Geants comes from the Valle d'Aosta patois, the language commonly spoken in the valley. A press kit translates it as "Giro dei Giganti", or Tour of the Giants. The press kit describes the first edition as 150 hours no-stop among the giants of the Aosta Valley.

The first edition was held from September 12 to 18, 2010. A year before, in 2009, an "Edition Zero" tested the idea. The official site describes the race as the first of its kind to cover an entire region, running along its paths at the foot of the highest Four-Thousanders in the Alps.
