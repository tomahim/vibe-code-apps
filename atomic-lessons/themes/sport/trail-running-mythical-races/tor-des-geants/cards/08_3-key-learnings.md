**1. Giro dei Giganti.** The name comes from the Aosta Valley patois, and the first race took place in 2010.

**2. 330 km, 150 hours.** There are no compulsory stages, and runners decide when to stop and sleep.

**3. A regional project.** The race promotes tourism in the municipalities of the Valle d'Aosta.
