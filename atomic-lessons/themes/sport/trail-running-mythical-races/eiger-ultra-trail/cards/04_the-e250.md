The longest race is the E250 UNESCO Trail. The 2027 listing shows 261 km and 18,000 metres of elevation gain. It is far longer than the E101 and requires days of continuous effort.

The name points to the UNESCO World Heritage setting of the region. The Eiger event kept its own identity, with views of the Eiger, Monch and Jungfrau. Length alone makes it an expedition, with runners managing sleep, food and weather over several days in the Swiss mountains.

The first Eiger Ultra Trail on July 20, 2013 was a 101 km race, and the 2027 listing shows the E101 at 6,700 metres of gain. At 261 km, the E250 is more than two and a half times longer.
