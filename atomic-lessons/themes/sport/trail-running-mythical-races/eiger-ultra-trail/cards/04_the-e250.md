The longest race is the E250 UNESCO Trail. The 2027 listing shows 261 km and 18,000 metres of elevation gain. It is far longer than the E101 and requires days of continuous effort.

The name points to the UNESCO World Heritage setting of the region. The creation of such a long distance shows how the sport changed after the first ultra trail in 2013: runners wanted multi-day tests like the Tor des Geants. The Eiger event kept its own identity, with views of the Eiger, Monch and Jungfrau. Length alone makes it an expedition, with runners managing sleep, food and weather over several days in the Swiss mountains.
