The first race in 2013 was the 101 km, and the DUV statistics record 6,685 metres of elevation gain. The 2027 listing on the official site shows the same E101 Ultra Trail at 101 km and 6,700 metres of gain, together with the E51 Panorama Trail, 51 km and 3,100 metres, the E35 North Face Trail, 35 km and 2,500 metres, the E16 Pleasure Trail and a Trail Surprise.

That family of distances is typical of modern race festivals. There is a flagship ultra and a ladder of shorter races for people who prefer less than 100 km. All the races take place in July in Grindelwald, in the middle of the Bernese Alps.
