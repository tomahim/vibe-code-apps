**1. Under the North Face.** The Eiger is 3,970 m high and its North Face was first climbed in 1938.

**2. Started in 2013.** The first race was in Grindelwald, the first ultra trail in the Bernese Oberland.

**3. Distances from 16 km to 261 km.** The E101 and E250 are the long races, with safety and sustainability as core values.
