The Eiger Ultra Trail uses the line "Run where legends rise". The official page explains that the Eiger stands at 3,970 metres above Grindelwald and has enthralled visitors for decades. It is known as the "last problem of the Alps".

The Eiger North Face was finally conquered in 1938 in a battle that lasted four days. Today, world-class climbers tackle it solo in less than two and a half hours. The race organisers say this is where legends have always risen, and now trail runners write their own chapter in that story. The Eiger gives the race its backdrop, and the history of the mountain gives it its tone.
