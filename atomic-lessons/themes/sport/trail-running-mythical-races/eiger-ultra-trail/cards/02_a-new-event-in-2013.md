The first Eiger Ultra Trail was held on July 20 and 21, 2013. A press release from December 2012 said Grindelwald was launching the Eiger Ultra Trail, the first ultra trail in the Bernese Oberland, at the foot of the Eiger, Monch and Jungfrau.

The planned course passed places such as Grosse Scheidegg, First, the Faulhorn mountain hotel, Schynige Platte, Wengen, Mannlichen, Lauberhorn and Kleine Scheidegg, with a traverse under the Eiger North Face. Start and finish were in the glacier village of Grindelwald, at the gateway to a UNESCO site. A runner who took part called it a flawless event on a first go, and credited Ralf Naf and his team.
