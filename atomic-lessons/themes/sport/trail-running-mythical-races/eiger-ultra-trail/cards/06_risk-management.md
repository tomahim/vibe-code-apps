The official values page states that safety is the number one priority. The organisers say they are aware of their responsibilities and try to manage risks in a connected way, so that runners and event staff return from the trail healthy and safe.

In a high alpine race this is an essential commitment. Weather can change quickly near the Eiger, and long distances increase risk. The organisers also keep their continuity in the core team, because experience matters for safety.

The values page also lists continuity in the core team and respectful, honest teamwork, and says that requests and suggestions by participants are examined.
