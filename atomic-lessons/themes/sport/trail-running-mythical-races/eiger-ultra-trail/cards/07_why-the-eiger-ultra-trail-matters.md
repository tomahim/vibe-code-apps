title: "Why the Eiger Ultra Trail matters"

---
The Eiger Ultra Trail matters because it brought the ultra trail format to the Bernese Oberland and linked it to one of the most famous mountains in the world. The race joined the UTMB World Series and is now officially called Eiger Ultra Trail by UTMB.

It also shows the typical story of a modern race: a strong location, a first edition with a volunteer-driven team, and growth through a ladder of distances. For runners, the appeal is the chance to run under the North Face, a place known to climbers worldwide since 1938. The race also links the sport's history to the history of mountaineering, since the same wall that was once the last problem of the Alps now sits above a running course.
