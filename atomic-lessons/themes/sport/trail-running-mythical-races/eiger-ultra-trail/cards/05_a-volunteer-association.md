The organisers describe the Eiger Ultra Trail community with a set of values. They stress continuity in the core team and the other volunteers, safety as number one priority, respectful and honest teamwork and high quality. They also say they want the race to generate meaningful value for the local economy and to be a role model for sustainable tourism.

The official values page says the team is open, honest, respectful and reliable. It also says that requests and suggestions by participants are taken seriously and examined. These values are a good example of how modern race organisers present their mission, with attention to the local community and the environment.
