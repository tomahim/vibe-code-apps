The race has grown into a large festival. The official site lists ten events for the 72nd edition on November 28 and 29, 2026. They include the main SaintéLyon with nearly 8,000 runners at the start, relay versions for two, three or four people, and shorter or special formats. Since 2019 there has also been the Lyon SaintéLyon, an ultra format that adds an outward leg from Lyon to Saint-Étienne before the return.

The 70th anniversary edition on November 30 and December 1, 2024 had more than 19,000 registered runners across all formats, according to CTLyon. The race is now the end-of-season rendezvous of French trail running.
