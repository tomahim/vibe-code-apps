title: "Why the SaintéLyon is mythical"
archived: false
---
The SaintéLyon is mythical because it combines history, mass participation and a unique atmosphere. A winter walk of cyclists became the oldest ultra in France, and a night start turned the line of headlamps between two big cities into an image known across the country. The race is run in late autumn, in cold and often wet conditions, so each year has its own story.

It also shows another side of trail running. Not every classic is a mountain race: some are mixed routes that connect cities and communities. It also shows how a small club project can grow: a walk by fifteen people in 1952 became a race with nearly 8,000 runners on the main start line.
