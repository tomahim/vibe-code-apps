The SaintéLyon began with cyclists, which its current director Michel Sorine calls the first paradox of the story. In the hard winters of the past, snow stopped the Lyon cyclotourists from riding in early December, so they walked to visit their friends in Saint-Étienne through the Monts du Lyonnais. The trip took them two days.

In 1951, cyclists from both cities decided to turn this into a timed winter event: a walk of about 60 km over two days, following the GR 7 long-distance path and reserved for walkers. The Cyclotouriste Lyonnais club, known today as CTLyon, created it, and the first event was planned for the winter of 1952.
