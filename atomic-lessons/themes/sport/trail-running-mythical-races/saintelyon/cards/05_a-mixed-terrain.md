The SaintéLyon is a trail race and a road race at the same time. CTLyon describes the course as 65 percent paths and 35 percent asphalt, and French Wikipedia says about two thirds of the course is on foot paths and the rest on asphalt. This is an honest way to place it in this path: it is not a pure mountain trail like Hardrock, but a mixed-terrain classic that is widely treated as a trail race.

The route crosses hills, forest tracks, villages and urban edges. The highest point is below 900 metres, so the difficulty comes from distance, mud, cold and darkness more than from altitude.
