**1. Cyclists started it.** Lyon and Saint-Étienne cyclotourists created a winter walk on the GR 7; the first edition was on January 26, 1952.

**2. A night run since early on.** The midnight start led to today's 23:30 departure from Saint-Étienne to Lyon.

**3. Mixed terrain.** About 65 percent paths and 35 percent asphalt, around 80 km with at least 2,000 m of climbing.
