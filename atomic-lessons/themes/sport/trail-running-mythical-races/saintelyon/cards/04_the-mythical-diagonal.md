Today the main race starts in Saint-Étienne and finishes in Lyon, at the Halle Tony Garnier. The organisers call it "the mythical diagonal", a historic route from Saint-Étienne to Lyon through the crests of the Monts du Lyonnais. The course changes every year, but it stays around 80 km with at least 2,000 metres of positive elevation gain.

One recent official page for the 78 km version listed 2,017 metres of ascent and 2,338 metres of descent, with several timed barriers on the way. Examples are Saint-Christo-en-Jarez after 4 hours 15, Sainte-Catherine after 7 hours and Lyon after 16 hours 30. The numbers show that it is a long night effort, but with a smaller climb than the big alpine ultras.
