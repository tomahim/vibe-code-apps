The first raid between Lyon and Saint-Étienne took place on January 26, 1952. According to CTLyon, fifteen pioneers set off at 1 pm into the snow, wearing mountain boots and carrying backpacks. French Wikipedia adds that 23 people had registered, seven did not start, and a Lyon insurance agent entered on cross-country skis and went ahead of the walkers.

The history describes an event with a small field and little attention. At the time it seemed impossible to link the two cities without stopping, especially at night. The raid was done in two stages, with a night in an inn at Sainte-Catherine. Roger Benetiere is named by Outside as the winner of the first edition.
