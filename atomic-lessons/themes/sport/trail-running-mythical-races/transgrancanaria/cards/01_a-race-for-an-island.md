Transgrancanaria is a mountain ultramarathon held on Gran Canaria, one of the Canary Islands in Spain. The Spanish encyclopedia says it was born in October 2003 with the goal of showing the beauty and the contrasts of the island through a mountain race. The organiser Arista Eventos has run it since the first edition.

The idea is to cross the island on foot, moving from the north coast across the interior to the south, passing through ravines, pine forests and volcanic landscapes. That route shows many faces of Gran Canaria in one race, from small mountain villages to dry coastal landscapes.
