An island race has natural advantages. The terrain changes quickly with altitude, from beaches to high ridges within a few kilometres, and the climate is mild for much of the year. Gran Canaria's ravines, pine forests and villages give organisers many different landscapes to choose from.

This lesson is about the ideas behind Transgrancanaria, and similar ideas appear in other islands you will see in this path, such as Reunion and La Palma. Runners can experience several landscapes in one outing, which is a strong reason for the popularity of island ultras. The sea always stays in view, which is part of the appeal.
