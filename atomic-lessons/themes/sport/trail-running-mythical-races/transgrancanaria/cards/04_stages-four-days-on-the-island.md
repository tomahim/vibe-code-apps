The official site also presents the Transgrancanaria Stages, a four-day experience with four different challenges, totalling 120 km and 6,886 metres of elevation gain. The stages include Open Agaldar, a new 21 km fast and runnable race debuting in 2027, and the KV El Gigante, an uphill race of 5.5 km with 1,090 metres of gain linking the Agaete Valley with the Tamadaba pine forests.

The third stage is the Promo, a 12 km race between Artenara and Tejeda, near the Tejeda Caldera. The fourth is the 81 km Advanced. The format shows how the race has evolved from a single ultra into a multi-day festival.
