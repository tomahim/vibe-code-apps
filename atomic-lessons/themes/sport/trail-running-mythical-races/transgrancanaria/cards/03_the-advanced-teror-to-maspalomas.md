Today the race is organised as a set of events. The official website describes the Advanced, one of the iconic races of the Transgrancanaria, covering 81 km from Teror to Maspalomas through the heart of the island. Teror is a town in the north of Gran Canaria, and Maspalomas is on the south coast, famous for its dunes.

The route goes from inland mountains to the sea, so runners finish where tourists come for the beach. That contrast is part of the race's character. It is a long descent in the second half after a strong test of climbing, and heat can be a factor in the south.
