**1. Born in 2003.** The race began in October 2003 to show the contrasts of Gran Canaria.

**2. It grew quickly.** A recap says the first edition had 78 runners and 3,000 registered by 2016.

**3. Today it is a festival.** The Advanced is 81 km from Teror to Maspalomas, with shorter races around it.
