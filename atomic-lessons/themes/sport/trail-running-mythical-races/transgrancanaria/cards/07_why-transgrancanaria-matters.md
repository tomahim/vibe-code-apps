Transgrancanaria matters because it shows how a race can build a destination. Since 2003 the event has put Gran Canaria on the map of international trail running.

It also shows how a race can change over time without losing its identity. The 2003 event with 78 participants and the stage races of today share a mission: to show the island's contrasts through running. The next lessons move to the Alps, where trail running became a global sport. Race and island have grown together, and each helps explain why the other is famous.

The organiser, Arista Eventos, has run the race since the first edition. The official stage page lists a 21 km fast race, a 5.5 km vertical kilometre, a 12 km Promo and the 81 km Advanced, so the event now offers a route for many kinds of runners on the same island.
