The official stage page shows constant innovation. A new fast race debuting in 2027, a vertical kilometre, a middle-distance Promo and a flagship Advanced give runners many entry points. The organiser says the Transgrancanaria is always looking ahead, trying to be at the forefront and creating proposals that serve as a reference for other races.

That attitude explains how the race moved from 78 runners to thousands. It built a calendar that suits beginners and experts, kept the scenic island routes, and kept the island's scenery at the center of every event. Each new format is an invitation for a different kind of runner to see the island.
