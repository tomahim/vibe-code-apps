A recap of the race's history says the first edition in 2003 had only 78 participants and a 64 km course. In 2016 the race had five different events, more than 400 volunteers and around 3,000 registered runners from 70 countries.

That growth is large, but it happened over about thirteen years. The recap also says Marco Olmo, a famous Italian ultrarunner, played an important role in making the race popular. The example shows how a regional event can become an international one with a mix of good organisation and a well-known name.
