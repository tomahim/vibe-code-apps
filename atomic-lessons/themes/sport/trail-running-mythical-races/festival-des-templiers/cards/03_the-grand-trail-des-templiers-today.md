The main race is the Grand Trail des Templiers, and the official description starts with the night start. The race begins in the dark in Millau area, first on the road toward the hamlet of Carbassas. Then runners climb to the plateau by the cherry trees path and by the steep Carbassas hill, a wide but very steep climb where about 2,500 headlamps can be seen in the night.

After sunrise the route passes across the causse by forest tracks, then reaches Peyreleau by a descent above the Tarn valley, where the first single track begins. Later sections follow the Jonte, climb to the hermitage of Saint Michel and cross villages like Saint-Andre-de-Vezines, where accordion players cheer runners.
