The route passes emblematic places such as the perched village of Montmejean, the cliffs of Rajol, and Roquesaltes with its famous natural arch. It also includes a lively section in the Riou Sec, followed by La Roque-Sainte-Marguerite with its narrow streets.

The official page gives a feeling of a journey through villages as much as through nature. Thousands of spectators gather at the first aid station and along the course. The Larzac and the Causse Noir are known as technical terrain for runners, with rock, limestone and exposed plateaus, which is why the paths are considered very demanding even without huge altitude.
