The route passes emblematic places such as the perched village of Montmejean, the cliffs of Rajol, and Roquesaltes with its famous natural arch. It also includes a lively section in the Riou Sec, followed by La Roque-Sainte-Marguerite with its narrow streets.

The official page gives a feeling of a journey through villages as much as through nature. Thousands of spectators gather at the first aid station and along the course.

In the official description, the first single track begins at Peyreleau, after a descent above the Tarn valley. Later sections follow the Jonte, climb to the hermitage of Saint Michel and cross villages such as Saint-Andre-de-Vezines, where accordion players cheer runners.
