Today the race is part of the Festival des Templiers, held in Millau in October. In 2024, a franceinfo report counted 13 races over three days with 11,000 participants, on the weekend of October 19 and 20 for the most symbolic races. The event was renamed Hoka Les Templiers for its 30th edition.

This growth shows how a single race can become a gathering for a whole sport. There are races for beginners and experts, and the festival includes the full community around trail running, from volunteers to spectators. The 1995 founders could not have imagined this size, but the original idea of a friendly challenge in the local landscape is still visible.
