The founders' model is a good example of how ideas travelled. The Western States Endurance Run, where a runner proved in 1974 that a person could run a horse trail, and the Leadville 100, created in 1983 to revive a mining town, inspired a French couple twelve years later to use their own landscape.

This transfer of ideas is the heart of trail running's history. Sources describe the Templiers as the oldest trail event in France and the place where French trail running started.

Gilles Bertrand and Odile Baudrier launched the race in spring 1995 in the Parc naturel regional des Grands Causses, in the Aveyron. The first start was on October 28, 1995, with 500 runners on a 65 km course.
