title: "Why the Templiers matter"
archived: false
---
The Templiers matter because they made trail running visible in France. The race is described as the oldest trail event in France, and it gave the country a first grand trail with a mass start, a night departure and a festival atmosphere.

For this learning path, the Templiers show how a race can combine sport and heritage. The Templar name, the shepherd's bag and the villages of the Larzac make the event a celebration of local culture as much as of running. It is also a reminder that the history of trail running is international, with ideas moving from the United States to France.
