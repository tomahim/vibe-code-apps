In spring 1995, Gilles Bertrand and Odile Baudrier took out of storage an old project: to create a trail race based on the model of the Western States and the Leadville 100, two reference races in the United States. They launched it in the Parc naturel regional des Grands Causses, in the Aveyron, in southern France.

The first start was given on October 28, 1995, to 500 runners at the foot of the ramparts of Sainte-Eulalie-de-Cernon, for a 65 km course. The event is considered the oldest trail event in France and is often described as the place where French trail running started.
