The first edition of the race took place in 1989, and back then it was not yet called La Diagonale des Fous, or even the Grand Raid de la Reunion. It was named "la marche des Cimes", the march of the summits. The distance was 112 km with 5,400 metres of positive elevation, running from Saint-Denis to Le Tremblet.

According to the official statistics, 500 people started and 314 finished, a finish rate of 62.8 percent. Since then the race has been held every year at the same time, at the end of October. The name La Diagonale des Fous means roughly the Crazy Ones' Diagonal, and the route crosses the island from south to north.
