The 34th Grand Raid was held from October 15 to 18, 2026. According to local media the main race started on Thursday, October 15, at 10 pm from the seafront of Saint-Pierre. It was described as the longest course in the history of the Diagonale, about 180 km with 10,200 m of climbing, with a route changed for the year.

For the first time the route did not pass through the Cilaos cirque, a historic central part of the race, because of constraints on the island. The change shows that even a race with a clear identity must adapt to land and safety conditions, just like the Angeles Crest 100 you saw earlier in this path.
