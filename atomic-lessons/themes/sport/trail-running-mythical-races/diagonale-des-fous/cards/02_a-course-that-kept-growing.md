The official table of editions shows how the race changed. In 1990 it measured 126 km with 5,200 m of climbing, starting from Saint-Joseph and finishing in Le Port. By 1997 it was 125 km with 7,900 m. In 2012 the course reached 170 km with 10,800 m of climbing, running from Saint-Philippe to Saint-Denis.

From 2013 the start moved to Saint-Pierre, and distances in the following years were around 163 to 167 km with roughly 10,000 m of climbing. So the Diagonale is not a fixed course but a route that organisers adapt to the island's conditions, always keeping the idea of a big crossing from south to north.
