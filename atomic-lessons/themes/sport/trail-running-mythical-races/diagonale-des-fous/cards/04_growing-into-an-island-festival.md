The numbers also show how the race grew. In 1989 there were 500 starters. By 2008, 2,195 people started the main race, and in 2012 and 2017 the number was above 2,500. The 2026 edition has 2,839 starters, according to a local news report.

This growth reflects the race's place in Reunion's culture. The Grand Raid runs several races over several days around the main event, five courses in the 2026 programme. The Diagonale des Fous, though, remains the race that everybody in Reunion and in the trail world means when they say the Grand Raid. It is a festival of the whole island more than a single start line.
