The Diagonale des Fous crosses volcanic terrain on an island in the Indian Ocean, and the early routes started in places such as Saint-Philippe, in the south, and ended in places like Saint-Denis, in the north. Over decades the finish shifted among towns such as Le Tremblet, Le Port, Sainte-Marie, La Possession and La Grande Chaloupe before settling in Saint-Denis.

The island offers extreme variety within a small area: coastal towns, tropical forest, high crater edges and cold early mornings. Runners often describe passing through several climates in one race. The official records include the time of the last finisher in some years, for example 65 hours 47 minutes in 2011.
