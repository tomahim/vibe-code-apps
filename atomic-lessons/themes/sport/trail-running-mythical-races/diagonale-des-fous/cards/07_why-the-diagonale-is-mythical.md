title: "Why the Diagonale is mythical"
archived: false
---
The Diagonale des Fous is mythical because it combines distance, climbing and island culture. It began as a 112 km "march of the summits" with 500 starters and grew into a ten-thousand-metre race with thousands of runners. Its official statistics show the dropout rates openly, which is part of its identity.

It also gives a clear picture of trail running's growth since 1989. The race's history, kept on the official site, shows editions side by side, so you can see how the route, the distance and the number of runners evolved. For many runners, finishing it once is a lifelong achievement.
