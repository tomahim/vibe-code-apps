title: "The hardest race in the world?"

The Diagonale des Fous is often cited as the hardest race in the world, according to the organisers themselves, so they decided to calculate the dropout rate for every year. The numbers support the reputation. In 2011, 2,350 runners started and 1,228 finished, a finish rate of 52.26 percent. In 2014, the finish rate was 51.99 percent.

In better years it is higher. In 2017, 2,573 runners started and 1,830 finished, a 71.12 percent finish rate. Even so, roughly three or four out of ten entrants usually do not finish. The race combines steep climbs and descents, a night start, tropical heat and sometimes rain, which wears down even experienced runners.
