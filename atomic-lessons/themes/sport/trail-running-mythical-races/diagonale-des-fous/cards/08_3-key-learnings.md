**1. It began as a march.** The 1989 'marche des Cimes' was 112 km with 5,400 m of climbing and 500 starters.

**2. It grew into a giant.** By 2012 it measured 170 km and 10,800 m, and recent editions reach about 180 km.

**3. It is very hard to finish.** Finish rates in the official table range from about 52 to 71 percent.
