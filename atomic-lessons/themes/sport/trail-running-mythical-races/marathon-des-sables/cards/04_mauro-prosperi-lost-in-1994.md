The most famous MDS story took place in 1994. Italian runner Mauro Prosperi was lost in a sandstorm and wandered alone for nine days. To survive he drank his urine and ate bats and small dead birds. He was eventually found by a nomadic family more than 200 km from the official route.

The story became one of the most striking in the race's history and was later told in the Netflix series "Losers", according to the official site. It showed how serious a desert race can be,. He later returned to running, and the incident remains one of the first stories people mention when they describe how seriously the desert must be treated.
