The most famous MDS story took place in 1994. Italian runner Mauro Prosperi was lost in a sandstorm and wandered alone for nine days. To survive he drank his urine and ate bats and small dead birds. He was eventually found by a nomadic family more than 200 km from the official route.

The story became one of the most striking in the race's history and was later told in the Netflix series "Losers", according to the official site. It showed how serious a desert race can be.

The official site places the story in 1994, eight years after the first race in 1986 with 23 pioneers. It is retold as one of the great survival stories of the Marathon des Sables.
