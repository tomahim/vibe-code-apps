One of the lighter stories came in 2019, when an unexpected competitor joined: a dog. The route passed near his home, and he started following runners on the second stage. To everyone's surprise he completed all five stages, covering more than 200 km.

The official history calls him Cactus, the four-legged hero. The story shows another side of the race, where unexpected events become part of the legend. It also reminds us that the MDS is a human adventure with a strong community and sense of humour. He did not wear a bib in the usual sense, but the race treated him as part of the field for the week, and his finish is now remembered as one of the sweetest stories in desert racing.
