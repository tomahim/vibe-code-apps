One of the lighter stories came in 2019, when an unexpected competitor joined: a dog. The route passed near his home, and he started following runners on the second stage. To everyone's surprise he completed all five stages, covering more than 200 km.

The official history calls him Cactus, the four-legged hero. The story shows another side of the race, where unexpected events become part of the legend. It also reminds us that the MDS is a human adventure with a strong community and sense of humour.

The dog joined the runners on the second stage, when the route passed near his home, and then kept going through all five stages, more than 200 km in total. The official history presents the story as part of the race's community spirit, next to the stories of Mauro Prosperi in 1994 and the floods of 2009.
