The Marathon des Sables, or MDS, was born in 1986 with 23 pioneers in the Moroccan Sahara. According to the official history, it was originally designed for ultra-trail runners ready to face more than 250 km in food self-sufficiency. It has since established itself as one of the toughest races in the world.

This lesson is included in a trail-running path with a clear note: the MDS is a desert stage race, not a mountain trail race. Runners cover the distance in stages and carry their own food, with a bivouac camp each night. It remains in the list because its stories are central to ultrarunning culture.
