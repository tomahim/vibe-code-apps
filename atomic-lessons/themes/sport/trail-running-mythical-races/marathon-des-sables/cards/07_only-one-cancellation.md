In 2020, for the first and only time since its creation in 1986, the MDS was cancelled because of the Covid-19 pandemic. The official history calls it a painful but inevitable decision and notes that it remains the only interruption in nearly four decades.

That record says a lot about how reliable the race has been. Floods, sandstorms and desert heat have shaped its stories, but the event continued every year until a global health emergency stopped it. It is a good final lesson about why the MDS belongs in this path, even if the terrain is sand instead of mountain.
