The MDS has also tried new places. In 2001 the Desert Cup was held in Jordan for three years, a non-stop ultra of about 168 km from Petra to the Wadi Rum desert, in food self-sufficiency. It was the first MDS experience outside Morocco.

In 2009 unprecedented floods submerged the bivouac and led to the cancellation of the first stage. The long stage was extended to 92 km, and the total distance was reduced to about 202 km. Winners called it the hardest edition they had ever run. In 2011 the 26th edition passed 1,000 runners for the first time.
