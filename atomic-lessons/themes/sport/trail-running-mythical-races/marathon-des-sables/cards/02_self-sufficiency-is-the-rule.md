The key idea of the MDS is that runners carry their own food for the whole race, which is what the organisers mean by food self-sufficiency. The race covers more than 250 km, so what a runner chooses to pack becomes a strategic decision in addition to the running itself.

Today the MDS offers seven formats across six destinations, with more than ten events each year. They all follow the same philosophy, which the organisers describe as living an unforgettable human and sporting adventure in some of the most unique landscapes on the planet. The original Sahara race is still the one most people mean when they say MDS.
