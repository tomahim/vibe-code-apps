In 1989 the official MDS logo was created: the face of a runner wrapped in a cheche, eyes protected by goggles and the pipette of a water bottle clenched between the teeth. The image became one of the most recognisable in ultrarunning.

In 1995, at the tenth anniversary, the history records that more than 1,500 men and women had marked the desert with their strides. That year, at the initiative of competitor Gilles Flamant, a water pump was installed for the people of the village of De'Ighef n'Rifi. The organisers call it a success of solidarity, and it was repeated in later years.
