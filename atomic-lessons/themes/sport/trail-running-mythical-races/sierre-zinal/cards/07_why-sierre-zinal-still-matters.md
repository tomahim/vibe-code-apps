In 2024 the race celebrated its 50th edition, and the WMRA described it as traditionally the most competitive of the World Cup long-distance races. It is still one of the most prestigious races in the Alps. The reasons are simple: a clear idea from its founder, a route that shows off the valley, and a philosophy that welcomes everyone.

It also shows how a local project can become global. A professor and mountain guide wanted to promote his valley, and half a century later the race attracts the best runners alive and thousands of Tourists who just want to run among the peaks. The next lesson moves to the United States and the 100-mile format.
