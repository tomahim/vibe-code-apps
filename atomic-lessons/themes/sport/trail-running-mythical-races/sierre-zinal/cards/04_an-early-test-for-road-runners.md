Sierre-Zinal grew into a classic for runners from different backgrounds. The winners list includes Jonathan Wyatt of New Zealand, who won in 2003 in 2:29:12, and Ricardo Mejia of Mexico, who won several times in the early 2000s. The 2002 edition is listed separately in the table with a much shorter winning time, so compare times carefully.

Because the course is only 31 km, it rewards speed as well as climbing. Road runners and mountain specialists both have a chance, which makes it a meeting point of the two worlds. The women have always raced the same distance as the men.
