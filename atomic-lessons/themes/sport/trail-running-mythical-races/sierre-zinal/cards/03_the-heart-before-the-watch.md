Sierre-Zinal has a humanist philosophy at its core. The organisers created a "Tourist" category, unique to the race, for runners whose only aim is to get together, push their limits and test themselves against a challenging trail. Tourist runners receive a certificate with their final time but no ranking.

The idea is summed up by the line "the heart before the watch." Personal goals, camaraderie, fun and appreciation of nature are valued as much as winning. Tourists run the whole distance both as participants and as spectators of an event attended by some of the best runners of their time. It is one reason the race feels both elite and welcoming.
