The name of the race says what it promises: the "Race of the Five 4000 m Peaks". Runners see the Weisshorn, the Zinalrothorn, the Obergabelhorn, the Matterhorn and the Dent Blanche, a group of summits the organisers call the "Imperial Crown". The Matterhorn, at 4,478 metres, is the best known of them.

The race starts in the town of Sierre and finishes in Zinal. The official site describes a 31 km mountain trail with a 2,200 metre elevation gain and a 1,100 metre vertical drop in the heart of the Valais Alps. Almost all of it is run high on the mountainside, so the view of the peaks is a large part of the experience.
