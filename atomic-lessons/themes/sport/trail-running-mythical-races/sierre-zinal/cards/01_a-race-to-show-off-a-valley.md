Mountain races were not common in the 1970s. Jean-Claude Pont, a doctor of mathematics, professor of history and philosophy of science at the University of Geneva and certified Swiss mountain guide, imagined a running event in the Anniviers mountains. His idea was that people could rediscover the virtues of physical effort by taking on a unique running challenge.

The race would also help the Anniviers valley in the central Valais. He prompted the tourist industry to focus on racing opportunities and wrote a book, "Courir dans une Cathedrale", about the first forty years of the event. The race was first run in 1974 and has always been a mix of sport and valley tourism.
