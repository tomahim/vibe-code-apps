A report from the WMRA on the 2004 edition describes a 31 km race with an ascent of 2,000 metres, run almost entirely at an altitude between 2,000 and 2,500 metres. That height explains the views and also the effort. Thin air makes climbing harder, and the weather can change fast in August.

The race is held in the Swiss canton of Valais each August. The route moves from the Rhone valley up into the Val d'Anniviers and finally down to Zinal, a ridge-line journey where you are never far from a summit. The WMRA noted that the organisers staged a wonderful event with a great atmosphere, a description that still fits.
