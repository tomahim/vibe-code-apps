The winners table shows how often one man took the title. Kilian Jornet of Spain won Sierre-Zinal at least seven times between 2009 and 2019, with wins listed in 2009, 2010, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018 and 2019. In the same period, Kenya's Lucy Wambui Murigi won repeatedly in the women's race, including 2015, 2017 and 2018.

These streaks show why the race is considered a world-class test. The same rivals returned year after year, and the course distance did not change, so the results are easy to compare from one season to the next. The event also became part of the World Mountain Running Association's long distance series.
