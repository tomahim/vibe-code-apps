Encouraged by the success of the Trail 100 Run, Chlouber and Maupin founded the Leadville Race Series, which now includes races through the year. Examples listed on the official site are the Silver Rush 50, the Leadville Trail Marathon and Heavy Half, and the Leadville Stage Race.

The series has become one of the most popular and prestigious endurance race series in the world, attracting thousands of athletes and spectators to Leadville each year. It shows how a single successful event can grow into a whole calendar, with local businesses, volunteers and hotels all benefiting. The series now runs through the year, which spreads the economic benefit across seasons rather than a single weekend.
