**1. A mining town in trouble.** Leadville boomed with silver in the 1870s and struggled after the Climax Mine closed in the 1980s.

**2. A race for tourism.** Ken Chlouber and Merilee Maupin founded the 100-mile run in 1983 so visitors would stay overnight.

**3. A race that gives back.** The Legacy Foundation was created in 2002 and funds scholarships for local graduates.
