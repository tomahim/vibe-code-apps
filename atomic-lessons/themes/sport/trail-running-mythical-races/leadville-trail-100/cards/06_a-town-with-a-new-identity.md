Today Leadville is described as a thriving town with a rich history and a bright future. Although mining has declined, its past is still visible in the architecture and landmarks, including the Tabor Opera House, the National Mining Hall of Fame and Museum, and the Matchless Mine.

The town now offers skiing, fat biking, fishing and hiking, but it is the Leadville Race Series that gave it a modern identity. The history says Leadville has become "synonymous with endurance sports". It is a good example of how an outdoor event can change what a place stands for. Visitors who come to run often return in other seasons, which is exactly the effect that Chlouber was hoping for.
