In 2002 Ken and Merilee created the Leadville Trail 100 Legacy Foundation, a nonprofit to address the ongoing needs of the Leadville and Lake County communities. Every dollar goes directly to meet community needs.

The Trail 100 Scholarship Program was created in 2009. Today the foundation awards a $3,000 scholarship to every graduating senior of Lake County High School, to use toward higher education. Runners can race as official charity athletes for the foundation. The race therefore returns value to the town that started it, which is the original idea of the 1983 event. That link between race and community is what makes Leadville different from a purely commercial event.
