After Gordy's success, a second runner, Ron Kelley, tried in 1975 but withdrew about three miles from the finish. In 1976 Ken "Cowman" Shirk completed the distance about 30 minutes over the 24-hour mark. Then, in 1977, 14 men from four states took part in the first official Western States Endurance Run, held alongside the Tevis Cup ride.

Runners were checked by Dr. Bob Lind at the veterinary stops built for the horses. Three of them finished: Andy Gonzales, age 22, in 22:57, and Peter Mattei and Ralph Paffenbarger, aged 53 and 54, who tied in 28:36. Their tie is the origin of the 30-hour award. In the fall of 1977 the board of directors for the run was formed.
