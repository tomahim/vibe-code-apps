**1. It began with horses.** Wendell Robie's 1955 ride and the Tevis Cup made the Western States Trail famous.

**2. One runner changed it.** Gordy Ainsleigh ran the trail in 23 hours 42 minutes on August 3, 1974.

**3. It became official in 1977.** Fourteen starters and three finishers began a race that grew to be independent in 1978.
