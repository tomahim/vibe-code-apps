The official winners list shows that in 2012 the UTMF measured 156.0 km with 8,530 metres of elevation gain. The winner was Julien Chorier of France in 18 hours 53 minutes 12 seconds, and the first woman was Nerea Martinez Uruzola of Spain in 24 hours 5 minutes 4 seconds.

In 2013 the distance was 161.0 km with 9,164 metres. Yoshikazu Hara of Japan won in 19:39:48, and Kristin Moehl of the USA won the women's race in 24:35:45. The official list shows how the route changed from year to year, as with other mountain races. The list also shows how international the sport was already in 2012, with winners from France, Spain, Japan and the United States in the first two years.
