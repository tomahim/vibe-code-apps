The first winners show the international side of the race. A French runner won in 2012 and a Spanish woman won the women's race. In 2014 the winner was Francois D'Haene of France, in 19:09:13, according to iRunFar.

Being linked with UTMB gave the race a model, and the Western States qualifier status connects it with the oldest 100-mile race. This chain of connections shows how trail running is a global community, where a Japanese race can refer to an Alpine one and an American one.

Julien Chorier won the 2012 race in 18 hours 53 minutes 12 seconds, and Nerea Martinez Uruzola of Spain was the first woman, according to the official winners list.
