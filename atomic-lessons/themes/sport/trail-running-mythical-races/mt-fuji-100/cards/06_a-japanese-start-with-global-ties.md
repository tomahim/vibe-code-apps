The first winners show the international side of the race. A French runner won in 2012 and a Spanish woman won the women's race, both associated with the brand Salomon. In 2014 the winner was Francois D'Haene, who appears in the runner path of this series.

Being linked with UTMB gave the race a model, and the Western States qualifier status connects it with the oldest 100-mile race. This chain of connections shows how trail running is a global community, where a Japanese race can refer to an Alpine one and an American one. Such links are one of the reasons the sport spreads so fast between countries.
