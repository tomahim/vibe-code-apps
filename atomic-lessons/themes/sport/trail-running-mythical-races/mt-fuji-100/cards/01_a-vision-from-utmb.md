The Ultra Trail Mt. Fuji, known as UTMF, was the brainchild of Tsuyoshi Kaburaki, a Japanese ultrarunner who performed well at UTMB and Western States. According to iRunFar's 2012 preview, he first went to UTMB five years earlier and that led to his vision of creating a similar race in Japan.

Preparation started about two years before the first race. It was meant to happen in 2011, but the tsunami forced its cancellation. The first UTMF was therefore held in 2012. The Japan Times calls it Japan's first 100-mile race and the first sister race to UTMB, which has been held in Europe since 2003.
