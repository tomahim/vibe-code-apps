Mt.FUJI 100 matters because it opened trail running to Japan at the 100-mile scale. It was Japan's first 100-mile race and it was founded by a runner who had seen the UTMB.

It also shows the sport's final lesson in this path: a good idea can travel. Western States inspired Leadville and the Templiers, UTMB inspired UTMF, and each race adapted the idea to its own landscape and culture. Mount Fuji is a sacred and symbolic mountain, and the race circles it as a journey through its communities. Seeing the loop as a cultural journey, not only a distance, is the idea that the organisers put at the center of their concept page, and it is a good way to end this path on the races that shaped trail running.
