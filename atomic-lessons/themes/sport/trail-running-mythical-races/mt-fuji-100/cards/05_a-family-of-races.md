The Mt.FUJI 100 is not only a single 100-mile race. The KAI 70K measures 67.9 km with 2,917 metres of gain. The ASUMI 40K is about 38.5 km with 1,481 metres of gain and is a World Trail Majors eligible race. The SAKUYA is a short race of 8.6 km, with a U20 race of 5.7 km.

The range lets different runners take part, from beginners to experienced ultrarunners. This is similar to the family approach used by Eiger, Lavaredo and UTMB. The same venue, Fuji Hokuroku Park, serves as the start and finish for several of the races. Each race has its own start time, recruitment number and qualifying role.
