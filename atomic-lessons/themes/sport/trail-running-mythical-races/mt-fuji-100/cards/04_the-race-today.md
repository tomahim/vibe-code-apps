The current official information lists the main race, FUJI, at 165.3 km with 6,463 metres of cumulative elevation gain. The start is at Mount Fuji Children's World, and the finish at Fuji Hokuroku Park, Fujisan GX Stadium. The recruitment is 2,000 people, with start waves of about 700 runners.

The race is a Western States Endurance Run qualifying race and part of the ITRA National League. Other races in the event include the KAI 70K, the ASUMI 40K and the SAKUYA, a new public race on a trial basis in accordance with mountain racing rules. The name Mt.FUJI 100 now covers the whole event.
