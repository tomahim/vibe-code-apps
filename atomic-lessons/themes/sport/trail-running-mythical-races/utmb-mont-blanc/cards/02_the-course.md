The official UTMB page says the race was created in 2003 and presents it as the most mythical and prestigious trail running race in the world, with approximately 170 kilometres and 10,000 metres of positive elevation gain around Mont Blanc through Italy, Switzerland and France.

The UTMB World Series description gives 171 km and 9,963 metres of total ascent, with a high point of 2,567 metres. The route changes slightly from year to year, and the 2026 listing showed 174 km and 9,700 metres of gain. The race starts in Chamonix on a Friday evening, with a maximum time of 45 hours 30 minutes in the 2026 listing.
