Internationalisation began in 2014 with the creation of the UTWT, bringing together international races. In 2017 the group developed UTMB International with OC Sports. Today, UTMB Group is the organisation behind the UTMB World Series, a global circuit of more than 70 events that let runners experience the spirit of UTMB closer to home.

The same group runs Livetrail, a digital platform for endurance race tracking and management. It also has a team of 120 employees and a network of partners, volunteers and organisers.

The official 2026 season announcement says nine new events joined the circuit that year, so it was still growing. The official calendar covers all five continents, which is what lets runners take part in the UTMB spirit without traveling to Chamonix.
