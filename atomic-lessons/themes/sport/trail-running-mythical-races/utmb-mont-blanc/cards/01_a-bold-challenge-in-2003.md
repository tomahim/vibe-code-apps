The UTMB Group story began in 2003 in the Chamonix valley. Catherine and Michel Poletti and a group of passionate friends launched a bold challenge: to run around Mont Blanc, crossing three countries, France, Switzerland and Italy. At that time, according to the official history, trail running was still relatively unknown.

From the start the organisers built an original model with a company and an association working hand in hand. The event quickly gained recognition and grew into a global celebration of trail running. The race is the heart of everything the group does today. Its mission is to bring people together and connect them with nature through responsible events in natural landscapes.
