title: "Why the UTMB matters"
archived: false
---
UTMB matters because it made trail running a global sport. It gave the discipline a flagship race, a live streaming platform, a world series and a clear path for runners to follow.

It is also a story about the power of a simple idea. A loop around the highest mountain in the Alps, with a start in Chamonix and a finish in Chamonix, was enough to build a worldwide movement. The official history says the project is a story of friendship that became a world benchmark. Whatever you think of its size, UTMB changed how races are organised. Many races in this path were already famous before it began, but it gave them a shared stage.
