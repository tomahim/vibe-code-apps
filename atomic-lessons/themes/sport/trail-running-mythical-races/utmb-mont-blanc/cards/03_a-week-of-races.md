Over the course of one week, the UTMB gathers eight races and about 10,000 runners from more than 100 nationalities. The finals include the UTMB itself, the CCC at 100 km and the OCC at 50 km. Experienced runners are selected by ballot with a fixed number of places for around ten years.

They race across preserved terrain while thousands of supporters cheer them on along the course and millions of fans follow the adventure on UTMB Live. The structure makes the week in Chamonix a festival, not only a race. The final runners often cross the line in the dark or at dawn, which gives the event a unique atmosphere.
