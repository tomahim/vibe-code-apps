The UTMB made a significant choice by using qualification and ballot. Runners collect qualifying points, called Running Stones in the race listings, and then enter the ballot for places. The official site says the number of places is fixed and has been for around ten years.

The system helps manage safety and the impact on local communities. It also made the process of getting to Chamonix a story in itself, with runners planning their season around qualifying races. That is one reason the world series spread so quickly, since each race gives runners a chance to qualify. The main goal is fairness for runners and a manageable event for the valley.
