The route passes through France, Italy and Switzerland, with mountain passes, villages and long climbs and descents. Because the loop returns to Chamonix, the race is simple to understand: run all the way around the mountain.

The 2026 start time of 19:45 on Friday means runners spend the first night on the trail and may see two sunrises before finishing. The time limit of 45 hours 30 minutes gives a cut-off for the slowest finishers. Together, these details show how structured and carefully managed the event is, in contrast to early trail races like Wasatch in 1980. For the runners, the race is an exercise in managing sleep as much as legs.
