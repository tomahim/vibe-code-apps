The island history says that despite being relatively young, Transvulcania has had exponential growth, becoming one of the most prestigious races with the highest participation in the calendar, not only in Spain but worldwide. It is described as one of the hardest mountain ultramarathons in the world and one of the most important in Spain.

The first winners in 2009 included Salvador Calvo Redondo among men and Marta Prat among women. The race's winners list is public, so you can compare times over the years. It shows an event that quickly attracted top athletes and became a regular fixture of the ultra calendar.
