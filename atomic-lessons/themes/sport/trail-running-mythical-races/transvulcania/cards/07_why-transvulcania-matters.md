title: "Why Transvulcania matters"

---
Transvulcania matters because it shows how a local government can create a world-class race. The Cabildo de La Palma, the island authority, organised the first edition in 2009 and used the race to promote the island.

It also gives a clear example of the lesson of island races in this path: a compact geography with huge elevation changes. In one day the runner starts at sea level, climbs to the island's highest point and finishes in a town square, with the race route linked to historic hiking trails. Few races give such a clear picture of an island in one day. It also shows how public authorities can use a sporting event to put a place on the international map.
