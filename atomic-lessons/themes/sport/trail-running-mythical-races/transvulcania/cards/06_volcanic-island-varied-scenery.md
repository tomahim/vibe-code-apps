La Palma's volcanic origin shapes the race. Runners move from black volcanic soil near the south to pine forests, high ridges and then a long descent to the sea in the west. The island history says the route offers images of volcanoes, forests and black sand beaches.

For runners the key challenge is the profile. The route climbs from sea level to the island's highest point and then drops steeply to the west coast. That makes it a test of climbing and descending. The contrast between the black volcanic soil of the start and the town square of the finish is part of the race's charm.
