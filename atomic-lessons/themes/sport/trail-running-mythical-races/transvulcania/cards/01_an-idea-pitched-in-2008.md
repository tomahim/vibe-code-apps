A history of the race says that in 2008 Julio Cabrera met the authorities and proposed the idea of a 74 km race with a spectacular landscape on La Palma, the island known as the beautiful island of the Canaries. The island has strong personality, volcanoes, forests and black sand beaches.

In April 2009 a newspaper announced the first Trans-Vulkania mountain ultramarathon on July 25, organised by the island's government, the Cabildo de La Palma, with support of the Canary Mountaineering Federation. Athletes from Germany, France and Italy were expected. The race has taken place since 2009. The island setting was chosen on purpose to show off La Palma's volcanic landscape.
