The official page describes the Transvulcania ultramarathon as 73.06 km. It starts at the Fuencaliente lighthouse at 6:00 in the morning, and it finishes at the Plaza de Espana in Los Llanos de Aridane.

The route follows the GR 131, also known as the Ruta del Baston, to the Puerto de Tazacorte. From there it connects to the GR 130, the Camino Real de la Costa, to the finish line. The plan is simple: start at the southern tip, cross the island's spine and finish in the west. The finish in the town square lets spectators greet runners in the middle of Los Llanos, after a day that began in the dark at the southern tip.
