The official page calls Transvulcania one of the toughest trail running races in the world. It has 4,350 metres of cumulative positive elevation gain and 4,057 metres of negative elevation gain. The start line is at sea level, and the course climbs up to the Roque de los Muchachos, the highest point of the island, before descending to Tazacorte and finishing in Los Llanos de Aridane.

The official table shows how fast the climbing begins: the first aid station, Los Canarios, is at 715 metres only 7.4 km from the start, and Las Deseadas at 1,827 metres is at 17 km. A long descent follows.
