title: "Why Zegama is mythical"

---
Zegama is mythical because it comes from a community instead of a business plan. A picnic, a federation's challenge and a hand-drawn route created a race that is now a pillar of international trail running. In 2007 a 19-year-old Kilian Jornet made his debut there and won, and he went on to win eleven times, nearly half of all editions held. In 2011 the race was broadcast live for the first time, and it joined the Golden Trail World Series in 2018.

It also shows what makes trail races last. Local pride, volunteers and a respectful relationship with the mountain keep the event authentic. A quarter of a century after its first edition, the race still carries the original spirit of that picnic in 2002, and runners still say that Zegama is Zegama.
