Few can say exactly when Zegama became a global reference. Aierbe recalls that in the second edition the race became part of the Spanish Mountain Running Cup, and by the third edition it had joined the World Cup, which began to attract the world's top athletes. The Golden Trail World Series suggests the answer lies in a village fully committed to its race, with care, authenticity and passion.

Today Zegama is often listed among the most prestigious trail races in the world. On May 17, 2026, it celebrated its 25th edition by opening the 2026 Golden Trail World Series calendar. It is proof that a small corner of the Basque Country can become, for one day, the global heart of the sport.
