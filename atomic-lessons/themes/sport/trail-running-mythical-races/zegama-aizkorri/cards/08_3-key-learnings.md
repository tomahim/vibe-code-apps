**1. Born from a picnic.** Villagers and the regional mountain federation turned a picnic into a mountain marathon in 2002.

**2. A hand-drawn route.** Alberto Aierbe's 35 km map evolved into today's 42 km course.

**3. A village of 1,500.** The community and its volunteers make the race, and 'Zegama is Zegama'.
