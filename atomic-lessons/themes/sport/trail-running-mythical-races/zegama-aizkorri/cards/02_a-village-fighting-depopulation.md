The idea of the race appeared in the early 2000s. The village wanted an event that would attract visitors and give new life to a place facing rural depopulation. Zegama is organised by a village of barely 1,500 inhabitants, as the Golden Trail World Series points out, and the race is an expression of collective passion by the Basque people.

The first edition took place in 2002. What began as a local initiative quickly gained momentum thanks to the enthusiasm of local institutions and volunteers, and the wild beauty of the surrounding mountains. Like Leadville in the United States, it shows how a race can help a small community.
