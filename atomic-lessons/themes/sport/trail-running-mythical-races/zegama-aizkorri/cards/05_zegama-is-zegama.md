The most famous phrase about the race was coined by Kilian Jornet: "Zegama is Zegama." The Golden Trail World Series says this phrase perfectly captures the spirit of a race unlike any other in the world.

What the phrase means is that the race cannot be compared to others. The crowds, the mountain, the weather and the atmosphere combine in a way that is specific to the place. The village of barely 1,500 inhabitants welcomes runners from all over the world, and the community's collective effort is visible on the course. It also explains why runners often describe the support as the loudest in trail running.
