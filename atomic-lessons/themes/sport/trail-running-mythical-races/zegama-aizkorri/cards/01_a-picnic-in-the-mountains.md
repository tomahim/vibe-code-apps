Zegama-Aizkorri began in a surprising way. According to the Golden Trail World Series, everything started with a group of local inhabitants and a simple idea: organise a picnic in the mountains to bring people together. For the first picnic, they sent an invitation to everyone from Zegama living outside the village.

After the success of the second gathering, the regional mountain federation raised a challenge: if the picnic worked, why not organise a mountain marathon? The response was immediate. Alberto Aierbe, one of the people behind the event from day one, said, "I already have the route." The race grew out of the community in a very natural way.
