The Golden Trail World Series describes the race as 42 kilometres with 2,736 metres of vertical gain, technical terrain and an atmosphere unlike anywhere else. The route goes through the mountains around Zegama in the Basque Country, with steep climbs and technical descents. Kilian Jornet called it special, and the race organisers describe it as one of the most iconic mountain races in the world.

Because the route has changed so little since the first editions, runners can compare performances across many years. It also means that the race's challenge comes from the terrain, not from added distance. The combination of a compact marathon length, big climbs and fast descents makes it a favourite of mountain specialists.
