The first route was drawn by hand on a 1:25,000 scale map. It was a 35 km course through the mountains around Zegama. With only minor adjustments over time, it evolved into the 42 km course that runners face today, which has remained virtually unchanged.

That stability is rare in modern racing. The original mountain marathon still follows an idea that one local organiser drew on paper, and the four summits of the Aizkorri range remain the heart of the course. The race's distance, 42 km, is the length of a marathon, but with the climbing and the technical terrain it takes far longer than a road marathon.
