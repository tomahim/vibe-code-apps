**1. It started with a notice.** Gordon Hardman announced it in Ultrarunning magazine in fall 1991 for summer 1992.

**2. It honours miners.** The route uses old mining trails linking Silverton, Lake City, Ouray and Telluride.

**3. It reverses direction.** Start and finish stay in Silverton, and the loop changes direction every year.
