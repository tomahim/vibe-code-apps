The official description says the course is on trails as much as possible, but it also includes road sections, maintained trails and cross-country sections. The organisers mark the entire course, though long road sections and maintained trails may not be marked at all, and the cross-country sections are marked more intensely.

The flags have reflective tags for night visibility, and in places the organisers use engineer tape or chalk. The page stresses that the event is not an orienteering race, but remoteness, weather, animals and people problems on the course make navigation important. Runners are responsible for knowing the prescribed course whether markers are present or not.
