title: "Silverton and the changing direction"

After the first year the organisers decided to keep the start and finish permanently in Silverton instead of moving between towns. They also decided to reverse the direction of the loop every year. The race is therefore run clockwise one year and counterclockwise the next.

This choice has an important effect. The same mountains feel different when you climb them in the opposite direction, and the order of passes and aid stations changes. A runner who has finished the race in one direction still faces a new puzzle the next year. The reversal is one of the traits that make Hardrock unlike any other 100-miler.
