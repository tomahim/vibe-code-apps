title: "Why Hardrock is wild"

---
Hardrock is called the wildest 100-miler because of altitude, route and spirit. Climbing to 14,048 feet and spending most of the race above 10,000 feet is unlike any other classic 100-mile race. The route follows trails and cross-country sections that were first used by miners.

It also has a distinct philosophy. The founders wanted to test runners against the mountains and to celebrate the history of the San Juan mining towns. The official description says the race is a test of runners against the mountains. The official page also warns that remoteness, weather and animals can all affect a run, so being self-sufficient between towns is part of the challenge, and respect for the mountains comes before any finishing time.
