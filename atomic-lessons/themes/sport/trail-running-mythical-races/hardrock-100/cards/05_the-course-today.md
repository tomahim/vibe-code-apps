The official course page describes four legs linking Lake City, Ouray, Telluride and Silverton. The course is 101.8 miles long, has a cumulative vertical gain of 33,441 feet and the same amount of descent, for a total elevation change of 66,882 feet. It takes place at an average elevation of about 11,000 feet, and the high point is 14,048 feet.

There are 14 aid stations, with major ones in towns and smaller ones between them. Runners are expected to be largely self-supporting between the towns. The page also says the course is closed, meaning that runners must follow the specified route, and that timberline is around 11,800 feet.
