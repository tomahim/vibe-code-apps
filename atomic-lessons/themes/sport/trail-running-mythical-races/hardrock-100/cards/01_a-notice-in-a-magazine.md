The story of Hardrock begins in the fall of 1991, when Gordon Hardman placed a notice in Ultrarunning magazine announcing that there would be a 100-mile run in the San Juan Mountains of southwestern Colorado in summer 1992. John Cappis contacted him and asked about the course.

The idea was to bring the route as close as possible to the four major mining towns of the San Juan district: Silverton, Lake City, Ouray and Telluride. Cappis volunteered to lay out the course, keeping it on trails as much as possible. It was a simple plan, but the scale of the mountains turned it into one of the most difficult races in the world.
