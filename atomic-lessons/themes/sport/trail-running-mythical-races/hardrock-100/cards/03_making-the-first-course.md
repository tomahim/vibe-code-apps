By January 1992 a route made entirely from map studies existed. Charlie Thorn of Los Alamos, New Mexico, then became active in the process, and by April the necessary government permits were obtained. In June, Thorn, Rick Trujillo and Cappis spent many days checking that the routes were feasible and adjusting them in the field. They added the Dives Little Giant section only one week before the race.

The first course ran clockwise with a nominal length of 98.8 miles and a cumulative vertical gain of 29,684 feet. The start and finish was at Silverton Memorial Park, with a large tent as run headquarters. The major points included Grant Swamp Pass, Oscar's Pass, Virginius Pass, Engineer Pass and Handies Peak.
