One of the clearest signs of Jornet's consistency is Zegama-Aizkorri, the Basque mountain marathon. His record lists many wins there, including a tenth victory and then an eleventh. Few runners have a streak at any race like that.

He has also won the Hardrock 100 several times. At Hardrock the course reverses direction each year, and Jornet set course records in both directions. One of his records was 21:36:24 in the clockwise direction. He also tied for the win in 2016, sharing first place with Jason Schlarb of the United States.

Zegama-Aizkorri is held in the Basque Country each spring and is famous for its huge, loud crowds along the trail. Winning it again and again means beating the best mountain runners in the world on very familiar ground.
