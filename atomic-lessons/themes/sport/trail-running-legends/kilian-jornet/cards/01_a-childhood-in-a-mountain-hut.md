Kilian Jornet Burgada was born on October 27, 1987, in Sabadell, near Barcelona, but he grew up high in the Pyrenees. His family lived in the Refugi de Cap de Rec, a mountain hut at 2,000 metres, close to the Lles cross-country ski area. Mountains were home, not a place to visit.

That start helps explain his style. He grew up skiing, hiking and climbing, and he moved quickly on steep ground without fuss. He also competes in ski mountaineering, and his sister Naila and his partner Emelie Forsberg compete in mountain sports too.

At 2,000 metres the air has noticeably less oxygen than at sea level. Growing up at that height from childhood helps explain why Jornet seems comfortable on high mountains, where many athletes from lower places need time to adjust.
