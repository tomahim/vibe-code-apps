Jornet also runs fast up famous mountains. In August 2013 he set a fastest known time on the Matterhorn from Breuil-Cervinia, going up and down in 2 hours and 52 minutes. He improved the time of Bruno Brunod from 1995 by more than 20 minutes. In July 2013 he did Mont Blanc from Chamonix in 4 hours and 57 minutes.

In September 2012 he completed the Innominata route linking Courmayeur and Chamonix in 8 hours and 42 minutes. In June 2014 he climbed and descended Denali in 11 hours and 48 minutes, using both skis and crampons.

A fastest known time, or FKT, is a record for a specific route, usually without a race organisation. Jornet's times on peaks such as the Matterhorn became famous because the routes are well known and easy for others to compare.
