Other records of Jornet include Kilimanjaro in 7 hours and 14 minutes in 2010. That time was later beaten by Karl Egloff. Records on big mountains often change hands because conditions, equipment and support differ.

He has also set the 24 hour uphill skiing record of 23,864 metres. Jornet has said he climbed Everest twice in 2017 without supplemental oxygen, but this claim has been widely disputed by other mountain runners, and he has not provided evidence that the community accepts. Fastest known times depend on trust and documentation, which is why honest reporting matters in this part of the sport.

Big mountain records are hard to compare because snow, wind, route choice and support can change from year to year. That is why other athletes keep breaking records, and why careful reporting of times is so important.
