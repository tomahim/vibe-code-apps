Jornet won the Skyrunner World Series in 2007, 2008 and 2009. At the time he was the youngest athlete to win that honour. Skyrunning means racing on steep, rocky terrain, often above 2,000 metres, where scrambling and sure footing matter as much as endurance.

He later won more Skyrunner World Series titles, including Ultra and Sky titles in 2012, 2013 and 2014 according to his medal record. He also won world and European championship medals in ski mountaineering. This mix of summer and winter sport is a big part of what makes his career unusual.

The Skyrunner World Series was a global circuit of mountain races in the Alps, Pyrenees, Dolomites and other ranges. Winning it three years in a row as a teenager and then young adult showed that his talent was clear very early.
