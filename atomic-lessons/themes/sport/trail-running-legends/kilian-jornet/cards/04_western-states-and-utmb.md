Jornet has won the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc several times. In 2022 he won it again in a course record of 19:49:30. He has also won the Western States 100 in California, in 2011, after a third place the year before.

These wins show that he is good at very different kinds of race. Western States is a fast, runnable course with canyon heat. UTMB is a long loop around Mont Blanc with huge climbs and descents. Winning both, along with Zegama and Hardrock, is why people often call him the greatest mountain runner of his generation.

Western States is fast and hot, while Hardrock is slow, high and very steep. These are close to opposite challenges, which is why winning both, plus UTMB and Zegama, is such a rare list of achievements in one career.
