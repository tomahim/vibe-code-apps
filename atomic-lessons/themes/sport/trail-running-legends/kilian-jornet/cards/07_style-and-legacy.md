On a mountain, Jornet's approach is light and fast. He has been known to climb in trail shoes with crampons rather than heavy boots. In 2013 he and Forsberg needed rescue by the alpine police at 3,800 metres on the north face of the Aiguille du Midi, a reminder that this approach carries risk.

His influence is large. Many mountain runners now see speed ascents, ski mountaineering and ultra racing as one connected sport, partly because of him.

Ski mountaineering, mountain running and climbing share many skills: efficient climbing, balance on steep ground and careful decisions about weather. Jornet's career shows how these sports can feed each other instead of being separate.
