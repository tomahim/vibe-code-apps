Walmsley was named UltraRunner of the Year in 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2023 and 2025. That is six selections, a mark of consistent excellence over a decade.

His career shows the effect of a bold approach. Even when he does not win, he forces other runners to respond. Many fans see him as one of the main reasons trail running has become faster in recent years.

The UltraRunner of the Year award is chosen by Ultrarunning Magazine after reviewing results across the season. Being chosen six times in ten years shows how consistently Walmsley has been at the top of the sport.

Source: [Western States Endurance Run, records](https://www.wser.org/records/).
