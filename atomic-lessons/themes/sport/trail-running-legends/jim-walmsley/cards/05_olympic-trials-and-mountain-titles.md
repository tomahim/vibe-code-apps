In 2019 Walmsley qualified for the 2020 US Olympic trials in the marathon with his performance at the Houston Half Marathon. He is a rare example of an athlete who can compete at both Olympic trials level on the road and at the front of 100 mile mountain races.

Winning the World Long Distance Mountain Running Championships in 2019 also shows he can do well in a different kind of race, without the rhythm of a long ultra. It is another example of how skills carry across the sport.

Olympic marathon trials need a very fast qualifying time, so reaching them means running close to the pace of the best American road runners. For an ultra specialist, this is a rare combination of skills.
