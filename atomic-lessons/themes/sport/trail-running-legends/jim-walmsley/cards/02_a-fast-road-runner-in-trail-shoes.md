Walmsley won the JFK 50 Mile in 2014, 2015 and 2016, the Lake Sonoma 50 in 2016 and 2018 and the Tarawera Ultramarathon in 2017. He is an ultra trail specialist, but his road speed is part of what makes him unusual.

He also set fastest known times, including the 44 mile Rim to Rim to Rim route across the Grand Canyon in 2016. He has set seven FKTs in total. His career blends fast roads, mountain trails and speed records on famous routes.

The JFK 50 Mile is the oldest ultramarathon in the United States, run since 1963 in Maryland on a mix of trail, towpath and road. Winning it three times shows that he is quick on both trails and fast, flatter surfaces.
