Walmsley won the Western States 100 in 2018, 2019, 2021 and 2024. He set course records in 2018 and 2019, according to Hoka, which lists him as a three time winner and course record holder up to 2021. The 2019 race saw him take another course record in a high snow year.

His racing style is bold. He often goes out fast and tries to build a gap, which means that races can become a test of nerve for both him and his rivals. He was named UltraRunner of the Year four years in a row, from 2016 to 2019.

Course records depend on weather and on the snow in the Sierra Nevada, so each year is different. Setting records in several years shows that he can handle many types of conditions and that his fitness is very high.
