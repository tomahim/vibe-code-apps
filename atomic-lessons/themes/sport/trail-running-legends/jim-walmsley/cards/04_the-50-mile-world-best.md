On May 5, 2019, at the Hoka One One Project Carbon X 100K Challenge, Walmsley set a new world best for the 50 mile distance. He ran 4:50:07, beating Bruce Fordyce's 36 year old record by 14 seconds.

This was a road effort in a project designed for speed, with pacers and carefully planned conditions. It showed his raw ability to run fast over ultra distances. Later that year he won the World Long Distance Mountain Running Championships in 2019.

Bruce Fordyce is a South African runner who set the 50 mile record in 1983 and won the Comrades Marathon nine times. Beating a record that old, even by 14 seconds, meant matching a legend of the sport.
