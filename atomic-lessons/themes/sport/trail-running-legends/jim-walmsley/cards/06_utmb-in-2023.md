In 2023 Walmsley won the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc in 19:37:43, becoming the first American man to win the race. The time was faster than Kilian Jornet's 19:49:30 from 2022, though the route is changed a little in many years, so UTMB times are compared with care.

For Walmsley this was a new kind of success. UTMB is very different from Western States, with huge climbs, a night in the mountains and cold weather. Winning there showed that his style could work on that kind of course too.

The first American man to win UTMB follows a long line of European and Spanish champions. His win showed that American runners, who dominate Western States, can also win on the biggest stage in Europe.
