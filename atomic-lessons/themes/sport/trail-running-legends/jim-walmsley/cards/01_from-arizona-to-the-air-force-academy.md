Jim Walmsley was born on January 17, 1990, and went to Horizon High School in Scottsdale, Arizona. In his senior year, 2007, he became the state champion in cross country for his division, winning every race he ran that season.

He then chose the Air Force Academy, where he studied and competed from 2008 to 2012. He was the captain of the cross country and long distance teams in 2012. His best times from that period were 4:04 for the mile, 13:52 for 5,000 metres, 29:08 for 10,000 metres and 8:41 for the steeplechase.

Scottsdale is in the Arizona desert, so Walmsley grew up in heat rather than in snowy mountains. That background may have helped him in hot races such as Western States, where afternoon temperatures in the canyons can be very high.
