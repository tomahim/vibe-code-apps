In 2017 Rai started the Mira Rai Initiative, which she says gives young female athletes from Nepal the same opportunities she had. Since 2017, every year five girls from different backgrounds are selected to be part of it.

She believes that people should grab the opportunity to live to their full potential. The initiative is an example of how an athlete can use fame to build something for the next generation.

In early February 2017 Nepal's youth and sports ministry also announced a plan to award her three million rupees, as NPR reported. Attention from the government and from a global audience came quickly after her rise in the sport.
