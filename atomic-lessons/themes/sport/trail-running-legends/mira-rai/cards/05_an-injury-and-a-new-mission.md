In early 2016 Rai suffered a knee injury during a race in the United Kingdom and had to take time off from international competition. During that time she turned to promoting trail running across Nepal and helping train other promising young women from rural areas.

She has organised many trail races in Kathmandu and in her home district of Bhojpur. This work helps young people find opportunities in sport. It also shows how an athlete can use fame to build something for the next generation.

Nepal has many mountain trails and very few organised races, so growing the sport helps young people find new paths. By training women from rural areas, Rai tries to give them chances that she did not have at their age.
