When Rai was 14, in the last years of Nepal's ten year civil war, she joined the Maoist army. She has said that the army was a way out of her village, and that she always wanted to leave and do something different with her life.

NPR reported that in her three years with the army she did not see combat. She practiced karate and ran in battalion races as part of her training. Rai says that sports intrigued her in the army, and that she began running and competing in local events there.

The civil war lasted about ten years, until 2006. Many young people, including children, joined the fighting, and after the peace deal they had to find a new place in society, often with very little education.
