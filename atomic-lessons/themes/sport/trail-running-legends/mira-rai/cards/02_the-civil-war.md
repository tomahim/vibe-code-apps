At 14 Rai left home in the middle of the night, without telling her parents, to join the Maoist insurgency when its recruiters came to her village. Because she was a minor, she was not eligible to join the Nepal Army when the civil war ended and was discharged.

After returning home, she wanted to do something more with her life to support her family. She travelled to Kathmandu to pursue karate and running. This is an important part of her story, but it is only the start of a larger journey.

Nepal's civil war lasted about ten years, from 1996 to 2006. Many young people, including children, joined the fighting, and after the peace deal they had to find a new place in society, often with very little education.
