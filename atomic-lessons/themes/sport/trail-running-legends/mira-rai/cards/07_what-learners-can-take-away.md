Mira Rai's story is one of resilience. She joined an army as a child, started running there, and later found her way to Kathmandu and to international trail racing. She also shows how access matters: a mentor, a first race and one invitation can change a life.

Her work in Nepal today continues through the Mira Rai Initiative, which selects five girls every year to receive the same opportunities she had.

Coaching, shoes, travel and visas are often the hidden costs of a career, and athletes from poorer countries often need local organisations to support them. Rai's initiative helps make that support easier to find.

Source: [Mira Rai's official website](https://www.mirarai.com/).
