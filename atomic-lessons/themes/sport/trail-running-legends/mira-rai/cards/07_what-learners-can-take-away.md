Mira Rai's story is one of resilience. She faced poverty, war and injury, and still found a way to compete and to help others. She also shows how access matters: a mentor, a first race and one invitation can change a life.

Her work in Nepal today continues to build trail running in the country.

Her work reminds us that access matters in every sport. Coaching, shoes, travel and visas are often the hidden costs of a career, and athletes from poorer countries often need local organisations to support them. Rai's work helps make that support easier to find.

Source: [Mira Rai's official website](https://www.mirarai.com/).
