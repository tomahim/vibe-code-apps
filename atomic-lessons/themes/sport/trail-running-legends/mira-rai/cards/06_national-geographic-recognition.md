Although Rai has never won a World Championship title, much of her fame came from winning the 2017 National Geographic Adventurer of the Year title. The award is given by National Geographic to explorers and adventurers, which shows how her story reached beyond running.

Her story reached a global audience. It also showed that titles are not the only measure of influence in sport. She changed how many people think about running, opportunity and where champions can come from.

The National Geographic Adventurers of the Year are chosen for their achievements and for the story they tell about exploration. Rai's selection shows that her life story has been as influential as her race results.
