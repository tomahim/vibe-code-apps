In January 2017 National Geographic named Rai its People's Choice Adventurer of the Year for 2017. National Geographic honours ten extraordinary people each year, chosen for big dreams realised in exploration, conservation, adventure sport or humanitarian work, and fans vote for the People's Choice.

The magazine introduced her as a woman who once hauled rice sacks through the Himalaya and who now wins international trail races and inspires young Nepali women. NPR wrote that she was declared National Geographic's Adventurer of the Year in January 2017.

Her story reached a global audience. It also showed that titles are not the only measure of influence in sport. She changed how many people think about running, opportunity and where champions can come from.
