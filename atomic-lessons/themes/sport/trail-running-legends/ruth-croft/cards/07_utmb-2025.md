In 2025 Croft won the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc. She had previously won the 100 kilometre CCC in 2015 and the 55 kilometre OCC in 2018 and 2019, so the main UTMB title completed a set of wins at the biggest races of the festival.

Her path to the top took more than ten years, from Taiwan and Garmin to the title in Chamonix. It is a clear example of patient progress.

Winning in Chamonix after a second place in 2024 shows how a strong runner can learn from a close finish. Her wins at CCC, OCC, Western States, Tarawera and UTMB make a wide, impressive record.

Source: [The Guardian on Ruth Croft's UTMB win](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/sep/19/ruth-croft-ultrarunner-new-zealand-ultra-trail-runner-utmb-mont-blanc).
