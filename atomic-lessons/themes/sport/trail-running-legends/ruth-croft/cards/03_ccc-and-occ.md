In 2015 Croft won the 100 kilometre CCC at UTMB. She followed that with fourth place at the highly competitive North Face 50 in San Francisco. She moved away from Taiwan in 2017 and began to split her time between Europe and New Zealand.

She won the 55 kilometre OCC in Chamonix in 2018 and 2019. In 2017 she won the Translantau 50K in Hong Kong, the 62 kilometre race at Tarawera and the 73 kilometre Les Templiers race in Millau, France. These wins showed her strength across shorter mountain ultras.

The OCC is the shortest of the main UTMB races, about 55 kilometres from Orsières in Switzerland to Chamonix. Winning it twice, in 2018 and 2019, showed that she was strong on fast, runnable terrain with big climbs.
