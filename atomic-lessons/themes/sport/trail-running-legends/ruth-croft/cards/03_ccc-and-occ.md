In 2015 Croft won the 100 kilometre CCC at UTMB. Her winning time was 12:54:53, according to the race results on u-trail.com. The CCC starts in Courmayeur in Italy, crosses into Switzerland and finishes in Chamonix in France, so a single race visits three countries. A win there was a major step from regional races to the UTMB week.

She won the 55 kilometre OCC in Chamonix in 2018 and 2019. These wins showed her strength across shorter mountain ultras.

The OCC is the shortest of the main UTMB races, about 55 kilometres from Orsières in Switzerland to Chamonix. Winning it twice, in 2018 and 2019, showed that she was strong on fast, runnable terrain with big climbs.
