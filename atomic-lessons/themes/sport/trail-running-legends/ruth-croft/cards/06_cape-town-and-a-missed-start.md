In November 2023 Croft won the 100 kilometre Ultra Trail Cape Town in South Africa. She had been scheduled to run UTMB in 2023 but came down with a virus a few days before the race and did not start.

In 2024 she returned to UTMB and finished second behind Katie Schide. Illness and near misses are part of long career in ultra running, and Croft's story shows how athletes come back after them.

Ultra Trail Cape Town is held on and around Table Mountain, with views of the ocean and the city. Winning it in 2023 was a sign that she could recover from the disappointment of missing UTMB and keep performing at a high level.
