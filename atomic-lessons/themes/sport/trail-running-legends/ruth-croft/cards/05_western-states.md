Croft ran her first 100 mile race at Western States in 2021. She finished second behind English runner Beth Pascall. She returned in 2022 and won, in a time of 17:21:30.

Tarawera has been another strong event for her. In 2021 she won the 100 kilometre race there in a course record of 9:21:03. She won again in 2024, and in February 2025 she defended her title and set another course record, her third win overall.

Tarawera is held in the Rotorua region of New Zealand, and the 100 kilometre race passes lakes, forest and volcanic landscapes. Winning there three times, including two course records, shows how well she knows the course.
