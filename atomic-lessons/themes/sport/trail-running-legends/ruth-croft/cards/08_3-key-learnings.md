**1. A varied path works.** Croft ran track, mountain races and trail ultras before 100 miles.

**2. Progress takes years.** She won CCC in 2015 and the main UTMB in 2025.

**3. Setbacks happen.** A virus kept her out of UTMB 2023, and she returned to win two years later.
