Ruth Croft was born on January 15, 1989, in Greymouth, on the West Coast of New Zealand's South Island, and grew up in the small town of Stillwater. At Rangi Ruru Girls' School in Christchurch she ran track and cross country. As a junior she was the under 20 national champion and competed for New Zealand in the 3,000 metres steeplechase at the 2006 World Junior Championships.

She also raced the World Mountain Running Championships as a junior, in 2005 and 2007. From early on she mixed track speed with mountain running.

Greymouth lies on the rainy, rugged West Coast of New Zealand's South Island, between the Southern Alps and the Tasman Sea. Growing up in a small town near wild mountains and rivers gave her an early love of the outdoors.
