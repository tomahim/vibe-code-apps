At the 2019 Trail World Championships in Miranda do Corvo, Portugal, Croft won silver behind Blandine L'Hirondel of France. It was the first medal for a New Zealand athlete in the history of the Trail World Championships.

The result made her a national star in New Zealand. It also showed that a runner from a small country could compete with the best in Europe, helped by a mix of road, track and mountain experience.

The Trail World Championships bring national teams together every year or two, usually on a hilly course. A silver medal there in 2019 meant beating nearly all of the top runners in the world on one day.
