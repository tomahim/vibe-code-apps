After school Croft briefly enrolled at Lincoln University but returned to the West Coast to work. She then moved to the United States on an athletic scholarship at the University of Portland in Oregon. After graduating she moved to Taiwan to teach English, where she lived for five and a half years.

Later she worked in marketing for the technology company Garmin. Croft began competing in more trail races while living in Taiwan and eventually became a sponsored athlete with Garmin. She won the Mt. Kinabalu International Climbathon 50k in Malaysia in 2013 and 2014.

Moving between New Zealand, the United States and Taiwan meant that Croft built her career far from the traditional trail running centres. She trained on the island's steep mountains and learned to race in very humid conditions.
