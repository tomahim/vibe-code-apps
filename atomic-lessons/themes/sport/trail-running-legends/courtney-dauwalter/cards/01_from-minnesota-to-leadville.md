Courtney Dauwalter was born on February 13, 1985, and grew up in Hopkins, Minnesota. In high school she competed in track, cross country and Nordic skiing, and Nordic skiing. She attended the University of Denver on a skiing scholarship and studied biology.

She then earned a master's degree in teaching and taught science to middle and high school students in the Denver area. She became a full time professional runner in 2017. She now lives and trains above 10,000 feet in Leadville, Colorado.

Hopkins is a suburb of Minneapolis, a city with long, cold winters. Growing up there, Dauwalter spent a lot of time outside, and Nordic skiing taught her to enjoy effort in cold conditions, which later helped in mountain races.
