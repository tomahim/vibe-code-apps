In 2017 Dauwalter won the Moab 240, a 240 mile race in Utah, finishing first overall. A 240 mile race takes more than two days, so it tests sleep management, food and mental focus as much as speed.

In 2018 she won the Western States 100 in 17 hours and 27 minutes. These results showed that she was strong in short ultras, huge distances and mountain races.

The Moab 240 is a 240 mile race through the red rock country of Utah, run over several days and nights. Winning by ten hours shows how much faster she was than the rest of the field, and how well she handled sleep and long effort.
