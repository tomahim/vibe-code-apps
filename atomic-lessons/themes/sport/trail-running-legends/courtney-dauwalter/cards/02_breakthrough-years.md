In 2016 Dauwalter set a course record at the Javelina Jundred 100K and won the Run Rabbit Run 100 mile race by 75 minutes. In 2017 she won the Moab 240 in 2 days, 9 hours and 59 minutes, finishing first overall and beating the runner up by more than 10 hours.

In 2017 she also set the then record for the longest run in 24 hours, 155.391 miles at the Riverbank One Day Classic. In 2018 she won the Western States 100 in 17 hours and 27 minutes. These results showed that she was strong in short ultras, huge distances and mountain races.

The Moab 240 is a 240 mile race through the red rock country of Utah, run over several days and nights. Winning by ten hours shows how much faster she was than the rest of the field, and how well she handled sleep and long effort.
