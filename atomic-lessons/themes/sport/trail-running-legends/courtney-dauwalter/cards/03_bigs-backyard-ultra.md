In 2018 Dauwalter competed at Big's Backyard Ultra, a race where runners complete one 4.17 mile loop every hour until only one person is left. She finished second overall after completing 67 laps, which set the women's course record at 279.268 miles.

Backyard races test endurance and mental focus more than speed.

A backyard ultra has no fixed finish line. Runners must start each new lap at the top of every hour, and the last person still running is the winner. It is a test of discipline and mental strength more than raw speed. Because every lap must be finished within the hour, a runner who is slow late in the race has less and less time to rest, which is why the format ends only when everyone but one person has stopped.
