In 2023 Dauwalter won Western States, breaking the women's course record set by Ellie Greenwood in 2012 with a time of 15:29:34. That made her the first athlete to set course records at Western States and Hardrock in the same year.

In September she won UTMB, and became the first person in history to win the triple of Western States, Hardrock and UTMB in a single year. Guinness World Records recognises this as the first completion of ultrarunning's Triple Crown. It is the clearest achievement of her career.

Western States, Hardrock and UTMB are three of the best known 100 mile races in the world. Winning all three in one summer meant three very different tests: heat and canyons, high altitude mountains and a long alpine loop.
