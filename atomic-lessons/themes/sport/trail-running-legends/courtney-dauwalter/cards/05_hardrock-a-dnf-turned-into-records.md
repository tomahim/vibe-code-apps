In 2021 Dauwalter entered the Hardrock 100 but did not finish. She returned in 2022 and won. In 2023 she won again, in 26:14:08, beating the previous counter clockwise women's record by an hour and four minutes.

Hardrock changes direction every year, and the women's records had been set by Diana Finkel. Dauwalter beat the record in both directions, by more than an hour each time. The story shows how a setback can become motivation.

Hardrock is run in Colorado's San Juan Mountains, with an average altitude above 11,000 feet and more than 33,000 feet of climbing. Dauwalter's records came on a course where many runners struggle just to finish within the time limit.
