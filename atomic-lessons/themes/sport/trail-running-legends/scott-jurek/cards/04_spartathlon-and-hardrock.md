In 2006 Jurek won the Spartathlon for the first time, a 153 mile race from Athens to Sparta in Greece. He won it three years in a row, in 2006, 2007 and 2008. The route follows the journey of Pheidippides, the messenger of Greek legend.

In 2007 he also won the Hardrock Hundred in Colorado and set a new course record. Hardrock is a hard mountain race with huge climbs at high altitude. With Western States, Badwater, Spartathlon and Hardrock on his record, Jurek had won on trails, roads, mountains and in the desert.

The Spartathlon follows the route that, according to Greek legend, Pheidippides ran to ask Sparta for help before the Battle of Marathon. Runners must reach several checkpoints before strict time limits, so pacing is essential.
