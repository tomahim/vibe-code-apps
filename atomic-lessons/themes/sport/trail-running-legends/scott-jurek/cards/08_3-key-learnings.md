**1. Streaks take patience.** Jurek won Western States seven times in a row, from 1999 to 2005.

**2. Range matters.** He won in the mountains, in Death Valley heat and on the road from Athens to Sparta.

**3. He kept going.** In 2015 he broke the supported Appalachian Trail record by about three hours.
