On May 14, 2010, at the 24-Hour World Championships in Brive-la-Gaillarde, France, Jurek ran 165.7 miles. That was a United States record for 24 hours and earned him an individual silver medal, with the American men's team taking bronze.

Jurek has been vegetarian since 1997 and vegan since 1999. He believes that eating plants helps his health and recovery, and he writes about it in his memoir. His diet is one reason many people outside the sport know his name. His mother had multiple sclerosis, and he has said she is a major source of his strength.

A 24 hour race is run on a short loop, so a runner cannot rely on scenery or climbing to break up the day. Success depends on steady pace, regular eating and mental focus, which is why Jurek's 165.7 miles was such a strong result.
