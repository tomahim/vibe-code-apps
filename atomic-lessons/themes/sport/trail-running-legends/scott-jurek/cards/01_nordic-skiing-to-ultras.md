Scott Jurek was born on October 26, 1973, in Duluth, Minnesota, and grew up in Proctor. As a child he hunted, fished and camped with his family. He did not run long distances until high school, when he used running as cross training for Nordic skiing. At first he hated it, but summers spent running trails with ski poles changed his mind.

In 1994 a training partner, Dusty Olson, challenged him to enter the Minnesota Voyageur 50 Mile. Jurek finished second in his first ultramarathon, even though he had never run a marathon in training. He then won that race in 1996, 1997 and 1998.

Proctor is a small town near Lake Superior with cold winters, so Nordic skiing was a natural sport there. The long, steady effort of cross country skiing helps explain the endurance Jurek later showed in 100 mile races.
