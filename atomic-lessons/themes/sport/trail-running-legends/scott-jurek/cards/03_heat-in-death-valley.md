In 2005, only a few weeks after winning Western States, Jurek ran the Badwater Ultramarathon and set a new course record. Badwater is among the hardest races in the world and takes place in Death Valley. Jurek came from behind to win in temperatures of 120 degrees Fahrenheit, about 49 degrees Celsius.

He handled the heat by stopping from time to time to sit in a cooler of ice. He repeated his Badwater win in 2006. These wins showed that his strength was not limited to mountain trails. He could also manage extreme heat on a long road course.

Badwater starts in Death Valley, one of the lowest and hottest places in North America, and finishes high on Mount Whitney's slopes. Runners must manage heat, road temperature and fuel for about 135 miles in the summer sun.
