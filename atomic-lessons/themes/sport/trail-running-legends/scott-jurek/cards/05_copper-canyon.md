In 2006 Jurek traveled to Mexico's Copper Canyon to race the Tarahumara, a group of runners from the area. The race was organized by Micah True, known as Caballo Blanco. Jurek narrowly lost to the fastest Tarahumara runner, Arnulfo Quimare. In 2007 he returned and won.

The story was told in Christopher McDougall's book Born to Run, which raised Jurek's profile far beyond ultrarunning. A reader did not need to know anything about trail racing to enjoy the story of a famous runner losing narrowly to a fast Tarahumara and coming back to win the next year.

The Tarahumara, also called the Rarámuri, are known for running long distances in simple sandals across Mexico's canyon country. Racing them in their home terrain was a unique challenge for a top American ultrarunner.
