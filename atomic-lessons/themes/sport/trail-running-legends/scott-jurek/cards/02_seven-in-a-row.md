Jurek won the Western States 100 every year from 1999 to 2005, seven wins in a row. He came from Minnesota, a flat state, but he beat runners who trained in mountains. He had earned a master's degree in physical therapy in 1998.

In 2004 he also completed the Ultra Running Grand Slam, finishing four classic 100 milers in one summer: Western States, Leadville (where he was runner-up), Vermont and Wasatch Front. Western States was where he became a star, and the streak made him the face of the sport in the early 2000s.

The Western States 100 is a point to point race through the Sierra Nevada, with heat in the canyons and long climbs. Winning it seven years in a row means adapting to different weather and rivals every single year.
