In 2003 True decided to organise a race for the Tarahumara that would help them preserve their culture and running heritage. The first race took place in Urique on Sunday, March 23, 2003. It grew into the classic 80 kilometre (50 mile) Ultra Marathon Caballo Blanco, with a marathon added in 2017, a half marathon in 2019 and a 100 mile race in 2025.

The race was created as an act of Korima, the circle of sharing, giving without expecting something in return. Over the years the event became internationally known as the Copper Canyon Ultramarathon through the book Born to Run.

The first race was small, a few local Rarámuri athletes and one man's dream, according to True Messages. The legendary Rarámuri runner Miguel Lara won the first edition. Today the event attracts more than a thousand endurance runners from over 20 countries, while keeping its focus on the Rarámuri.
