In 2003 True decided to organise a race for the Tarahumara that would help them preserve their culture and running heritage. The first race took place in Urique on Sunday, March 23, 2003, as a trail marathon, and later it grew into the 50 mile Ultra Maratón Caballo Blanco.

The race was created as an act of Korima, the circle of sharing, giving without expecting something in return. Over the years the event became internationally known as the Copper Canyon Ultramarathon through the book Born to Run.

The first race was small and simple, held in the canyon town of Urique with local runners. Over the years it grew into a larger event that draws runners from many countries, while keeping its focus on the Tarahumara.
