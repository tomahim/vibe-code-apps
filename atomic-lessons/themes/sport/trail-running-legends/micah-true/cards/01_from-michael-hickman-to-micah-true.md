Micah True was born Michael Randall Hickman on November 10, 1953, in Oakland, California. His father was a Marine Corps gunnery sergeant who served in the Korean War, and the family moved between military bases. At Humboldt State University he studied Eastern religions and Native American history.

To earn money he fought in informal boxing bouts. Between 1974 and 1982 he was a professional middleweight, with a record of nine wins and eleven losses. He later changed his name to Micah True. Micah came from the Bible, and True came from the name of a beloved pet, True Dog.

Humboldt State University is in northern California, close to redwood forests and the Pacific coast. Studying Eastern religions and Native American history there fits with the interest True later showed in simple living and in the Tarahumara.
