Micah True was born Michael Randall Hickman on November 10, 1953. He took his new name from the Bible and from a beloved companion, a dog named True. Later he earned another name, Caballo Blanco, or White Horse.

The New York Times described his path as a long and fascinating one, from a furniture mover and boxer in Colorado to an ultrarunner in the canyons of Mexico. He lived simply, with very few possessions, and delivered furniture in an old pickup truck to pay for his way, according to the film Run Free.

Boulder, Colorado, sits at the foot of the Rocky Mountains, with trails close to town. It is the home that True kept between his winter journeys, and a long way from the Mexican canyons where he became famous.
