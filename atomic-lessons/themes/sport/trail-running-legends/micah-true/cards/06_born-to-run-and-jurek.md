True became famous because of Christopher McDougall's 2009 book Born to Run, in which he is a central character. Some readers credited the book as their inspiration to take up running.

In 2006, as Scott Jurek's lesson explained, a group of runners including McDougall and Jurek travelled to the race. Jurek narrowly lost to Arnulfo Quimare of the Tarahumara, and in 2007 Jurek returned to win. True's quiet project had become a global story.

Born to Run was a bestseller that brought barefoot and minimalist running into the news. It also made readers curious about the Tarahumara, about ultras, and about the idea that humans may be built for long distance running.
