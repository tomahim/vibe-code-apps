**1. Running can be a way of life.** True spent winters running in Mexico and Central America for almost 20 years.

**2. He built a race for others.** In 2003 he organised a race to help the Tarahumara preserve their running heritage.

**3. Stories travel.** Born to Run made his project famous around the world.
