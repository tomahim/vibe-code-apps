Micah True died in March 2012 during a run in southwestern New Mexico. Reuters reported later that an autopsy found he died of heart disease, an unexplained form of cardiomyopathy. He was 58.

After his death the race was returned to the name by which it was always known in the canyons, Ultra Marathon Caballo Blanco, in honour of True. The race continues in Urique each year, run by the Municipality of Urique with support from True Messages, a nonprofit that continues his work.

It reminds runners that the sport can be about community, sharing and respect for local cultures, not only about records. The event gives vouchers to all Rarámuri runners, used for food for their families such as maize flour, beans and rice.

Source: [True Messages, the Ultra Caballo Blanco](https://www.truemessages.org/ultra-caballo).
