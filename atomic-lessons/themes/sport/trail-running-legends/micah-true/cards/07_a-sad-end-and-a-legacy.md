Micah True died on March 27, 2012, during a run in the Gila Wilderness in southwestern New Mexico. Friends, including Scott Jurek, joined the search for him. After his death the race was renamed back to the name he always used, Ultra Maratón Caballo Blanco.

The race continues in Urique each year. It reminds runners that the sport can be about community, sharing and respect for local cultures, not only about records.

After his death, the Ultra Maratón Caballo Blanco kept going, supported by local organisers and friends from the running community. Money raised by the race helps Tarahumara families and keeps his idea of sharing alive.

Source: [True Messages, the Ultra Caballo Blanco](https://www.truemessages.org/ultra-caballo).
