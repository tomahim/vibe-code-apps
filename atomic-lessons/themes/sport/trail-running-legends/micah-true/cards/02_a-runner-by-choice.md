True was an American ultrarunner from Boulder, Colorado. The film Run Free describes him as a gifted athlete who ran for long distances on the trails around Boulder. When he was not running, he liked to sit outside a cafe with his dog and talk about art, spirituality and nature.

He lived simply and had very few material possessions. He worked as a furniture mover to earn only what he needed, and he organised his life around running rather than around a career or money.

A person who lives this way is sometimes called a trail running bum, like a surfing bum or a climbing bum. It is a different path from an athlete who follows a training plan toward titles: the running came first and the rest of life was arranged around it.
