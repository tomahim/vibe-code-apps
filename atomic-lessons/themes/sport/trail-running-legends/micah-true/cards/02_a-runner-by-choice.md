True once spent ten months living in a cave in Hawaii. He said he took up long distance running after a love ended. By 1982 he had moved to Boulder, Colorado, where he worked as a self employed furniture mover and became what he called a trail running bum, like a surfing bum or climbing bum.

For almost 20 years he spent winters running in Mexico, Guatemala and Central America, averaging 170 miles a week. In summer he returned to Boulder to earn just enough to live on for the rest of the year.

A trail running bum is someone who organises life around running rather than around a career. True moved furniture in the summers to earn money, then spent the winters in warm places where he could run in the mountains for hours every day.
