**1. It is never too late.** Olmo won UTMB in 2006 at 58 and again in 2007 at 59.

**2. Experience counts.** Ultras reward patience and consistency, not just youth.

**3. Background does not limit you.** He was a woodcutter, a lorry driver and a quarry worker before becoming a champion.
