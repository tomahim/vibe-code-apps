In 2007 Olmo won UTMB again, aged 59, in 21 hours, 31 minutes and 58 seconds. Two wins in a row, in his late fifties, are among the most remarkable results in the history of trail running.

His time was slower than in 2006, but the win mattered. It proved that the first was not luck. For many people he became a symbol of what perseverance and love of the mountains can do, without a big team or a career built from youth.

A second win a year later proved that the first one was no accident. Many athletes can have one perfect day, but winning two years in a row requires planning, health and calm when the race becomes difficult.
