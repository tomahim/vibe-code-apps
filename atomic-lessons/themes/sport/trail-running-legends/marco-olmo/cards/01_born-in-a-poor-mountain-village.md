Marco Olmo was born on October 8, 1948, in Robilante, near Cuneo in the Italian Alps. He has described being born into the poverty that the war had left in the mountains. His family lived from the chestnut tree, which they simply called the tree.

As a boy he went to school and then to the pasture. He has said that life was hard for the mountain boys, and while luckier children played football, he went down to the pitch in clogs. This simple, hard childhood shaped his strength and his view of sport.

Robilante is a village in Piedmont, at the foot of the Maritime Alps, close to the French border. The area is known for its mountains, chestnut woods and farming traditions, which shaped the life of people like Olmo.
