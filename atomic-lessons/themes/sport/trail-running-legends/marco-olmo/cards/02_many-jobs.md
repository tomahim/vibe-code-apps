Olmo worked as a woodcutter, then as a lorry driver and finally in a quarry for the cement company Buzzi Unicem. He worked as a labourer through his life, and he raced on the side.

Unlike many champions, he did not become a full time athlete when he was young. Long years of physical work are a different background from most elite runners. He began winning major ultra distance events in 1996.

Physical jobs such as woodcutting and quarrying build strength, but they also take time and energy. A runner with such a job has to fit training around work, and has to be patient to improve.
