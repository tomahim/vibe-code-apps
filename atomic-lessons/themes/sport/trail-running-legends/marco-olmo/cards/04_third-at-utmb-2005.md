In 2005 Olmo finished third at the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc, in 21 hours, 49 minutes and 57 seconds. The UTMB was only in its third edition at the time. The race had just started to grow into a major event.

It was a strong result for a runner aged 56. It also shows that his 2006 win was no accident. He had already proven that he could handle the long night, the climbs over passes in France, Italy and Switzerland and the final descent into Chamonix.

The UTMB began in 2003, so in 2005 it was only in its third edition. The course was already known for its three countries, big climbs and long night in the mountains.
