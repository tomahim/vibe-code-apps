In 2006 Olmo won UTMB outright, aged 58, in 21 hours, 6 minutes and 6 seconds. He beat much younger men in a race of about 166 kilometres through the Alps.

His win made headlines in the running world. It showed that age can be less important than experience in ultra distance racing, and it made him a favourite of fans, who saw a working man win on the world's biggest trail stage.

His age made headlines because most winners of elite endurance races are much younger. Winning at 58 means that he beat athletes who were in their twenties and thirties, often with full time training and support teams.
