Olmo is remembered as a legend because of his background as well as his results. He came from a poor mountain family and worked with his hands, but he reached the top of an international sport.

His story also helps explain why UTMB became so loved. The race belongs to the mountain villages as well as to elite athletes, and Olmo is part of that history.

UTMB passes through many mountain villages, where local people cheer and offer food and drink throughout the night. Olmo's story fits this tradition, because he came from a similar community and ran with its toughness.

Source: [Dryarn, interview with Marco Olmo](https://www.dryarn.com/en/world-en/news-en/interview-with-marco-olmo/).
