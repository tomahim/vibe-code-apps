Lizzy Hawker was born on March 10, 1976, and is a British endurance athlete. She studied Natural Sciences at Cambridge University and went on to earn a PhD in physical oceanography. She worked as a polar oceanographer and researcher for the British Antarctic Survey.

In 2005 she was finishing her PhD and working for the survey, deploying instruments and analysing data aboard a ship at the edge of Antarctica to study climate change, according to National Geographic. She has said that she fell into long distance running that same year: visiting friends she had met on the Welsh 1000m Peaks, she entered a 40 mile track race for fun, and the England selector there asked her to represent Britain.

Physical oceanography studies how water moves, from currents to waves and tides. Collecting and analysing data over long periods needs patience and careful attention, skills that also help in long distance running.
