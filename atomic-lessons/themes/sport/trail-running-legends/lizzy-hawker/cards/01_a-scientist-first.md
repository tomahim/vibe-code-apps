Lizzy Hawker was born on March 10, 1976, and is a British endurance athlete. She completed a PhD in physical oceanography at Southampton University in 2005 and worked for the British Antarctic Survey, studying climate change along the Antarctic coast. She was a researcher until 2007.

Running was not her first career. She came to ultras through a love of mountains and endurance, and her scientific training shaped how she thought about training and recovery. She was known for recovering quickly after big races, which is rare in such a hard sport.

Physical oceanography studies how water moves, from currents to waves and tides. Collecting and analysing data over long periods needs patience and careful attention, skills that also help in long distance running.
