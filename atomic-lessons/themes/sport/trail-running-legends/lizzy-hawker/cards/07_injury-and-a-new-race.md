Hawker suffered six stress fractures in the two years up to 2015, a hard period for any athlete. She then turned some of her energy to creating a race. While training for UTMB she had run the Tour de Monte Rosa footpath many times, and this led to the Ultra Tour Monte Rosa.

The route covers 93 miles from Grächen to Zermatt, goes through villages in Italy and returns to the start, with more than 32,000 feet of ascent and descent. The first Zero Edition took place in August 2015.

The Tour de Monte Rosa circles the Monte Rosa massif, crossing between Switzerland and Italy. Hawker used her knowledge of the trail to design a race that gives other runners a chance to experience it.

Source: [Guinness World Records, most UTMB wins (female)](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/506379-most-ultra-trail-du-mont-blanc-wins-female).
