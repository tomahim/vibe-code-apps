After her years of racing, Hawker turned part of her energy to creating a race. In 2014 she announced the Ultra Tour Monte Rosa, which follows the Tour de Monte Rosa footpath around the Monte Rosa massif, starting from Grächen in Switzerland. National Geographic describes the full loop as 93 miles around the massif, with 38 peaks above 13,000 feet in the region.

The first Zero Edition took place in August 2015 as a three stage race of 106 kilometres from Cervinia in Italy to Grächen, and 119 runners from 17 countries started. Hawker was the race director. After a spell away from racing, she was working on the project in Zermatt.

The Tour de Monte Rosa circles the Monte Rosa massif, crossing between Switzerland and Italy. Hawker used her knowledge of the trail to design a race that gives other runners a chance to experience it.
