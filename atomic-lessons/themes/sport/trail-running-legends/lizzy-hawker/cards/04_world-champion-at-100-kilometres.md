In 2006 Hawker won the IAU 100 km World Championships, which were held in Seoul, South Korea. This is a road race, which shows she could also run fast on flat ground, not only in the mountains.

In 2010 she took bronze at the same championship in Gibraltar, in 7:33:26, behind Ellie Greenwood and Monica Carlin of Italy. Her own results list also shows a bronze medal at the 2009 IAU Trail World Championships. She could compete in many formats, from mountain ultras to fast road races, and that is one reason she was so respected.

The IAU is the International Association of Ultrarunners, which organises world championships on roads and tracks. A road 100 kilometre race needs steady pace and good fuelling, not climbing skill, so it tests a different side of ultra running.
