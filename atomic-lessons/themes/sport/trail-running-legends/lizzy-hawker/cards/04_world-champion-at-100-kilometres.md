In 2006 Hawker won the IAU 100 km World Championships. This is a road race, which shows she could also run fast on flat ground, not only in the mountains. In 2010 she led for most of the same championship but faded late and took bronze behind Ellie Greenwood and Monica Carlin.

That result also gave her a bronze in the jointly hosted European Championships. She was a runner who could compete in many formats, from mountain ultras to fast road races, and that is one reason she was so respected.

The IAU is the International Association of Ultrarunners, which organises world championships on roads and tracks. A road 100 kilometre race needs steady pace and good fuelling, not climbing skill, so it tests a different side of ultra running.
