Hawker won UTMB five times, which is a record for either women or men. Her wins came in 2005, 2008, 2010, 2011 and 2012. Guinness World Records lists her as the woman with the most UTMB wins.

A record like this means staying healthy, fit and focused over many years. UTMB can go wrong in many ways, from stomach problems to storms to a bad night. Winning it five times shows how well she handled each stage of the race, and how well she planned her seasons.

Winning five times means arriving in good shape on the same weekend over many years, with weather, stomach trouble and strong rivals each time. Few athletes in any sport can repeat a top result on such a hard day so often.
