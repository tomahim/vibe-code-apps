In 2007 Hawker set a record for running the 199 miles between Mount Everest South Base Camp and Kathmandu, Nepal. She did it in 77 hours and 36 minutes. In 2011 she improved the record with a time of 71 hours and 25 minutes.

The route passes through mountain villages and over big hills, and it is an adventure as well as a race against the clock. These runs show another side of Hawker's career: she is not only a racer but also an explorer who enjoys long journeys on foot.

The route from Everest South Base Camp to Kathmandu follows trails through Sherpa villages, over high passes and along river valleys. It is a long journey of nearly three days, with little of the support a normal race has.
