In 2007 Hawker and fellow runner Stephen Pyke ran from Everest Base Camp to Kathmandu in 3 days, 2 hours and 36 minutes, beating the previous record set in 2000 by Kumar Limbu of Nepal. In 2011 Hawker ran the 199 mile route again and improved the time to 2 days, 23 hours and 25 minutes, which is 71 hours and 25 minutes.

She broke her own 2007 record by more than three hours, even though she stopped for eight hours during a storm on the first night, according to Outside. The route passes through mountain villages and over big hills, and it is an adventure as well as a race against the clock.

These runs show another side of Hawker's career: she is not only a racer but also an explorer who enjoys long journeys on foot, with little of the support a normal race has.
