Tom Evans was born on February 3, 1992, and grew up in Sussex, England. He was active in sport from a young age, running in national school competitions and playing rugby. At 18 he joined the British Army and trained at the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst.

He later served as an officer in the Welsh Guards and became a captain. His military background has been a recurring part of his public profile, especially after the salute he gives at the finish of major races. He is one of the few elite trail runners with a career in the armed forces.

The Welsh Guards are one of the five regiments of Foot Guards in the British Army and are known for ceremonial duties at Buckingham Palace as well as operational service. Evans carried that discipline into his running career.
