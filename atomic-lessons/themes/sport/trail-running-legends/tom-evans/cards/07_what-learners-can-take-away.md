Evans's route into ultrarunning was unusual: a bet, a desert race and then the mountains. He shows that talent can appear in unexpected ways, and that running in the military can build the discipline needed for long events.

His career also shows how a strong 100 mile debut can be a start, not an end. After third place at Western States, it took until 2023 to win it.

The salute at the finish line has become a trademark for Evans. It links his military past with his new career and gives him a personal ritual that fans can recognise from photographs and video clips around the world.
