Evans had knee surgery in 2021 and returned to major international racing in 2022. A return after surgery needs patience, because training load has to be rebuilt slowly, week by week, and a runner must trust that the knee will hold up under hard work again.

He came back strongly. In 2023 he won the Western States Endurance Run. This was a major achievement, since Western States is one of the most famous 100 mile races in the world.

Tarawera is a well known trail race near Rotorua in New Zealand, through forests, lakes and geothermal land. His course record of 8:03:29 in 2020 shows the high level of the race, which attracts international runners.
