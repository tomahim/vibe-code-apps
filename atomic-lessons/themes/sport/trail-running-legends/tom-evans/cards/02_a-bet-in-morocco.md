During a ten month Army posting to Kenya in 2015, Evans returned to regular running and trained in a country associated with elite distance running. In 2017 he gained international attention at the Marathon des Sables, a multi stage desert ultramarathon in Morocco.

He entered the race after a bet with friends and finished third overall in 19:49:33. That made him one of the leading European performers in the race's history. Later in 2017 he finished fourth in the CCC race at the UTMB festival.

The Marathon des Sables is a six day, roughly 250 kilometre race across the Sahara in Morocco, where runners carry their own food and equipment. Finishing third on a first try is a very strong result for a newcomer.
