In 2025 Evans won the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc. He is best known for his Western States win in 2023 and his UTMB win in 2025. Winning the two most prestigious 100 mile races shows he can perform on both a fast, runnable course and a long alpine loop.

As at Western States, he saluted at the finish of UTMB. The salute has become a kind of signature, linking his military career with his sport.

UTMB has a huge field and a deep group of elite men. A British winner is unusual, and Evans's win shows that the sport is becoming more international, with strong runners coming from many countries and backgrounds. Evans's military career is part of that wider variety.
