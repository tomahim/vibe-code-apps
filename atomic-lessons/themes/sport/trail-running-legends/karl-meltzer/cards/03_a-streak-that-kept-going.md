On October 17, 2020, Meltzer won the No Business 100 Mile Race. That win added to a streak of 19 consecutive years in which he won at least one 100 mile race.

He also had huge single seasons. In 2006 he won six 100 milers, four of them in record performances, with the final four in a period of eight weeks. He also won five in 2007 and five in 2009, and four in 2005. Four times in his career he won at least four 100 milers in a calendar year.

Winning six 100 milers in one year, with four of them in a record time, is a sign of extraordinary fitness and recovery. It also needs a calm approach to travel and food, because races come only weeks apart.
