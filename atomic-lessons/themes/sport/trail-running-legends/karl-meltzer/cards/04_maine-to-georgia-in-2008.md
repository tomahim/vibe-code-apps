In 2008 Meltzer ran the Appalachian Trail from Maine to Georgia, covering 2,176 miles with about 500,000 feet of climbing. He finished in 54 days, 21 hours and 12 minutes, which was the fourth fastest Appalachian Trail thru hike at the time.

He stayed in touch with fans with a catchy campaign called Where's Karl? People followed his progress online while he ran through 14 states. The run showed that he enjoyed long journeys as well as races, and it led him to try again later.

The Appalachian Trail runs from Georgia to Maine through the eastern United States. Running it means hiking boots, rocks, roots and weather for weeks, so a fast time needs planning, support and a lot of mental toughness.
