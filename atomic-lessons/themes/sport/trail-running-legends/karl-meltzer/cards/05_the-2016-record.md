On September 18, 2016, Meltzer set an Appalachian Trail record by completing the 2,190 miles over 14 states in 45 days, 22 hours and 38 minutes. A 2017 documentary called Made to Be Broken followed the attempt.

The record did not last very long. It was beaten in September 2017 by Joe McConaughy in a self supported run. The current fastest known time was set by Tara Dower in 2024. Fastest known times change often because every new attempt learns from the previous ones.

Documentaries like Made to Be Broken help share the human side of record attempts: the tired legs, the weather and the support crew. They show that a record is a team effort as well as an individual achievement.
