On September 18, 2016, Meltzer set an Appalachian Trail record by completing the roughly 2,190 miles of the trail in 45 days, 22 hours and 38 minutes. A 2017 documentary called Made to Be Broken followed the attempt.

The record did not last very long. It was beaten at the end of August 2017 by Joe McConaughy, who finished in 45 days, 12 hours and 15 minutes. Fastest known times change often because every new attempt learns from the previous ones.

Documentaries like Made to Be Broken help share the human side of record attempts: the tired legs, the weather and the support crew. They show that a record is a team effort as well as an individual achievement.
