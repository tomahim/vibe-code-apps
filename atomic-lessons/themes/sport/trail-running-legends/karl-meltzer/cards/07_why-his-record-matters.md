A 100 mile win depends on conditions, competition and luck. Meltzer's record of 45 wins by the end of 2021 shows how many times he put himself in a position to win and then did it. Other champions of the sport win fewer races but at bigger events, so the two kinds of record are different.

His success also shows the value of starting a career in your thirties. He turned professional in 1999 and kept winning for more than twenty years.

The record for 100 mile wins counts all races at that distance, not just the largest ones. That is why it is a record of longevity and consistency as much as of one big day, and why it will be hard to beat.
