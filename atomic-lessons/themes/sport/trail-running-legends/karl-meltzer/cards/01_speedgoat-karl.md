Karl Meltzer was born on December 8, 1967, and is nicknamed Speedgoat Karl. He lives in Sandy, Utah. He has been a professional ultramarathoner since 1999 and a coach since 2007. His trail runs have been featured in news outlets from Ultrarunning.com to NBC Sports.

Many ultrarunners are vegetarian and avoid alcohol or caffeine, but Meltzer became known for the opposite. He was seen with bacon, Red Bull and craft beer, and he told reporters that candy and beer fuelled some of his record runs. His relaxed style made him popular with fans and journalists.

Sandy, Utah, sits at the foot of the Wasatch Mountains near Salt Lake City, with trails and altitude close to town. Living there gave Meltzer constant access to the kind of terrain on which many 100 mile races are run.
