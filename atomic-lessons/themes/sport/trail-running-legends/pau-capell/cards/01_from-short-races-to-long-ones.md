Pau Capell began as a runner in shorter distances, but he soon found that he was better at long and hard races. In 2014 he started to compete regularly in ultra running events, and he found that he did better the longer and tougher the race.

That year he won the Ultra Sierra Nevada, a 103 kilometre race, and the Ultra Trail Mallorca, a 112 kilometre race. These early wins showed his talent for endurance on rough terrain, and they led to bigger invitations from sponsors and race organisers.

The Ultra Sierra Nevada takes place in Spain's Sierra Nevada mountains near Granada, and Mallorca's Ultra Trail is held on a Mediterranean island. Winning both in the same year showed that he could handle different mountain conditions.
