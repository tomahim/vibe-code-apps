Capell has won the Transgrancanaria Classic four times. This race crosses the island of Gran Canaria, in Spain's Canary Islands, from north to south, with steep climbs, volcanic terrain and heat.

Winning the same demanding race four times means he knows the course and plans his effort well. Transgrancanaria is also a major event in the international trail calendar, and many top runners visit it in winter. It helped Capell build his reputation in the early part of the season.

Gran Canaria is a volcanic island with deep ravines, steep ridges and a warm climate. The race crosses the island from one coast to the other, with steep climbs and long descents between the two.
