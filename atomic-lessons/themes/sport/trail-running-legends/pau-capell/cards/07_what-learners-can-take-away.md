Capell's story shows that a runner can find a niche late. He moved from shorter distances to ultras and then grew into a champion. It also shows that a record can depend on the course and conditions, so it should be read with context.

Finally, it shows the value of racing in many places, from the Canary Islands to Patagonia and the Alps.

His results also show how professional trail running has grown. Runners now travel to races on several continents and plan their seasons around a handful of big events, with sponsors and teams supporting them.

Source: [The North Face athlete profile, Pau Capell](https://www.thenorthface.com/en-gb/athletes/pau-capell).
