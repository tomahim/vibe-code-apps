Capell has also won the Eiger Ultra Trail, the Patagonia 100 Miler and the TDS Ultra Trail du Mont Blanc. The Eiger Ultra Trail runs in the Swiss Alps near the Eiger mountain, the Patagonia 100 Miler in South America and TDS in the Alps.

Taken together, these wins show that he can succeed in very different mountains. He is not a specialist in one terrain. That variety is a sign of a strong runner who adapts quickly to new places, climates and styles of racing.

The TDS is one of the UTMB week races, running about 145 kilometres through the Italian and French Alps. Winning TDS first and UTMB later followed a path that several champions have used, building up distance step by step.
