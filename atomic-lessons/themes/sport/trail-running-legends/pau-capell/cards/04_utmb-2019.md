His biggest win came at UTMB in 2019. Capell won the 171 kilometre race, with about 10,200 metres of climbing, in 20 hours, 19 minutes and 7 seconds. At the time, this was the second fastest winning time in the history of the race.

The fastest at that time was François D'Haene's 19:01:32 in 2017, which used a slightly shorter course. Chamonix.net reported at the time that he finished in the second fastest time ever for the race.

The UTMB 2019 course was about 171 kilometres long with more than 10,000 metres of climbing. A finishing time of 20:19:07 means averaging more than 8 kilometres per hour over mountains, including a night section.
