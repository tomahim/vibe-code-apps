title: "Why a fast time matters"
archived: false
---
A fast winning time in a mountain race depends on conditions. Weather, snow, route changes and competition all change the result. That is why organisers and journalists describe records with some care.

Still, a time under 20 and a half hours on a course of over 170 kilometres is extremely fast. It required a steady pace, strong hill climbing and skilful fuelling. Capell's 2019 UTMB shows how the best runners combine all of these skills over more than a day.

Race organisers sometimes shorten or change routes because of snow, storms or safety concerns. For this reason, a record on one year's course may not be exactly comparable with the winning time of another year.
