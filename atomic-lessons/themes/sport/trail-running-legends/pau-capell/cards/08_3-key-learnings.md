**1. Distance can be a strength.** Capell found he did better as races got longer and tougher.

**2. Variety builds a record.** He won Transgrancanaria four times and also won Eiger, Patagonia and TDS.

**3. A fast time needs context.** His 20:19:07 at UTMB 2019 was the second fastest at the time.
