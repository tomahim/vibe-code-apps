UTMB starts and finishes in the town of Chamonix, at the foot of Mont Blanc, and the finish is a huge public event. Runners come back into town after more than a day on the trails, to crowds who stay up all night to cheer them in.

For a winner like Capell, arriving first means a short moment of celebration in front of the whole festival. The race is shown live around the world, which is one reason UTMB champions become famous. Spanish runners, such as Capell, Kilian Jornet and Núria Picas, have won there at the highest level.

The finish area in Chamonix has a large arch and a crowd, and the winners' names are announced to thousands of spectators. It is one of the most famous finish lines in running, and winners often speak about its emotional impact.
