Emelie Forsberg grew up in Sweden. Her father died when she was a newborn, and her mother worked hard to combine work, studies and raising two daughters. Orienteering, trekking, climbing, skiing and picking mushrooms and berries were part of her life from a very young age.

In a 2017 public lecture she said that between the ages of 15 and 20 she saw herself as a climber, not a runner. That outdoor foundation gave her strong legs and a calm confidence on rough ground, long before she entered an organised race.

Sweden has mountains in the north and west, but they are much gentler than the Alps. Growing up there, Forsberg learned to move easily in the outdoors, and later she found out how well that suited steeper, higher ranges.
