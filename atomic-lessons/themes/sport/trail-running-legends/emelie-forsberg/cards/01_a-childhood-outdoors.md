Emelie Forsberg grew up in the small village of Noraström on Sweden's High Coast. Her father died when she was a newborn, and her mother brought up Emelie and her sister Tess while also working and studying. Like most children in the 1990s she played outside a lot, but she was more outdoorsy than most.

She enjoyed orienteering, trekking, climbing, skiing and foraging for mushrooms and berries, which she says came mostly from her grandparents. That outdoor foundation gave her strong legs and calm confidence on rough ground, long before she entered an organised race.

The High Coast is an area of Sweden's Baltic coastline with low hills and forest, much gentler than the Alps. Growing up there, Forsberg learned to move easily in the outdoors, and later she found out how well that suited steeper, higher ranges.
