Besides racing in summer, since 2012 Forsberg has also excelled at ski mountaineering in winter. By 2013 she had won the Norwegian Ski Mountaineering National Championships. She sees her greatest ski mountaineering achievement as winning the Trofeo Mezzalama in 2015 with teammates Axelle Mollaret and Jennifer Feichter, one week before she switched to running shoes to win the 2015 Transvulcania Ultramarathon.

CNN described her in 2014 as both a world and a European champion in skyrunning. She told CNN that she was European champion in both the long and the short distance and second in the vertical the year before.

Her medals in short, steep races and in long ultra events show a wide range. Championships exist at both European and world levels, and they often take place in the same mountain regions as famous races.
