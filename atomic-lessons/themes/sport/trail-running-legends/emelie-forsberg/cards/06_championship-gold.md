At the 2014 Skyrunning World Championships in Chamonix, Forsberg won gold in the Ultra SkyMarathon. At the European Championships she won the Sky title in Canazei in 2013, the Ultra title in Vicenza in 2013 and the Ultra in Val d'Isère in 2015.

She also has a silver in the Vertical event in Canazei in 2013. Her medals in both short, steep races and long ultra events show a wide range. She also competes in ski mountaineering, which fits the mountain lifestyle she describes.

Championships exist at both European and world levels, and they often take place in the same mountain regions as famous races. Winning medals in several formats shows that she was strong in short, steep events and in long ones.

Source: [CNN on Emelie Forsberg and sky running](https://www.cnn.com/2014/10/08/sport/emelie-forsberg-sky-running).
