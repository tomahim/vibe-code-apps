**1. Start from curiosity.** Forsberg began by running in the mountains for fun and to get around.

**2. Improvement can be fast.** In the Fjällmaraton she improved by 30 minutes in 2011 and another 30 in 2014.

**3. Range wins titles.** She won world and European medals in sky, vertical and ultra events.
