**1. Start from curiosity.** Forsberg began by running in the mountains for fun and to get around.

**2. Improvement can be fast.** She won the Fjällmaraton in 2009 and again in 2011, then moved on to the world series.

**3. Range wins titles.** She won the Dolomites Skyrace, Pikes Peak Marathon and North Face 50 Mile in 2012 and most Ultra series races from 2013 to 2015.
