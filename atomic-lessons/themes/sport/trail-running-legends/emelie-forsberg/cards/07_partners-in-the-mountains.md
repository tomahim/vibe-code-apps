Forsberg's partner is Kilian Jornet, the Catalan mountain runner who is the subject of an earlier lesson. CNN reported in 2014 that they lived together in the French ski resort of Chamonix, under Mont Blanc, which it called the perfect training ground for the sport's golden couple.

The CNN story also describes how the two of them ran into trouble near the summit of the Aiguille du Midi, a near 4,000 metre mountain, when the weather got worse quickly. It is a reminder that mountain sport carries risk, however experienced the athletes are.

Their story shows how mountain sport can be a shared lifestyle. Forsberg is often described as one of the faces of this new generation of skyrunners because of her enthusiasm for racing and for the outdoors.

Source: [iRunFar, an interview with Emelie Forsberg](https://www.irunfar.com/for-the-love-of-it-an-interview-with-emelie-forsberg).
