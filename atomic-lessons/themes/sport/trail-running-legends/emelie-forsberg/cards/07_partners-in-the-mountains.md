Forsberg's partner is Kilian Jornet, the Catalan mountain runner who is the subject of an earlier lesson. They train and travel together in the Alps and the Pyrenees, and they have shared adventures and projects.

Their story shows how mountain sport can be a shared lifestyle. It also shows how skyrunning grew from a small niche into an international sport, with athletes from Sweden, Spain, Italy and more. Forsberg is often described as one of the faces of this new generation because of her enthusiasm for racing and for the outdoors.

Mountain couples often train together, share routes and travel to races side by side. For Forsberg and Jornet, this means a shared life built around the outdoors, and it keeps their interest in new projects alive.

Source: [iRunFar, an interview with Emelie Forsberg](https://www.irunfar.com/for-the-love-of-it-an-interview-with-emelie-forsberg).
