In 2012 alone Forsberg won the Dolomites Skyrace and the Pikes Peak Marathon, and she finished on the podium at the Zegama-Aizkorri Marathon in Spain and at the Trofeo Kima. She also won The North Face Endurance Challenge 50 Mile Championships and took second at the Grand Trail des Templiers, which showed that she could race ultramarathons too, according to iRunFar.

She studied in Tromsø in Norway, where in 2011 she met the Catalan runner and ski mountaineer Kilian Jornet while cheering at a ski mountaineering World Cup race. They were just friends at first, then met again at a Salomon trail running camp.

The Dolomites are a mountain range in northern Italy with dramatic rock towers. Winning a skyrace there in her first international season was a sign that a new star had arrived.
