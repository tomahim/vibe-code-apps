In May 2012 Forsberg was invited to the Zegama-Aizkorri race in Spain, one of the most famous mountain marathons, and finished third. In July 2012 she won the Dolomites Skyrace. She later said it was insane to realise that what she did alone in the Swedish mountains was a big sport in the Alps.

After that she moved from Storulvån to Tromsø in Norway to study. From then on she combined her studies with a fast growing racing career, and soon she was competing against the best skyrunners in the world.

The Dolomites are a mountain range in northern Italy with dramatic rock towers. Winning a skyrace there only two months after Zegama showed fans and organisers that a new international star had arrived in the sport.
