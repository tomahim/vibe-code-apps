In November 2014 Greenwood lined up for the third time at the IAU 100 km World Championships. She is a four time 100 km world champion, winning both the individual and team titles in 2010 and 2014.

Ultrarunning Magazine named her North American Female Ultrarunner of the Year in 2011, 2012 and 2014. She is also a former champion and course record holder for the Canadian Death Race, Western States and the JFK 50 Mile. Her career combined mountain trails, fast roads and a very long road race in South Africa.

Ultrarunning Magazine names a North American Ultrarunner of the Year after looking at results across the season. Being chosen three times is a sign of consistent results over many races, not just one big win.
