Greenwood showed that a runner could succeed at many types of races. She won on mountain trails, set a record on a fast 50 mile course and won on the road at Comrades.

Her story also shows how athletes build careers step by step. She started ultras at 26, won her first 100K trail world record at 31 and broke a legendary Western States record at 33. Anyone who feels late to the sport can find encouragement in her path.

Greenwood began ultras at 26 and set her biggest records in her early thirties. Her story is useful for anyone who comes to a sport later than most people, and who needs patience to get past injuries.

Source: [iRunFar interview with Ellie Greenwood, 2012 Western States champion](https://www.irunfar.com/ellie-greenwood-2012-western-states-100-champ-interview).
