That sequence shows how a record moves forward in stages: Trason's 1994 time, Greenwood's 2012 time and Dauwalter's 2023 time. It also shows that progress in women's ultrarunning came from several athletes who each pushed the limit a bit more.

A women's course record shows the progress of the sport over time. From 17:37:51 in 1994 to 16:47:19 in 2012 and then 15:29:34 in 2023, the best women have taken more than two hours off the record in three decades.
