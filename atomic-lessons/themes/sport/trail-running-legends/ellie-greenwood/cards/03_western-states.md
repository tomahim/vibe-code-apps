In 2011 Greenwood took on the Western States 100 in California and won on her first try. She won again in 2012, in a course record of 16:47:19. The previous record of 17:37:51 had been set by Ann Trason in 1994, 18 years earlier.

The record was a landmark. Trason was already the most successful woman in the race, so beating her time meant running faster than an all time great. In the same year Greenwood also broke the course record at the JFK 50 Mile, finishing tenth overall, and won the UTMB CCC race.

Western States is held on the last weekend of June, and the weather can be very hot in the canyons. Breaking a record there needs good conditions, a well paced plan and a very strong second half of the race.
