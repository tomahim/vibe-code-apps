Ellie Greenwood was born on March 14, 1979, in Dundee, Scotland. When she was eight her family moved to Norfolk, England. After university she moved to Canada in 2001 to work for a ski tour operator, and she has lived there since. She is both British and Canadian.

She began her ultramarathon career in 2005. One of her first races was the 30 mile Knee Knackering North Shore Trail Run near Vancouver, a short course known for steep climbs and descents. Outdoor company Montrail began sponsoring her in 2005.

Dundee is a coastal city on the east coast of Scotland, and Norfolk is a flat county in eastern England. Greenwood grew up far from big mountains, and she discovered trail running only after moving to Canada's mountains near Vancouver.
