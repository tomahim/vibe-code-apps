Greenwood has run South Africa's 90 kilometre Comrades Marathon four times. She came fourth in 2011 and second in 2012, just 72 seconds behind the winner. In 2013 she was plagued by injuries.

She returned to running slowly and focused on Comrades 2014 to stay motivated. With 18 kilometres to go she was eight minutes behind the leader, but she recovered and won in 6:18:15. She was the first British woman ever to win the race. It was a fine example of patience and nerve.

The Comrades Marathon runs between Durban and Pietermaritzburg in South Africa, and it changes direction each year, with an up run and a down run. It is the world's oldest and largest ultramarathon, with thousands of runners.
