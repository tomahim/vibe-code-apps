In 2019 Thévenard won the Ultra-Trail Mt. Fuji, known as UTMF, in Japan. The same year he also won the Mont-Blanc 90 km in Chamonix.

Winning in Japan showed that his success was not limited to the Alps. UTMF circles Mount Fuji and has different terrain, climate and food. A runner can use the same basic skills, but must adjust to heat, humidity and different trail surfaces.

UTMF circles Mount Fuji, the highest mountain in Japan at 3,776 metres, and it is part of the UTMB World Series. Runners face humid weather and a different food and aid station culture from European races.
