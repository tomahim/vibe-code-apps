In 2017 Thévenard won the Mont-Blanc 80 km, a race of the Marathon du Mont-Blanc weekend in Chamonix. The course is shorter than UTMB but still demanding, with big climbs and fast descents.

It is a good example of how one championship career can include many different race lengths. A runner who is strong at 80 kilometres, 100 kilometres and 170 kilometres can choose between many events in the same season, which suits a professional schedule.

The Marathon du Mont-Blanc weekend in Chamonix includes several races of different lengths, from short vertical climbs to ultras. It is held every year in late June and is a popular event with both amateurs and professionals.
