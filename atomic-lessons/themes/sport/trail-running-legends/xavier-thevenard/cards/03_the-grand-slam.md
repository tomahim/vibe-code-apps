Thévenard was the first athlete to achieve the UTMB Grand Slam, with victories in all four individual races of the UTMB week: the CCC in 2010, the TDS in 2014, the OCC in 2016 and the main UTMB in 2013, 2015 and 2018.

These races have different distances, from about 55 kilometres (OCC) to about 170 kilometres (UTMB), and different profiles. Some reward speed, others endurance and fuelling. Winning all four shows a rare ability to adapt, and few other athletes have a record that spans every distance in the festival.

The OCC runs from Orsières in Switzerland to Chamonix, the CCC from Courmayeur in Italy to Chamonix, and the TDS goes through the Aosta Valley and Savoie. Together these races cover many mountain landscapes around Mont Blanc.
