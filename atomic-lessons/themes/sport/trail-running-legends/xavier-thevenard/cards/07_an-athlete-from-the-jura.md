Thévenard's story is about local roots and long term development. A skier from a small village became the only person to win every individual UTMB race.

His career also shows that talent often starts in another sport. Skiing and biathlon taught him how to push hard in cold, steep terrain and manage effort. Many trail runners have winter sports in their background.

His story is a reminder that many trail runners start in another sport. Cross country skiers, biathletes, orienteers and mountain bikers often switch to trail running once they find that they enjoy long days in the mountains.

Source: [Guinness World Records, first UTMB Grand Slam](https://guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/709348-first-ultra-trail-du-mont-blanc-grand-slam).
