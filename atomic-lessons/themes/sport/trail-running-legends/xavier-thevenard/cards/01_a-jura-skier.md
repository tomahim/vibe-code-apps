Xavier Thévenard was born on March 6, 1988, in Nantua, France. He comes from Les Plans d'Hotonnes, a small ski resort in the Jura mountains. As a young athlete he was a cross country skier and a biathlete, a sport that combines skiing and rifle shooting.

He started running in summer as physical training for winter sport. That background built a strong engine and good technique on hills. He entered his first ultra running competitions in 2010, and soon it became clear that his talent for long mountain races was bigger than his talent for biathlon.

The Jura is a range of low mountains along the French and Swiss border, known for cross country skiing in winter. Thévenard's background in biathlon also taught him to control his breathing and heart rate before shooting.
