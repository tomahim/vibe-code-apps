In 2017 Picas finally won UTMB. For the first 150 kilometres she built a comfortable lead. Then, during the long climb to La Flégère, she had an asthma attack and her lead shrank sharply.

World Athletics reported that it would not have taken much for her dream to disappear just a few kilometres from the finish. She held on and crossed the line first. The story shows how a race can change in the final hours, and why even a large lead is not safe in a 170 kilometre mountain race.

La Flégère is a mountain station above Chamonix, and the climb to it comes late in the race, when runners are already very tired. An asthma attack there could have ended her hopes, but she was able to keep moving until the finish.
