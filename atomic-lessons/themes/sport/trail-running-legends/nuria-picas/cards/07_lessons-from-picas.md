Her career has three clear lessons. First, a tour title shows consistency over a whole season. Second, a big race can have many chapters: two second places, a withdrawal, a break and finally a win. Third, a lead in an ultra is never final until the finish.

These lessons apply beyond running. Long projects often involve setbacks, and success can come from trying again with more experience.

Setbacks, illness and near misses can be part of any long project, and the way a person reacts can matter as much as the moment of success itself. In Picas's case the long break from racing came first, and the win followed after it.

Source: [iRunFar, Núria Picas 2017 UTMB champion interview](https://www.irunfar.com/nuria-picas-2017-utmb-champion-interview).
