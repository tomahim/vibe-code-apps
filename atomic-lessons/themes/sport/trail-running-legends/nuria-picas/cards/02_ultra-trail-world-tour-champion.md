Picas won the Ultra-Trail World Tour in 2014 and again in 2015. The World Tour was a series of major trail races around the world, with points awarded across the season. The 2014 ranking list shows her first among the women, ahead of Fernanda Maciel of Brazil. In 2014 her wins included Transgrancanaria, the Mount Fuji race and The North Face 100 in Australia, according to Marca.

A tour title rewards a different skill from a single race win. It requires staying healthy, travelling, recovering and performing at several events. Winning it twice means she was consistently strong against the best runners on several continents.

The Ultra-Trail World Tour combined results from a selection of famous races, and athletes earned points based on their best finishes. It rewarded runners who could perform on different continents and in different weather.
