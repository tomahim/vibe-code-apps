Picas won the Ultra-Trail World Tour in 2014 and again in 2015. The World Tour was a series of major trail races around the world, with points awarded across the season. Winning it twice means she was consistently strong against the best runners on several continents.

A tour title rewards a different skill from a single race win. It requires staying healthy, travelling, recovering and performing at several events. In 2014 the first men's title went to François D'Haene.

The Ultra-Trail World Tour combined results from a selection of famous races around the world, and athletes earned points based on their best finishes. It rewarded runners who could perform on different continents and in different weather.
