UTMB denied Picas for a long time. She finished second twice, and twice more she tried the race but could not start or finish. According to iRunFar, she came to the 2017 edition after a physical and mental break from international racing of about a year and a half.

This history gave the 2017 race extra meaning. Many runners finish a famous race once. Picas kept returning, hoping to turn near misses into a win, and each attempt required a full season of preparation.

At UTMB, runners spend about a day and a night in the mountains, and the final hours often decide the result. Coming close twice, without winning, can be hard to accept, and returning for a third time takes real determination.
