UTMB denied Picas for a long time. She finished second at UTMB twice, in 2013 and 2014. In 2015 she and Tòfol Castanyer were among the favourites but both withdrew with physical problems. According to iRunFar, she came to the 2017 edition after a physical and mental break from international racing of about a year and a half.

Ian Corless wrote that she was missing from racing in the later part of 2015 and pretty much throughout 2016. This history gave the 2017 race extra meaning. Many runners finish a famous race once. Picas kept returning, hoping to turn near misses into a win, and each attempt required a full season of preparation.

At UTMB, runners spend about a day and a night in the mountains, and the final hours often decide the result. Coming close twice without winning can be hard to accept, and returning takes real determination.
