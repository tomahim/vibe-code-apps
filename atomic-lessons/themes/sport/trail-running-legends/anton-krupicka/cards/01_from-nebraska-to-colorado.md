Anton Krupicka was born on August 2, 1983, and grew up near Niobrara, Nebraska. He studied at Colorado College, where he earned a BA in physics and philosophy in 2005, and a second BA in geology in 2006.

He was a graduate student at the University of Colorado but left to become a professional runner. His studies in physics, philosophy and geology fit with the way he talks about mountains, as places to understand slowly as well as to run fast.

Niobrara is a very small town in northern Nebraska, far from the high mountains. Krupicka moved to Colorado for college, and the contrast between the plains of his childhood and the Rockies helps explain how strongly the mountains drew him.
