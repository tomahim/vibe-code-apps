Krupicka won the Leadville 100 twice. He also won the Rocky Raccoon 100 Miler, the Miwok 100K, the Collegiate Peaks 50 Miler, the High Mountain 50k and the Estes Park Marathon.

Leadville is a high altitude race in the Colorado Rockies, with the course staying above 9,000 feet. Winning it twice means handling altitude, cold nights and a big field. It made him one of the most recognisable names in American ultrarunning.

The Leadville 100 starts at about 10,200 feet and goes over a pass above 12,000 feet. The thin air, cold nights and big fields make it a special race, and winning it twice is a strong sign of toughness.
