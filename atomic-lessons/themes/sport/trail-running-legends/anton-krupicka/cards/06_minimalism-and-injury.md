Krupicka is known for his long hair and beard, for often running without a shirt and for lightweight, minimal running shoes. He became a face of the minimalist running movement in the 2000s and early 2010s.

His career has been strongly shaped by injuries. He branched out into climbing, skiing and especially cycling. In August 2016 he and climber Stefan Griebel set the fastest known time for the Longs Peak Triathlon: a 79 mile bike ride from Boulder, a 14 mile run and a climb of the Diamond on Longs Peak, all in 9 hours and 6 minutes.

Minimalist running became popular after 2009, partly because of Born to Run and runners like Krupicka. The idea is to use lighter shoes and let the foot work more naturally, though many coaches advise adding it very slowly.
