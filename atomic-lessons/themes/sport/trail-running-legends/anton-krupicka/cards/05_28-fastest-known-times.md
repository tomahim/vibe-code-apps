Krupicka has set 28 fastest known time records. One of them is the 28 mile Maroon Bells 4 Passes Loop in Colorado, which he ran in 2009.

A fastest known time, or FKT, is a record on a specific route, not in a race. There are no entry fees, no aid stations and no organisers, only a runner, a route and a clock. FKTs fit Krupicka's character, because they let him choose beautiful routes and run them in his own style.

Colorado's Maroon Bells are two famous peaks near Aspen, and the 4 Passes Loop circles them over four high mountain passes. The route is popular with hikers, so an FKT there shows how fast Krupicka moves over rough ground.
