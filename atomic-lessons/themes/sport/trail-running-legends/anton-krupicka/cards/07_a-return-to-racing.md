In September 2024 Krupicka placed second at the Grindstone Trail Running Festival by UTMB 100 Miles. That result earned him a place at the 2025 UTMB World Series Final in Chamonix, France, his first appearance there since 2014.

His return shows a long career with many chapters. After years of injuries and adventures in other sports, he was back at the biggest ultra event in the world.

Grindstone is a 100 mile race in Virginia, part of the UTMB World Series, which awards qualifying places for the final in Chamonix. Finishing second there at age 41 shows how long a runner can stay competitive.
