Trason's Western States career began in 1987, but it did not begin well. She did not finish her first two entries, and she could not complete the race until 1989. When she did, she was the first woman across the line, which means she won the women's race at her first finish.

That 1989 result started one of the most dominant streaks in the sport. The Western States 100 runs 100 miles from the Sierra Nevada foothills near Squaw Valley to Auburn. Trason knew those trails, and she used that knowledge to turn a rough beginning into a long reign.

Western States is one of the oldest 100 mile trail races in the United States. Finishing it at all is hard: the course climbs and descends thousands of metres and crosses canyons where afternoon temperatures can be very high.
