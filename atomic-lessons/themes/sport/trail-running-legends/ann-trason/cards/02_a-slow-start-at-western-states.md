Trason's Western States career began in 1987, but it did not begin well. She did not finish that year because of a knee problem, and in 1988 she dropped out because of dehydration. She then tried the Leadville 100 in Colorado and won it in 1988. She would go on to win Leadville four times.

In 1989 she finished Western States for the first time and was the first woman across the line. That result started one of the most dominant streaks in the sport. The Western States 100 runs 100 miles from Olympic Valley, near Lake Tahoe, through the Sierra Nevada to the finish in Auburn, California.

Western States is one of the oldest 100 mile trail races in the United States. Trason's early dropouts show that even a future champion can need a few attempts to learn how to pace a long mountain race in the heat.
