**1. She won where she lived.** Trason won Western States 14 times, finishing in her hometown of Auburn.

**2. Her records lasted.** Her 1994 course record stood 18 years and her 50 mile world record stood from 1991 to 2015.

**3. A slow start can lead to greatness.** She did not finish her first two Western States entries, then won it for years.
