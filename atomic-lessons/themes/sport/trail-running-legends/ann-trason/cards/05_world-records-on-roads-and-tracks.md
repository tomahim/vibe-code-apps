Trason did not only race on mountain trails. Her world record of 5:40:18 for 50 miles, set in 1991, was unbeaten until 2015, which is 24 years. She also set records at other ultra distances, and in total she held 20 world records.

She also raised the bar at the American River 50 Mile. She came back to that first race 8 years after her debut and ran an hour faster, setting a 6:09 course record that is still listed as the women's best. Speed on fast courses and strength on mountain trails are different skills, and Trason showed both.

A 50 mile world record is a different challenge from a mountain 100 miler. It needs steady speed on fast ground over about five and a half hours, and it shows that Trason's strength was not limited to one kind of race.
