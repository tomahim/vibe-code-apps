Trason did not only race on mountain trails. Her world record of 5:40:18 for 50 miles, set in 1991, was unbeaten until 2015, which is 24 years. She also set world records at 100 kilometres, 12 hours and 100 miles, and in total she set 20 world records.

She also raised the bar at the American River 50 Mile. She came back eight years after her 1985 win and lowered her course record by a whole hour, to 6:09:08. Speed on fast courses and strength on mountain trails are different skills, and Trason showed both.

In 1996 she became the first American woman to win the Comrades Marathon in South Africa, a road ultra of about 55 miles between Durban and Pietermaritzburg, with an uphill course record of 6:13:23. Winning on roads, tracks and mountain trails is why her record is so wide.
