In 2020 Ann Trason was inducted into the Ultrarunning Hall of Fame, and by then many people considered her the most successful female ultrarunner of all time. Her record at Western States, her 20 world records and her 14 wins make a strong case.

Her story also shows how an athlete can succeed after setbacks. She missed finishes early, she dealt with injuries for years, and she still became the standard for others. Younger women who later set Western States records, including Greenwood and Dauwalter, raced on a course that Trason had already made famous.

The Ultrarunning Hall of Fame honours athletes who changed the sport. Her induction in 2020 came decades after her best years, but it confirmed how long her records and her example have influenced runners who came after her.

Source: [Ultrarunning History, Ann Trason](https://ultrarunninghistory.com/hall-of-fame/hall-of-fame-ann-trason).
