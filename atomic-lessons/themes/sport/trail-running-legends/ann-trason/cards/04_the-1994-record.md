In 1994 Trason ran Western States in 17 hours, 37 minutes and 51 seconds, a women's course record. The record then stood for 18 years. It was finally broken in 2012 by Ellie Greenwood, who ran 16:47:19.

That gap shows how far ahead of her time Trason was. A record that lasts almost two decades survives changes in shoes, training, nutrition and prize money. Greenwood needed a great day and strong conditions to beat it. Later in this path you will see how Courtney Dauwalter lowered the women's record again in 2023.

A record is only as strong as the athletes who try to beat it. In the years after 1994, many talented women ran Western States, yet none broke 17:37:51 until Greenwood. That shows how strong Trason's run really was.
