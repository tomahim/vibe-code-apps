In 1994 Trason ran Western States in 17 hours, 37 minutes and 51 seconds, a women's course record. The record then stood for 18 years. It was finally broken in 2012 by Ellie Greenwood, who ran 16:47:19.

That gap shows how far ahead of her time Trason was. A record that lasts almost two decades has to survive many rival champions, and in the years after 1994 many talented women ran Western States without breaking 17:37:51. Greenwood needed a very good day to beat it. Later in this path you will see how Courtney Dauwalter lowered the women's record again in 2023, to 15:29:34.

Only four weeks after her 1994 record, Trason raced the Leadville 100 and finished second overall, a result that leads into a famous book in a later card.
