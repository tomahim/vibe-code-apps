D'Haene won the Grand Raid on Réunion, the island's famous mountain ultra, in 2013, 2014 and 2016. Réunion is in the Indian Ocean and has volcanic terrain with steep slopes. The race is nicknamed the Diagonale des Fous.

In 2014 he won the first Ultra-Trail World Tour, a season long series across several major races. This made him the first champion of that tour. Winning in both Chamonix and Réunion shows how adaptable his racing is.

The Diagonale des Fous, or Grand Raid, crosses Réunion from the south of the island to the north, passing through volcanic craters and tropical forest. It is about 165 kilometres long, with very steep climbs and descents.
