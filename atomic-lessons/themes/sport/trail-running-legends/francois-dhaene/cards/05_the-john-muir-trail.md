On October 17, 2017, D'Haene set a new record on the John Muir Trail in California's Sierra Nevada. He finished the 211 mile route in 2 days, 19 hours and 26 minutes.

An FKT like this has no crowds or aid stations. The runner carries a plan, supporters help along the way, and the clock is the only opponent. D'Haene's run came only weeks after he won UTMB in 2017, which shows how fast he can recover and move to the next project.

The John Muir Trail runs through the Sierra Nevada from Yosemite Valley to Mount Whitney. It passes many high mountain passes above 3,000 metres, so a fast time needs good altitude fitness and careful planning of food and rest.
