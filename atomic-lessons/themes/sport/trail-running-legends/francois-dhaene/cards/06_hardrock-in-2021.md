On July 17, 2021, D'Haene won the Hardrock 100. He finished in 21:45:51 and broke both the counter clockwise and the overall course record, both previously held by Kilian Jornet.

A month later he won UTMB in 20:45:59, a rare double. Hardrock is run at high altitude in the San Juan Mountains of Colorado, while UTMB goes around Mont Blanc. To win both in a single summer, with such a short gap, he needed careful planning of rest, travel and training.

Because Hardrock is run at altitude and reverses direction every year, a record needs to be compared with times in the same direction. That is why D'Haene's win counted as a record in both counter clockwise and overall terms.
