D'Haene won the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc in 2012, 2014, 2017 and 2021. UTMB is the most famous trail race in the world, a loop of roughly 170 kilometres around Mont Blanc with more than 10,000 metres of climbing.

In 2021 he won in 20:45:59 in August. Between wins he also raced the world's best elsewhere. His four titles put him among the very few runners who have won UTMB so many times. He is part of a small group that includes Kilian Jornet and Xavier Thévenard.

UTMB takes place in late August or early September and attracts thousands of runners and many more spectators. The route goes through Les Houches, Saint-Gervais, Courmayeur and Champex before finishing back in Chamonix.
