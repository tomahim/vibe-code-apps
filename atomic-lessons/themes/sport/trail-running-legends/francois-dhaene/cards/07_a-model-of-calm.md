D'Haene's career is a model of steady excellence. He combines a regular life with a family and a vineyard with a global racing schedule. He is not known for showy behaviour, but he is known for results.

For learners, his story offers a simple message: consistent routines can build extraordinary performances. He proved it from 2012 to 2021, in races with very different terrain.

Many top runners are known for intense, public rivalries, but D'Haene is known for calm. His example shows that different personalities can succeed at the top of the same sport.

Source: [World Athletics report on UTMB 2017](https://worldathletics.org/news/report/umtb-2017-francois-dhaene).
