François D'Haene was born on December 24, 1985, in Lille, France. He used to work as a physiotherapist and became a wine grower in 2012. He lives in the Beaujolais winemaking region with his wife and three children.

His life away from running is quiet and rooted in the land. That calm shows in the way he races. He rarely makes dramatic gestures, preferring steady, well planned efforts that often win over many hours. Fans call him the quiet king of ultras.

The Beaujolais region lies north of Lyon, with rolling hills covered in vines. Working in a vineyard involves physical work outdoors, which fits well with a life of mountain training and long runs in nature.
