D'Haene set record times at UTMB. In 2014 he won in 20 hours, 11 minutes and 44 seconds, a new record at the time. In 2017 he won in 19 hours, 1 minute and 32 seconds, the quickest race in UTMB history until then.

In 2017 the organisers changed the route at the last minute because of a storm, taking out two sections, so that time is not directly comparable with a full course. Even so, the 2017 run was a landmark: he finished the UTMB in under 20 hours. Later, Kilian Jornet and Jim Walmsley also ran very fast.

Because the course changes slightly from year to year, the official distance and climbing can vary. That is why runners and fans compare times with care and often focus on race wins and rivalries more than on one exact time.
