D'Haene holds records for the UTMB course. In 2014 he ran the 167 kilometre course in 20 hours, 11 minutes and 44 seconds. In 2017, on a 170 kilometre course, he improved the speed record to 19 hours, 1 minute and 32 seconds.

Course lengths change slightly from year to year because of weather and route changes, so records are compared with care. Even so, the 2017 run was a landmark: he finished the UTMB in under 20 hours. Later, Jim Walmsley and others also ran very fast.

Because the course changes slightly from year to year, the official distance and climbing can vary. That is why runners and fans compare times with care and often focus on race wins and rivalries more than on one exact time.
