World mountain running events use courses with a lot of climbing, and the long race format can include both an ascent and a descent. The courses are usually on trails or rough tracks, over distances much shorter than ultramarathons, which makes them fast and hard.

This is different from a 100 mile race. A mountain runner does not need to save energy for a whole day, because the effort lasts about an hour or two. Wyatt's mix of track speed, marathon endurance and comfort on steep ground made him an ideal fit for that format.

Most mountain running events take about an hour or two. That is long enough to need endurance but short enough to demand a hard pace, so athletes need both strong climbing and bold, confident downhill running.
