Wyatt also took part in the Taipei 101 Run Up, a race up the stairs of one of the world's tall skyscrapers. A photo from the 2010 edition shows him being interviewed after the event.

Stair and vertical races are a different kind of mountain running, because every step goes up. They reward strong legs and efficient breathing. Wyatt's willingness to enter unusual events helped show that running uphill can take many forms, from alpine trails to city towers.

Run up races on skyscrapers usually take only a few minutes and involve climbing more than a thousand steps. They are a good example of how running uphill can be turned into a short, explosive event that anyone can follow.
