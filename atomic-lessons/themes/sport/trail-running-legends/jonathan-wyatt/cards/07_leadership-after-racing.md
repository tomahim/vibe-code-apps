Wyatt works for the sports brand La Sportiva. In 2017 he became president of the World Mountain Running Association council, the body that organises world mountain running events.

That role matters because mountain running is spread across many countries and styles. Skyrunning, trail running and classic mountain running have different rules, and an experienced champion helps connect them. Wyatt is a bridge between road, track and mountain athletics.

The World Mountain Running Association organises the world championships and works with national federations. A former champion in a leadership role can speak for athletes and help choose courses, rules and safety standards.

Source: [World Athletics profile of Jonathan Wyatt](https://worldathletics.org/athletes/new-zealand/jonathan-wyatt-14215949).
