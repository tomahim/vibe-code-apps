Jonathan Wyatt was born on December 20, 1972, and runs for New Zealand. He started as a track and road runner. At the 1994 Commonwealth Games he ran the 5,000 metres and reached the final. He holds the New Zealand national record for 5 kilometres at 13:46.

He was a two time Olympian, racing the 5,000 metres in 1996 and the marathon in 2004. At the 2002 Commonwealth Games he finished sixth in the marathon. This background gave him speed on flat ground, which he later carried into steep courses where many specialists lacked such pure pace.

Moving from the track to the hills is not easy, because hills ask for different strength and technique. Wyatt's Olympic level training gave him a fast base, and he added the skill of climbing and descending to it over time.
