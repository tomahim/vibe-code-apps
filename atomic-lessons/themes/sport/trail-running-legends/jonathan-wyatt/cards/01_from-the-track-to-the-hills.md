Jonathan Wyatt was born on December 20, 1972, and runs for New Zealand. He started as a track and road runner.

He was a two time Olympian, first at the Atlanta Games in 1996, according to the Olympic record, and later in the marathon, where he placed 21st. This background gave him speed on flat ground, which he later carried into steep courses where many specialists lacked such pure pace.

Moving from the track to the hills is not easy, because hills ask for different strength and technique. Wyatt's Olympic level training gave him a fast base, and he added the skill of climbing and descending to it over time.
