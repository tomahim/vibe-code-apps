Besides his world titles, Wyatt is an eight time winner of the world mountain running Grand Prix series, a season long competition across several races. A Grand Prix title rewards consistency across a whole season, not one great day.

Winning one title can happen on a good day, but winning eight Grand Prix series suggests a runner who could perform in different conditions for years.

A season long series rewards athletes who can travel, recover and perform many times. Eight series wins show not only talent but also good health, careful planning and the ability to adapt to many types of mountain.
