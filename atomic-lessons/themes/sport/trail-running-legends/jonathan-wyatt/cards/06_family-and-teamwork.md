Wyatt is married to Antonella Confortola, an Italian sky runner and cross country skier. They live in northern Italy with their daughter. The couple returned to the NutenOpp race in Rosendal, Norway, several times, and each held a course record there, his from 2008 and hers from 2010.

Their partnership is one of several famous couples in the mountains, alongside Kilian Jornet and Emelie Forsberg. It shows how mountain running is often a family lifestyle built around training outdoors.

Mountain running communities are often small and international, and many athletes meet their partners through the sport. Wyatt and Confortola both compete on the mountains and have shared training and travel for years.
