Wyatt is a six time world mountain running champion. According to his medal record he won the long race at the World Mountain Running Championships in 1998 in Dimitile, 2000 in Bergen, 2002 in Innsbruck, 2004 in Sauze d'Oulx, 2005 in Wellington and 2008 in Crans-Montana.

In 2006 in Bursa he finished second. Six wins over ten years is a rare record in any sport. Mountain running championships take place in a different country each year, so winning meant adapting to new terrain, weather and altitude again and again.

The World Mountain Running Championships have been held in many countries since the 1980s, and they move to a new mountain each year. That makes it hard to dominate, because the course and the altitude are different every time.
