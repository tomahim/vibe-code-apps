title: Coppi versus Bartali
archived: false
---
Cycling's greatest rivalry played out between Coppi and Gino Bartali, nine years his senior and Italy's established champion. They raced on the same national team and sometimes the same trade team, which produced surreal scenes: at the 1948 World Championship neither would help the other, and Italy lost a race it could have won. Bartali was the devout, conservative hero of rural Italy; Coppi the modern, urbane star.

Their duel reached its peak at the 1949 Tour and the 1949 and 1952 Giros, where Coppi repeatedly came out on top, while Bartali's 1948 Tour victory, coming days after an assassination attempt on communist leader Palmiro Togliatti, was credited with calming a nation on the edge of unrest. Italy never quite stopped arguing about which of them was greater.
