Angelo Fausto Coppi was born on 15 September 1919 in Castellania, a village in the Piedmont hills of northern Italy. A sickly child with little interest in school, he left at thirteen to work for a butcher in Novi Ligure, riding a heavy delivery bicycle through the hills every day. Those work rides were his first training.

His uncle helped him buy a proper bike, and the delivery boy started racing. He turned professional before the Second World War and immediately showed a rare all-round talent: he could climb with the specialists, time trial against anyone, and sprint well enough to win small groups. Italian fans would soon give him a title no one else has carried since: Il Campionissimo, the champion of champions.
