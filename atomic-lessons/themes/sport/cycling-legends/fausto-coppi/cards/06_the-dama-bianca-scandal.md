Coppi's later career mixed success with personal turmoil. He won the World Championship road race in 1953 and took his fifth Giro d'Italia that same year, adding to a palmares that already included five Giri di Lombardia, three Milan-San Remo titles, a Paris-Roubaix and a Flèche Wallonne.

At the same time his relationship with Giulia Occhini, known as the Dama Bianca, the lady in white, scandalised conservative Italy. Both were married to other people, and their affair was pursued by the press, the courts and public opinion at a time when adultery was a criminal matter in Italy. The affair damaged his popularity and clouded his final seasons, even as his results kept him among the very best in the world.
