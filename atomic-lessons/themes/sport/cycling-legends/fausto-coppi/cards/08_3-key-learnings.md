**1. The first Giro-Tour double.** Coppi won the Giro and the Tour in the same year, 1949, something no one had done before, then did it again in 1952.

**2. A complete rider.** Five Giros, two Tours, a world title, five Lombardias and the 1942 hour record show an all-round talent without weakness.

**3. Half of cycling's greatest rivalry.** His duel with Gino Bartali divided post-war Italy and remains the sport's most famous rivalry.
