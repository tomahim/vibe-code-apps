Vos has shaped her sport off the bike as much as on it. She was a founding member of Le Tour Entier, the campaign group that pushed for a women's Tour de France and for better pay, coverage and conditions in women's cycling generally.

The campaign worked. The Tour de France Femmes was reborn in 2022, and Vos, then thirty-five, won stages and wore the yellow jersey in its first edition. For younger riders she is both a rival and the reason their races exist on bigger stages. She has said she wants to leave women's cycling in a better state than she found it; by any measure, she already has.
