Vos's Olympic record spans two very different Games. At Beijing 2008 she won gold on the track in the points race. Four years later in London she won the women's road race, escaping in a downpour on the final circuits and sprinting to gold on The Mall.

The London win is remembered as one of the great wet-weather rides: a three-woman breakaway, driving rain, and Vos fastest at the end. Between the two golds she had also known Olympic disappointment, which made the second sweeter. For many Dutch fans, the image of Marianne Vos with both arms in the air in the London rain is the single most iconic picture in the country's rich cycling history.
