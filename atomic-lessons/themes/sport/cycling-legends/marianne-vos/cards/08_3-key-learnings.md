**1. Fifteen rainbows, four disciplines.** Road, cyclo-cross, track and gravel: no one has won world titles in so many ways.

**2. Two Olympic golds.** Track points race in 2008, road race in the London rain in 2012.

**3. A builder of her sport.** She co-founded Le Tour Entier, then won stages and wore yellow when the Tour de France Femmes returned in 2022.
