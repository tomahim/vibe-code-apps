In 2006, aged nineteen, Vos became senior World Champion twice in one year: first in cyclo-cross, then in the road race in Salzburg. Winning two world titles in different disciplines in a single season announced a new kind of champion.

She kept collecting. On the road she won the World Championship again in 2012 and 2013. In cyclo-cross she has taken the world title eight times, from 2006 to 2022, a record span of sixteen years at the top of one discipline. Add track world titles in the points race and scratch race, and later two gravel world titles in 2024 and 2026, and her total stands at fifteen world championships across four disciplines. Nobody else in cycling, man or woman, has won rainbows in so many ways.
