title: The finest of her generation
archived: false

Comparisons between eras are always unfair, but within women's cycling Vos's claim is as strong as it gets: fifteen world titles in four disciplines, two Olympic golds, three Giri, and a classics collection few can match, across two decades at the top. Commentators have compared her to Eddy Merckx as the finest cyclist of her generation, and the comparison is earned.

She also changed what a women's career can look like: multi-discipline, multi-decade, influential beyond results.

Her palmares spans road, cyclo-cross and track, with world titles in all three and overall wins at the Giro d'Italia Femminile - a range no one else in the modern peloton matches.
