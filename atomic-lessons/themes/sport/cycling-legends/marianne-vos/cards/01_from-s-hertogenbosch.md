Marianne Vos was born on 13 May 1987 in 's-Hertogenbosch in the Netherlands and grew up in the small village of Babyloniënbroek. She started cycling at six after watching her older brother race, training with his team because girls were not allowed to enter races, and adding cyclo-cross in winter.

Her talent was obvious as soon as she was allowed to compete. She won junior European and World titles on the road, and by the time she reached the senior ranks she was racing on the road, on the track and in cyclo-cross at the highest level simultaneously. Dutch cycling had known great women before, but nothing like this: a teenager who could win a world title in January's mud and another on August's tarmac.
