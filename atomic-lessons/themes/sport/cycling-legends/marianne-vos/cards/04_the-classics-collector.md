Vos's road palmarès reads like the calendar itself. She has won the Giro d'Italia Women overall three times, La Flèche Wallonne five times, the Amstel Gold Race, the Tour of Flanders, Strade Bianche and countless other races, and she topped the UCI women's rankings season after season.

What sets her apart is range across the whole season: sprints, hills, cobbles and stage races all fell to her at some point. She also won the points classification at all three of the biggest women's stage races, the Giro d'Italia Women, the Tour de France Femmes and La Vuelta Femenina. Rivals learned that beating Vos required either the hardest course imaginable or a very lucky day.
