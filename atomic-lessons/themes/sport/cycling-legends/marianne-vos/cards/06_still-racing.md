title: Still racing, still winning

Vos rides for Visma-Lease a Bike under a lifetime contract, a deal that reflects her unique status in the sport. Into her late thirties she has kept winning at the highest level: world gravel titles in 2024 and 2026, and her eighth cyclo-cross world crown in 2022, sixteen years after her first.

Her longevity rests on versatility and intelligence. When raw speed faded slightly, she won with positioning, timing and experience, and she keeps adding disciplines rather than dropping them. Each season brings the same question - how long can she go on - and, so far, the same answer from Vos: at least one more race.
