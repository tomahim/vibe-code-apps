Hinault's last Tour, in 1986, is one of the most debated in history. Riding for the La Vie Claire team alongside the young American Greg LeMond, he had publicly promised to help LeMond win after the American had supported him in 1985. Then he attacked anyway, launching long raids through the Pyrenees and Alps that forced LeMond to chase his own teammate.

Hinault later claimed he attacked to toughen LeMond up and wear out the rivals; LeMond never quite bought it. The American won the Tour, with Hinault second, and the Badger retired at the end of the season at just thirty-two, at the top and on his own terms. The 1986 race remains a case study in team tactics, promises and ambition.
