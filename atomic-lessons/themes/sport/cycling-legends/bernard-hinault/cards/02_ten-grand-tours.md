Hinault's Grand Tour record is second only to Merckx. He entered thirteen Grand Tours in his career: he abandoned one while leading, finished second twice, and won the other ten. Five Tours de France (1978, 1979, 1981, 1982, 1985), three Giros d'Italia and two Vueltas a España make him one of only six riders to have won all three.

His supremacy was built on being strong everywhere. He could time trial with the specialists, climb with the pure climbers and sprint out of small groups. Rivals called him the patron of the peloton because races ran on his authority: when Hinault said a strike would happen, it happened, and when he attacked, the race was usually over.
