Hinault's place in history rests on more than results. Ten Grand Tour wins, a world title, five Monuments and victory in essentially every major race he targeted put him in the tiny group of riders with a claim to be the greatest ever, usually filed just behind Merckx.

As of today he remains the most recent French winner of the men's Tour de France. His mix of physical completeness, tactical authority and sheer combativeness made him the archetype of the patron: the rider who owned the race.

Five Tours, three Giros, two Vueltas, a world title, Liège-Bastogne-Liège and Paris-Roubaix: the palmares has no gaps. France has been waiting for his successor since 1985.
