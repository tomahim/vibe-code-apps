Hinault walked away from professional racing at the end of 1986, still one of the strongest riders in the world. He went home to Brittany to farm, and stayed involved in cycling through the Tour de France organisation, where for years he was the face of the podium ceremonies, presenting jerseys to a new generation of winners.

His departure closed an era. No French rider has won the men's Tour de France since his fifth victory in 1985, a drought that has become one of French sport's most discussed statistics. Every July, French television returns to Hinault for analysis, and every year the question is the same: who will finally follow him?
