Bernard Hinault was born on 14 November 1954 in Yffiniac, Brittany, and grew up on the family farm. He came to racing through the tough local club scene rather than any academy, and it showed: from the beginning he rode with a stubbornness that teammates and rivals learned to respect.

He signed with Gitane-Campagnolo and turned professional, breaking through in 1977 by winning both Liège-Bastogne-Liège and the Critérium du Dauphiné Libéré. A year later, in 1978, he won the Vuelta a España and then the Tour de France. A first-year Grand Tour winner at twenty-three, he was immediately the boss of the peloton, a position riders say he defended with his elbows, his tongue and his legs.
