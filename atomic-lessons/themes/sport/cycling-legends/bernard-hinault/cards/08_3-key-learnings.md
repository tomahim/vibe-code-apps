**1. Ten from thirteen.** Hinault won ten of the thirteen Grand Tours he entered: five Tours, three Giros and two Vueltas.

**2. A complete and combative champion.** The Badger also took five Monuments and the 1980 world title at Sallanches, won with an 80 km solo.

**3. France still waits.** His 1985 Tour win is the last by a Frenchman, and his 1986 duel with teammate LeMond remains cycling's great rivalry debate.
