**1. The season opens here.** Since 1933, eight days from the cold north to the Mediterranean: the Race to the Sun.

**2. Kelly owns it.** Sean Kelly won seven consecutive editions, 1982 to 1988; Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault and Indurain also won it.

**3. It changed the sport.** Kivilev's death in 2003 led to compulsory helmets; the race keeps making history in miniature.
