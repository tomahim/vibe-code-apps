Paris-Nice has one patron above all others: Ireland's Sean Kelly, who won it seven consecutive times from 1982 to 1988. In a race that suits complete riders, Kelly - sprinter, classics king and Grand Tour winner - was untouchable for a decade.

Seven wins in a row is one of the most remarkable streaks in cycling, and it is fitting that it belongs to one of the hardest men the sport has known. Behind Kelly, the winners' list includes Jacques Anquetil, Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault, Miguel Indurain and Alberto Contador, plus modern Grand Tour champions like Geraint Thomas. Paris-Nice is not a consolation race: riders who win it tend to win much bigger things.
