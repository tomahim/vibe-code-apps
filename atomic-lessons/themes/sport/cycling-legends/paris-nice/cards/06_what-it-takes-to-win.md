title: What it takes to win
archived: false
---
Paris-Nice rewards the complete stage racer. There is no single decisive monster climb, so the winner must time trial well, survive the wind in the open northern stages, handle the punchy southern hills, and stay upright for eight days. It is a Grand Tour in miniature.

That is why its winners list reads like a who's who of stage racing, and why teams treat it as a form check that actually means something. Weakness anywhere is punished: a bad day in the crosswinds can cost minutes that the Col d'Èze will not give back. The Race to the Sun flatters no one.
