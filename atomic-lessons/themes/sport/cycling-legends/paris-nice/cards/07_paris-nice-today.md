Paris-Nice is organised by ASO, the Tour de France company, and remains a fixture of the WorldTour calendar each March. Modern editions keep the classic shape - cold north, sunny south, the Col d'Èze or the hills above Nice to finish - while adding summit finishes when the route allows.

Almost a century after 1933, the logic still works: the whole sport watches the first big stage race of the year to see who is flying.

Recent winners include Grand Tour champions and the best young stage racers of each generation, and the hills above Nice remain the classic final exam. Eight days in March still tell us more about the coming season than any other week of spring.
