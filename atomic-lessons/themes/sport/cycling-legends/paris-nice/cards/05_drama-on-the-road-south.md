For an eight-day race, Paris-Nice produces an unusual amount of history. The 2003 edition was overshadowed by the death of Kazakh rider Andrey Kivilev after a fall, a tragedy that led directly to the UCI making helmets compulsory in professional races, one of the most important safety changes in the sport's history.

In 2020, as the COVID-19 pandemic shut down world sport, Paris-Nice was the last race standing, finishing under gathering clouds days before everything stopped. And in 2016 and other snowy years, stages have been neutralised or cancelled by the weather that the race's first half always risks. The Race to the Sun does not always start in the sun.
