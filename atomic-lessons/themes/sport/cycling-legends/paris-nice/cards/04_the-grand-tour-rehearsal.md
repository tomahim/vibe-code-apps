Paris-Nice matters because of where it sits: early March, when the stage racers reveal their form for the first time. A win or a strong ride here announces a Tour de France contender, and the roll of honour proves the point - Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault and Indurain all used it as a springboard to Tour glory.

The race also serves as a laboratory. Teams test leaders, sprint trains and new equipment against a week of real racing before the Grand Tours. For young riders, a stage win in the Race to the Sun is often the first line of a big palmares: Tadej Pogačar's early seasons included learning weeks in races like this one before he targeted the Tour itself.
