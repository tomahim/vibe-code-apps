The modern Vuelta is three weeks in late August and September, owned by the Tour de France organiser ASO, and firmly established as the equal of the Tour and Giro in prestige, if not yet in age. A women's race, La Vuelta Femenina, has been part of the Women's WorldTour since 2023, completing the set of women's Grand Tours.

Spain's race has grown from the fragile event of 1935 into the season's final battlefield. Red jersey, steep walls, end-of-season drama: the third Grand Tour is nobody's little brother any more.

The red jersey has also become a marker of greatness: Primož Roglič's four titles put him level with Roberto Heras at the top of the honour roll.
