title: The youngest Grand Tour

The Vuelta a España was first organised in 1935, more than three decades after the Tour and twenty-six years after the Giro, inspired by the success of both. The first edition ran between Madrid and Valladolid and produced the race's first great duel: Belgium's Gustaaf Deloor beat Spain's Mariano Cañardo to become the first winner.

The young race had a fragile start. The Spanish Civil War and then the Second World War stopped it for years, and it only settled as an annual event in the 1950s. For decades it lived in the shadow of its two older brothers, raced in spring and short of star fields. The modern Vuelta was created by a calendar move: from 1995 it shifted to late August and September, and found its identity as the season's fiery finale.
