The Vuelta's leader wears red, but the colour is younger than you might think. Over its history the race has dressed its leader in orange, white, gold and even the colours of the Spanish flag, before settling on the maillot rojo, the red jersey, from 2010.

The red jersey caps the Grand Tour set: yellow in July, pink in May, red in late summer. The race also awards a points jersey, a mountains jersey and, a Vuelta tradition, a combination classification for the best all-round rider. With the World Championships following soon after, the Vuelta's leader jersey is often the last big prize of the road season, contested by riders chasing one more result before winter.
