The Vuelta's record book belongs to Spain and Slovenia. Roberto Heras, a climbing specialist, won the race four times between 2000 and 2005, the first rider to reach four victories. His final win was overshadowed when he later tested positive for EPO, lost the title on appeal, and was eventually reinstated years after, a messy chapter typical of that era.

Primož Roglič then built the modern dynasty, winning in 2019 - the first Slovenian to win any Grand Tour - then 2020, 2021 and 2024 to join Heras on four. Britain's Chris Froome took two, including 2017. The Vuelta rewards repeat specialists: riders who love steep, explosive climbing return year after year and stack victories.
