**1. The youngest Grand Tour.** First run in 1935 and won by Belgium's Gustaaf Deloor, it became an annual fixture only after the wars.

**2. Red means leader.** After decades of changing colours, the leader's jersey has been red since 2010.

**3. Steep and late.** Summit finishes like the Angliru and a late-season slot make it the most unpredictable Grand Tour; Heras and Roglič share the record with four wins.
