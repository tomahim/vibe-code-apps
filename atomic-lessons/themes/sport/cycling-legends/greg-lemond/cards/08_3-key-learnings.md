**1. A pioneer.** LeMond was the first American to win the Worlds (1983) and the Tour de France (1986).

**2. The greatest comeback.** Shot in a 1987 hunting accident, he returned to win the 1989 Tour by eight seconds, the closest finish ever, and a third Tour in 1990.

**3. A tech revolutionary.** Aerobars and carbon frames entered the peloton with him, and his anti-doping stance was vindicated by history.
