LeMond's rise was fast. In 1983, in Altenrhein, Switzerland, he won the World Championship road race, becoming the first American man to take the professional rainbow jersey. He was twenty-two and suddenly one of the biggest names in the sport.

His first Tour de France experiences were spent in Bernard Hinault's shadow. In 1985, riding for La Vie Claire, he was arguably the stronger man but was ordered to wait for team leader Hinault, who took his fifth Tour with LeMond second. The American swallowed the decision on a promise: next year, the team would ride for him. That promise set up the most famous intra-team war the Tour has ever seen.
