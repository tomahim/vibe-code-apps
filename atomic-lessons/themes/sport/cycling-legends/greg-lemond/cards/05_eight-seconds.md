LeMond arrived at the 1989 Tour de France with no expectations and left with the yellow jersey. He traded the lead with two-time winner Laurent Fignon all race, and started the final stage, a 24.5-kilometre time trial into Paris, 50 seconds behind. Taking that much time back on a rider of Fignon's class over so short a course looked impossible.

LeMond gambled on technology, using aerodynamic triathlon handlebars and an aero helmet while Fignon rode a conventional setup. He averaged over 54 km/h, still one of the fastest Tour time trials ever, and beat Fignon overall by eight seconds, the smallest margin in Tour history. Months later he added the 1989 World Championship, and in 1990 he won his third Tour.
