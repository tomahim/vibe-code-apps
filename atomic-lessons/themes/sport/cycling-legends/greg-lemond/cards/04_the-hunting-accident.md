title: The hunting accident

---
In April 1987, during the off-season, LeMond was accidentally shot while turkey hunting in California. His brother-in-law's shotgun blast hit him with dozens of pellets, including some that lodged near his heart and liver. He lost a huge amount of blood and underwent two surgeries; about thirty pellets were never removed.

He missed the next two Tours de France, and most of the sport wrote him off. Sponsors left, results were ordinary, and by 1989 he was ranked among the also-rans. What followed is the reason his name is known far beyond cycling: the greatest comeback in the history of the sport, capped by the closest Tour de France ever ridden.
