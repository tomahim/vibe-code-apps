title: '1986: the war with Hinault'

The 1986 Tour de France pitted LeMond against his own teammate and mentor. Hinault attacked relentlessly from the first week, claiming he was softening up the opposition and testing his heir. LeMond had to treat the five-time champion as both leader and rival, never sure whether the next attack was for the team or against him.

LeMond stayed patient, marked the danger men in the mountains and won the Tour with Hinault second. He was the first American, and the first non-European, to win the race. The victory should have launched a decade of dominance. Instead, the following spring brought an accident that nearly ended not just his career but his life.
