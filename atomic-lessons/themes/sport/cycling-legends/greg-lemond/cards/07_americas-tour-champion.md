After Lance Armstrong's seven Tour titles were stripped in 2012, LeMond's three wins, in 1986, 1989 and 1990, regained their full standing: he is the only American officially recognised as a Tour de France winner. His 1989 victory, eight seconds after fifty down, remains the benchmark for drama in the race.

His story has every element a legend needs: a pioneer from outside the sport's heartland, a career nearly ended by a hunting accident, a comeback against all logic, and a second act as the sport's conscience.

He won the Tour in 1986, 1989 and 1990 and took two world titles, in 1983 and 1989. Decades later, every American who races in Europe still rides in his slipstream.
