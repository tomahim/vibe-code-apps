Greg LeMond was born on 26 June 1961 in Lakewood, California, and grew up near Reno, Nevada. A talented skier, he took up cycling partly to stay fit for the slopes, and within a few years he was beating professionals while still a teenager. There was no American road to the top of European cycling, so he made one.

In 1982 he won the mountainous Tour de l'Avenir, the amateur Tour de France, by a record ten minutes and eighteen seconds, a margin that stunned Europe and confirmed that an American really could become a fuoriclasse, an out-of-class champion. The French team Renault signed him, and the sport's old world prepared to meet its first great champion from the new one.
