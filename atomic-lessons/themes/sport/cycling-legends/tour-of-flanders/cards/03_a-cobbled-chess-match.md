Winning the Ronde is as much about position as power. The roads are narrow, the wind cuts across the fields, and every berg is followed by a fight for the front that begins kilometres before the climb itself. Being tenth wheel into the Kwaremont can end your race before you start pedalling uphill.

That is why the great Flanders riders are complete classics racers: strong enough to ride the front for an hour, smart enough to save energy, and brave enough to attack on the last bergs. Modern editions have been decided by solo raids from far out, like Tadej Pogačar's long-range wins, and by small-group sprints in Oudenaarde. The race rewards the rider who understands that on cobbles, every corner is a choice.
