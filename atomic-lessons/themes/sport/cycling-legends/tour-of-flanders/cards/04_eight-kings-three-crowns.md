The Ronde has a unique record table: not one patron but eight. Achiel Buysse, Fiorenzo Magni, Eric Leman, Johan Museeuw, Tom Boonen, Fabian Cancellara, Mathieu van der Poel and Tadej Pogačar have each won the race three times, and no rider has ever won four. Pogačar joined the club in 2026, dropping van der Poel on the final Kwaremont.

The women's race, run since 2004 on the same day and finale, has its own queen: Belgium's Lotte Kopecky holds the record with three victories. That the men's record is shared by eight riders across eight decades tells you something true about the race: on the bergs, nobody stays on top for long.
