The women's Tour of Flanders has run since 2004, over a shorter distance but with the same cobbled finale: the Kwaremont and the Paterberg decide it just as they do for the men. It is now one of the biggest one-day races in women's cycling.

The roll of honour mixes Dutch and Belgian royalty: Annemiek van Vleuten, Marianne Vos, Elisa Longo Borghini and record-holder Lotte Kopecky have all won it. For Flemish fans, a home winner is the perfect Sunday, and Kopecky's victories have made her a national hero in the same streets where the men's race plays out an hour later.
