The Ronde today is organised by Flanders Classics and sits at the heart of the cobbled season, one week before Paris-Roubaix. The finish in Oudenaarde, the museum there, and the hundreds of thousands lining the bergs make it equal parts race and festival.

More than a century after Van Wijnendaele's idea, the race still does what it was built to do: tell Flanders a story about itself, in Flemish, on cobbles.

The Ronde is a Monument and the beating heart of the cobbled spring, with a women's edition sharing the same Sunday since 2004. A small club of greats share the record of three wins, and Belgium still measures its champions by this race alone.
