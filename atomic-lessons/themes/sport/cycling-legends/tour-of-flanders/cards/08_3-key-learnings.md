**1. Flemish since 1913.** Sportwereld and Karel Van Wijnendaele created it; Paul Deman won the first after twelve hours of racing.

**2. The bergs decide.** Oude Kwaremont, Paterberg, Koppenberg and the Muur: short, steep, cobbled, decisive.

**3. No one wins four.** Eight riders share the record of three wins, Pogačar the newest; Kopecky holds the women's record too.
