The Ronde is a game of short, steep, cobbled climbs called hellingen. Over about 270 kilometres the race strings together seventeen or more of them, each narrow, rough and lined with fans. The decisive sequence comes late: the Oude Kwaremont, a long cobbled drag, then the Paterberg, short and savagely steep, before the flat run to the finish in Oudenaarde.

Other climbs are pure legend. The Koppenberg is so steep and slippery that even the best are sometimes forced to walk. The Muur van Geraardsbergen, capped by its chapel, was the race's traditional decisive climb until a 2012 rerouting moved the finish; public love brought the Muur back to the course in 2017. The bergs are small on paper. On cobbles, after 240 kilometres, they are walls.
