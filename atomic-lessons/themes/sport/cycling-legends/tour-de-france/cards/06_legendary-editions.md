Ask fans for the greatest Tour and 1989 usually wins: Greg LeMond began the final-day time trial 50 seconds behind Laurent Fignon and won the race by eight. The 1964 duel between Jacques Anquetil and Raymond Poulidor, elbow to elbow up the Puy-de-Dôme, remains the most replayed image of the black-and-white era.

Other editions are famous for darker reasons. In 1904 the cheating was so widespread, from sabotage to riders taking trains, that the first four finishers were disqualified. The 1998 Tour was consumed by the Festina doping affair, which blew open the EPO era. The Tour's history is honest about both: it is a chronicle of human greatness and human weakness, run through the same mountains.
