Five Tour victories is the summit no one has passed. Jacques Anquetil was the first to reach it, followed by Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault, Miguel Indurain - the only man to win five in a row - and Tadej Pogačar, who equalled the mark in 2026. Lance Armstrong's seven consecutive wins from 1999 to 2005 were stripped for doping, so those years officially have no winner.

Other records tell the race's story. Mark Cavendish took his 35th stage win in 2024, passing Merckx's 49-year-old mark of 34. The closest Tour was 1989, decided by eight seconds. The youngest winner, Henri Cornet, was nineteen in 1904. Every record is a different way of measuring the same three weeks of suffering.
