title: How the race works

The modern Tour covers around 3,500 kilometres over 21 stages in 23 days, with two rest days. Most stages are road stages for the whole peloton; some are individual time trials raced alone against the clock. Riders' times on every stage are added together, and the lowest cumulative time leads the general classification.

Inside the race there are several races. The yellow jersey goes to the overall leader. The green jersey rewards the most consistent finisher in the points classification, usually a sprinter. The polka-dot jersey crowns the best climber, and the white jersey the best rider under twenty-six. Teams of eight riders work for their leaders, which makes the Tour as much a team chess match as an individual endurance test.
