The most famous garment in cycling was introduced in 1919, and the first rider to wear it was Eugène Christophe. Yellow was chosen because L'Auto, the newspaper that created the Tour, was printed on yellow paper. A marketing trick became the sport's supreme symbol.

Wearing the maillot jaune, even for a day, makes a rider's career; hundreds of professionals have built their legacies on a single morning in yellow. The jerseys for the other classifications followed the same logic of visibility and sponsorship. Today the yellow jersey is arguably the most recognisable piece of clothing in world sport, instantly readable even to people who have never watched a stage.
