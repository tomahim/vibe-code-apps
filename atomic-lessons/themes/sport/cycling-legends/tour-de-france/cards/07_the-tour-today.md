title: The Tour today, and the women's race

The modern Tour is a global broadcast giant, watched by hundreds of millions and run by Amaury Sport Organisation. Women's racing has grown alongside it: after decades of short-lived attempts, the Tour de France Femmes was relaunched in 2022 as an eight-day stage race and has quickly become the biggest event in women's cycling.

The essentials, though, are those of 1903: ride around France for three weeks, survive the mountains, and bring the lowest total time to Paris. Everything else - the jerseys, the caravan, the crowds on Alpe d'Huez - is decoration on Henri Desgrange's original stunt.

Source: [Tour de France on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_de_France).
