**1. A newspaper stunt.** The Tour began in 1903 to sell L'Auto; Maurice Garin won the first edition over 2,428 km in six stages.

**2. Five is the record.** Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault, Indurain and Pogačar share it; Armstrong's seven titles were stripped.

**3. The mountains rule.** Since the Pyrenees in 1910, Tours are decided on climbs like the Tourmalet and Alpe d'Huez.
