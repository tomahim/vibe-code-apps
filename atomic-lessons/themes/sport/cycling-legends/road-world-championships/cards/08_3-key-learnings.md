**1. One day for a year.** The pro Worlds began in 1927 at the Nürburgring; the winner wears the rainbow jersey until the next edition.

**2. The rarest crown.** Only Merckx (1974), Roche (1987) and Pogačar (2024) have won Giro, Tour and Worlds in one year.

**3. A moving feast.** From Sallanches 1980 to Kigali 2025, the first Worlds in Africa, the changing course keeps every edition a new puzzle.
