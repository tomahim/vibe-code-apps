title: A different race every year

Because the Worlds move every year, each edition is a new puzzle. The 1980 Sallanches course in the French Alps was so hard that Bernard Hinault won it with an 80-kilometre solo and only fifteen riders finished. Flat circuits have crowned pure sprinters; hilly circuits have gone to climbers and puncheurs.

This rotation keeps the honour roll remarkably balanced between types of rider, and it means a world title says something different each year. Winning on a course that suits you is expected; winning on one that does not, as Hinault did in 1980 and as Pogačar did in the 2025 hills of Kigali, is how legends are made.
