The record for elite men's road titles is three, shared by five riders: Alfredo Binda, Rik Van Steenbergen, Eddy Merckx, Óscar Freire and Peter Sagan. Sagan's three came consecutively, from 2015 to 2017, a streak no one else has managed. Marianne Vos has three women's titles, and France's Jeannie Longo holds the record with five, won between 1985 and 1995.

In the time trial, added in 1994, the specialists have their own pantheon: Miguel Indurain, Fabian Cancellara, Tony Martin and Remco Evenepoel among the multiple champions. The Worlds reward one perfect day above all else, and some riders built entire legacies on producing exactly that.
