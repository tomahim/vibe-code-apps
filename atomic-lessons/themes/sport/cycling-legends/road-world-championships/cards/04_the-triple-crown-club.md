The rarest feat in cycling links the Worlds to the Grand Tours: winning the Giro d'Italia, the Tour de France and the World Championship in the same year, the Triple Crown. Only three riders have ever done it.

Eddy Merckx did it first, in 1974, his annus mirabilis. Ireland's Stephen Roche matched it in 1987, still the only Irishman to win the Tour. And Tadej Pogačar joined them in 2024, capping his Giro-Tour double with a 100-kilometre solo at the Worlds in Zurich. Three names in a century of racing: the Triple Crown may be the most exclusive club in all of sport.
