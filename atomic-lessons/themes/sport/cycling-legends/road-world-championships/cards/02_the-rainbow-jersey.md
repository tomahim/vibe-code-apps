The winner earns the right to wear the rainbow jersey - white with the five UCI colours across the chest - in every race of that category until the next championships. It is the most recognisable honour in cycling after the yellow jersey, and riders say it changes how the peloton treats you: a world champion is always watched.

With the honour comes a myth: the curse of the rainbow, the belief that world champions suffer bad luck in their year in the bands. Crashes, illnesses and winless seasons have fed the legend for a century. The statistics say champions simply get marked out of races, but ask around the peloton and you will find plenty of riders who half-believe in the curse anyway.
