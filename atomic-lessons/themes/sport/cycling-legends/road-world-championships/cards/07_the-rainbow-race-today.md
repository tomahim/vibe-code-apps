The modern Worlds are a week-long festival of racing for national teams, from juniors to elite, across road races, time trials and the mixed team relay. After Kigali 2025, the 2026 edition moved to Canada, continuing the championship's push beyond its European heartland; recent hosts have ranged from Yorkshire to Wollongong, and each course writes a different kind of race.

What never changes is the format's cruelty and charm. There are no stages to recover, no team classifications to hide in, no next week. One day, one course, one jersey for a year: it is the simplest format in cycling and, many riders say, the hardest title to win.

Source: [UCI Road World Championships on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCI_Road_World_Championships).
