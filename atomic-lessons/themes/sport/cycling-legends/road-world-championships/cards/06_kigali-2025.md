title: 'Kigali 2025: the Worlds reach Africa'

The 2025 championships in Kigali, Rwanda, were the first Road Worlds ever held in Africa, over a hilly course around the Rwandan capital with more than 5,000 metres of climbing in the men's road race. Thousands of local fans lined the roads for a week of racing that became a celebration as much as a competition.

Tadej Pogačar won the men's race with an attack 100 kilometres from the finish, riding the last 77 alone to defend his title ahead of Remco Evenepoel and Ireland's Ben Healy. It made him the first rider to win the Tour and the Worlds in consecutive years, and it showed what the moving championship is for: taking the sport's biggest one-day race to places that had never seen it.
