**1. The oldest cobbled Monument.** First run in 1896; the Hell of the North name comes from the landscapes ruined by the First World War.

**2. The stones are the opponent.** Thirty cobbled sectors, three five-star ones, and a cobblestone trophy at the velodrome finish.

**3. Two kings with four.** De Vlaeminck and Boonen share the record; Lizzie Deignan won the first women's race in 2021.
