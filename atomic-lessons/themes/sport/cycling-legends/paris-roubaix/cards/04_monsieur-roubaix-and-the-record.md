Two riders share the record of four victories. Roger De Vlaeminck - Monsieur Paris-Roubaix - won in 1972, 1974, 1975 and 1977, mastering the stones like no one before him. Tom Boonen matched him decades later, winning in 2005, 2008, 2009 and 2012.

Behind them sits a crowd on three: Rik Van Looy, Eddy Merckx, Francesco Moser, Johan Museeuw, Fabian Cancellara and Mathieu van der Poel among them. Winning Roubaix even once changes a rider's status permanently. The roll of honour contains almost no lucky winners: the race is too hard for accidents, which is why its champions are held in such particular esteem.
