Roubaix's folklore is written in mud. The 1980s editions in apocalyptic conditions, Andre Tchmil's 60-kilometre solo in 1994, Johan Museeuw's emotional 2000 comeback win after nearly losing a leg, and the 2021 autumn edition - the first ever run in rain-soaked October after the pandemic postponement - all live in the memory.

The 2021 race made history twice over, because the day before the men, the first Paris-Roubaix Femmes was held. Britain's Lizzie Deignan won it with an 80-kilometre solo over the stones, finally giving women a start line in the Hell of the North, 125 years after the men first raced it.
