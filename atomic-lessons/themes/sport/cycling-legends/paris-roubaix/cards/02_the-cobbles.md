What makes Roubaix Roubaix is the pavé: around thirty sectors of rough cobblestone farm roads, each rated from one to five stars for brutality. The three five-star sectors are the Trouée d'Arenberg, a dead-straight trench through a forest where the race explodes with 100 kilometres still to go; Mons-en-Pévèle; and the Carrefour de l'Arbre, the last hard sector before the finish.

Riding the stones is a skill of its own: float over the crown of the road, hands loose on the bars, power high, eyes up. Equipment matters too, with reinforced wheels and frames built for comfort. Crashes, punctures and broken bikes decide as much as legs do. Roubaix is the Monument where the road itself is the main opponent.
