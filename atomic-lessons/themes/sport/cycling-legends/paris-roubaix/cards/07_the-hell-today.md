The modern race covers about 260 kilometres with some thirty cobbled sectors, organised by ASO a week after the Tour of Flanders as the climax of the cobbled spring. The women's race has grown rapidly since 2021 and now shares the Sunday spotlight.

Nothing about its essence has softened: farm tracks, five-star sectors, the velodrome and a cobble for the winner. As long as cycling exists, there will be a Sunday in hell.

The modern race is organised by ASO and remains one of the five Monuments, and since 2021 a women's edition, Paris-Roubaix Femmes, has shared the cobbles and the velodrome. Around 250 kilometres with some 55 kilometres of pavé, it is still the race riders fear and dream about in equal measure.
