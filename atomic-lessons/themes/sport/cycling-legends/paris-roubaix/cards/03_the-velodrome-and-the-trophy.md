title: The velodrome and the cobble trophy

Since 1943 the race has finished, with rare exceptions, in the Roubaix velodrome: one and a half laps of the old outdoor track in front of packed stands. A sprint on the velodrome banking after 250 kilometres of stones is one of sport's strangest and most beautiful sights.

The winner's trophy is simply one of the cobbles, a granite sett mounted on a plinth: the humblest and most coveted award in cycling. Just as famous are the velodrome's showers, bare concrete stalls each carrying a brass plaque with a winner's name. Riders from Merckx to Boonen have described standing in those showers as a moment of communion with everyone who ever survived the race.
