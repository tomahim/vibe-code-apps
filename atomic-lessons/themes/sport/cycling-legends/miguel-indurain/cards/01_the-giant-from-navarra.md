Miguel Indurain was born on 16 July 1964 in Villava, in Spain's Navarra region. Tall and unusually powerful for a Spanish climber, he rode the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics as an amateur and turned professional that September with the Reynolds team.

Scientists at the University of Ferrara later tested him and found an extraordinary engine: his blood carried about seven litres of oxygen a minute, roughly double an ordinary person, and his resting heart rate and lung capacity were off the normal scale. Those numbers explain the nickname Big Mig and the style that came with it. He was not flashy. He simply produced more power, more calmly, for longer than anyone else.
