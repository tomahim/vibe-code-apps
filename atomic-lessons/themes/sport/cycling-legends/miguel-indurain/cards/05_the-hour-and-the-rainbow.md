title: The hour, the rainbow and Olympic gold

In September 1994, after his fourth Tour, Indurain attacked the hour record in Bordeaux and covered 53.040 kilometres, adding his name to a list that includes Coppi, Anquetil and Merckx. For a rider of eighty kilograms, built for three-week races rather than the track, it was a staggering performance.

His collection of time-trial honours kept growing. He won the World Championship individual time trial in 1995, a week after nearly winning the road race too, and at the 1996 Atlanta Olympics he took the gold medal in the time trial. In the mid-1990s, against the clock, Miguel Indurain was quite simply the best there had ever been.
