**1. Five in a row.** Indurain is the only rider to win five consecutive Tours de France, from 1991 to 1995.

**2. A laboratory engine.** Tests measured roughly seven litres of oxygen a minute; his time trialling won the Tours, his climbing defended them.

**3. More than July.** Back-to-back Giro-Tour doubles in 1992 and 1993, the 1994 hour record, a world time-trial title and 1996 Olympic gold complete the record.
