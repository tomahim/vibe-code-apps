Indurain was not content with July. In 1992 he won the Giro d'Italia and then the Tour, the Giro-Tour double that is one of the hardest feats in cycling. In 1993 he did it again, becoming the first rider to win the double in consecutive seasons.

Those 1992 and 1993 seasons put him alongside the very greatest stage racers. The double demands holding peak form from May to late July, and Indurain managed it twice running, all while riding with the same metronomic style. Some critics called his racing robotic. His rivals, who kept finishing minutes behind, called it something else: unbeatable.
