From 1991 to 1995 Miguel Indurain won the Tour de France five consecutive times. Four riders had reached five Tour wins before him, but Anquetil, Merckx and Hinault all lost Tours in between; Indurain is the only one to win five in a row.

His method was identical every July and devastating. He crushed the long time trials, gaining minutes on everyone, then defended in the high mountains with a calm that frustrated the pure climbers. Remarkably, during his five consecutive Tour wins he never won a stage that was not a time trial: he did not need to. Rivals attacked him constantly and almost never took a second back when it mattered.
