Indurain's legacy is the streak: five Tours in five years, plus two Giros, an hour record, a world time-trial title and Olympic gold. In Spain he triggered a cycling boom, and he remains a national sporting monument, admired as much for his modesty as for his palmarès.

He almost never gave a headline quote, never humiliated a rival, and let the stopwatch speak. In an era that would soon be darkened by doping scandals, the image of the big, silent Basque-country farmer's son riding alone against the clock retains a rare purity for many fans.
