Anquetil retired having reset what was possible. Five Tours, two Giros and a Vuelta made eight Grand Tours, more than anyone before him, and his hour record and nine Grand Prix des Nations titles underlined that he was the greatest time triallist of his age. Every later five-Tour champion - Merckx, Hinault, Indurain, Pogačar - followed a road he opened.

He was never the most loved champion, but he may have been the most modern: scientific about effort, unsentimental about risk, and clear-eyed about his sport. France eventually gave him his due as one of its greatest athletes.
