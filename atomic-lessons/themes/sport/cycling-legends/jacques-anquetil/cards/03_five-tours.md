Anquetil won his first Tour de France in 1957, at twenty-three. Before the 1961 edition he made an outrageous promise: he would take the yellow jersey on day one and keep it to Paris. With past winners Charly Gaul and Federico Bahamontes in the field, few believed him. He did exactly what he said.

He added the Tours of 1962, 1963 and 1964, becoming the first rider ever to win five Tours, and the first to win four in succession. The 1964 duel with Raymond Poulidor, shoulder to shoulder up the Puy-de-Dôme, is still replayed as one of the race's great images. Anquetil also won the Giro d'Italia in 1960 and 1964 and the Vuelta a España in 1963, eight Grand Tours in all, a record when he retired.
