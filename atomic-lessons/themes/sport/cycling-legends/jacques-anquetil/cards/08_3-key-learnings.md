**1. First to five.** Anquetil was the first rider to win the Tour de France five times, including four in a row from 1961 to 1964.

**2. The stopwatch was his weapon.** An hour record and nine Grand Prix des Nations titles made him the defining time triallist of his era.

**3. Brutally honest.** His mineral-water remark about doping and his cold professionalism made him cycling's first modern champion.
