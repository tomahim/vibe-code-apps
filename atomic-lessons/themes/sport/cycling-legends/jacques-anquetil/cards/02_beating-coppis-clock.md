title: Beating Coppi's clock
archived: false
---
In 1956, aged twenty-two, Anquetil took on one of the sport's sacred marks: the hour record that Fausto Coppi had held since 1942. The attempt turned into a saga. Scheduled slots were missed, nerves frayed, and on his first real attempt he abandoned, exhausted, saying he could not go on.

When he finally rode again on a rigid schedule, he beat Coppi's distance at the third attempt, covering 46.159 kilometres, 311 metres further than the Italian. Breaking a fourteen-year-old record held by the Campionissimo announced Anquetil as more than a promising time triallist: he was now a genuine star, and the Tour de France was his obvious next target. He won it the following year.
