title: The longest Monument
archived: false
---
At around 290 to 300 kilometres, Milan-San Remo is simply enormous. Riders spend more than six hours in the saddle before the decisive racing even begins, which is what makes it unique: the race is almost impossible to control, yet for most of its length almost nothing happens.

That length shapes everything. Teams must feed and protect their leaders for seven hours and still have them fresh at the end. The tension builds absurdly slowly, because everyone knows the entire race will be decided in the last half hour on two small coastal climbs. Win it and you have won a Monument; lose the positioning battle by one place on the wrong corner and six hours of work are gone.
