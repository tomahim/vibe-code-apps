Milan-San Remo remains the season's first Monument and one of the most-watched one-day races of the year, run by RCS Sport. After years without one, a women's edition returned to the top calendar: Milano-San Remo Donne has been part of the UCI Women's WorldTour since 2025.

The race's charm is that it cannot be modernised away. Three hundred kilometres, the Turchino, the capi, the Poggio, the Via Roma: the formula of 1907 still decides who wins.

Organised by RCS Sport, it opens the Monument season every March, and its Via Roma finish remains the longest shot in cycling: seven hours of racing decided by six minutes on the Poggio.
