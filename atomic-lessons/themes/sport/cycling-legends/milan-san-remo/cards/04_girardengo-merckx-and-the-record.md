Milan-San Remo's first king was Costante Girardengo, Italy's great champion between the wars, who won it six times and stood on the podium eleven times. For decades his record looked untouchable.

Then came Eddy Merckx. The Cannibal won Milan-San Remo seven times between 1966 and 1978, a record for any Monument that still stands. His wins came in every style - solo, from sprints, after crashes - because San Remo suited his completeness. Behind those two, the winners' list reads like a history of the sport, from Fausto Coppi and Louison Bobet to Erik Zabel, Mark Cavendish and Mathieu van der Poel. Winning once makes a career; winning seven times is a monument to the Monument.
