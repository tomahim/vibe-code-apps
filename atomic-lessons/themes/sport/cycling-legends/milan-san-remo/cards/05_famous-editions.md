Some San Remos pass in a sprint; others become folklore. The 2013 edition was struck by snow on the Turchino, forcing riders onto buses before a frozen restart. In 2022, Slovenia's Matej Mohorič attacked on the Poggio descent, using a dropper seatpost to ride the downhill faster than anyone dared, and held on to win.

The race has resisted most attempts to break it open. Big-name attackers from Coppi to Pogačar have tried to drop the sprinters early and usually failed, which is exactly why the rare successful raid is so celebrated. When Pogačar finally won it in 2026, out-sprinting Tom Pidcock on the Via Roma after attacking on the Cipressa, it was his first San Remo after years of trying.
