**1. The longest Monument.** Around 300 km from Milan to the Ligurian coast, first run in 1907 and won by Lucien Petit-Breton.

**2. Decided on two hills.** The Poggio (1960) and Cipressa (1982) exist to break the sprinters, and the Via Roma finishes the argument.

**3. Merckx owns it.** Seven wins, ahead of Girardengo's six; a women's edition joined the WorldTour in 2025.
