title: Cipressa and Poggio

The race's finale is defined by two climbs added to keep the sprinters honest. In 1960 race director Vincenzo Torriani introduced the Poggio, a 4-kilometre ascent just before the finish, and in 1982 the Cipressa, near Imperia, was added as well. Together with the earlier capi - Mele, Cervo and Berta - they are the only obstacles in the last hour.

The formula is perfect. Attackers must go on the Cipressa or the Poggio; sprinters' teams must survive them; and the Poggio's plunging descent into Sanremo has decided countless editions. The race ends on the Via Roma, where a reduced sprint, a solo escapee or a two-man break can all win. No other Monument compresses so much tension into so few kilometres.
