Recent editions have belonged to the biggest names in the sport. Remco Evenepoel won with a long solo, and Tadej Pogačar has made the Redoute his private launchpad, winning three consecutive editions from 2024 to 2026 - the third by 45 seconds after dropping his companions on the Roche-aux-Faucons.

The race has also become a showcase for the new generation, with young Frenchman Paul Seixas pushing Pogačar hardest in 2026. La Doyenne keeps its old shape - out to Bastogne, back through the côtes - but the speed and aggression of the modern peloton have turned its final hour into one of the most reliably explosive in the calendar.
