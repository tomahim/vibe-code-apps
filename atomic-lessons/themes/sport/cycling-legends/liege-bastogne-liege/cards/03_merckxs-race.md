No rider owns a Monument the way Eddy Merckx owns Liège-Bastogne-Liège. He won it five times between 1969 and 1975, including three consecutive editions from 1971 to 1973, and his 1969 victory - a long solo in freezing rain - is still described by those who saw it as one of his finest days.

Behind Merckx, two riders won La Doyenne four times: Italy's Moreno Argentin in the 1980s and early 1990s, and Spain's Alejandro Valverde in the 2000s and 2010s. Both were Ardennes specialists who built their spring around the race. The record book confirms what the course suggests: this is a complete rider's classic, where climbers who can time trial and punch up walls have always ruled.
