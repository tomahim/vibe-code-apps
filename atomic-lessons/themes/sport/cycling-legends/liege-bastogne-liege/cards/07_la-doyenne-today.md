Liège-Bastogne-Liège closes the spring: after it, the peloton turns to the Grand Tours. It ends the Ardennes week that begins with the Amstel Gold Race and La Flèche Wallonne, and it remains the classic that climbers circle first.

More than 130 years after Léon Houa, the race still runs out and back between the same two Ardennes towns. The Old Lady has aged well.

The men's race remains a Monument and the hardest one-day test of climbing endurance, finishing in Ans above Liège. A women's edition has run since 2017, completing the Ardennes week after the Flèche Wallonne. The record still belongs to Eddy Merckx, who won La Doyenne five times.
