Liège-Bastogne-Liège Femmes was inaugurated in 2017, completing the Ardennes week for the women's peloton, and it immediately became a target for the very best: Anna van der Breggen, Annemiek van Vleuten, Demi Vollering and Lotte Kopecky have all won it.

The women's race uses the same Ardennes côtes over a shorter distance, and it has quickly produced editions as tactical and hard as the men's. Its creation was part of the broader rebuilding of women's classics in the 2010s, which also restored women's races at the Tour of Flanders's level of prestige and brought the Tour de France Femmes back in 2022. The Old Lady finally has a daughter.
