**1. The oldest Monument.** First run in 1892, professional from 1894: La Doyenne, the Old Lady.

**2. A complete rider's classic.** The Redoute and the Roche-aux-Faucons decide it; Merckx's five wins are the record, ahead of Argentin and Valverde on four.

**3. Hard in any weather.** Hinault's 1980 snowstorm win and Pogačar's 2024-2026 hat-trick span the race's legend; a women's edition has run since 2017.
