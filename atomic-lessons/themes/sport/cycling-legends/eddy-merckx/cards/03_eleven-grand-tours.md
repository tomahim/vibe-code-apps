Merckx's record in the three Grand Tours stands alone. He won the Tour de France five times (1969, 1970, 1971, 1972 and 1974), the Giro d'Italia five times, and the Vuelta a España once, in 1973. His total of eleven Grand Tour victories has never been equalled; Bernard Hinault is next with ten.

Four times between 1970 and 1974 he won two Grand Tours in the same season, the Giro-Tour double that only a handful of champions have ever managed. He was not a rider who hid in the peloton and defended a lead: he won time trials, he won mountain stages, he won sprints. Across his career he also took 34 Tour de France stages, a record that stood until Mark Cavendish passed it in 2024, nearly half a century later.
