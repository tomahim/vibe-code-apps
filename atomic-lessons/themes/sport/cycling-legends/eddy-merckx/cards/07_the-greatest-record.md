Merckx retired in 1978 with 525 professional victories, a total no one else has approached. Beyond the numbers he changed how champions ride: instead of choosing targets and defending them, he raced to win from February to October, and his hunger became the standard against which every later champion is measured. When Tadej Pogačar attacks today, commentators reach for Merckx as the comparison.

He later ran a successful bicycle brand, stayed close to the sport as a race ambassador, and was made a baron by the Belgian king. Ask riders, journalists or fans to name the greatest cyclist of all time and most still give the same answer.

Source: [Eddy Merckx on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddy_Merckx).
