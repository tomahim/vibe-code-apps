The five Monuments are cycling's oldest and most prestigious one-day races: Milan-San Remo, the Tour of Flanders, Paris-Roubaix, Liège-Bastogne-Liège and Il Lombardia. Merckx did not just win each of them; he won them all repeatedly. He took Milan-San Remo seven times, Liège-Bastogne-Liège five times, Paris-Roubaix three times, and the Tour of Flanders and Il Lombardia twice each.

That makes nineteen Monument victories, a record total. Only three riders in history have won all five Monuments in their career: Merckx and his fellow Belgians Rik Van Looy and Roger De Vlaeminck. His seven wins at Milan-San Remo remain the record for a single Monument, a mark of how completely he mastered every kind of course, from the long flat run to Sanremo to the cobbles of Roubaix.
