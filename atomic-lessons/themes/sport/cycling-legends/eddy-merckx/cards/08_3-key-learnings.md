**1. He won everything.** Eleven Grand Tours, all five Monuments, three world titles and the hour record: no other rider has filled the sport's entire record book.

**2. He always attacked.** The Cannibal won 525 races by racing to win every single day, not by defending a handful of goals.

**3. His records still lead.** Nineteen Monuments, seven Milan-San Remo wins and five Tours keep him at the top of cycling's history half a century later.
