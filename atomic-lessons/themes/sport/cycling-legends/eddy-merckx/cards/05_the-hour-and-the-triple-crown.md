title: The hour record and the Triple Crown
archived: false
---
In October 1972 Merckx travelled to Mexico City to attack the hour record, the purest test in cycling: how far can one rider go in sixty minutes, alone, on a track? He started too fast and suffered badly, but recovered to cover 49.431 kilometres, extending the record by almost 800 metres. He was carried off afterwards and said the pain was the worst he had ever known on a bike.

Two years later he completed the greatest single season anyone had ever had. In 1974 he won the Giro d'Italia, the Tour de France and the World Championship road race, becoming the first rider to take cycling's Triple Crown. Only Stephen Roche in 1987 and Tadej Pogačar in 2024 have matched it since. He won the world title three times in total, a mark only Alfredo Binda, Rik Van Steenbergen, Óscar Freire and Peter Sagan share.
