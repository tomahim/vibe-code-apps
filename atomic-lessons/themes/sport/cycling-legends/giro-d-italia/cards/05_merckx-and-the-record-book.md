The Giro's palmares is a roll of the greatest names in cycling. Coppi and Eddy Merckx share the record with five victories each; Merckx took his between 1968 and 1974, often as half of a Giro-Tour double. Alfredo Binda, Bartali's predecessor, also won five, back in the 1920s and 1930s.

Bernard Hinault won three Giros, Miguel Indurain two, and the race has been kind to great all-rounders because it rewards everything: climbing, time trialling and nerve. The Giro is also where Merckx suffered his most controversial day, expelled while leading in 1969 after a positive test he always disputed. The race's history, like the Tour's, mixes the sublime and the murky.
