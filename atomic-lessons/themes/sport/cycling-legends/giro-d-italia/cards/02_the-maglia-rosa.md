The Giro's leader wears the maglia rosa, the pink jersey. The colour comes straight from the newspaper: La Gazzetta dello Sport has always been printed on pink paper, just as the Tour's yellow matches L'Auto. The jersey was added to the race in 1931, and the first rider to wear it was Learco Guerra.

The pink jersey carries the same weight in Italy as yellow does in France. Riders who have built careers in the world's biggest teams still speak of pulling on the maglia rosa as a childhood dream. Other classifications follow the familiar pattern: points, mountains and young rider jerseys are contested over the same three weeks, but in Italy everything is measured against the pink one.
