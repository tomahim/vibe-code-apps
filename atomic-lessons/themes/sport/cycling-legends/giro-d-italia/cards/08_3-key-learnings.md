**1. Pink like the paper.** The Giro began in 1909 to sell La Gazzetta dello Sport; Luigi Ganna won the first, and the maglia rosa dates from 1931.

**2. Coppi and Merckx own it.** Both won five Giros; the race's highest summit is the Cima Coppi.

**3. The chaos Grand Tour.** Snow on the Gavia, 80 km ambushes on the Finestre: the Giro rewards audacity like no other race.
