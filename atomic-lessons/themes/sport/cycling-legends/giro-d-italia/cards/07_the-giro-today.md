The Giro remains the second Grand Tour, raced each May over three weeks and owned by RCS Sport, the company behind the Gazzetta. It sits in the calendar between the spring classics and the Tour de France, which makes the Giro-Tour double one of cycling's supreme challenges.

Women's racing has its own institution: the Giro d'Italia Women, running since 1988, is the biggest stage race in the women's calendar, won three times by Marianne Vos. Both races share the same ingredients, pink jerseys and impossible mountains, that have made the Giro a national ritual for well over a century.
