title: Coppi's five

Il Lombardia's record was set by Fausto Coppi, who won it five times: four consecutive victories from 1946 to 1949, then a fifth in 1954. For seventy years that record stood alone, fittingly, in a race whose church is the spiritual home of Italian cycling.

Coppi's Lombardias were built the same way as his Grand Tours: long solo attacks over the hills, often decided long before the finish. His dominance of the autumn classic mirrored his spring supremacy elsewhere, and the Coppi name is woven into the race as deeply as the Ghisallo itself. No Italian rider since has won it five times, though one Slovenian has now matched him.
