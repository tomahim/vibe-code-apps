Il Lombardia has traditionally been the season's curtain call: the final Monument, raced when leaves turn above the lakes. For decades it came a week after the World Championships, which made it a favourite target for freshly crowned rainbow jerseys riding on emotion.

Its late-autumn slot gives the race a special mood: low sun, mist on the lakes, tired legs after a long season. Winners of Lombardia are rarely flukes, because only the most complete riders still have form and fire in October. When the podium confetti falls in Como, the road season is truly over.

Its winners' list - Girardengo, Binda, Coppi, Merckx, Hinault, Moser - reads like a hall of fame of the complete one-day rider.
