Lombardia's history is full of days when great riders announced themselves. Damiano Cunego won as a twenty-two-year-old in 2004, hailed as Italy's next champion. Paolo Bettini won in 2006 weeks after becoming world champion, in tears days after the death of his brother.

The race also staged one of cycling's great what-ifs: the fog-shrouded descents and rain of editions like 2010 and 2012, where descenders such as Vincenzo Nibali showed how the race can be won downhill as well as up. In the falling leaves, bravery on wet hairpins has always counted for as much as climbing power.

Fausto Coppi won the race five times, including four in a row from 1946 to 1949, a record that has defined its honour roll ever since.
