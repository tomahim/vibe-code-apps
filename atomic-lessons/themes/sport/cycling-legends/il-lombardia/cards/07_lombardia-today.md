The modern Lombardia is run by RCS Sport each October, finishing in Como or Bergamo depending on the year, always over the Ghisallo and the Lombard hills. It remains the fifth and final Monument and one of Italy's two, with Milan-San Remo.

The race of the falling leaves has outlasted almost everything from its era. The names change - Gerbi, Coppi, Pogačar - but the hills above the lake do not.

Modern editions still race around Como or Bergamo in mid-October, and the Madonna del Ghisallo remains its sacred climb. Tadej Pogačar's run of victories in the 2020s has given the old race a modern patron worthy of Coppi.
