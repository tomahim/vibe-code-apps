The Giro di Lombardia, Il Lombardia, was first held in 1905, making it the youngest of the five Monuments. The first edition, then called Milano-Milano, drew huge crowds and ended with a sensation: Giovanni Gerbi, the devil-may-care star of early Italian cycling, won forty minutes ahead of Giovanni Rossignoli and Luigi Ganna.

Because it comes in autumn, the race earned its poetic name: the Classica delle foglie morte, the classic of the dead leaves. Only the 1943 and 1944 editions were ever cancelled, for the war, giving Lombardia the fewest interruptions of any classic. It closes the Monument season, the last big one-day target before winter.
