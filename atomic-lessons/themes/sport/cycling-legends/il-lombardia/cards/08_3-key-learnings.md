**1. The autumn Monument.** First held in 1905, won by Giovanni Gerbi, and cancelled only for the Second World War.

**2. The Ghisallo rules.** The climb beneath the patroness of cyclists decides the race; Coppi and Pogačar share the record of five wins.

**3. The climbers' classic.** Steep walls like the Muro di Sormano and wet descents make it the Monument where Grand Tour riders shine.
