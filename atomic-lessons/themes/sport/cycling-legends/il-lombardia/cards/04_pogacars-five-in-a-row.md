title: Pogačar's five in a row

From 2021 to 2025, Tadej Pogačar won Il Lombardia five consecutive times, equalling Coppi's record of five victories and surpassing him for consecutive wins. His style in the race has been pure Pogačar: long-range attacks, sometimes with fifty kilometres still to race, that turn the finale into a time trial no one else can answer.

The streak also underlines the race's modern role: the last Monument of the year is where Pogačar has repeatedly closed his season with a statement, including his Triple Crown year of 2024. A crash at the 2026 Vuelta ended that season early and cost him the chance to defend the title, leaving the door open for a new name on the roll of honour.
