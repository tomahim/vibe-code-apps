**1. A record-equalling five Tours.** His 2026 Tour win put him level with Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault and Indurain.

**2. The third Triple Crown.** Giro, Tour and Worlds in 2024, a feat only Merckx (1974) and Roche (1987) had managed.

**3. Fearless by default.** From La Planche des Belles Filles in 2020 to 100 km solos at the Worlds, he races to attack, and a 2026 Vuelta crash showed the cost.
