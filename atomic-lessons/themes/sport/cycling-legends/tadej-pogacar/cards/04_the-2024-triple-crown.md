Pogačar's 2024 season belongs in the very short list of the greatest years any cyclist has had. He won the Giro d'Italia on his debut, by nearly ten minutes, then the Tour de France, then the World Championship road race in Zurich with a 100-kilometre solo attack.

Giro, Tour and Worlds in one season is the Triple Crown of cycling, achieved only twice before: by Eddy Merckx in 1974 and Stephen Roche in 1987. Pogačar added Liège-Bastogne-Liège and a fourth consecutive Il Lombardia for good measure. At twenty-five he was no longer the future of the sport; he was its present, with the most complete season since the Cannibal himself.
