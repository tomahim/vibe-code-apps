title: The new Cannibal

Still in his twenties, Pogačar's record already includes five Tours, a Giro, two world titles, three Triple-Crown seasons' worth of stage-race and monument wins, and a monument tally in double figures, second only to Merckx among modern riders. The comparisons with the Cannibal are no longer lazy: like Merckx, he wins Grand Tours and Monuments alike, and attacks when logic says wait.

His riding style has changed what fans expect: 100-kilometre solos are supposed to be suicidal, and he keeps making them work. However his career ends, he has already widened the sport's idea of what one rider can attempt.
