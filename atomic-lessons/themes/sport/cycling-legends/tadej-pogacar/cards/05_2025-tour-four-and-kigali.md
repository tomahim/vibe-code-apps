In 2025 Pogačar won his fourth Tour de France, reached 100 professional victories during the race, and collected three Monuments in one season: the Tour of Flanders, Liège-Bastogne-Liège and Il Lombardia, something only Eddy Merckx had done before.

That September he defended his world title in Kigali, Rwanda, at the first Road World Championships held in Africa. He attacked with about 100 kilometres to go and rode the last 77 alone, finishing nearly a minute and a half ahead of Remco Evenepoel. No rider had ever won the Tour and the Worlds in consecutive years. The records he chases now are not modern ones; they are Merckx's.
