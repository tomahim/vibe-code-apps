Tadej Pogačar was born on 21 September 1998 in Komenda, Slovenia, a small country of two million people with an outsized cycling tradition. He was a gifted junior, and in 2018 he announced himself by winning the Tour de l'Avenir, the race that traditionally reveals the next Tour de France champion.

UAE Team Emirates signed him for 2019, and his first professional season was extraordinary. He won the Tour of California, then rode his first Grand Tour, the Vuelta a España, and finished third overall with three stage wins, at twenty years old. Riders simply do not do that in their first three-week race. The sport immediately understood that something rare had arrived.
