The 2026 season showed both faces of his story. In spring he won Milan-San Remo for the first time and a third Tour of Flanders and a third straight Liège-Bastogne-Liège. In July he won his fifth Tour de France, equalling the record of Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault and Indurain, with a stage win on Alpe d'Huez timed by Reuters at 35 minutes 27 seconds, a record for the climb.

In August, leading the Vuelta a España after three stage wins, he crashed on stage eight and abandoned a Grand Tour for the first time, with a concussion, a broken collarbone and a fractured vertebra. The injuries ended his season; his team said he would recover and return in 2027, on a contract extended to 2032.
