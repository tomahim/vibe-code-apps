Pogačar won the Tour again in 2021, but the following years gave him a rival worthy of him. Denmark's Jonas Vingegaard beat him at the 2022 and 2023 Tours, twice leaving Pogačar second, and their duel - the attacking Slovenian against the controlled Dane - became the axis of stage racing.

The defeats showed something important about him: he kept racing the same way. He won Liège-Bastogne-Liège, Il Lombardia, the Tour of Flanders and Strade Bianche in those years, riding one-day races with the same all-in aggression he brought to the mountains. Where other Tour winners defended their status, Pogačar attacked monuments as if they were the only races that existed.
