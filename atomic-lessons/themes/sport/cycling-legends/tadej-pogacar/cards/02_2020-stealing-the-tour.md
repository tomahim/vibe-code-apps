Pogačar's first Tour de France, in 2020, became an instant classic. All race long he shadowed his fellow Slovenian Primož Roglič, the favourite, who led into the penultimate day: a time trial finishing at the top of La Planche des Belles Filles, the Plank of the Beautiful Girls.

What happened there is the most famous time trial of the modern era. Pogačar did not just beat Roglič; he took almost two minutes out of him, winning the stage and ripping away the yellow jersey one day before Paris. At twenty-one he was the second-youngest Tour winner ever, with the points and mountains jerseys too. It was the kind of ambush that cycling talks about for decades.
