Armstrong's legacy inside cycling is cautionary. The scandal accelerated better testing, biological passports and a culture in which extraordinary performances are audited rather than simply celebrated. The Tour record book keeps 1999 to 2005 deliberately blank: seven years with no official winner.

As a lesson, his story is complete: a genuinely gifted rider and cancer survivor, a comeback that inspired millions, and a doping program that poisoned all of it. He remains banned for life from sanctioned cycling.

The USADA case that ended his career remains the reference for how organised doping gets exposed: not by a single positive test, but by teammates' testimony and patient documentation.

Source: [Lance Armstrong on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Armstrong) and the [USADA reasoned decision](https://www.usada.org/wp-content/uploads/ReasonedDecision.pdf).
