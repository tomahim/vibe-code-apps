In August 2012 Armstrong stopped fighting the charges, and USADA stripped him of every result from August 1998 onward and banned him for life. That October, the UCI ratified the decision: all seven Tour titles were erased, and the record books now show no winner for 1999 to 2005, the stripped victories deliberately not reallocated.

In January 2013 he admitted doping in a televised interview with Oprah Winfrey, a limited confession after years of aggressive denial. The IOC stripped his 2000 Olympic time-trial bronze, which he returned; sponsors had already fled, and he stepped down from Livestrong. Lawsuits over sponsorship money followed for years. The fall was as total as the rise had been spectacular.
