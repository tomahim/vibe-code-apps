**1. The comeback was real.** He survived metastatic testicular cancer in 1996 and returned to the top of professional sport.

**2. The titles are gone.** USADA's 2012 case stripped all seven Tours; 1999-2005 officially has no Tour winner.

**3. The story changed the sport.** Better testing and scepticism of miracle performances are Armstrong's unintended legacy.
