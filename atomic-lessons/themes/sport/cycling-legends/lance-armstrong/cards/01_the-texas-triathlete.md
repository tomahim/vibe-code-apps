Lance Armstrong was born on 18 September 1971 in Plano, Texas, and was a sporting prodigy before he ever raced a bike seriously: a champion triathlete in his teens, he switched fully to road cycling and turned professional with the Motorola team in 1992.

His early career showed flashes of brilliance rather than consistency. In 1993 he won a Tour de France stage and then, at twenty-one, the World Championship road race in Oslo, one of the youngest world champions ever. He added the Clásica de San Sebastián in 1995 and the Tour DuPont twice. He was seen as a classics rider and stage hunter: nobody, including Armstrong himself, talked about him as a future Tour winner.
