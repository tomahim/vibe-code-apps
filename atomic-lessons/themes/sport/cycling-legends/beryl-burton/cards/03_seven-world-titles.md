Burton's international record matched her home dominance. She won the women's world road race championship in 1960 and 1967 and finished second in 1961. On the track she specialised in the individual pursuit, where she was world champion five times, in 1959, 1960, 1962, 1963 and 1966, and took medals across three decades.

Seven world titles in total, won while working and without a professional team behind her. In 1967 she received the rare honour, for a woman, of an invitation to ride the Grand Prix des Nations, then one of the sport's most prestigious time trials, a small crack in a door that was almost always closed to women. She kept pushing at it for thirty years.
