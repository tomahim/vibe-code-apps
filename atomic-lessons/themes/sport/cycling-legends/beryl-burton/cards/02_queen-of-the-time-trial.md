title: Queen of the time trial

In British time trialling, Burton was not just dominant; she was permanent. She won the women's Best British All-Rounder competition, the season-long benchmark over 25, 50 and 100 miles and 12 hours, for 25 consecutive years, from 1959 to 1983. Across her career she collected around ninety domestic championships and set record after record at every distance.

Her standards were so far ahead that comparisons with other women stopped making sense. The only meaningful yardstick was the men's record book, and Burton measured herself against it directly. She was the first woman to break two hours for a 50-mile time trial, then four hours for 100 miles, marks that many strong male club riders of the day could not touch.
