Burton died in 1996, aged fifty-eight, of heart failure while out riding her bike, which is how those who knew her best said she would have wanted to go. Her records stood for years, some for decades, and British cycling honours her with memorial rides and a place in every hall of fame it has.

Her legend is double: the numbers themselves, and the era they came from. A working Yorkshire mother held a men's national record outright. It had never happened before and has never happened since.

Her 1967 twelve-hour ride of 277.25 miles stood as the women's record for fifty years.
