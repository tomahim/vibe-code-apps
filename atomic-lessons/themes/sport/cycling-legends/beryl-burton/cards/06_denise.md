One of the strangest chapters of Burton's career was racing against her own daughter. Denise Burton grew into a strong international rider, and at the 1976 national road championship mother and daughter fought for the same title. Denise won; Beryl, fiercely competitive to the last, famously struggled to congratulate her.

The episode is usually told gently, because it reveals rather than diminishes her: after twenty years of winning everything, even family got no free pass. Beryl and Denise later set a British 10-mile tandem record together in 1982, riding 21 minutes 25 seconds. Racing with, and against, her daughter kept Burton competitive into her forties.
