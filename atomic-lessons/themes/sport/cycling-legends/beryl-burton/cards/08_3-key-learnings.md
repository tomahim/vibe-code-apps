**1. Twenty-five years on top.** Burton won the women's Best British All-Rounder every year from 1959 to 1983.

**2. She beat the men.** Her 1967 12-hour record of 277.25 miles stood above the men's record for two years.

**3. Seven world titles.** Two on the road and five in the pursuit, won while working, with no women's professional structure behind her.
