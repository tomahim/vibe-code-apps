title: An amateur by necessity

---
Burton's achievements came despite, not because of, the system around her. In her era there was no women's Tour de France, no women's Olympic road cycling, no professional women's teams. The World Championship was essentially the only international target, and everything else was self-funded club racing fitted around work and family.

She earned her living with her hands, including farm and office work, and she and Charlie ran her racing life themselves. Her story is a reminder of how much talent women's sport simply wasted for decades: one of the greatest endurance cyclists Britain ever produced spent her best years racing at dawn because that was the only stage available to her.
