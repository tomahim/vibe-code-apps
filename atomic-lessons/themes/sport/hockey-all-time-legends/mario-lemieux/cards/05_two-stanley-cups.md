With stars such as Jaromir Jagr around him, Lemieux led the Penguins to the Stanley Cup in 1991 and 1992. He won the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP both times. The titles transformed a struggling franchise into a champion.

He also won the Art Ross Trophy six times in total and the Hart Trophy three times, while scoring more than 100 points ten times. His career totals ended at 690 goals and 1,723 points in 915 games, one of the highest points-per-game averages in NHL history. Bobby Orr, one of the greatest players ever, told Sports Illustrated that Lemieux could do things that Orr could not.
