**1. Talent was obvious early.** He broke junior scoring records and was drafted first overall in 1984.

**2. Health tested him.** Back problems and Hodgkin's lymphoma cut seasons short, yet he kept winning scoring titles.

**3. He saved the club twice.** First as a champion in 1991 and 1992, then as the owner who rescued the Penguins from bankruptcy.
