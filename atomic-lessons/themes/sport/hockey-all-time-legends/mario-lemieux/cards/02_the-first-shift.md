The Pittsburgh Penguins had won only 16 games the year before, and attendance was under 7,000 a game, with talk of relocation. They took Lemieux first overall in the 1984 draft. On October 11, 1984, in Boston, he forced All-Star defenceman Ray Bourque into a turnover on his very first shift, grabbed the puck and scored on his first shot.

He finished the season with 100 points and won the Calder Trophy as rookie of the year. His team was still weak, but fans could see what he might become. At 6 foot 4 and 230 pounds he combined reach and power with delicate hands, and he could score with a defender hanging on his back, much like Maurice Richard before him.
