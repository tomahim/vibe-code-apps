In 1987-88 Lemieux won his first Art Ross Trophy and his first Hart Trophy. In 1988-89 he recorded career highs of 85 goals, 114 assists and 199 points, the most by anyone except Wayne Gretzky at that point. His play in the 1987 Canada Cup had given him confidence: he led all players with 11 goals, including the winner in the final.

On New Year's Eve of that season he scored five goals against New Jersey, each of a different kind: at even strength, on the power play, shorthanded, on a penalty shot and into an empty net. To the best of anyone's knowledge it is the only time this has been done. He later scored 100 points ten times in his career.
