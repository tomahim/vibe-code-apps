Mario Lemieux was born on October 5, 1965, in Montreal. He began skating at three, and when it was too cold or dark for the neighborhood rink, the front room of the family house became the rink, with his father packing snow onto the carpet. Hockey coach Scotty Bowman, who saw him at 13, called him the best he had seen at that age.

With Laval in the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League he scored a record 282 points in 1983-84, passing the points record of his idol Guy Lafleur. In the final game of that season he needed three goals to tie Lafleur's goal mark of 130 and scored six. He wore number 66 in junior, an inversion of Gretzky's 99.
