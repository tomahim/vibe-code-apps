Lemieux fought serious injuries throughout his career. In 1989-90 he had a 46-game point streak while in severe back pain, and surgery that summer proved dangerous. Then in January 1993 he was diagnosed with Hodgkin's lymphoma and began radiation treatments.

He returned and still won the scoring title that season. The effects of radiation caused anemia and fatigue, and a second back operation in July 1993 limited him to 22 games the next season. He sat out 1994-95 to heal. After two healthy seasons he won the Hart Trophy in 1996, and he retired in 1997 at 31. Doctors, patients and fans saw his persistence as an example.
