Lemieux's return season of 2000-01 produced 35 goals and 76 points in 43 games. One reason to come back was that his four-year-old son Austin wanted to see him play. In 2002 he won an Olympic gold medal with Canada, the one honour he had missed.

When Sidney Crosby was drafted first overall in 2005, he lived in Lemieux's house, and the two played briefly together. Lemieux retired for good in December 2005 after being diagnosed with an irregular heartbeat. His story shows how talent, discipline and patience can help a person return from setbacks.
