Women's hockey became an Olympic sport in 1998, and Wickenheiser was named to Canada's first team, which took the silver medal. She then won gold in 2002, 2006, 2010 and 2014, four titles in a row. She was named tournament MVP in 2002 and 2006.

She appeared in five Winter Olympics, more than any other athlete in her sport, and at her retirement she was the all-time scoring leader in Olympic women's hockey with 51 points in 26 games. She carried Canada's flag at the 2014 Opening Ceremony and took the athletes' oath in English at the 2010 Games. Few players in any sport have matched that Olympic record.
