Wickenheiser became the first woman to play full-time professional hockey in a position other than goaltender. She joined HC Salamat, a men's team in Finland's third division, and scored twice and added 10 assists in 23 games. She briefly played with the team in 2003-04 after it moved to the second division.

She kept trying. She had a tryout with IFK Arboga in Sweden's men's third division in 2007 and signed with HC Linden in the same league in 2008-09. Her experiences showed that women could compete at a high level against men and helped open the way for more women in the sport.
