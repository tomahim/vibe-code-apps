Wickenheiser was inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame in 2019 and into the IIHF Hall of Fame the same year. HHOF calls her the most dominant skater of her era, as a member of Canada's National Women's Team over a 23-year career.

Around the world, women's hockey has grown since her debut. The Professional Women's Hockey League opened its first season on January 1, 2024, with six teams and a 72-game schedule, and its first game was between Toronto and New York in Toronto, which New York won 4-0. Pioneers like Wickenheiser are part of why that league had a foundation to build on.
