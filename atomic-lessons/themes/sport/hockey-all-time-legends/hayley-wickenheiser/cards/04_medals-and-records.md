The IIHF says Wickenheiser has won more medals than any other woman at its events: 13 medals, seven gold and six silver. She holds or shares eight Olympic records and four Women's World Championship records. In 61 games at the Women's Worlds she scored 37 goals and added 49 assists for 86 points.

She also played in Canadian clubs and universities. At the University of Calgary she played for the Dinos in 2010-11 and won the Brodrick Trophy as the best player in Canadian university women's hockey. She was named an Officer of the Order of Canada in 2011. Those totals put her at the top of the IIHF record lists.
