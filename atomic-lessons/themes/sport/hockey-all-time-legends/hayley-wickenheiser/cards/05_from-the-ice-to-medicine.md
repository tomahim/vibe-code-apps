Wickenheiser retired as a player in January 2017 and returned to university to get her medical degree. In 2018 the Toronto Maple Leafs hired her as an assistant director of player development, which meant she worked in the NHL's front offices.

In 2014 she was elected to the International Olympic Committee's Athletes' Commission. Her second career shows that a top athlete can use the discipline of sport to move into a very different field. She received the Hockey Hall of Fame's call in 2019. She combined hockey and medicine, two demanding fields, without leaving either behind completely. The Athletes' Commission role also connected her to the wider Olympic movement, beyond hockey itself, through sports governance.
