Hayley Wickenheiser was born on August 12, 1978, in Shaunavon, Saskatchewan. She played on boys' teams until she was 13. At age 12 she played for Alberta in the Under-18 tournament of the Canada Winter Games, where she scored the tournament-winning goal and was named MVP of the finals.

Just two years later, at 15, she was selected for Canada's National Women's Team and played in the 1994 World Championship, helping the team win gold. She went on to play in 13 World Championships, winning gold in 1994, 1997, 1999, 2000, 2004, 2007 and 2012. That early start against older boys shaped the player she became.
