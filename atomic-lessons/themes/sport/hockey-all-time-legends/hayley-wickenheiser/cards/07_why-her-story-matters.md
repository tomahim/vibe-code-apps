Wickenheiser's career shows how a player can push the boundaries of a sport at several levels. She won Olympic gold four times, played against men in Europe, and then became a doctor. The IIHF describes a personality that never said enough, never said compromise.

For younger players, her story offers a reminder of the importance of persistence. For fans, it gives a picture of how far women's hockey has come since the Olympic debut in 1998, from a single tournament each four years to a professional league.

Her career spans the sport's early Olympic years and its newest chapter.
