On December 2, 1995, Montreal lost 12-1 at home to Detroit. Coach Mario Tremblay left Roy in net for nine goals, and when he was finally pulled he told team president Ronald Corey it was his last game for the club. Montreal traded him to the Colorado Avalanche on December 6, 1995, along with Mike Keane.

Within months he had won another Stanley Cup, this time with Colorado in 1996. In 2001 he led the Avalanche to a second Cup, his fourth overall, and won the Conn Smythe Trophy a record third time. He also played for Canada's 1998 Olympic team.
