**1. Two clutch careers.** Roy won four Cups and three Conn Smythe Trophies; Hasek won two Cups and two Hart Trophies.

**2. Roy set the win record.** His 551 wins stood as the NHL record when he retired in 2003.

**3. Hasek won on the world stage.** His 1998 Olympic gold is one of the great goaltending performances.
