A 1992 trade to Buffalo allowed Hasek to become a starter. In 1994 he won his first of six Vezina Trophies. In 1996-97 he also won the Hart Trophy as league MVP, and the next season, with 13 shutouts, he won the Vezina and the Hart again. He is the only goaltender to win the Hart twice.

He earned the nickname the Dominator because he did whatever it took to keep pucks out, often in acrobatic style. In 1998-99 he took Buffalo to the Stanley Cup Final against Dallas with a career-best 1.87 goals-against average and a .937 save percentage.
