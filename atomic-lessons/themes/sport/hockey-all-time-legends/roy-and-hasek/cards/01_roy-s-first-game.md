Patrick Roy was drafted 51st overall by the Montreal Canadiens in 1984, after playing for the Granby Bisons of the Quebec junior league. The Hall of Fame notes he came from the area of the Quebec Nordiques, Montreal's fierce rivals.

In February 1985 Montreal called him up, supposedly just to watch from the bench. On February 23 he replaced the starting goalie, Doug Soetaert, in a 4-4 game at the start of the third period. The Canadiens won 6-4, so Roy had his first NHL win after only 20 minutes of play. He then led Sherbrooke to the AHL's Calder Cup that spring, which showed his strength under pressure.
