Dominik Hasek was born on January 29, 1965, in Pardubice, Czechoslovakia. At a try-out for five-year-olds he showed up wearing skate blades that screwed onto his shoes, and a group of nine-year-olds, who needed a goalie, recruited him. By 16 he was playing professionally for HC Pardubice.

Chicago drafted him 199th overall in 1983, but at that time NHL teams were cautious about players from Communist countries. He kept playing in Czechoslovakia and won the top goaltender award every year from 1986 to 1990. He made his NHL debut in 1990-91 as backup to Ed Belfour, with goaltending coach Vladislav Tretiak.
