Early in 2000-01, Roy passed Terry Sawchuk's career win total of 447, a number many fans thought was untouchable. In 2002-03 he became the first goaltender to play 1,000 NHL games. He retired on May 28, 2003, as the NHL's all-time winningest goaltender with 551 wins.

He won three Vezina Trophies, five Jennings Trophies and played in 11 All-Star Games. After playing he bought the Quebec Remparts of the QMJHL and coached them to the Memorial Cup title in 2006. He entered the Hall of Fame in 2006. Roy's wins record was a major part of his Hall of Fame case, and it placed him alongside the best goalies the league has known.
