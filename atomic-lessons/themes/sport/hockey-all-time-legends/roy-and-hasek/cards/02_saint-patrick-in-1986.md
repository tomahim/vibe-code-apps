In his rookie season, 1985-86, Roy played 47 games and took over as the starter when the playoffs arrived. Montreal won an improbable Stanley Cup, and Roy, at 20, won the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP. Fans in Montreal called him Saint Patrick.

He won the Cup again with Montreal in 1993 and again took the Conn Smythe Trophy. Hockey fans widely considered him the best clutch goaltender of his generation, and the Hall of Fame describes him as the first wave of a new breed of goalies from Quebec that helped make the province a leading source of goaltenders.
