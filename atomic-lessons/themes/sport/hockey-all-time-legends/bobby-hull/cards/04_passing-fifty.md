title: "Passing fifty"

---
On March 12, 1966, Hull became the first player to pass 50 goals in a season when he scored his 51st goal against the New York Rangers at Chicago Stadium, a straight slap shot with defenceman Harry Howell in the penalty box. The crowd gave him a long ovation. He finished that season with 54 goals, and in 1968-69 he scored 58.

He won the Hart Trophy as the league's most valuable player twice, in 1964-65 and 1965-66, and later was elected to the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1983. His success showed that scorers could move beyond the old limit of 50, and it set a target for later stars.
