Hull's slap shot was a thing of legend. He fired it with great force off a crudely bent stick blade, and players told stories of it. One joke said he could fire the puck through a car wash and it would not get wet. Toronto's Bobby Baun learned the power of the shot in a game in Chicago when Hull slapped a puck off his toe and ripped off a toenail.

NHL.com also describes a photograph from the 1960s in which Hull winds up for a slap shot while Canadiens captain Jean Beliveau stands in front of the net. Beliveau later laughed and said he was trying to get as thin as he could. Hull led the NHL in goals seven times.
