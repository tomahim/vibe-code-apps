Hull finished his career in 1979-80 with 27 games for the Jets and the Hartford Whalers. In the NHL he scored 610 goals, third most all-time when he retired, behind Gordie Howe with 801 and Phil Esposito with 710. He had 1,170 points in 1,063 NHL games between 1957 and 1980.

He said later that he had been at loggerheads with the NHL over leaving for the WHA, and he was proud to enter the Hall of Fame in 1983. His relationship with the game also had controversies off the ice, and some people judged him for his words and actions. Even so, his hockey impact in the 1960s was large.
