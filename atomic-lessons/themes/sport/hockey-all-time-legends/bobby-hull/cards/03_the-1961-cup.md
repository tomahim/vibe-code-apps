Hull helped the Blackhawks win the Stanley Cup in 1961, Chicago's first in 23 years. He won the Art Ross Trophy as the scoring leader three times, in 1959-60, 1961-62 and 1965-66. In 1961-62 he tied the record of 50 goals in a season held by Maurice Richard and Bernie Geoffrion.

Hull's popularity in Chicago stayed strong long after he left the team. NHL.com notes that the Golden Jet remained a reminder of a championship won when Chicago hockey rocked the historic Stadium, and when Black Hawks was spelled as two words. Chicago's 1961 title was its first in 23 years, so it meant a great deal to the city, and Hull was one of its main stars.
