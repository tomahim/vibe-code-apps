Bobby Hull was born on January 3, 1939, in Point Anne, Ontario. He took his first strides on an open-air rink just down the road from the family home. In 1957 he joined the Chicago Blackhawks and stayed for 15 seasons, until 1972.

He was nicknamed the Golden Jet for his blond hair and his speed. Toronto's Dave Keon said that in the early 1960s Hull was probably the player who carried the National Hockey League. He was dynamic, good-looking, a fast skater and an enormous shooter, and the league needed that kind of star after Maurice Richard retired in 1960.
