**1. A shot that defined an era.** The slap shot made Hull one of the biggest stars of the 1960s.

**2. He broke a barrier.** In 1965-66 he passed 50 goals in a season for the first time in NHL history.

**3. He changed the market.** His 1972 jump to the WHA showed rival leagues could pay stars far more.
