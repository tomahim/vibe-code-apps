In June 1972 Hull left the Blackhawks for the Winnipeg Jets of the new World Hockey Association. He received a check from the WHA for 1 million dollars as a bonus and then signed a ten-year contract to play and coach the Jets. The New York Times reported a package worth at least 2.5 million dollars.

The move shocked the sport, because it showed that a rival league could pay NHL-level stars far more. Hull spent seven seasons in the WHA, from 1972 to 1979. Hull said later that the contract made the future secure for his family, but there were also issues with Chicago management that disenchanted him.
