Hull's son Brett also became a Hall of Fame scorer. Brett was inducted in 2009 after scoring 741 goals and 1,391 points with five teams in 19 seasons. Bobby said that Brett was the greatest sniper in NHL history and could score in so many ways that it made his head spin.

The pair show how a family can shape a sport across generations. Bobby brought the slap shot to a new level in the 1960s, and Brett carried the scoring tradition into the 1990s and 2000s. Bobby Hull died on January 30, 2023, at age 84, and Hall of Fame peers such as Dave Keon remembered him as a star who galvanized the league.
