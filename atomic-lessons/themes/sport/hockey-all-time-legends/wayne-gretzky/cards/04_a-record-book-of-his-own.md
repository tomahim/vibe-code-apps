In 1981-82 Gretzky scored 92 goals, still the NHL record for one season. That year he also scored 50 goals in only 39 games, beating the old mark of 50 in 50 games set by Maurice Richard in 1944-45. He scored four goals in game 38 and five in game 39 to get there.

In 1985-86 he recorded 163 assists and 215 points, both single-season records. Between 1981-82 and 1986-87 he averaged 203.2 points a season. His career totals were 894 goals, 1,963 assists and 2,857 points in 1,487 games, and he held 61 NHL records when he retired. He won ten Art Ross Trophies as scoring leader, including seven in a row.
