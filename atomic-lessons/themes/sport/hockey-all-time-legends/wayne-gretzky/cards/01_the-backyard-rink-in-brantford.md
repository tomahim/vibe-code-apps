Wayne Gretzky was born in Brantford, Ontario. His father, Walter, first took him skating at the age of two on the Nith River below his grandparents' farm, and later built what may be the world's most famous backyard rink. Wayne skated on it morning, noon and night.

He was already playing for the Brantford novice all-star team at five, alongside boys five and six years older. In the peewee season he spent at ages ten and eleven, wearing the number 9 of his idol Gordie Howe, he scored 378 goals in 85 games. By 13 he had scored more than 1,000 goals in minor hockey, so he was a celebrity across Canada long before he turned professional.
