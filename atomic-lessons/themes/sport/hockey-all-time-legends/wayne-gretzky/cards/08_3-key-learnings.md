**1. Early practice mattered.** A backyard rink, a father's drills and years of play against older boys prepared him for the NHL.

**2. He saw the ice differently.** Playing from behind the net and passing to space produced 1,963 assists, still the career record.

**3. His marks stand apart.** 92 goals and 215 points in a season, 2,857 career points, four Cups with the Oilers and a number 99 retired league-wide.
