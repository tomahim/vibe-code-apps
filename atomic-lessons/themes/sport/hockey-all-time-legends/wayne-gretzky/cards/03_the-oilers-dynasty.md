Edmonton built a fast, attacking team around Gretzky, with Mark Messier, Jari Kurri, Paul Coffey and goaltender Grant Fuhr. The Oilers won the Stanley Cup in 1984, 1985, 1987 and 1988, which is four titles in five seasons. Gretzky won the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP in 1985 and 1988.

In nine NHL seasons with Edmonton he scored 583 goals and added 1,086 assists. His coach Glen Sather said that hitting Gretzky was like trying to hit confetti: he was thin, but he was almost impossible to catch. The team also scored more goals than any rival, and many hockey historians still see those Oilers as one of the best teams ever.
