The 1987 Canada Cup final against the Soviet Union was a best-of-three series in which every game finished 6-5. Gretzky set up Mario Lemieux for the winning goals in games two and three, the last one with 1:26 left on the clock. Gretzky was named tournament MVP with 21 points in nine games.

He retired on April 18, 1999, after a 20-season career with Edmonton, Los Angeles, St. Louis and the New York Rangers. He entered the Hockey Hall of Fame that same year. On February 6, 2000, the NHL retired number 99 for every team during the All-Star Game, an honour no other player has received. His name is now shorthand for greatness in the sport.

Source: [Hockey Hall of Fame honoured members](https://www.hhof.com/honouredmembers/honouredmembers.html).
