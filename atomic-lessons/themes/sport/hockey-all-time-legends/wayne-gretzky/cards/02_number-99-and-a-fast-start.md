Gretzky spent one season in major junior hockey with the Sault Ste. Marie Greyhounds. His preferred number 9 was already taken by a veteran, so coach Muzz MacPherson suggested wearing two nines. That is how he first became number 99.

At 17 he turned pro with the Indianapolis Racers of the World Hockey Association. The financially struggling club traded him to the Edmonton Oilers less than three months before his 18th birthday. The Oilers joined the NHL for 1979-80, and Gretzky won the Hart Trophy as the league's most valuable player in that very first NHL season. He won it eight years in a row, from 1979-80 to 1986-87.
