On August 9, 1988, the Oilers traded Gretzky to the Los Angeles Kings, along with Marty McSorley and Mike Krushelnyski. Edmonton received Martin Gelinas, Jimmy Carson, three first-round draft picks and 15 million dollars in cash. He had married actress Janet Jones just 24 days earlier, and Canada reacted as if a national treasure had been sold.

In California, he helped the Kings reach their first Stanley Cup Final in 1993. On October 15, 1989 he passed Gordie Howe for the NHL's career points lead, and on March 23, 1994 he scored his 802nd goal against Vancouver to pass Howe's goals record, with Howe watching from the stands. Many credit his arrival with the growth of hockey in California and the American sunbelt.
