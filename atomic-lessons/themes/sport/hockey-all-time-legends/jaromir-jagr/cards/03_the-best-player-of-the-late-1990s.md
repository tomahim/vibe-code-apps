title: "The best player of the late 1990s"

In 1994-95 Jagr became the first European-born and trained scoring champion in NHL history. That was the first of five Art Ross Trophies, which made him one of just six players with five or more scoring titles. In 1995-96 he had 149 points, the first of five 100-point seasons.

From 1994 to 2001 he was the most productive player in the NHL, with 760 points, far more than anyone else. During his 11-year Penguins tenure he registered more points than any other NHL player, and NHL.com notes he held the unofficial title of Best Player in the Game over the second half of the 1990s. Many saw him as the heir to Gretzky as the face of the league.
