Jagr and Lemieux led the Penguins to the Stanley Cup in 1991 and 1992. Those turned out to be Jagr's only Cups, but the records were only beginning. In his first four seasons his scoring rose from 57 points to 69, 94 and 99.

He was a big presence in the 1992 playoffs too. When Lemieux was out for five games, Jagr scored four goals and seven points, with three game-winning goals. Pittsburgh was still led by Lemieux, who had been the franchise's main star for years, so Jagr learned inside a championship team. Those two Cups came when Jagr was only 19 and 20 years old, early in a career that eventually lasted 24 NHL seasons, which is why the 1991 and 1992 titles are such an important starting point in his story.
