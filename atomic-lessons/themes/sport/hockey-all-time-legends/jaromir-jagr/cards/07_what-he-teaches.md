Jagr's career is a lesson in staying power and adaptation. He rose in a European system, learned from Mario Lemieux, won scoring titles, and then kept playing across many years and countries. He also brought attention to the Czech game, from the 1998 Olympic gold to many World Championships.

His numbers sit near the top of the NHL's all-time lists. Only Gretzky has more career points, and Jagr's game-winning goal total is a record. For fans in Pittsburgh and in the Czech Republic, he is remembered as a player who delivered at key moments and who could not be written off.
