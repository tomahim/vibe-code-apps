Jagr was at his best when the stakes were high. He has 14 playoff game-winning goals and four overtime goals, both Penguins club playoff records. His Game 6 overtime goal against New Jersey in 1999 is credited with helping keep the team afloat so that Lemieux could buy it and keep it in Pittsburgh.

Jagr himself called it his most important goal. The story connects two lessons in this path: Lemieux saved the Penguins as an owner, and Jagr's playoff heroics helped make that rescue possible. Later Jagr played for other teams, but Pittsburgh remained his best-known NHL home. That is why Pittsburgh fans remember the goal.
