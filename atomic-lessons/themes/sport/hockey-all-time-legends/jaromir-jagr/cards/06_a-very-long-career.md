Jagr played 24 NHL seasons and recorded 1,921 points, more than anyone in history except Wayne Gretzky. His 135 game-winning goals are more than any other player has scored. He also played in the Czech Republic and elsewhere, and NHL.com and IIHF both note how long he stayed active.

In 2024, when he was inducted into the IIHF Hall of Fame, the IIHF noted that Czechia's Number 68 was still playing for his club team, Kladno, at 52 years old. Kladno is the city where he grew up, and he has been its player, owner and supporter all his life. Few athletes have competed at a high level for so long.

Source: [IIHF, Jaromir Jagr](https://www.iihf.com/en/events/2024/wm/static/60736/jaromir_jagr).
