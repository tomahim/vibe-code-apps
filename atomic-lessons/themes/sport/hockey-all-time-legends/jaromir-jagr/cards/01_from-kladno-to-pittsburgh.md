Jaromir Jagr was born on February 15, 1972, in Kladno, Czechoslovakia. He developed in his home city's system. At the 1990 World Junior Championship he dominated, recording 18 points in seven games and helping Czechoslovakia win bronze.

A few months later he was the youngest player on the Czech team that won bronze at the senior Men's World Championship in Switzerland. That summer Pittsburgh drafted him fifth overall, and he joined the Penguins in the fall as the youngest player in the league, on a team led by Mario Lemieux. His career then spanned more than two decades, a long road that began in a small city.
