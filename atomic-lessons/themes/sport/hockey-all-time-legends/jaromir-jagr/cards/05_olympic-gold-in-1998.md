Jagr's most memorable international moment came in 1998 at the Nagano Olympics, when the Czech Republic won its first Olympic gold and sent a nation into a frenzy. Jagr scored only one goal in the tournament, but it was the game winner against the United States.

It was the first of his five Olympic appearances. In 2006 he helped the team win bronze, defeating Russia 3-0. His international career spanned 25 years, from the 1990 World Juniors to his final tournament, the 2015 Men's World Championship at home. He also played at the 1991 Canada Cup at age 19 and at the World Cups of 1996 and 2004.
