Orr's final great tournament came in the 1976 Canada Cup. He played on a team many consider the greatest ever assembled, scored nine points to lead the tournament and was voted MVP. He said there was nothing like winning for your country.

He entered the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1979. His career totals were 270 goals, 645 assists and 915 points in 657 games. Players such as Darryl Sittler said that Orr was better on one leg than anybody else was on two. Offensive defencemen who followed him play a style that Orr made possible.

Source: [NHL.com, Bobby Orr 100 Greatest](https://www.nhl.com/news/bobby-orr-100-greatest-nhl-hockey-players-285636896).
