Bobby Orr started organized hockey at age 8 in Parry Sound, Ontario. He began as a forward, but his minor-hockey coaches moved him back to defence so that his speed and stickhandling would help the team more. He practiced from morning until dark on school rinks and in parking lots, shooting pucks filled with lead at sheets of plywood.

By 1962-63, at only 14, he was playing with the junior Oshawa Generals against players up to six years older. He scored 72, 93 and 94 points in his three junior seasons. By 18 he had signed with the Boston Bruins, a team that had missed the playoffs seven seasons in a row before he arrived.
