The Bruins missed the playoffs in Orr's rookie season, but four years after his debut they were Stanley Cup champions for the first time since 1941. Early in his second season, the club also acquired forward Phil Esposito from the Chicago Blackhawks, and the two stars drove a high-scoring Boston team.

Boston won the Cup again in 1972, so Orr won two titles in total. The change in the franchise was huge. A team that had missed the playoffs for seven straight seasons before Orr arrived became a champion within four years. Esposito later said that all Bobby did was change the face of hockey by himself.
