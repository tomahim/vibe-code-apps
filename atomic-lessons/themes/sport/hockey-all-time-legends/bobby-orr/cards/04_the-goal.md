On May 10, 1970, Orr scored the Cup-winning goal 40 seconds into overtime of Game 4 of the Final against the St. Louis Blues. Blues defenceman Noel Picard tripped him just as he scored, and Orr flew through the air in front of goalie Glenn Hall with his arms stretched wide.

A photographer froze that instant, and the picture became one of the most famous in hockey history. A statue based on it stands outside Boston's TD Garden. Hall, a Hall of Fame goalie, joked that he had signed that photograph more than any other. Boston called it simply The Goal, and the name became a proper noun in the city.
