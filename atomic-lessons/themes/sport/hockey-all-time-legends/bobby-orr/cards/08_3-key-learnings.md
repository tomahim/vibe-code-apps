**1. Position changed.** Orr moved from forward to defence and then used speed and skill to attack from the blue line.

**2. He led Boston to the top.** The Bruins won the Cup in 1970 and 1972, and The Goal became a symbol of the city.

**3. Injuries ended his prime.** Knee problems cut his career short, and he retired at 30.
