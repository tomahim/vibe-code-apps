Orr won the Calder Trophy as top rookie in 1966-67, and then the Norris Trophy as best defenceman eight times in a row, from 1967-68 through 1974-75, which is still a record. In 1969-70 and 1974-75 he won the Art Ross Trophy as top scorer, the only defenceman ever to do so. He also won the Hart Trophy as league MVP three years in a row, from 1969-70 to 1971-72.

In 1970-71 he set records of 102 assists, 139 points for a defenceman and a plus-minus rating of plus-124. In 1969-70 he is the only player to win the Cup, the Conn Smythe, the Hart, the Art Ross and the Norris in the same season. He led the NHL in assists five times.
