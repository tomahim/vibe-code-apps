Orr's knees were his problem. He had so many operations that it was said he saw the knife more often than a dinner plate. He missed the 1972 Summit Series because of a knee injury, a fact that left many fans wondering how Canada's team would have looked with him.

In June 1976 he signed as a free agent with the Chicago Black Hawks on the advice of his agent, Alan Eagleson. He later said he had not known he could have had an ownership stake in the Bruins if he had re-signed. In Chicago he played only 20 games in 1976-77 and six more in 1978-79 after sitting out a season following surgery. He retired at only 30.
