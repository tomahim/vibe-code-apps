Howe's line with centre Sid Abel and left winger Ted Lindsay was nicknamed the Production Line. Together they helped make Detroit the dominant team of the early 1950s. The Red Wings finished first in the regular season seven years in a row, from 1949 to 1955.

Detroit won the Stanley Cup four times in that stretch: 1950, 1952, 1954 and 1955. Howe played on all four winning teams. The line was a good example of how three different styles could work together. Abel set up plays, Lindsay brought energy and edge, and Howe, on the right wing, supplied finishing and strength.
