The Gordie Howe hat trick is a goal, an assist and a fight in a single game. It is an informal tribute rather than an official statistic. Hockey fans use it because Howe symbolized a complete forward: a gifted scorer who could also defend himself and his teammates.

His toughness helped him last. Players who treated the star carelessly could expect an answer, and opponents gave him room. Britannica describes his combination of puck handling, wrist shots and legendary toughness as the reason he earned the nickname Mr. Hockey. Broadcasters still announce a Gordie Howe hat trick when a player completes the unusual set, even decades after his playing days.
