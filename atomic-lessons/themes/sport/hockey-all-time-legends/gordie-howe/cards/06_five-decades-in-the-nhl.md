When the WHA's New England club entered the NHL in 1979 as the Hartford Whalers, Howe went with them. At 51 he played all 80 games of the 1979-80 season, scoring 15 goals and adding 26 assists, and he appeared in the All-Star Game. It was the end of a career that covered five different decades in the NHL.

In September 1997 he signed a one-game contract with the Detroit Vipers of the International Hockey League and played at 69, which meant that he had played professional hockey in six decades overall. That appearance was partly a publicity event, but it showed how closely he was tied to the sport. Few athletes have competed so long.
