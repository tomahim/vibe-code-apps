title: "Six scoring titles and six Harts"

Howe won the Art Ross Trophy as the NHL's top point scorer six times, in 1951, 1952, 1953, 1954, 1957 and 1963. He also won the Hart Trophy as most valuable player six times, with the first awards coming in the early 1950s and the last in 1963, a gap of more than a decade.

He was a rare scorer with either hand, since he could take a strong wrist shot from both sides. NHL.com also notes 22 consecutive seasons of scoring at least 23 goals and 23 All-Star Game appearances. His best season came in 1968-69, when he was 40 and scored 103 points, an age at which most players had retired.
