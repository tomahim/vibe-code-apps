At his retirement, Howe held NHL records of 1,850 points and 801 goals. Wayne Gretzky passed the points total on October 15, 1989 and the goals total on March 23, 1994. Howe watched the second record fall and later wrote that it was in good hands.

He entered the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1972 and the Order of Canada was awarded to him in 1971. In 2008 he received the first NHL Lifetime Achievement Award. Howe died on June 10, 2016, at the age of 88. When players talk about longevity and a complete game, they still point to him.
