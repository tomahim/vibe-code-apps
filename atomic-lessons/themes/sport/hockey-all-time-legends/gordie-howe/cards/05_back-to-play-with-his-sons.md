Howe retired after the 1970-71 season and took a job in Detroit's front office. In 1973 he came out of retirement to play for the Houston Aeros of the World Hockey Association, alongside his sons Mark and Marty. He scored 100 points in his first season there and was named the WHA's most valuable player in 1974.

The Aeros won the league title in 1974 and again in 1975. After four seasons in Houston, Howe moved in 1977 to the New England Whalers, and his sons joined him again. Playing beside his own children, in his mid-40s, was rare for a major professional athlete and added a human side to his legend.
