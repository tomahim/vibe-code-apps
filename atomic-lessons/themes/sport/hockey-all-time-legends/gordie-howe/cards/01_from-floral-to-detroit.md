Gordie Howe was born on March 31, 1928, in Floral, Saskatchewan. He started playing hockey at five. In 1944 he moved to the United States, where he was put on the Detroit Red Wings' negotiating list, and in 1945 he signed with Omaha of the United States Hockey League.

After a season in Omaha he joined the Red Wings in 1946, wearing number 9. He soon became known for both his scoring and his competitiveness. He stayed with Detroit for 25 seasons, and his career eventually covered a record 1,767 NHL games over 32 seasons, which is still the standard people use when they talk about hockey longevity.
