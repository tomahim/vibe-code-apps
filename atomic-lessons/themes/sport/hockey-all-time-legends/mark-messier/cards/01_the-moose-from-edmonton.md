Mark Messier grew up in Edmonton. At four he was already attending his father's minor-league practices, and at eleven he was a stick boy for the Spruce Grove Mets. Five years later he starred for the same team. His nickname, the Moose, is a tribute to his size, strength and determination.

At 16 he weighed close to 200 pounds, and he skipped major junior hockey and college entirely. In 1978 the Indianapolis Racers of the World Hockey Association gave him a five-game tryout, and he then played 47 games for the Cincinnati Stingers. The Edmonton Oilers chose him in the third round, 48th overall, of the 1979 NHL Entry Draft.
