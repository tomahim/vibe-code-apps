In 2006 both the Rangers and the Oilers retired his number 11 in pre-game ceremonies. That same year the NHL created an award that carries his name, the Mark Messier Leadership Award, which recognizes leadership on and off the ice.

The Hockey Hall of Fame inducted him in 2007. HHOF describes him as the most complete player of his generation, a power forward with talent, size and an unpredictable mean streak. His reputation did not come from numbers alone. People remember a captain who guaranteed a win and then delivered one. That mix of skill and edge is the reason for the title.

Source: [Hockey Hall of Fame, Mark Messier](https://www.hhof.com/induction_archives/ind07Messier.shtml).
