In the 1994 Eastern Conference final, the Rangers trailed New Jersey three games to two. Messier publicly guaranteed a win in Game 6. He then scored a hat trick in the third period to bring his team back from elimination and force a Game 7.

The Rangers beat the Devils and then won the Cup in a thrilling seven-game series against Pavel Bure and the Vancouver Canucks. Messier scored the Cup-winning goal in Game 7. It was the franchise's first title in 54 years, and Messier became the first player to captain two different teams to Stanley Cup titles. It remains one of the great moments in New York sports history.
