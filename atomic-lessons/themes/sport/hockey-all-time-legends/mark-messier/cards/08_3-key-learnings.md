**1. He grew up in hockey.** From stick boy to the WHA at 17, his path was unusual.

**2. He led two teams.** Messier captained both Edmonton and New York, and won the Cup in 1990 and 1994.

**3. Leadership is his legacy.** His 1994 guarantee and the NHL leadership award that carries his name show why.
