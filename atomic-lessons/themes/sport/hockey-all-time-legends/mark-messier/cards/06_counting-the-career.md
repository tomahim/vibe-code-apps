Messier finished with six Stanley Cups, five with Edmonton and one with the Rangers, and two Hart Trophies as most valuable player. He scored his 600th goal in October 1998, and he, Gretzky and Jari Kurri were three of only ten players to reach that mark at that time.

He returned to the Rangers in 2000. During 2003-04 he passed Gordie Howe to become the second-highest point getter in NHL history, and he finished with 1,887 points. For Canada he played in three Canada Cups (1984, 1987 and 1991), one World Championship in 1989 and one World Cup in 1996. He retired in the summer of 2005.
