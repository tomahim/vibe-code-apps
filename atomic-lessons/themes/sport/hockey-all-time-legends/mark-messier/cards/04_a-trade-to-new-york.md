The small-market Oilers struggled after the 1990 title, and Messier was traded to the New York Rangers before the 1991-92 season, in a deal that brought Bernie Nicholls, Steven Rice and Louie DeBrusk to Edmonton. He took the leadership role with him.

In New York, the Rangers had not won the Stanley Cup in decades, and the pressure was heavy. Messier's production declined a bit by 1993-94, but he still scored more than 20 goals for the 14th time. His playoff form was the point: he was a captain who raised his play when the games mattered most, and the Rangers needed exactly that after a long drought.
