When the Oilers traded Gretzky to Los Angeles in 1988, Messier became captain. Many people predicted that Edmonton's success would leave with the Great One, and in the 1989 playoffs Gretzky's Kings upset the Oilers in the first round.

But in 1989-90 Messier had a career year. He finished second to Gretzky in scoring with 129 points and won the Hart Trophy as league MVP. In the semifinal against Chicago, with the Oilers down 2-1 in games, he scored two goals and added two assists in a 4-2 win in Game 4. Edmonton won the remaining games and then beat Boston in the Final, giving Messier his fifth Cup with the Oilers.
