Messier joined the Oilers in 1979-80. He scored 50 goals in 1981-82, his third season, which doubled his total from the year before, and he was named to the NHL First All-Star Team. He played alongside Wayne Gretzky, Paul Coffey and Grant Fuhr, and the group grew into a champion.

Edmonton won its first Stanley Cup in 1984, and Messier was named the most valuable player of the playoffs, taking the Conn Smythe Trophy with 26 post-season points. The Oilers were an attacking team, but they were also a tight defensive group. Messier was fast, powerful and physical, which made him a two-way player who could excel at both ends of the ice.
