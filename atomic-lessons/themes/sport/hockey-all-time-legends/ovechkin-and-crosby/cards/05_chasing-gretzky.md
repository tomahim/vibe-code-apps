For years Ovechkin chased Wayne Gretzky's all-time record of 894 goals. Gretzky had held it since he scored his 802nd on March 23, 1994. The chase was branded The Gr8 Chase, after Ovechkin's number 8.

On April 6, 2025, Ovechkin scored his 895th career goal, a power-play shot against the New York Islanders at Elmont, New York, assisted by Dylan Strome and Tom Wilson. At 39, he became the NHL's all-time goals leader, the tenth player in the league's 107-year history to hold that title outright. The number 8 gave the chase its name, and every goal in the final seasons brought him closer to the record. The achievement was also a link back to Gretzky, who had set the standard for scoring more than thirty years earlier.
