On February 28, 2010, Crosby scored in overtime of the Olympic final in Vancouver to give Canada the gold medal. The goal gave Canada gold on home ice.

Four years later he captained Canada to a second consecutive Olympic title in Sochi, in 2014. Crosby also won a World Championship in 2015 and was MVP of the 2016 World Cup. His Olympic overtime goal made him a national hero, and it showed again that he performed in the biggest moments. The Vancouver goal is one of the most famous in international hockey, and it came in the gold-medal game, in overtime, on home ice, which is the most dramatic setting a player could ask for. Sochi in 2014 then showed that the first title was not a one-time event for Canada or for its captain.
