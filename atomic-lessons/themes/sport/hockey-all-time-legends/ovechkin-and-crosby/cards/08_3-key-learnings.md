**1. Same era, different paths.** Ovechkin was picked first in 2004, Crosby in 2005, and each rebuilt a franchise.

**2. Crosby won everywhere.** Three Cups, two Olympic golds, and the 2010 overtime goal.

**3. Ovechkin passed Gretzky.** His 895th goal came on April 6, 2025, ending a record that stood since 1994.
