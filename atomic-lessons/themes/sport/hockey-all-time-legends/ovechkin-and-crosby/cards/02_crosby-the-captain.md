Crosby was the youngest captain ever to win the Stanley Cup, at 21 in 2009. He later won the Cup again in 2016 and 2017. In both of those seasons he also won the Conn Smythe Trophy as the playoff MVP.

He won the Hart Trophy as league MVP in 2007 and 2014 and the Art Ross Trophy as scoring leader in those same seasons. He lived in Mario Lemieux's guest house as an 18-year-old, and Lemieux mentored him. His game is based on effort, edge work on his skates, a strong backhand and many small details, as Sports Illustrated's Michael Farber described it.
