The Washington Capitals selected Alexander Ovechkin first overall in the 2004 NHL Entry Draft. The Pittsburgh Penguins took Sidney Crosby first overall in 2005. Both players were expected to lift struggling teams, and both did.

Ovechkin scored 52 goals as a rookie in 2005-06 and won the Calder Trophy. Crosby had 102 points that year, and at 18 years old he was the youngest player to reach 100 points in an NHL season. He finished as runner-up to Ovechkin for the Calder. NHL.com writes that Ovechkin is often considered Crosby's closest rival. The two draft years set up the rivalry that followed.
