Ovechkin was known for his shot. In 2015-16 he became the first Russia-born player to score 500 goals in the NHL. His team had struggled to win a championship despite his scoring, but in 2017-18 he led the Capitals to their first Stanley Cup and won the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP.

The contrast with Crosby is clear. Crosby is known as a complete centre who creates for everybody. Ovechkin is known as a shooter. Both played in the same era, and NHL.com calls Ovechkin Crosby's closest rival. He remains one of the sport's best-known goal scorers, and his shot is the main reason why.
