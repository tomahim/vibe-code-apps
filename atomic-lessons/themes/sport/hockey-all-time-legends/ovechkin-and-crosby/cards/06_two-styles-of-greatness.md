Crosby's greatness is measured in titles and awards: three Stanley Cups, two Olympic golds, two Hart Trophies and two Conn Smythe Trophies. Ovechkin's is measured in goals, plus a Stanley Cup, a Conn Smythe Trophy and the career goals record.

In 2025, after Ovechkin set the record, Crosby and teammate Evgeni Malkin paid tribute. Crosby said the record was pretty incredible. The two rivals also show how hockey leaves room for different kinds of stars: a player who wins with energy and detail, and another who wins with one of the best shots the sport has seen. Their careers still run in parallel.
