Phil Esposito led Canada with seven goals and 13 points. Paul Henderson also scored seven goals, tied with the Soviet Aleksander Yakushev. Tretiak played all eight games for the USSR with a 3.87 goals-against average. Canada used two goalies, Tony Esposito and Ken Dryden, in four games each.

Canada finished with four wins, three losses and one tie, so the series was decided only in the final minute of the final game. Soviet coach Anatoli Tarasov, the godfather of Soviet hockey, said the Canadians battled with the ferocity of a cornered animal, and that the Soviet players were better conditioned and stronger in skills, but could not match them in heart and desire.
