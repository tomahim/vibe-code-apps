Phil Esposito led Canada with seven goals and 13 points. Paul Henderson also scored seven goals, tied with the Soviet Aleksander Yakushev. Tretiak played all eight games for the USSR with a 3.87 goals-against average, and Canada used both Tony Esposito and Ken Dryden in goal.

Soviet legend Anatoli Tarasov said the Canadians battled with the ferocity of a cornered animal, and that the Soviet players were better in skills and conditioning but could not match them in heart and desire. His statement shows how the series helped each side learn from the other. The scoring leaders show how close the teams were.
