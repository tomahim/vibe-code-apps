Coach Harry Sinden changed his lineup for Game 2 in Toronto, which Canada won 4-1, but Game 3 in Winnipeg was a 4-4 tie. In Game 4 in Vancouver, Team Canada took careless penalties and lost 5-3. Fans booed the Canadians loudly.

Phil Esposito answered in a passionate television interview, saying he could not believe people were booing, and promising that he would apologize if the Russians boosted their players like some Canadian fans did. HHOF says his speech marked a turning point for the team. With the series at 2-1-1 in the Soviets' favour, Canada flew to Sweden to adjust to the bigger ice, then to Moscow.
