Game 8 was played on September 28. The Soviets led 5-3 after two periods. Goals by Phil Esposito and Yvan Cournoyer tied it in the third period.

With 34 seconds left, Henderson scored. Canada won 6-5, and the series 4-3-1. Henderson scored the winning goal in each of the last three games. The Soviets led 5-3 after two periods, and the score was tied only midway through the third. Henderson scored the winning goal in Games 6, 7 and 8, which is why he is the series' best-known Canadian, and the 6-5 result gave Canada four wins, three losses and a tie in the series.
