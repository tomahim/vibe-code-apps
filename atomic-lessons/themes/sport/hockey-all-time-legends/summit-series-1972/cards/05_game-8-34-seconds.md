Game 8 was played on September 28. The Soviets led 5-3 after two periods. Goals by Phil Esposito and Yvan Cournoyer tied it in the third period.

With 34 seconds left, Henderson scored. Canada won 6-5, and the series 4-3-1. Henderson scored the winning goal in each of the last three games. Henderson's three goals in the last three games are why he is the series' best-known Canadian, and the 34-second finish is one of the most replayed moments in the history of the sport. Tretiak and the Soviets had come very close to leaving Moscow with a better result than they did.
