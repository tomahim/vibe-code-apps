Game 1 was played at the Montreal Forum on September 2, 1972. Canada scored twice in the first 6:32, and it looked like the Canadians were right. Then the Soviets took over, and the USSR won 7-3.

Valeri Kharlamov scored twice, and A young goaltender named Vladislav Tretiak made 29 saves. The result stunned the country. Tretiak's saves and Kharlamov's goals were the story of the night. Canada had led early, so the 7-3 final score came as a real shock to fans who had expected a comfortable series, and it forced the Canadian side to rethink how it prepared, how it trained and how it used its lines for the rest of the games.
