Game 1 was played at the Montreal Forum on September 2, 1972. Canada scored twice in the first 6:32, and it looked like the Canadians were right. Then the Soviets took over, and the USSR won 7-3.

Valeri Kharlamov scored twice, and A young goaltender named Vladislav Tretiak made 29 saves. The result stunned the country. Team Canada assistant coach John Ferguson recalled that NHL players of that time never worked on their upper-body strength and seldom used off-ice training, which was not enough against the supremely fit Soviets, and the 7-3 result showed Canada that the series would be far harder than expected.
