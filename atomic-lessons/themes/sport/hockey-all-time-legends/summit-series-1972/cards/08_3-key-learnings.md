**1. Canada expected to win easily.** The first game, a 7-3 Soviet win, changed that.

**2. Esposito and Henderson led the comeback.** Esposito's Vancouver speech and Henderson's three straight winners were key.

**3. The finish was dramatic.** Canada won Game 8, 6-5, on a goal with 34 seconds left.
