In Moscow, 3,200 Canadian fans arrived to support the team. In Game 5, on September 22, Canada led 4-1 and lost 5-4. In Game 6 Canada won 3-2 on a goal by Paul Henderson. In that game Bobby Clarke slashed Kharlamov on an already sore ankle.

In Game 7, Henderson scored at 17:54 of the third period for a 4-3 Canada win, evening the series at 3-3-1. HHOF also mentions Moscow troubles: poor hotels, officiating complaints and some little-used players going home early. The team stayed together and pushed toward the finish. Moscow was the setting for the last four games, where every result mattered.
