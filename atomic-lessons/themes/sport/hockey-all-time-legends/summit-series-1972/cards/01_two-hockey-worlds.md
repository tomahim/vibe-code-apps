For years Canada's best amateur teams could win world championships and Olympic gold. By the 1960s that had changed, and in 1970 Canada withdrew from international competition after the IIHF refused to allow professional players. The Soviets had dominated world and Olympic play since 1962.

Canadian fans wanted a series that would pit their best professionals against the best the Soviets had. In September 1972 they got it. Most Canadians expected an easy win. When Canadian officials saw the Soviets' practices and equipment, talk of a one-sided series only increased. They did not expect how fit the Soviets were. The series gave both sides a chance to find out who was right.
