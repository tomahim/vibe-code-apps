Jean Beliveau was born on August 31, 1931, in Trois-Rivieres, Quebec, the first of eight children. As a boy he was an altar boy, sang in the choir and played baseball in summer. At 16 he was offered a minor-league contract to play baseball in Alabama, but his mother declined on his behalf.

His family moved to Victoriaville when he was six. He left home at 18 to play for the junior Quebec Citadels in 1949, and from 1951 to 1953 he played for the senior Quebec Aces. A center at 6 foot 3 and 205 pounds, he had a reputation as a stickhandler with a powerful shot long before he joined the Canadiens.
