After retirement Beliveau stayed with the Canadiens as an executive vice president. In that role he won seven more Stanley Cups, which means his name appears on the trophy 17 times, an unprecedented total. He had ten Cups as a player and seven as an executive.

Beliveau entered the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1972, alongside Gordie Howe. It was the first time that the usual three-year waiting period was waived, since he had retired in 1971. People remembered him not only for his play but for his ability to make anyone he met feel important, and for his quiet charity work.
