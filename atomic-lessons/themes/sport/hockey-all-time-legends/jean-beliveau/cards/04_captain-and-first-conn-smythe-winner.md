Beliveau captained the Canadiens for a decade, until his retirement. In 1965 he was awarded the first Conn Smythe Trophy as most valuable player of the Stanley Cup playoffs. The trophy was new that spring and has been given every year since.

He was also known for his calm dignity. NHL.com describes him as widely regarded as the greatest ambassador the game has known. The Canadiens remained a powerhouse through the 1960s, winning more Cups in 1965, 1966, 1968 and 1969, and Beliveau was at the center of that success. His leadership turned him into a symbol of the team.
