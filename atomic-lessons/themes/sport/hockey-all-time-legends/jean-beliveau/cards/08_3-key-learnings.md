**1. A long courtship.** The Canadiens waited until 1953 and then paid him the NHL's largest contract.

**2. Winning was constant.** Ten Cups as a player, including five straight, and the first Conn Smythe Trophy in 1965.

**3. Class mattered.** Seven more Cups as an executive and a reputation as hockey's greatest ambassador.
