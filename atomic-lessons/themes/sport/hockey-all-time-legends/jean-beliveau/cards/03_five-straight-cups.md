By 1955-56, his third full season, Beliveau was hailed by Toronto owner Conn Smythe. That same season he won both the Hart Trophy as league MVP and the Art Ross Trophy as scoring leader. Montreal began a historic run of five consecutive Stanley Cups from 1956 to 1960.

Conn Smythe wrote that Beliveau was the greatest thing that could have happened to the modern game, asking who had ever been a better stickhandler or got a shot away faster. Beliveau also won a second Hart Trophy in 1964. He appeared in 13 All-Star Games and missed the playoffs only once in his 18 seasons, in 1969-70.
