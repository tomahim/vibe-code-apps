Beliveau finished his NHL career with 507 goals and 712 assists for 1,219 points in 1,125 games, all with the Canadiens. Including playoffs, he had 586 goals and 809 assists in 1,287 games. Most of that was played in the pre-expansion NHL.

He retired on June 9, 1971, after a Stanley Cup season during which he scored his 500th goal. He said he hoped he had made a contribution to a great game. In total he won ten Cups as a player. His production helped establish the Canadiens' reputation for winning with skill and class. Those numbers rank him among the great Canadiens centres.
