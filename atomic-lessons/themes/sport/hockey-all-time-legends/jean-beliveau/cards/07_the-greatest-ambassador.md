NHL.com calls Beliveau widely regarded as the greatest ambassador the game has known. He would address you by name, ask more questions than you asked him and, years later, remember the details of your brief conversation. He lived his father's lesson that your good name is your greatest asset.

Beliveau died in 2014. NHL.com writes that his aura is still aglow around the Canadiens today. Those who met him say the same thing: he treated every person he met with real attention and respect, whether the person was a teammate, a reporter or a fan. For many people that kind of character is as much a part of his legacy as the ten Stanley Cups he won as a player and the goals that he scored for Montreal.

Source: [NHL.com, Jean Beliveau 100 Greatest](https://www.nhl.com/news/jean-beliveau-100-greatest-nhl-hockey-players-284179630).
