The Canadiens wanted Beliveau for years. He earned a princely wage in Quebec and was adored there, so general manager Frank Selke had to work hard to convince him. Beliveau finally signed on October 3, 1953, to a five-year, 105,000 dollar contract, the most generous in the NHL at the time.

Selke joked that all he did was open the Forum vault and say, Jean, take what you think is right. Rocket Richard had already said that Beliveau had the greatest shot he had ever seen. He was also acting on advice from his father, who taught him that loyalty is another form of responsibility, which stayed with him throughout life.
