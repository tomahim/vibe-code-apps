**1. A huge underdog.** Soviet full-timers faced American college players and amateurs.

**2. Preparation made the difference.** Herb Brooks built a team of mostly Minnesota players with 63 games before the Games.

**3. The win was only the semifinal.** The USA still had to beat Finland 4-2 for gold.
