On February 22, 1980, the USSR took an early lead, but the Americans tied the game at 2-2 by the end of the first period. In the second period, Tikhonov replaced his famous goalie Vladislav Tretiak with the backup, Vladimir Myshkin, to the amazement of both teams. The Soviets scored again to lead 3-2.

In the third period the Americans tied the game, and then Eruzione found himself unmarked in front of the net with about ten minutes left and scored. The USA led 4-3. Eruzione said it was a long ten minutes, and Jim Craig made 39 saves to hold the lead.
