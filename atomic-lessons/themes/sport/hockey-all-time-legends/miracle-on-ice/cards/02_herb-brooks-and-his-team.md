The American coach was Herb Brooks, an inspirational and demanding man. He told his young team they did not have the talent to beat the Soviets and that they would have to rely on discipline and team spirit instead. The team played 63 games before the Olympics, and most of its players came from the University of Minnesota.

Brooks taught his players the fluid European skating style and made them aggressive and flexible, able to play any position. Eruzione is a Boston University graduate. Brooks coached the team through the Games. Both choices turned a group of college players into a unit that could face the Soviets, and they were the base of everything that followed.
