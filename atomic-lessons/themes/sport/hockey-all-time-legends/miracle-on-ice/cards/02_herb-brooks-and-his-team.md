The American coach was Herb Brooks. He told his young team they did not have the talent to beat the Soviets and that they would have to rely on discipline and team spirit instead. The team played 63 games before the Olympics, and most of its players came from the University of Minnesota.

Olympics.com says that military-style discipline and that run of 63 games, which included a 10-3 defeat by the Soviets, forged an indomitable team out of the young players. Both choices turned a group of college players into a unit that could face the Soviets, and they were the base of everything that followed.
