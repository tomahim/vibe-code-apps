Days before the Opening Ceremony, the Soviet Union had beaten the USA 10-3 in an exhibition match at Madison Square Garden. The Americans started the Olympics badly, trailing Sweden 2-1 until Brooks pulled goalie Jim Craig for an extra attacker, and the team scored with 27 seconds left to earn a 2-2 draw.

Then came a surprise: they trounced Czechoslovakia 7-3. Victories over Norway, Romania and West Germany followed, which sent the team into the medal round with Finland, Sweden and the Soviets. The medal round was next. The loss to the Soviets in the exhibition made the later result more surprising, and the quick comeback against Sweden showed the team's resilience.
