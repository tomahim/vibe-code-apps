The USA had not yet won gold. They still had to beat Finland, and they did, 4-2, to win the Olympic gold medal. There was still one more game to play.

The Soviets finished with the silver medal. The American victory was one of the biggest upsets in Olympic history. Winning the gold completed the story and made sure the win over the USSR was remembered as a step to a title, not only as an upset. Finland lost to the Americans 4-2 in the final game, and the Soviets took the silver medal, which completed one of the most remarkable runs in Olympic hockey history.
