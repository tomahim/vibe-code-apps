The USA had not yet won gold. They still had to beat Finland, and they did, 4-2, to win the Olympic gold medal. There was still one more game to play.

The Soviets, who had been favoured in every game, finished with silver. The American victory was one of the biggest upsets in Olympic history. Winning the gold completed the story and made sure the semifinal was remembered as a step to a title, not only as an upset. The Soviets, who had been favoured in every game, took the silver medal, and Finland finished behind the Americans in that last game.
