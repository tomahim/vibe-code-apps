As the clock ran down with the Americans still leading 4-3, broadcaster Al Michaels summed it up: Do you believe in miracles? Yes. In 2016 Sports Illustrated declared the game the greatest moment in sporting history.

The win sent the American team into the gold-medal game and became a symbol of the Cold War years. The final score was 4-3. The goal by Eruzione and the saves by Craig are the two things most remembered about that game, and the call by Al Michaels is often replayed whenever the Miracle on Ice is discussed. Fans of many sports still use the phrase for any big upset.
