Richard died on May 27, 2000. Like Howie Morenz decades before him, he lay in state in a Montreal arena, and tens of thousands of fans filed past. Books, documentaries, miniseries and statues celebrate him, and a film about his life was widely praised.

Since 1999, the NHL has presented the Maurice Richard Trophy each year to the league's leading goal scorer. To many French Canadians he was far more than a gifted athlete, because he came to represent pride and resistance at a time of political need. His own view was simple: he often said he was just a hockey player.
