**1. Goals defined him.** First to score 50 goals in 50 games and the first to reach 500 career goals.

**2. Montreal won with him.** Eight Stanley Cups, including five in a row from 1956 to 1960.

**3. He was a symbol.** The 1955 riot and the trophy named for him show his meaning to Quebec.
