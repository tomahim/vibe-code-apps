Maurice Richard was the oldest of eight children, raised in the north end of Montreal during the Great Depression. As a boy he was a better baseball player than hockey player. He played his first organized hockey for a parish team and loved the game so much that he sometimes skated six days a week on a half dozen teams.

His family could not afford a ticket to the Montreal Forum, so he never saw a game there before he played in one. He joined the Canadiens in 1942, and a few broken bones slowed his early career. Nicknamed the Rocket first by teammates and then by the press, he grew into the most feared forward of his generation.
