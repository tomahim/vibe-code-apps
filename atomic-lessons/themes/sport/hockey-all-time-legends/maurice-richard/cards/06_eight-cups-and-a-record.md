Richard won the Stanley Cup eight times, including the five straight from 1956 to 1960 that remain a record for consecutive titles. He retired in 1960 as captain of the Canadiens. His younger brother Henri, nicknamed the Pocket Rocket, joined the club in 1955 and went on to win 11 Cups.

He finished with 544 regular-season goals, an NHL record at the time, which Gordie Howe passed in 1963. Fans debated for years which of the two was better. Howe was the more complete player, but if you needed a goal at a critical time, many said you wanted the Rocket on the ice.
