In the 1944-45 season Richard became the first player to score 50 goals in 50 games. That mark stood until Mike Bossy tied it in 1980-81 and Wayne Gretzky beat it with 50 in 39 games in 1981-82. He was also the first player to reach 500 career goals.

He had a spectacular night on December 28, 1944. After spending the whole day moving house, he tried to skip that evening's home game against Boston, but coach Dick Irvin convinced him to play. Richard scored five goals and added three assists in a 9-1 Canadiens win. He won the Hart Trophy in 1946-47 and led the league in goals five times.
