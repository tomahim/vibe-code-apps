On March 13, 1955, Richard punched a linesman during a melee with the Bruins. NHL president Clarence Campbell suspended him for the last three games of the regular season and the entire playoffs. The ban cost Richard his best chance at a scoring title, which he lost by one point to teammate Bernie Geoffrion.

On March 17, fans in the Montreal Forum turned on Campbell. A tear-gas canister forced the building to evacuate, Montreal forfeited the game against Detroit, and the unrest spilled into the streets. Many in Quebec saw it as a protest against the English-speaking powers of that time, and historians have connected the event to major changes in Quebec politics.
