A coach in hockey chooses lines, decides who plays against whom and sets the mood of a team. The best coaches also adapt their methods to the players they have. Three names stand out: Scotty Bowman in the NHL, Toe Blake with the Canadiens and Viktor Tikhonov with the Soviet national team.

Scotty Bowman was born in Montreal in 1933. He dreamed of playing in the NHL, but a severe head injury in junior hockey ended his playing career. He began coaching youth, junior and minor-league teams and reached the NHL in 1967 with the St. Louis Blues, an expansion team.
