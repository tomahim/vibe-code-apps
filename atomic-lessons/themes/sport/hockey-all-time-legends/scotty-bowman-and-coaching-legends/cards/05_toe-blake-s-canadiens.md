Hector Toe Blake was named Montreal's coach on June 8, 1955, replacing Dick Irvin. As a player Blake had been part of the Punch Line with Maurice Richard and Elmer Lach until a broken leg ended his career in 1948.

The Canadiens won the Cup eight times in 13 seasons under Blake, including five straight from 1956 to 1960, before he stepped down in 1968. He was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 1966 and appointed to the Order of Canada in 1982. For decades his eight Cups were the NHL coaching record, until Bowman broke it in 2002.
