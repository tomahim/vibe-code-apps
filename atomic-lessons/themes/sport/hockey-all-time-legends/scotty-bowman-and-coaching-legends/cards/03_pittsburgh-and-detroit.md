In 1990 Bowman joined the Pittsburgh Penguins' front office as director of player development, and the Penguins won the 1991 Cup. When coach Bob Johnson became ill during the 1991-92 season, Bowman took over the bench, and Pittsburgh repeated as champions in 1992.

In 1993 he joined the Detroit Red Wings. His Detroit teams reached the 1995 Cup Final and set an NHL record of 62 wins in 1995-96. In 1997 he won his 1,000th regular-season game and the Cup, becoming the only coach to win it with three teams. He won again in 1998 and 2002. Those results show how he adapted to different rosters.
