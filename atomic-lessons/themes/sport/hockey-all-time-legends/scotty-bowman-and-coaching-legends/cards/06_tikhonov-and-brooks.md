Viktor Tikhonov led the Soviet Union to Olympic gold in 1984, 1988 and 1992, and to silver in 1980. He is also remembered as the coach whose team lost the Miracle on Ice, where he replaced Tretiak after the first period against the United States.

Herb Brooks was Tikhonov's opposite in 1980. He took a team of American college players and, with discipline and a flexible European-style system, beat the Soviet team in the medal round and then won gold. Both men show that coaching can mean sharp tactical decisions and strong leadership under pressure. Their stories show two different coaching styles.
