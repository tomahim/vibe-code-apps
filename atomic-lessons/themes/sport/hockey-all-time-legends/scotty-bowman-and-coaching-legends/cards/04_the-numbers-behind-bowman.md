Bowman finished with a record 1,244 regular-season wins and 223 playoff wins over 30 seasons with St. Louis, Montreal, Buffalo, Pittsburgh and Detroit. He won the Stanley Cup nine times as a head coach, in 1973, 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979, 1992, 1997, 1998 and 2002.

Including executive roles, he is credited with 14 Stanley Cup championships. He broke Al Arbour's record for games coached, 1,606. He entered the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1991, and many see him as the single greatest coach in league history. Those totals are the NHL record for a coach, and they cover 30 seasons in the league, from 1967-68 to 2001-02, with five different teams.
