Bowman led the Blues to three Stanley Cup finals before signing with the Montreal Canadiens in 1971. He guided Montreal to five championships in 1973 and 1976 through 1979.

In 1977 he won the Jack Adams Award as the NHL's coach of the year. Later he moved to the Buffalo Sabres, first as general manager and, from 1979 to 1987, as coach. He returned to the NHL in 1990. Montreal's Cups under Bowman are among the best of any NHL coach. After Montreal he moved to Buffalo, where he worked as both general manager and coach, which gave him control over the roster as well as the bench. This move showed his wish to shape a whole organization and not only the lines, which became a pattern in his later jobs at Pittsburgh and Detroit as well.
