Vladislav Tretiak was born on April 25, 1952. He played his first hockey game at 11 and quickly drew the attention of Soviet officials. At 15 he was allowed to practice with the Central Red Army club, CSKA Moscow, and two years later he was added to the team's roster.

From 1969 to 1984 he played his entire career for CSKA. The team won 13 Soviet titles with him and 14 European Champions Cup titles, both records. He was selected as the MVP of the Soviet Championships five times. His club career shows how the Red Army system produced a superb team around a single goalie.
