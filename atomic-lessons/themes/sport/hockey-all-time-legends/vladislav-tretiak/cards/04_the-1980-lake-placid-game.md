At the 1980 Lake Placid Olympics, Tretiak was pulled from the net after the first period against the United States. Coach Viktor Tikhonov replaced him with Vladimir Myshkin, to the amazement of both teams, and the United States went on to win 4-3 in the Miracle on Ice.

Tretiak himself said afterwards that the change cost the Soviets a gold medal. The USSR did win silver, and four years later Tretiak got his third gold at the 1984 Games. The 1980 game is the event most Americans know him for. Whatever the cause, the match remains one of the most discussed moments of his career, and the later gold shows that he returned to the top of the sport.
