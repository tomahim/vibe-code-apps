**1. A career at one club.** Tretiak played for CSKA from 1969 to 1984 and won 13 Soviet titles.

**2. He was central to big events.** The 1972 Summit Series, three Olympic golds and the 1981 Canada Cup.

**3. A Hall of Fame first.** In 1989 he became the first Soviet player and the first without an NHL game to be inducted.
