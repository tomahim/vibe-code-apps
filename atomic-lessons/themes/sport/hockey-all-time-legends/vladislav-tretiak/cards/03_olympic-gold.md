Tretiak won three Olympic gold medals for the USSR, in 1972, 1976 and 1984, plus a silver in 1980. He also won ten World Championship titles. International goalies of his era rarely achieved a goals-against average as low as his 1.78.

He was named best goaltender at the 1974, 1979, 1981 and 1983 World Championships. Britannica also reports that he received three Gold Stick awards as the top European player. In 1981 the Soviets won the Canada Cup, which meant they won on Canadian ice against Canada's best players. His Olympic and World Championship record made him the leading goalie of the international game in his time.
