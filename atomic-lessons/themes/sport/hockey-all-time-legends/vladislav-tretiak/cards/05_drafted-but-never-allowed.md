In 1983 the Montreal Canadiens drafted Tretiak, but Soviet authorities never allowed him to play for them. This was the main reason he retired from hockey in 1984, at only 32.

After retiring he worked as an official with CSKA until 1990, when the Chicago Black Hawks hired him as a goalie coach. He coached some of the best goalies of the next 15 years, including Ed Belfour, Dominik Hasek, Jocelyn Thibault and Martin Brodeur. From 1998 to 2002 he also coached with the Russian national team. His coaching list shows that his influence reached the NHL through the goalies he taught, even though he never played there himself, which is an unusual path for a Hall of Famer.
