Soviet hockey changed how the world thought about the sport. It showed Canadians in 1972 that training, conditioning and teamwork could match the NHL's professionals. HHOF notes the Soviets had dominated world and Olympic play since 1962, and that they were supremely fit.

Tretiak is a link between the eras: he played in the Summit Series, coached Hasek and Brodeur, and became a Hall of Famer in North America. He remains a link between the Soviet and North American games, and a reminder of how much each side learned from the other after 1972, in both goaltending and training, over many years.

Source: [Olympics.com, Vladislav Tretyak](https://www.olympics.com/en/athletes/vladislav-tretyak).
