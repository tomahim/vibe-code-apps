Tretiak was inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1989. He was the first Soviet player so honoured and the first to be inducted without having played a game in the NHL. In 1997 he was inducted into the IIHF Hall of Fame too.

Later he moved into politics and sport administration. He was elected to the State Duma in 2003 and became president of the Russian Ice Hockey Federation in 2006. Olympics.com says Tretiak may well be the greatest goalie to ever play the game. He is a Hall of Famer in both the Canadian and the international halls.
