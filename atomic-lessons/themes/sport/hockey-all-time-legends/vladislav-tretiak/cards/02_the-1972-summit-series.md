In September 1972, the Soviet Union played Team Canada's NHL stars in the Summit Series. Tretiak, then 20, was in goal for all eight games. Most Canadians had not heard of him, but in Game 1 in Montreal he made 29 saves in a 7-3 Soviet win that shocked the country.

He kept the Soviets in the series with a magnificent performance in the 4-4 tie in Winnipeg. Canada eventually won the series 4-3-1, but Tretiak finished with a 3.87 goals-against average and became a household name. He also took part in the 1974 Summit Series. The series made him known to fans across the world.
