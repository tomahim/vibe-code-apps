The most famous feature of Sipadan is its reef wall. Because the island is the top of a sea mountain, the seabed drops away steeply right next to the shore. Sabah Tourism notes that a diver needs to walk only about 9 m on the beach before entering the water and dropping off the edge, with the wall falling to around 600 m.

A wall like this concentrates life. Deep, nutrient-rich water meets the sunlit coral at the edge, and currents sweep past carrying food for small fish, which in turn attract larger predators. The island has about twelve named dive points around it, and divers often argue about which is best, but the reason for the richness is always the same: an abrupt change from shallow reef to open deep sea.
