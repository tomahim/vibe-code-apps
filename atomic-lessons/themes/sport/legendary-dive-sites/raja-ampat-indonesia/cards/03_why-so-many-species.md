title: "Why so many species?"

No single factor explains the numbers, but some facts help. The islands are in the warm tropics, where corals grow well. The reefs include both shallow reefs and mesophotic reefs, which are deeper reefs that receive less light, so there are many kinds of habitat close together. The region also has a long and changing history of land and sea.

Archaeological evidence suggests humans first visited the islands more than 50,000 years ago. Excavations in a cave on Waigeo suggest that these early visitors were already processing plants in complex ways, including making resins from tree sap, according to researchers at the University of Oxford and the Australian National University. Land and sea have changed over time, and scientists continue to study how that history shaped the life we see today.
