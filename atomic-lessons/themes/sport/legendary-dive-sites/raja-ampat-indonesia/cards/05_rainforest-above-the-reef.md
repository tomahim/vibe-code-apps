Raja Ampat is not only about the sea. The islands are covered by rainforest that is part of the Vogelkop-Aru lowland rain forests ecoregion. The forests are the habitat of many birds, mammals, reptiles and insects, so a trip to Raja Ampat can be about the forest as well as the reef.

Two species of bird-of-paradise, the red bird-of-paradise and Wilson's bird-of-paradise, are found on Waigeo, Gam and Batanta. A palm tree called Wallaceodoxa raja-ampat is found only in the archipelago. Land and sea are linked: healthy forest holds the soil on steep slopes, keeping mud out of the water, and clear water is what lets corals get the sunlight they need.
