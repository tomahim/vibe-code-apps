In December 2018 two submarines descended into the Blue Hole to map its interior. Using sonar scanning, the team made a 3-D map of the hole. One of their discoveries was a layer of hydrogen sulfide deep in the hole.

Below that layer the water is dark, anoxic, which means without oxygen, and devoid of life. In other words, the deepest part of the hole is not a hidden reef or a lost world, it is a dark zone where animals cannot live. The same expedition also located the bodies of two divers who had gone missing, and out of respect it left them where they were. It is a reminder that this site has a serious side as well as a beautiful one.
