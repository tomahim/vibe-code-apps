**1. The shape does the work.** A triangular plateau with steep walls funnels current and food, so reef sharks and big schools gather.

**2. Currents set the rules.** Tide decides when sites like Blue Corner and Peleliu Corner are sensible to dive, which is why local guides matter.

**3. Policy followed the sharks.** Palau declared the first shark sanctuary in 2009 and a 502,539 km² marine sanctuary in 2015.
