In 2015 Palau went further and created the Palau National Marine Sanctuary, under President Thomas Remengesau Jr. It covers about 502,539 square kilometres, and it is one of the largest protected areas in the world. All extractive activities, including commercial fishing and mining, are prohibited in 80% of the area, while domestic fisheries are permitted in the remaining 20%.

The no-take rule came into effect in 2020 after a five-year phase-out plan. The sanctuary has been described at the United Nations Ocean Conference as one of the most ambitious ocean conservation initiatives in the world. Protection is a continuing decision, not a single event.
