Palau has another famous current dive at the southern tip of Peleliu island, called Peleliu Corner or Peleliu Express. Here the reef runs out of island and the open Pacific takes over. Dive operators describe it as the most demanding drift dive in Palau.

When the tide is right, divers hook onto the reef edge and watch grey reef sharks, barracuda and trevally hang in a river of moving water. When the tide is wrong, nobody dives it at all. The key idea is that for these sites the conditions decide whether a visit is sensible or not, and local knowledge of tide and current is what turns a dangerous place into a managed one. It is a good example of why famous sites are usually dived with local experts.
