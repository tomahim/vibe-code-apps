**1. A merchant ship, not a warship.** The Thistlegorm carried supplies for Allied forces in Egypt and was sunk by German bombers on 6 October 1941.

**2. The cargo is the treasure.** Trucks, motorcycles, boots and two steam locomotives are still where the ship lost them, at about 30 m.

**3. It is fragile and a war grave.** Rust and mooring damage are destroying it, and divers are asked to look without taking anything.
