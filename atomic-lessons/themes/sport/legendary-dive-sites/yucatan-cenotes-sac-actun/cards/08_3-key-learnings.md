**1. Rain carved the caves.** Slightly acidic rain dissolved Yucatán's limestone into tunnels, and collapsed roofs became cenotes.

**2. Fresh water floats on salt water.** The halocline blurs visibility and changes buoyancy where the layers meet.

**3. The caves hold history.** Sac Actun is among the longest known underwater cave systems, and Hoyo Negro preserved Naia, about 12,000 years old.
