**Monkey**
> *Singe*
Say it: **The monkey chatters.**
> *Le singe bavarde.*

**Turkey**
> *Dindon*
Say it: **The turkey gobbles.**
> *Le dindon glougloute.*

**Pigeon**
> *Pigeon*
Say it: **The pigeon coos.**
> *Le pigeon roucoule.*

**Whale**
> *Baleine*
Say it: **The whale sings.**
> *La baleine chante.*
