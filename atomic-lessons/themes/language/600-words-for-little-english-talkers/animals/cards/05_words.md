**Bee**
> *Abeille*
Say it: **The bee buzzes.**
> *L'abeille bourdonne.*

**Wolf**
> *Loup*
Say it: **The wolf howls.**
> *Le loup hurle.*

**Snake**
> *Serpent*
Say it: **The snake hisses.**
> *Le serpent siffle.*

**Bear**
> *Ours*
Say it: **The bear growls.**
> *L'ours gronde.*
