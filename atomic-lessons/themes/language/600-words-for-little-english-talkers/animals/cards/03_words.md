**Duck**
> *Canard*
Say it: **The duck quacks.**
> *Le canard cancane.*

**Frog**
> *Grenouille*
Say it: **The frog croaks.**
> *La grenouille coasse.*

**Cow**
> *Vache*
Say it: **The cow moos.**
> *La vache meugle.*

**Sheep**
> *Mouton*
Say it: **The sheep baas.**
> *Le mouton bêle.*
