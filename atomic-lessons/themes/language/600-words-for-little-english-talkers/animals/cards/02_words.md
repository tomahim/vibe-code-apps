**Turtle**
> *Tortue*
Say it: **The turtle glides quietly.**
> *La tortue glisse silencieusement.*

**Dog**
> *Chien*
Say it: **The dog barks.**
> *Le chien aboie.*

**Cat**
> *Chat*
Say it: **The cat meows.**
> *Le chat miaule.*

**Lion**
> *Lion*
Say it: **The lion roars.**
> *Le lion rugit.*
