The cheapest refrigeration is the kind you switch off. Whenever the outside air or water is cool enough, economizers bypass the chillers: air-side economizers filter and duct cool outside air straight into the halls; water-side economizers run the tower water through a heat exchanger to chill the loop without compressors. In cool climates, these modes can cover most hours of the year, which is a major reason the industry builds in Oregon, Ireland and Scandinavia.

Free cooling is the payoff of the temperature discipline from lesson 12. Every degree of higher setpoint adds hours per year when outside conditions suffice, and each such hour saves the chiller's compressor energy, the single largest overhead load. This is the deep connection between the ASHRAE envelope, the containment architecture and the energy bill: the whole facility is designed so that, as often as physics allows, the atmosphere cools the data center directly, and the megawatt machinery of refrigeration sits silent.
