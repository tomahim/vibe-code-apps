**1. Liquid beats air by orders of magnitude as a heat carrier**, and the GB200 generation made direct-to-chip liquid cooling a requirement, not an option.

**2. The stack is cold plate, manifold, CDU, facility loop:** plates capture heat at the silicon, the CDU keeps the IT coolant clean, pressurized and above the dew point, and warmer facility water can increase chiller-free operating hours when climate and equipment limits permit.

**3. Liquid enables density and heat reuse.** It is the technology behind the 120 kW rack, and its warm return water is potentially useful input for heat recovery where temperature, demand and distance fit.
