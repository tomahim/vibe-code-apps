title: 3 key learnings

---

**1. Liquid beats air by orders of magnitude as a heat carrier**, and the GB200 generation made direct-to-chip liquid cooling a requirement, not an option.

**2. The stack is cold plate, manifold, CDU, facility loop:** plates capture heat at the silicon, the CDU keeps the IT coolant clean, pressurized and above the dew point, and warm facility water lets towers or dry coolers work without chillers.

**3. Liquid enables density and heat reuse.** It is the technology behind the 120 kW rack, and its warm return water is the best feedstock yet for waste-heat recovery.
