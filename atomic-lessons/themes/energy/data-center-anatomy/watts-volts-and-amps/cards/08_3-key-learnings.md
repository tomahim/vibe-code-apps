**1. Three quantities describe electricity.** Voltage is the pressure (volts), current is the flow (amps), and DC power is their product (watts = volts x amps); AC requires power factor and phase context.

**2. High voltage exists to beat the I-squared-R loss.** Transmitting power at high voltage keeps current, and therefore heat losses, low; the voltage is stepped down near the equipment.

**3. Capacity is power, consumption is energy.** Data centers are sized in MW of critical IT power and billed in kWh; global data center demand was estimated at 415 TWh in 2024; 945 TWh in 2030 is the IEA base-case projection, not a certainty.
