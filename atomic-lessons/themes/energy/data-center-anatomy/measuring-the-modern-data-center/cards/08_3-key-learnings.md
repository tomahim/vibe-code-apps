**1. PUE grades the machine: total energy over IT energy.** The world averages about 1.54; the best hyperscalers run near 1.1, and the gap is measured in terawatt-hours.

**2. Tiers rate the uptime promise**, from Tier I's single path to Tier IV's fault tolerance; cloud 'nines' are service-level promises layered on top of many buildings.

**3. The data center is two rivers: electricity down to the chip, heat up to the sky.** The gigawatt era scales those rivers until they reshape grids, supply chains and energy policy.
