Power usage effectiveness (PUE) is the metric that grades everything built in this course. Defined by The Green Grid, it is total facility energy divided by IT energy. A perfect, impossible facility scores 1.0: every watt reaches the servers. Every real facility scores higher, and the excess is the cooling, conversion losses, lighting and overhead from lessons 7 through 14.

The numbers tell the industry's story. Uptime Institute's 2025 survey puts the global average around 1.54, roughly flat for six years, with newer large facilities near 1.44 and the best hyperscale builds at 1.2 and below; Google's fleet reports around 1.1. At 415 TWh of global data center consumption, the gap between 1.54 and 1.2 is not accounting: it is tens of terawatt-hours, the consumption of whole countries, decided entirely by the design choices of the last eight lessons. PUE is where engineering becomes ethics.
