Facing that demand, the industry pushes on every lever this course has covered. On power: fewer conversion stages, high-efficiency UPS modes, rack-level DC distribution. On cooling: containment, warm setpoints, free-cooling hours, liquid loops running warm enough to skip chillers. On utilization: schedulers that keep expensive GPUs busy, because reserved capacity does not prove the GPUs are doing useful work.

Water and carbon add the newer dimensions. Operators publish WUE alongside PUE, sign renewable power purchase agreements to match consumption, experiment with batteries and generators as grid assets, and design for heat reuse where cities can take it. The honest summary is SemiAnalysis's definition once more: deliver power to IT equipment efficiently, safely, at the lowest total cost. In the gigawatt era that sentence has planetary weight, and the difference between a 1.5 and a 1.2 is measured in power plants.
