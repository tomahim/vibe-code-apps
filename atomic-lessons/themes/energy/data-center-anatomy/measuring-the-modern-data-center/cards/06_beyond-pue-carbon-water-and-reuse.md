PUE measures energy overhead, but the modern scorecard has more columns. WUE tracks water per unit of IT energy. CUE, carbon usage effectiveness, relates total carbon emissions to IT energy, and operators increasingly report the carbon-free energy share of their consumption, hour by hour, because buying annual renewable certificates is no longer considered the same as running clean around the clock.

The frontier metric is usefulness per watt. A facility at PUE 1.2 running GPUs at 85% utilization delivers far more computation per kilowatt-hour than a PUE 1.1 site running idle, which is why scheduling and utilization have joined cooling and conversion as first-class efficiency levers. And heat reuse, where it works, reframes the ledger entirely: energy that warms a district is energy used twice. The direction of the industry is toward reporting not just how little it wastes, but how much each watt accomplishes, a standard every lesson in this course has quietly been building toward.
