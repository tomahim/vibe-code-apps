**1. Transformers trade voltage for current through magnetism**, and the grid runs on AC precisely so they can do it; data centers step voltage down at every boundary from substation to data hall.

**2. MVA is apparent power, MW is real power;** equipment must be sized on apparent power, and modern IT loads keep the power factor near 1.

**3. Redundancy is bought in units: N+1 adds one spare, 2N doubles the system**; and in the AI era, transformer lead times have become a critical constraint on construction.
