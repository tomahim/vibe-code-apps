From this lesson's vantage point, the top of the power chain is complete. The utility delivers high or medium voltage. Transformers step it down in stages, sized in MVA with N+1 or better redundancy. Switchgear routes and protects at every node, isolating faults and enabling safe maintenance. Medium voltage flows to transformers near each data hall, which deliver low voltage to the equipment inside.

What remains is the continuity machinery: what happens when the grid itself fails. That answer has two halves, one fast and one slow. The fast half is the UPS with its batteries, responding in milliseconds. The slow half is the diesel generator, arriving within a minute and capable of running for days. SemiAnalysis describes exactly this pairing, with the automatic transfer switch in between deciding where power comes from. The next two lessons take them in turn.
