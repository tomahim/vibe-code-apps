Everything so far happened upstream of the data hall. Within the building, the medium-voltage feed meets its final transformations: transformers step down to low voltage, the automatic transfer switch stands ready to move the load to generators, the UPS bridges every blip, and distribution gear carries power the last meters to the racks. The next four lessons take those one at a time.

The design intent of the whole chain is worth stating once, because SemiAnalysis states it plainly: deliver power to IT equipment efficiently, safely, and at the lowest total cost over the hardware's life, with enough redundancy that routine failures and maintenance never interrupt computing. Every component we are about to meet, from the transformer yard to the rack's power strips, is an answer to one part of that sentence. The chain is long because the promise is strong: the servers must never, ever see the lights flicker.
