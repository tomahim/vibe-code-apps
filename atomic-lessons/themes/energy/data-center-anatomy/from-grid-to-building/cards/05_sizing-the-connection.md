How big a grid connection does a data center need? Start from critical IT power, the maximum draw of the IT equipment, then add the overhead: cooling, lighting, losses and auxiliary systems, and finally divide by the expected utilization. SemiAnalysis notes typical power utilization of 50-60% for cloud workloads and above 80% for AI training, which cuts both ways: AI campuses use almost all the capacity they buy, while enterprise sites often run far below theirs.

The connection itself is then engineered with margin and redundancy, often with two feeds from the grid where geography allows. Capacity is contracted from the utility years ahead, which is why headlines track gigawatts of future commitments. A gigawatt of contracted capacity is, in effect, a promise by a grid to a data center: that much power, that reliably, starting on that date. In the AI era those promises have become some of the most contested pieces of infrastructure planning on Earth.
