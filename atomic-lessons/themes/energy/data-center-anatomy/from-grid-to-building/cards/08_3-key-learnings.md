title: 3 key learnings
archived: false
---

**1. Power travels far at high voltage to beat I-squared-R losses**, then steps down through a cascade of transformations that ends at about 1 V in the chip.

**2. Hyperscale campuses need transmission-level connections and their own substations**; access to power now shapes where data centers are built and how fast they can grow.

**3. Capacity is contracted in megawatts years ahead.** A 300 MW campus rivals a mid-sized city's consumption, which makes data centers a serious actor in energy planning.
