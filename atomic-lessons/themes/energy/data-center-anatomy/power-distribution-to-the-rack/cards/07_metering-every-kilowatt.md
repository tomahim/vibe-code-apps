A theme running through the distribution chain is measurement. Modern PDUs, busway tap-offs and rack strips are instrumented: they report volts, amps, watts and cumulative energy per circuit or per outlet, streaming the data into the facility's monitoring systems. The data hall is one of the most metered environments on Earth.

The uses are immediate. Operators balance loads across phases so no cable runs hot while its neighbor idles. Capacity planners see which rows have headroom for the next deployment. Colocation providers bill customers against committed and consumed kW, and alert them before a breaker trips. And the efficiency program closes its loop: the PUE of lesson 15 is computed from exactly these meters, total facility energy at the intake divided by the sum of all these IT measurements. You cannot improve what you do not measure, and in a data center, everything is measured.
