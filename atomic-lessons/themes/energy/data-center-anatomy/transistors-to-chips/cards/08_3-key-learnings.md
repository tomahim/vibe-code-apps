title: 3 key learnings

**1. Computation turns electricity into heat, watt for watt.** Billions of transistors switching billions of times per second make the chip the hottest point of the facility.

**2. Free scaling is over.** Since Dennard scaling broke down, some recent accelerators raise maximum power per chip: 700 W for an H100, over a kilowatt for Grace-Blackwell modules.

**3. Chip power sets the scale of everything else.** Racks, power distribution and cooling are sized from the complete IT system and its expected peak load, which is why AI forced a redesign of the whole data center.
