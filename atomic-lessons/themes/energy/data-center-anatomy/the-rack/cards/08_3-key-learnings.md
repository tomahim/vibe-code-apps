**1. A 19-inch-mounting, 42U rack is a common unit of IT capacity**, with its own power budget, network aggregation and place in the cooling design.

**2. Density is the dial that drives design.** Racks went from 3-5 kW to 10-30 kW commonly and about 120 kW for AI systems like the GB200 NVL72, forcing new power and cooling approaches.

**3. A dual-fed rack straddles two power paths.** A side and B side, with dual-corded equipment, deliver the concurrent maintainability that Tier III facilities promise.
