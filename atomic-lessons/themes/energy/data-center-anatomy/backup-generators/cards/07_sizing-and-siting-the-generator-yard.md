title: Sizing and siting the generator yard
archived: false
---

Generator plants are sized against the whole facility load, not just IT: chillers, pumps and the UPS recharge current all demand power during an outage, so the yard must carry the building at full tilt, typically with N+1 units on top. A 20 MW facility might field a dozen or more 2 MW gensets, electrically paralleled so they share the load like a small power station.

Siting is its own discipline. Generators need combustion air, exhaust paths, cooling airflow and fuel piping, and they produce noise and emissions that regulators and neighbors care about, which is why yards sit outdoors or in purpose-built rooms with enormous louvers. Cold climates add block heaters and winter-grade fuel; hot climates derate the engines' output. And because the generators are the last line of defense, their supporting systems get the same paranoia as everything else in this course: dual starters, dual batteries, dual fuel pumps. When everything else has failed, nothing here may fail.
