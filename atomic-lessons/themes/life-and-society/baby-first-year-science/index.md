Twelve sourced lessons on how babies move, sense and communicate in their first year, and how science studies all of it. Organized by subtheme, with developmental ranges instead of rigid monthly deadlines. Built from the [CDC developmental milestones](https://www.cdc.gov/ncbddd/actearly/milestones/), the [WHO motor development windows](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards/motor-development-milestones), and landmark infancy studies.
