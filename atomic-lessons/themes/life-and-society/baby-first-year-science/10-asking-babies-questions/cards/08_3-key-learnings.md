**1. Baby science runs on proxies.** Looking time, sucking rate, head-turns and heart rate turn infant behavior into answers.

**2. Boredom and surprise are instruments.** Habituation reveals discrimination; violation-of-expectation probes what babies expect.

**3. Replication is the referee.** Consortia like ManyBabies re-test classic claims with big pre-registered samples; converging results earn confidence.
