title: "3 key learnings"
archived: false
---
**1. Touch comes first.** It develops earliest and does regulatory work: skin-to-skin contact stabilizes newborn physiology and reduces pain responses.

**2. Flavor learning starts before birth.** Amniotic fluid and breast milk carry the mother's diet, shaping later food acceptance.

**3. Smell is social ID.** Newborns recognize their mother's odor within days, and familiar scent calms them.
