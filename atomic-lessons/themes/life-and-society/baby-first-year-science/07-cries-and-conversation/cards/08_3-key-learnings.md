title: "3 key learnings"

**1. Crying is a signal with a curve.** Normal crying peaks around 6-8 weeks and declines by 3-4 months; the peak is development, not failure.

**2. Conversation precedes speech.** Cooing, smiles, turn-taking and gesture rehearse dialogue before any word exists.

**3. Comprehension leads production.** One-year-olds understand far more than they can say; talking to babies feeds a system already listening.
