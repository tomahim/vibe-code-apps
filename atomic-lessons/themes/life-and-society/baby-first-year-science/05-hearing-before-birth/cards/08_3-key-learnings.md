title: "3 key learnings"

**1. Hearing starts before birth.** From the third trimester, fetuses hear, and newborns prefer their mother's voice and native language rhythms.

**2. Baby talk is functional.** Infant-directed speech carries emotion and makes speech sounds easier to learn.

**3. Perception tunes to experience.** Perceptual narrowing in the second half of the first year specializes the brain for the language it hears.
