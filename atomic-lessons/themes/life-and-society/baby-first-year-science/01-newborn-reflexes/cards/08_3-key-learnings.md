**1. Newborns arrive pre-wired.** Reflexes like rooting, grasping and Moro are automatic circuits that support feeding and protection from day one.

**2. Reflexes fade as control grows.** Most early reflexes integrate into voluntary behavior over roughly the first six months as the cortex matures.

**3. Reflexes inform, they do not diagnose.** Clinicians read presence, strength and symmetry alongside the whole child; a single observation settles nothing.
