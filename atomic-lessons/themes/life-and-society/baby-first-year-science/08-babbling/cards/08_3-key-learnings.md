**1. Canonical babbling arrives on schedule.** Well-formed syllables like baba emerge around 6-9 months as the vocal tract becomes a speech instrument.

**2. Babbling is linguistic practice.** Deaf infants exposed to sign babble with their hands; the brain rehearses language units in any channel.

**3. Babble drifts toward home.** By late in the first year, babbling carries the melody and sounds of the surrounding language, and it predicts early words.
