**1. First words come around one year, with wide range.** A word is a stable sound-meaning pairing; the CDC line for one or two words is set conservatively at 15 months.

**2. Children fast-map.** From a single exposure they sketch a word's meaning and refine it, which powers the 18-month vocabulary explosion.

**3. Responsive talk feeds words.** Quantity matters less than quality: turns, expansions and shared books beat background noise.
