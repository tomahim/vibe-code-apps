**1. Vision starts blurry and close-range.** Newborn acuity is around 20/400, improving steeply across the first year.

**2. Faces and contrast lead.** Preferential looking to high contrast and face-like patterns appears from birth and sharpens into face expertise.

**3. Babies steer their own seeing.** Looking choices, depth perception around 3-4 months, and joint attention build vision through experience.
