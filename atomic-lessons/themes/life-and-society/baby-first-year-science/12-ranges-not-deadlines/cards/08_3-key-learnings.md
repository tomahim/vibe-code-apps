**1. Development has architecture, not a timetable.** Sequences are orderly; timing spans months of healthy variation.

**2. Know what the tools are.** CDC checklists are surveillance conversation-starters at a 75% line; WHO windows describe the full normal spread; neither diagnoses.

**3. Watch trajectories, not points.** Steady skill-building across domains is the healthy pattern; losing an established skill is the signal to seek evaluation.
