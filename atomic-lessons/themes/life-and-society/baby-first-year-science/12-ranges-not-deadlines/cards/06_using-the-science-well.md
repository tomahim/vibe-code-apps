What does a scientifically literate caregiver or reader do with all this? First, supply the inputs the system is built to expect: floor time for movement, responsive conversation for language, varied flavors for the budding eater, shared attention for everything. None of these accelerates a child beyond their range; they simply give the self-assembling system its raw material.

Second, hold milestones as questions, not verdicts. A checklist line crossed late is a prompt for a conversation with someone qualified, and a line crossed early is no scholarship. The WHO windows exist to say that normal is a span nearly a year wide for the skill parents watch most.

Third, enjoy the vantage point. The first year is the fastest construction project in a human life: reflexes integrating, senses calibrating, a language being absorbed from the air. Knowing the science does not add worry - it replaces worry with wonder, which is where this course ends.
