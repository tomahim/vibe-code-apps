**1. Maturation describes, practice explains.** Thelen's stepping experiments showed skills emerge from the whole body-environment system, not a fixed genetic clock.

**2. Practice is massive and posture-specific.** New walkers take thousands of steps and dozens of falls daily, and each posture must be calibrated separately.

**3. Experience moves the schedule.** Tummy time, floor play and cultural handling practices shift timing within wide healthy ranges; milestones reflect what babies do all day.
