title: "3 key learnings"

**1. The first-year brain roughly doubles.** Overproduction then pruning sculpt circuits by use; experience selects what survives.

**2. Each window sees differently.** EEG gives timing, fNIRS gives awake cortical location, MRI gives anatomy - mostly from sleeping babies.

**3. Sensitive periods are windows, not cliffs.** Vision has true deadlines; most systems merely learn best early. Plasticity in the first year is real but not limitless.
