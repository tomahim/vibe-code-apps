**1. Development runs head to toe, center to edge.** Babies control the head first and the fingertips last, which shapes the classic motor sequence.

**2. Normal spans months, not weeks.** WHO windows put walking alone anywhere from 8.2 to 17.6 months, and some healthy babies skip crawling entirely.

**3. Milestones are maps, not deadlines.** CDC checklists mark what at least 75% of children do by an age; they start conversations, not diagnoses.
