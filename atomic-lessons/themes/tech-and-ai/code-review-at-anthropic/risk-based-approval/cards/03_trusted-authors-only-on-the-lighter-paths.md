title: Trusted authors only on the lighter paths
archived: false
---
The zero- and one-person paths are not open to everyone. Only authors on the repository's trusted list, mostly the team that owns it, get the lighter paths. Everyone else needs a second person to approve.

This is how the system stays safe as it loosens. The lighter paths are earned through context: trusted authors know the conventions, feel the blast radius of their area, and carry the pager when it rings. An outside contributor, however skilled, has not accumulated that context, so their changes get more eyes. The design generalizes a pattern from open source, where maintainers merge their own typo fixes casually but review external patches carefully. The classifier automates the risk side; the trusted list keeps the accountability side human.
