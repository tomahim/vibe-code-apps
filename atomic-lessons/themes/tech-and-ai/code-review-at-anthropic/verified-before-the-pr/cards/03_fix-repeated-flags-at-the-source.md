When the automated reviewer keeps flagging the same thing, the team does not just fix that instance. They fix the source: in the skill that guides the agent, with a new lint rule, or with updates to the instructional files the agents read.

This turns review feedback into infrastructure. A comment that an engineer would otherwise leave for the tenth time becomes a rule that prevents the eleventh occurrence. The same instinct appears everywhere in their process: repeated human effort is a bug in the system, not a fact of life. Two instructional files carry most of this load, CLAUDE.md and REVIEW.md, and the next two cards cover what each one says and why their design matters for review quality.
