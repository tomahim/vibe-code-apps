title: From a gate to a spellcheck

---
Before this shift, review was a gate at the end of the road: write the code, open a pull request, and wait for a colleague to find time. The gate model assumes the author is a person who wrote every line and can explain each choice. It also makes the reviewer a bottleneck, because nothing merges until someone reads it.

Now review runs continuously, more like spellcheck while you write. Most changes have been reviewed and fixed by Claude at least once before a human is asked to look. This catches most routine mistakes at the moment they are made, which is when they are cheapest to fix. Team attention is saved for higher-level questions and scrutiny: whether the change should exist at all, whether its approach fits the system, and whether the evidence of testing is real.
