From June to September, merged pull requests to Claude Code's codebase more than doubled. Over the same period, the share of those changes that had to be reverted fell by more than 40 percent. Shipping more than twice as much while breaking less is the opposite of what most teams expect from loosening human review.

Those two numbers frame the whole approach. The goal is explicitly not zero process; it is the minimum human review time that does not raise reverts or incidents. Every mechanism in the lessons ahead, from pre-PR verification to risk-tiered approvals to the incident eval set, exists to push human time down while holding the revert line. When a new idea cannot show it protects that line, the team treats the saved minutes as an illusion rather than a win.
