title: The one resource that does not scale

When agents write most of the code, compute and even reviewer agents scale on demand. Human time and attention do not. That makes them the scarce resource the entire review system should be built around.

The team's stated goal is precise: most pull requests in the Claude Code repositories, covering the CLI, the desktop and web apps, and the mobile apps, should merge with at most one human in the loop, and that human can be the author. Crucially, this comes with a condition: no increase in reverts or incidents. Even at the current rate of change, the team stresses there is still an incredible amount of work for humans to do, and new kinds of challenges show up almost daily. The point is to spend that limited attention where it matters, not to pretend it is unnecessary.
