Alongside the rules, the team changed what they measure. They now track rounds-to-pass alongside recall, so a reviewer change that catches one more bug by adding two more rounds shows up as a trade, not a free win.

This is the same guardrail thinking from the lesson on human time, applied to machines. Recall without a cost metric invites a reviewer that never stops. Rounds-to-pass without recall invites one that rubber-stamps. Holding both numbers visible keeps the reviewer honest in both directions, and it gives the convergence rules a scoreboard: if round caps and diff-only re-review were hurting bug-catching, the recall side of the scorecard would say so.
