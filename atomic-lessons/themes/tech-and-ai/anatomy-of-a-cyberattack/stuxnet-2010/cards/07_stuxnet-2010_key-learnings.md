title: "3 key learnings"

1. An air gap is not a guarantee. Isolated networks are reached through USB drives, contractors and laptops, so isolation needs controls on media and maintenance access.

2. Protect signing keys and trust anchors. Stolen certificates made malicious drivers look legitimate, so guard the secrets that prove software is genuine.

3. Verify physical reality, not only dashboards. Independent sensors and integrity checks help detect a system that reports normal values while behaving abnormally.
Keep fact and report apart: the code's behaviour is documented, while authorship is based on media reporting and has never been officially confirmed.
