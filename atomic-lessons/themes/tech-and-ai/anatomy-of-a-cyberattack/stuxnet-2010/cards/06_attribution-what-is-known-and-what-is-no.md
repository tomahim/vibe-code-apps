No government has officially claimed Stuxnet. A 2012 New York Times report said it was part of a US and Israeli operation, citing officials. Neither government has openly admitted responsibility. Those are credible reports, but they are not an official confirmation or a public technical attribution.

Symantec's technical report describes how the code works but does not name a creator. A careful reader should therefore separate the three layers: what the code did, which is well documented; who was probably behind it, which is reported by media; and what exactly was achieved at Natanz, which is estimated. Stuxnet also inspired later industrial-control attacks, which is why the field now takes control-system security seriously. Source: [Symantec W32.Stuxnet Dossier](https://docs.broadcom.com/docs/security-response-w32-stuxnet-dossier-11-en).
