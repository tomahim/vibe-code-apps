The US Office of Personnel Management, OPM, handles personnel records for the federal government and runs background investigations for security clearances. Those investigation records hold deep personal detail, which makes few datasets more valuable to an intelligence service.

In 2015 OPM disclosed two linked breaches. OPM said one incident involved personnel data of 4.2 million current and former federal employees. It later concluded with high confidence that Social Security numbers of 21.5 million people were stolen from the background investigation databases: 19.7 million applicants and 1.8 million non-applicants. About 5.6 million records included fingerprints. Source: [OPM, Cybersecurity Incidents](https://web.archive.org/web/20230516032003/https:/www.opm.gov/cybersecurity/cybersecurity-incidents/).
