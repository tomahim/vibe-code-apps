The CRS brief records that, amid criticism of how the agency managed its response and secured its systems, OPM's director, Katherine Archuleta, stepped down. The House committee also found that OPM misled the public on the extent of the damage and made false statements to Congress. Those are the committee's findings.

This is a lesson in communication. In a breach, early estimates are often wrong, and stating uncertainty plainly is better than giving a confident number that later has to be revised upward. OPM's figures grew from 4.2 million personnel records to 21.5 million Social Security numbers in the background investigation databases. Trust is harder to restore when the public feels they were told less than the truth.
