1. Act on audit findings. The OPM weaknesses were known for years, and a risk list without owners and deadlines does not protect anyone.

2. Modernise and apply zero trust. Old systems are hard to protect, and checking every request limits the harm when one account is stolen.

3. Concentrated sensitive data raises the stakes. Minimise what you keep, such as Social Security numbers, so one intrusion does not expose a generation of records.
Attribution to China was cited by some but not set out with full public evidence.
