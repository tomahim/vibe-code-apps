The House committee's report refers to a foreign adversary. The CRS brief says that many have speculated that the OPM data were taken for espionage rather than for criminal purposes, and that some have cited China as the source of the breaches. It adds that it was unclear how the data might be used.

So the public documents cited here do not set out the full evidence behind an attribution to China. Students should treat it as an assessment cited by some officials and reports, not as a court finding. The core engineering lessons are independent of attribution: the weaknesses were documented and fixable. Source: [CRS, Cyber Intrusion into U.S. Office of Personnel Management](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R44111/R44111.4.pdf).
