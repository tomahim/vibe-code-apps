The committee summarised the failures as leadership, technology and process. Leaders did not prioritise cybersecurity or fix known issues, even after the Inspector General's warnings. Its recommendations point to old technology that needed modernising, zero trust, fewer Social Security numbers and empowered chief information officers.

A further problem was concentration. Putting decades of sensitive records in a few systems meant that a single successful intrusion produced a huge result. Zero trust is an approach in which no user or device is trusted just because it is inside the network, and every request is checked. Reducing the use of Social Security numbers across agencies would reduce the value of such a stolen dataset. The public summaries do not give a complete technical account, so detailed claims beyond these should be checked against the full report.
