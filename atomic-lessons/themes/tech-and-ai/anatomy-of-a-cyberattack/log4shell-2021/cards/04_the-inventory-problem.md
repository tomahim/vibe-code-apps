The hardest part was knowing where Log4j was running. It might be in a software product, inside another library, or in a vendor's service, and companies had to ask their suppliers whether they were affected. Many teams had to search their environments and wait for vendor patches.

This is the problem that a software bill of materials, a list of the components in each product, is meant to solve. If an organisation has up-to-date lists, it can search them for a vulnerable component much faster. The incident became a standard argument for dependency tracking and for scanning tools that identify known vulnerable libraries.
