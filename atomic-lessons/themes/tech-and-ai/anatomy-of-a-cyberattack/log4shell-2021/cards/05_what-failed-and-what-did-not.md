Log4Shell is not a single attack on one victim. It is a vulnerability that many attackers used in many places, so it is different from the other cases in this course. Its lessons are about structure. The Cyber Safety Review Board noted that the volunteer-led open-source community is thinly resourced, and that organisations struggled to know which components they ran.

The board recommended better inventory and vulnerability response. Layers of defence, such as limiting what servers can reach, are a sensible lesson in general, though the sources cited here do not measure their effect on Log4Shell.

None of this is a verdict on open-source software, which is widely used because it works. The point is that heavy use creates shared responsibility, and organisations that depend on a component should understand it, track it and, where possible, support it.
