The Cyber Safety Review Board's July 2022 summary called Log4j an endemic vulnerability that may last a decade or longer. It recommended better software inventory, support for open-source maintainers and stronger vulnerability response practices.

The review's recommendations are aimed at both industry and government, which fits the problem: no single party owns the whole software supply chain, so improvement depends on many parties doing small, steady work on inventory and patching.

No single government or group was officially named as responsible for Log4Shell. It is a flaw, not an operation, and CISA described active scanning by sophisticated actors without a single culprit. Careful learners should avoid stories that give a single culprit. Sources: [CISA guidance AA21-356A](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa21-356a) and [CSRB summary](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/CSRB-Log4J-Key-Findings-and-Recommendations-Summary-508c.pdf).
