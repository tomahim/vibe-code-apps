The Senate report says that, according to reports, the attackers stole credentials from a refrigeration and heating contractor, Fazio Mechanical Services, at least two months before the breach, using emails infected with malware. The contractor had remote access to Target's network for billing, contract submission and project management. Contractors often need remote access for billing or monitoring, and their security may be much weaker than the customer's.

The report notes that the outside portal was likely not fully isolated from the rest of Target's network. The credentials were meant for a limited business purpose, but the attackers were able to move on from there. The point for defenders is that your security includes everyone with a login, because attackers choose the easiest door, not the most direct one.
