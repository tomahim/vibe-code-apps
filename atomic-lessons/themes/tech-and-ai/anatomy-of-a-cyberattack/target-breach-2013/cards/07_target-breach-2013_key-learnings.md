title: "3 key learnings"
archived: false
---
1. Your suppliers are part of your attack surface. Limit what a vendor account can reach, require strong authentication and monitor it.

2. Segment the network. A breach in one area should not give a path to payment systems, so separate them and restrict traffic between them.

3. An alert needs an owner and a response plan. Detection tools are only useful when someone is empowered to act quickly.
Separate what is documented, such as the staff report, from what is only reported.
