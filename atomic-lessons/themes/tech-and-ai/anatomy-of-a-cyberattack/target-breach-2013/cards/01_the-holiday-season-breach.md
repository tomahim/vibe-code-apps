From 27 November to mid-December 2013, during the busiest shopping period of the year, criminals captured payment card data from checkout systems in Target stores across the United States. On 19 December 2013 Target confirmed that about 40 million payment card accounts were exposed, and the Senate report says personal information of as many as 110 million customers was taken.

A US Senate Commerce Committee staff report released in March 2014, based on media reports and expert analyses, described the event as a series of missed chances, using the kill chain model, in which an attack is broken into stages from entry to data theft. Each stage is an opportunity to detect or stop the attacker, which makes the case a good teaching example of layered defence.
