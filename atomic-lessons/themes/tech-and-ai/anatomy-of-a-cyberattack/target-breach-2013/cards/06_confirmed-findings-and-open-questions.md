Some details come from Target's statements and the Senate staff report, which is a majority staff analysis and not a court finding. The account of how the vendor was compromised comes largely from press reports and security firms cited in that report, and Target has not published a full forensic report. The report says a version of BlackPOS malware, sold on criminal forums, was reportedly installed on the terminals and that data went to a server in Eastern Europe, but it does not identify the individuals responsible.

When you study an incident, mark each claim with its source type: company statement, government report, court record or press account. The Target story is well documented in its main outline and less certain in some details. Source: [Senate Commerce staff report, A Kill Chain Analysis of the 2013 Target Data Breach](https://www.commerce.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/media/doc/2014%200325%20Target%20Kill%20Chain%20Analysis.pdf).
