A joint statement in January 2021 said the actor was likely Russian in origin. On 15 April 2021 a White House fact sheet formally named the Russian Foreign Intelligence Service, the SVR, also known as APT 29, Cozy Bear and The Dukes, as responsible. These are government assessments, and the evidence behind them is not fully public.

For learners, the useful distinction is between what the technical advisories document and what the governments assessed. The mechanism of the compromise is described in public technical sources, while the identity of the actor rests on official statements.

The event shaped how organisations think about supplier trust and software integrity.
