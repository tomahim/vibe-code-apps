On 13 December 2020, CISA issued Emergency Directive 21-01 ordering federal civilian agencies to disconnect or power down Orion products. The directive reflected how serious CISA judged the risk.

Cleaning up was difficult. Defenders could not just remove the infected software, since the attackers may have stolen credentials or created other ways in. Affected organisations rebuilt systems, reset credentials and certificates, and hunted for hidden access. The incident showed that supply chain compromise can affect trust in the update process itself.

Cleaning up after a compromise like this is hard because the attackers may have stolen credentials or created other ways in. Affected organisations typically rebuild systems, reset credentials and hunt for hidden access, which takes much longer than removing one product.
