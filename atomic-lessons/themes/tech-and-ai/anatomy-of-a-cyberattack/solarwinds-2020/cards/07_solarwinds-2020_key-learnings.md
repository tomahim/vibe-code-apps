1. Secure the build pipeline as carefully as the product. Isolate build systems, review changes and verify output independently.

2. Trusted updates can carry harm. Verify build integrity and monitor how new software behaves after installation.

3. Limit privileged tools. Restrict what management software can reach on the network and watch for unusual outbound connections and identity activity.
The US attribution to the SVR is an official government assessment.
