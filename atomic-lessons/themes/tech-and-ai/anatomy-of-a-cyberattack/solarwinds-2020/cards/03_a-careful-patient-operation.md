CISA describes an adversary with patience, operational security and complex tradecraft. Only a subset of the roughly 18,000 affected organisations received follow-on activity. A January 2021 joint statement from the FBI, CISA, ODNI and NSA said fewer than ten US government agencies had been the target of follow-on activity.

CISA's alert says the attackers abused SAML tokens to reach cloud resources after getting into victim networks. Quiet behaviour like this means detection depends on unusual identity and access patterns. This is a lesson from the CISA description, and the public sources cited here do not cover every technique.

For defenders the lesson is to watch identity and cloud access, not only malware on servers. Unusual logins, new tokens or changes to trust settings are places where a patient intruder can leave signs, and these logs need to be kept and reviewed.
