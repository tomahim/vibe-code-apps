SolarWinds sells Orion, a network monitoring and management platform used by many companies and government agencies. Because Orion needs wide access to see everything on a network, it is a powerful piece of software. Customers install its updates routinely, and they trust that the vendor's updates are safe.

In December 2020 SolarWinds told the US Securities and Exchange Commission that a vulnerability had been inserted into Orion updates through a compromise of its build system. CISA's alert AA20-352A describes the resulting activity, which became known as the SolarWinds supply chain compromise.

This is a case where the trust that makes updates convenient also created the risk. A customer cannot inspect every update line by line, so the supplier's own build and release controls matter to everyone downstream, including the customers who never see them.
