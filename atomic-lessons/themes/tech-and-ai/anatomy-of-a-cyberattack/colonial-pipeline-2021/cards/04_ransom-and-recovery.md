According to the Department of Justice, Colonial paid a ransom of about 75 bitcoin on 8 May 2021.

On 7 June 2021 the Department of Justice announced that it had seized 63.7 bitcoin, worth about $2.3 million, which it traced to the ransom payment. This showed that cryptocurrency payments can sometimes be followed. It was a partial recovery and it did not undo the harm to customers who suffered disruptions.

Paying a ransom is a hard decision that organisations should think through in advance, with legal advice and law enforcement contact. The money trail can matter later: the seizure shows that investigators can sometimes follow payments, but recovery of the funds is partial and not something to plan around.
