DarkSide was operated as a service. Its developers wrote the malware and ran a payment site, and affiliates carried out intrusions and shared a part of the ransom. CISA's alert describes DarkSide as ransomware-as-a-service and links the Colonial attack to it.

The division of labour matters for defenders. The people who get in are often different from the people who sell the malware, and the same weak spots, such as unprotected remote access, are used by many affiliates. Fixing the basics therefore helps against many groups at once.
