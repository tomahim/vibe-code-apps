Colonial Pipeline operates a system of more than 5,500 miles of pipelines that carries fuel from Texas to the US Southeast and East Coast. CEO Joseph Blount told the Senate that it carries nearly half of the East Coast's fuel. Many airports, filling stations and businesses depend on it.

The Department of Energy lists the shutdown as happening on 7 May 2021 after a ransomware attack. CISA's alert says Colonial proactively disconnected certain operational technology systems and that there were no indications of lateral movement to them. The decision to stop the pipeline was the company's, to contain the risk.
