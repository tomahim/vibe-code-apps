Colonial's chief executive, Joseph Blount, told the US Senate that the attackers got in through a legacy virtual private network profile that was not intended to be in use, and that the company was still trying to determine how the credentials were obtained. Reuters reported that day that the attackers used a single stolen password and that the account lacked multifactor authentication.

An old account protected only by a password is a common gap. Organisations often forget to close accounts that nobody uses. A second factor, such as a code or security key, would have meant that the stolen password alone was not enough to log in.
