The ransomware, called DarkSide, encrypted data on the company's IT systems. CISA's alert says there were no indications of lateral movement to the operational technology. Even so, the company disconnected certain systems proactively, to make sure the attack did not spread.

This dependency is the main lesson. A company may separate IT and operational networks, but a ransomware attack on the business side can still lead to a shutdown of operations. Plan for that case before it happens.

A useful exercise for any operator is to list which business systems, such as billing or scheduling, must work before operations can safely continue. If the answer is a long list, then a ransomware attack on the office network is also an operational risk, and the plan should say who decides about shutdown and restart.
