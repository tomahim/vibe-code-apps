1. Close unused accounts and require multifactor authentication on remote access. A single leaked password should not be enough to enter.

2. Know your business dependencies. Understand which IT systems an operation needs, and design fallback procedures so you can keep working or restart safely.

3. Ransom decisions are hard, and prevention is cheaper. Paying does not guarantee fast recovery, so keep tested backups and an incident plan.
