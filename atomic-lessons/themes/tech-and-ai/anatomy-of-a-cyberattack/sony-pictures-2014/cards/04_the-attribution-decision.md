On 19 December 2014 the FBI said it had enough information to conclude that the North Korean government was responsible. It listed reasons: similarities between the data-deletion malware and malware previously developed by North Korean actors, overlap between the infrastructure used and other malicious activity the US government had previously linked to North Korea, and similarities to a March 2013 cyber attack against South Korean banks and media. The FBI also said that the need to protect sensitive sources and methods precluded it from sharing all of its information.

So the public record contains part of the evidence, not all of it. The responsible position is to describe it as a US government attribution backed by partial public evidence.
