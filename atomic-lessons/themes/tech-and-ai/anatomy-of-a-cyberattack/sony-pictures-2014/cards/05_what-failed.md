The FBI said Sony had been a great partner in the investigation and that the company reported the incident within hours, which is what the FBI hopes all companies will do when facing a cyber attack. It said the quick reporting made it easier for investigators to identify the source of the attacks.

The public record does not describe Sony's internal weaknesses, so be careful about what failed. What it does show is the scale of the disruption: a destructive attack can take a whole network offline. Organisations therefore need backups that attackers cannot reach, a rehearsed plan for rebuilding systems, communication plans for staff and partners, and law enforcement contacts before a crisis. These are lessons drawn from the case, not findings of the FBI.
