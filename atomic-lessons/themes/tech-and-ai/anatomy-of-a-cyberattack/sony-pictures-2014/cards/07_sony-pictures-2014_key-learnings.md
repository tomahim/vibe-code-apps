1. Plan for destruction, not only theft. Keep offline or immutable backups and rehearse rebuilding systems from scratch.

2. Report fast. The FBI said Sony reported the incident within hours, which helped the investigation, so know in advance whom to call.

3. Prepare communication before a crisis. Staff, customers and partners need clear information when systems are down and data is leaked.
Attribution here is a US government conclusion with partial public evidence.
