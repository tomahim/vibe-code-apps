The group that claimed the attack did not simply ask for money. According to the FBI, it issued threats against Sony, its employees and the theaters that distribute its movies. The FBI described such acts of intimidation as falling outside the bounds of acceptable state behavior, and said it takes seriously any attempt, through cyber-enabled means, threats of violence or otherwise, to undermine the economic and social prosperity of US citizens.

For employees, the harm was personal, because their personal information and confidential communications were among the stolen material. The case showed that a data breach can turn into a campaign of public pressure, and that companies need a plan for communication with staff, partners and the public, as well as for technical recovery.
