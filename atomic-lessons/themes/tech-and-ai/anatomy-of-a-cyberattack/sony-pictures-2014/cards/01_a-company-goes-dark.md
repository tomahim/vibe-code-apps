In late November 2014 Sony Pictures Entertainment confirmed it had been hit by a cyber attack that destroyed systems and stole large quantities of personal and commercial data. A group calling itself the Guardians of Peace claimed responsibility. The FBI said the attack rendered thousands of Sony's computers inoperable, forced the company to take its entire network offline and significantly disrupted its business operations.

This was not the usual theft-for-profit case. The group later issued threats against Sony, its employees and theaters that distribute its movies. The case is a lesson in what happens when the aim is damage and pressure, not quiet data collection. Source: [FBI, Update on Sony Investigation](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/update-on-sony-investigation).
