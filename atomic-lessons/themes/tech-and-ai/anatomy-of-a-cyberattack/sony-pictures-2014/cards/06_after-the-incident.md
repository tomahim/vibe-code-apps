In September 2018 the US Department of Justice unsealed a criminal complaint, filed in June 2018, charging Park Jin Hyok, a North Korean citizen, with a conspiracy that included the attack on Sony Pictures. The department described a North Korean hacking team as responsible for the Sony attack, the Bangladesh central bank cyber theft and WannaCry. The complaint contains allegations that have not been tested in a trial.

For learners, the case also raises a question of proof. Public attribution is a political and legal decision as well as a technical one. It combines evidence that can be shared with evidence that cannot, and readers must decide how much weight to give each. Source: [US Department of Justice, 2018 announcement](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/north-korean-regime-backed-programmer-charged-conspiracy-conduct-multiple-cyber-attacks-and).
