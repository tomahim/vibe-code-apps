title: "3 key learnings"

1. Keep a working manual fallback. Operators who could switch to manual operation kept recovery possible, so practise operating without automation.

2. Separate business and control networks, and protect remote access. Use strong authentication and monitor commands sent to operational systems.

3. Expect attackers to target recovery too. Plan for blocked phone lines, wiped computers and damaged devices.
