The CISA alert was updated in July 2021 to attribute this campaign to Russian state actors, and Ukrainian officials blamed Russian security services. Because the attribution is by governments and based partly on non-public evidence, it should be presented as an official assessment, not as a courtroom finding.

A careful reader keeps three layers apart: what the technical alerts document, what the attribution statements say, and what remains unknown. The CISA alert itself says the role of BlackEnergy in the outages was unknown.

For students, the 2015 case is a base example: attackers studied operations patiently, used legitimate remote tools, and tried to make recovery harder. Source: [CISA alert on the cyber-attack against Ukrainian critical infrastructure](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-alerts/ir-alert-h-16-056-01).
