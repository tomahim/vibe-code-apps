On 23 December 2015, power companies in western Ukraine reported unscheduled outages. A US interagency team, including ICS-CERT, the FBI and the Department of Energy, travelled to Ukraine and interviewed staff at the affected organisations. The CISA alert says three regional electric power distribution companies were affected and that about 225,000 customers lost power.

The attacks were not a huge blackout, and experts had warned about such risks. The value of the case is that it let the public study, with real evidence, how an operation against a grid can unfold, and what defenders can do about it. Source: [CISA alert](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-alerts/ir-alert-h-16-056-01).
