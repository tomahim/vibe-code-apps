E-ISAC/SANS reports that operators were forced to manual mode, and that at one company substations were disconnected for three hours. Recovery was possible because the equipment could still be operated by hand and staff knew how. The analysts note that less automated equipment can be a safeguard, since it cannot be disabled as completely as a fully automated grid.

This point deserves attention: an organisation that has fully automated its processes needs a way to operate without the automation. Whether it is manual switching, paper forms or a backup system, the fallback must be tested in practice. Teams that practise fallback procedures recover far faster than teams that read about them for the first time during an outage.
