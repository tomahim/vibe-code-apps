Several layers failed. Phishing led to a foothold, legitimate credentials were used for remote access over a VPN to the control systems, and firmware on some devices could be corrupted remotely. The public sources do not give a full account of every weakness, so the points below are lessons rather than audit findings.

The lesson is not that the grid was uniquely weak. Many sectors share the same design: a corporate network with email and office documents connected, at some distance, to an operational network. The security job is to make that distance real with firewalls, one-way flows, strong authentication and monitoring for unusual commands, and to practise what to do when control is lost.
