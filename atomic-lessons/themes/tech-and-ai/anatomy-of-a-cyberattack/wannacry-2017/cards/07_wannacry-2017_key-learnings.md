1. Patch fast and know what you cannot patch. Install critical updates quickly, and keep an inventory of old or special devices so they can be isolated.

2. Segment networks. A worm spreads easily across flat networks, and separation limits how far it can go.

3. Practise the emergency plan. Hospitals and other critical services need tested procedures for working without IT and for restoring from backups.
