In December 2017, the White House attributed WannaCry to North Korea in a press briefing on 19 December, and the UK Foreign Office issued a statement attributing it to a North Korean actor. These are government assessments, and the evidence behind them has not been fully published.

In 2018, the US Department of Justice filed a complaint against a North Korean programmer, Park Jin Hyok, that referred to this and other attacks. The Department of Justice described a North Korean hacking team as responsible for WannaCry 2.0. As with other indictments, that is an allegation. The facts that are not in dispute are the mechanism, the available patch and the impact. Source: [UK National Audit Office, Investigation: WannaCry cyber attack and the NHS](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/investigation-wannacry-cyber-attack-and-the-nhs/).
