On the evening of 12 May a security researcher activated a kill switch, according to the NAO. The malware had tried to contact an unregistered web address, and registering that domain stopped it from spreading in many cases.

The kill switch slowed the spread but did not repair infected machines. The episode shows how much rested on public researchers acting within hours, and why defenders cannot rely on such luck.
