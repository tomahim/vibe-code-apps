WannaCry exploited a vulnerability in Windows. Microsoft had released a security update in March 2017, two months before the attack. The NAO reports that NHS Digital had sent alerts about the patch to NHS organisations in March and April, and that most affected machines were running Windows 7 that had not been patched.

So there was a fix available before the attack. Machines that had installed the update were protected. NHS Digital told the NAO that the majority of infected NHS devices were unpatched but on supported Windows 7, while unsupported XP devices were a minority. Machines that had not been patched were exposed. For defenders this meant that routine patching was the main protection.
