On Friday 12 May 2017 a global ransomware attack, known as WannaCry, began to spread rapidly. Ransomware locks the files on infected computers and demands payment to restore them. Estimates say it affected more than 200,000 computers in at least 100 countries, including large companies, telecoms and public services.

The NAO and Microsoft describe an attack that exploited a vulnerability in Windows for which Microsoft had released a security update in March 2017. Machines that had not installed the update were exposed, and the NAO reports that the NHS was hit hard. The sections that follow explain what the NAO found and what defenders can learn from it, and they keep audited findings apart from government attribution.
