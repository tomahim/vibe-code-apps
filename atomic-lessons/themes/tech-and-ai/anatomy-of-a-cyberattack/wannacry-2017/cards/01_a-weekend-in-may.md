On Friday 12 May 2017 a ransomware program began to spread rapidly across the world. It encrypted the files on infected computers and demanded payment in Bitcoin to restore them. Estimates say it affected more than 200,000 computers in at least 100 countries, including large companies, telecoms and public services.

What made it different from ordinary ransomware is that it acted like a worm. It could spread from one computer to others on the same network and across the internet without anyone opening an attachment. A single unprotected machine could start a chain reaction across an organisation, which turned a nuisance into an emergency within hours.
