The UK National Audit Office investigated the effect on England's National Health Service. It found that at least 80 of the 236 trusts were affected, and that 603 other NHS organisations, including 595 GP practices, were also hit. It identified 6,912 cancelled appointments and estimated that more than 19,000 were cancelled in total. NHS Digital believes no patient data was compromised.

The NAO also reported that the Department of Health had been warned about the risk of cyber attacks a year before and had given no formal written response until July 2017. Most affected devices were running Windows 7 without the patch.
