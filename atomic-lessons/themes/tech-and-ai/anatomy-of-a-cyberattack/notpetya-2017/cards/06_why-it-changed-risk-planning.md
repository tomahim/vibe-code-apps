The losses were large and unusual. A White House assessment put the total damage at more than $10 billion, and Maersk alone estimated $200 to $300 million. The attack began on 27 June 2017 in Ukraine and reached companies around the world within hours.

For companies, the practical message is that supply chain compromise, destructive intent and fast movement between machines should be part of business continuity planning. Offline backups, tested rebuild procedures, restricted administrator access and a way to isolate segments quickly can decide whether recovery takes days or months. Maersk's recovery, as Wired tells it, depended partly on luck.
