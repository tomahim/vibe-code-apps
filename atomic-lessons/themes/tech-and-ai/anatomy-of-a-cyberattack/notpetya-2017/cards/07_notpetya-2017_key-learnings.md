title: "3 key learnings"
archived: false

1. Treat software updates as part of your attack surface. Verify suppliers, monitor update channels and limit what updated software can access.

2. Protect credentials and segment the network. Stolen administrator passwords let malware move even where patches are installed.

3. Plan for data that cannot be recovered. Keep offline backups and rehearse rebuilding, since a ransom note may hide a wiper.
Attribution to the Russian military is an official US government assessment.
