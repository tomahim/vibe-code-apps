The weak points were both technical and structural. Trust in a supplier's update was assumed. Patches were missing on some systems, and credentials could be stolen and reused to move between machines. The public sources do not give a full account of each victim's weaknesses.

There was also a mismatch of expectations. Many companies treated ransomware as a financial crime where paying could restore data. NotPetya showed that a destructive operation can use ransomware's appearance, and that a plan which relies on a decryption key may fail. A good recovery plan assumes the data cannot be returned and rebuilds from clean backups.
