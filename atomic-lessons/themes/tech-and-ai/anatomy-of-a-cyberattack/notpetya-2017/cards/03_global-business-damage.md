Although the main target seemed to be Ukraine, the attack spread through company networks to subsidiaries worldwide. Wired reports that Maersk rebuilt about 4,000 servers and 45,000 PCs in around ten days, and CNBC reported that Maersk estimated a cost of $200 to $300 million. Other firms, including Merck, TNT, Saint-Gobain and Mondelez, were also hit, and a White House assessment put total damage at more than $10 billion.

A widely reported detail about Maersk is that its systems were restored partly because one domain controller in Ghana was offline during the power cut at the time of the attack, and so it kept a clean copy of an essential directory. This story is reported by Wired, not a formal investigation, but it illustrates how close the company came to a much longer recovery.
