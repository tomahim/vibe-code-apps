On 27 June 2017 organisations in Ukraine, and soon around the world, saw computers display a ransom note and then stop working. The malware became known as NotPetya because it resembled an older ransomware called Petya, but investigators concluded that it was not designed to give files back. It was destructive.

The first infections spread through the update system of M.E.Doc, a Ukrainian tax and accounting program, which Wired compares to TurboTax. Cisco Talos analysed it as a supply chain attack in which attackers used stolen credentials and the software's update server to deliver the malware, with a destructive payload disguised as ransomware.
