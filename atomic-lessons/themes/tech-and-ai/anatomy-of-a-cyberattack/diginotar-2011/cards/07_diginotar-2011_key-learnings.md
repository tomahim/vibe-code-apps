title: "3 key learnings"

1. Protect the keys that others trust. A CA's signing systems should be isolated, tightly monitored and audited, because a breach there harms every user.

2. Segment and harden. Separate critical servers, avoid one administrator account that reaches everything, and keep anti-malware effective on them.

3. Public systems need transparency. Certificate Transparency logs let anyone see which certificates exist, so a rogue one cannot stay hidden for long.
Fox-IT's interim report did not identify the attackers, so treat attribution as unproven.
