According to Fox-IT's interim report, DigiNotar detected the signing of 128 rogue certificates on 19 July 2011 and revoked them, but at least one rogue *.google.com certificate was not found at that time. In late August 2011 an Iranian user reported a browser warning for a Google certificate that turned out to be fraudulent. The Dutch government's investigation was carried out by the security firm Fox-IT, which dates the creation of that certificate to 10 July.

Fox-IT's interim report counted 531 fraudulent certificates, covering domains including Google as well as other well-known sites, and said it could not rule out that further rogue certificates existed. The company's trust was gone within days, because users and browsers had to assume that any certificate it had signed might be unreliable.
