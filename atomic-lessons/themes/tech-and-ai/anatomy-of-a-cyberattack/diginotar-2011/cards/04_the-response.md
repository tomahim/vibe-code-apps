Browser makers removed DigiNotar from their lists of trusted CAs from around 29 August 2011. On 3 September the Dutch government took over the company's operations, and DigiNotar was declared bankrupt in September 2011, according to the encyclopedia article on the company. Government services that relied on its certificates had to be moved to other providers.

Revoking a certificate is a slow and uneven process, because a browser must learn that a certificate is no longer valid. Removing the CA from the trust store was the decisive fix. That is a blunt tool, which cuts off every legitimate certificate issued by the company, but it was the only reliable way to protect users quickly.
