title: "A botnet made of ordinary devices"

A botnet is a large group of infected devices that an attacker can command together. The Mirai botnet, first seen in 2016, was made mostly of internet-connected cameras, video recorders and home routers, often called the Internet of Things. These devices are always on, rarely updated and seldom watched by their owners.

A 2017 academic study presented at the USENIX Security conference measured the botnet over time. It found that at its peak Mirai had about 600,000 infections. Its growth came from a simple idea: find devices that are reachable from the internet and still protected only by the password the factory chose.
