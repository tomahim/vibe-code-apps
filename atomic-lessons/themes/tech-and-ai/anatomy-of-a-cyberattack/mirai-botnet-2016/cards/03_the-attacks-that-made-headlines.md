In September 2016, the security journalist Brian Krebs's website was hit by a denial-of-service attack that exceeded 600 gigabits per second, according to the USENIX paper. On 21 October 2016, an attack targeted Dyn, a company that provides domain name system services. Krebs reported that sites including Twitter, Amazon, Tumblr, Reddit, Spotify and Netflix were affected.

The point of a denial-of-service attack is to flood a service with requests so legitimate users cannot get through. By aiming at DNS, the internet's address book, the attackers affected many websites at once, even though none of those sites was the direct target.
