The initial access used a vulnerability in Apache Struts, a widely used open-source framework for building web applications. The House committee says the flaw was disclosed on 7 March 2017 and that the Department of Homeland Security alerted Equifax the next day. The fix was not applied to the affected system, and the attack began on 13 May and lasted 76 days. The House committee concluded that the breach was entirely preventable.

It is easy to blame one person, but the congressional investigation looked at the process. A fix that exists but is not applied gives no protection.

A good way to read this section is as a question about ownership. For each critical vulnerability notice, who is responsible, how is completion checked, and what happens if a system is missing from the list? A fix that exists but is not applied gives no protection.
