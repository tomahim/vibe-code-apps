Equifax is one of the three major US credit reporting agencies. It holds financial histories, names, addresses, birth dates and social security numbers for a large part of the US adult population. People do not choose to be in its databases, which makes its security a matter of public interest.

On 7 September 2017 the company announced that attackers had accessed its systems between mid-May and late July. The US Government Accountability Office reported that at least 145.5 million people were affected, up from an initial figure of 143 million, and the House committee cites 148 million. Counts vary by source.
