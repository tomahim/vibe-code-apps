Equifax is a US credit reporting agency. Equifax stated that the information accessed by attackers included names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses, driver's license numbers, credit card numbers and dispute documents, according to the GAO. People do not choose to be in a credit bureau's databases, which makes its security a matter of public interest.

On 7 September 2017 the company announced that attackers had accessed its systems between mid-May and late July. The US Government Accountability Office reported that at least 145.5 million people were affected, up from an initial figure of 143 million, and the House committee cites 148 million. Counts vary by source.
