Equifax had a tool that inspects encrypted traffic leaving its network, which is a common way to find data theft. The House Oversight Committee report found the monitoring device had been inactive for 19 months because of an expired certificate, while GAO put the expiry at about 10 months before the breach. The sources differ, but both describe the tool being blind to encrypted traffic.

On 29 July 2017, after the certificate was renewed, the tool revealed suspicious traffic, and the company then took the affected application offline and began an investigation. This is a striking case of a basic maintenance task, renewing a certificate, hiding a serious attack. It shows why security tools must be monitored too, since a silent failure can look like a calm network.
