Congress held hearings and published a House report in December 2018.

In July 2019 Equifax agreed to a settlement with the Federal Trade Commission, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and 50 states and territories worth at least $575 million and up to $700 million. A key difference to earlier breaches is that the data included items that cannot be changed, like a social security number.

The data in this case included items that cannot easily be changed, such as Social Security numbers, so the harm for consumers can last for years. That is why the settlement included compensation and security commitments, and why minimising stored data is a real defence.
