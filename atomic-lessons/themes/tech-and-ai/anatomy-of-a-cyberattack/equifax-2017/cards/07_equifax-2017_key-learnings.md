1. Know what you run and patch quickly. A complete inventory of systems and a way to verify that fixes were applied would have closed the entry point.

2. Do not store credentials in the open and segment access. Plain text passwords and flat networks let one compromise reach many databases.

3. Monitor your monitoring. An expired certificate left a detection tool blind for months.
The counts of people affected vary slightly by source, and the indictment of PLA members is an allegation.
