The government reports, the GAO and the House Oversight Committee, document the technical failures in detail, and these are the strongest sources. On attribution, the US Department of Justice announced in February 2020 an indictment of four members of China's People's Liberation Army for hacking Equifax. The indictment alleges a three-month campaign that affected nearly 150 million Americans.

An indictment is an allegation. Public technical reports do not themselves prove who the attackers were. A careful lesson therefore separates three things: the documented control failures, the legal settlement, and the alleged state involvement. Source: [US GAO report GAO-18-559 on the Equifax breach](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-18-559.pdf).
