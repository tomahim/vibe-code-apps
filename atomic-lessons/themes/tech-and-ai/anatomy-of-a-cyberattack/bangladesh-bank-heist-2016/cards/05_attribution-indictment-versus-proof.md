BAE Systems researchers told Reuters that the tools used in this case were linked to the malware used in the 2014 Sony Pictures attack, and Symantec linked the activity to the Lazarus group. In 2018 the US Department of Justice charged a North Korean programmer, Park Jin Hyok, in connection with these and other attacks, and described him as part of a state-backed hacking programme.

An indictment is a set of allegations that has not been tested in a trial, and the Department of Justice announcement describes the allegations as such. Students should therefore say that US prosecutors allege North Korean state involvement, supported by technical analysis from private firms, and avoid describing it as a judicial finding.
