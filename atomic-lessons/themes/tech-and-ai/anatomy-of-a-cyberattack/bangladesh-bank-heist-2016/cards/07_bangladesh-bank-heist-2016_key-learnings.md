1. Secure the endpoint of a trusted network. A secure global network can still be abused through a weak local system.

2. Do not let one compromised system control both execution and checking. The malware hid evidence on the same machines that staff used for reconciliation, so verification needs an independent path.

3. Timing and review matter. Extra scrutiny of unusual, urgent or out-of-hours payments gave other banks the chance to stop some transfers.
Attribution comes from private research and a US criminal complaint, which is an allegation.
