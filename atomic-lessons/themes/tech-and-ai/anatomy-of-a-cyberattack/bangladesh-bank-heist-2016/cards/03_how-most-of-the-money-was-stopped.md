The New York Fed received the instructions and processed some, but it also questioned others. A fifth payment of $20 million to a Sri Lankan entity was stopped after Deutsche Bank noticed that the word foundation was misspelled as "fandation" and asked for clarification. The New York Fed said it then blocked the remaining requests.

Four requests had already gone through, about $81 million to accounts in the Philippines. According to Reuters, much of that money moved through casinos and was dispersed, and about $18 million was recovered. BAE says $81 million remained unaccounted for. The New York Fed said its own systems were not breached.
