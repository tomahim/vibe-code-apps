This is general guidance for banks with payment connections. Sensible controls include separating the payment terminal network, requiring a second person to approve messages, restricting remote access, and monitoring for unusual transaction patterns, particularly outside working hours. These are lessons drawn from the case, not requirements quoted from a source here.

The Bangladesh Bank case also showed the value of human review and good luck. A spelling error stopped a large payment, which is an inspiring but fragile defence, because no organisation should depend on a lucky typo or a single sharp-eyed clerk to prevent a theft of this size. Source: [BAE Systems Threat Research, Two bytes to $951m](https://baesystemsai.blogspot.com/2016/04/two-bytes-to-951m.html).
