Investigators, including security firms working with the bank and reporters who read the reports, concluded that the attackers had compromised the bank's internal network beforehand and obtained the means to operate its SWIFT terminals. BAE Systems analysed malware found in the case and described how it altered the software that checks messages and hides the transactions from the bank's own records.

The malware also interfered with the printer used to produce paper confirmations of SWIFT messages, which the bank relied on for daily reconciliation. Staff therefore did not see the transfers on the usual printouts. The attackers understood the bank's operations well enough to find the checks staff used and to blind them.
