Many plausible sequences will be absent from training text. Absence from the sample does not make a sequence impossible.

A new phrase can be absent simply because the corpus is finite. It might be grammatical, meaningful, and useful even if no one has written it before. For example, a freshly invented product name can be placed in a sentence using familiar grammatical patterns. The model may infer how to continue around it without having a stored occurrence of the complete sentence. Conversely, a sequence seen frequently in a dataset is not guaranteed to be true. Frequency and possibility answer different questions.
