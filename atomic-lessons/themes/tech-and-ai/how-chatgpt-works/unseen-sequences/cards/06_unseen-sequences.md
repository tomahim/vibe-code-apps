The approach works when patterns learned from past text help predict new text. That empirical success is the essay's starting puzzle.

The essay asks why a finite neural network can produce text that often resembles human writing. One answer is that language has reusable structure: the same grammar, topic relationships, and rhetorical patterns appear in many places. Training on examples can capture some of those regularities, allowing predictions beyond exact matches. This remains an empirical claim about what the trained system does, not a proof that all meaningful language has been reduced to a simple rule. Surprising failures remain part of the picture.
