title: "The number of possible phrases"
archived: false
---
A vocabulary can be combined into vastly more phrases than any dataset contains. Exact lookup cannot cover every useful continuation.

Consider the phrase “a green bicycle under a purple moon.” It is easy to understand, but it may never have appeared in the training text exactly. A system that only searches for matching phrases would find no entry and have little basis for continuing. Language is productive: speakers combine familiar parts into new arrangements all the time. The model must therefore use patterns that transfer across sentences. Exact memorization may occur in some circumstances, but it cannot be the whole method for handling open-ended prompts.
