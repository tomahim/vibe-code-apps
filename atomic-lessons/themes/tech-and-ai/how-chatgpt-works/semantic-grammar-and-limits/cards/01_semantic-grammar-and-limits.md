Wolfram proposes rules about which concepts can sensibly combine. This goes beyond the syntax that says which word orders are grammatical.

Syntactic grammar can say that “the elephant traveled” has a normal English structure. Semantic grammar, in Wolfram's proposed sense, would help describe which concepts can combine into a plausible meaning: objects can move, people can buy things, and quantities can measure them. “The elephant traveled to the Moon” is conceptually intelligible even if it is not a report of a real event. The proposal tries to capture regularities in meaningful discourse more explicitly. It is a research direction, not a known rulebook already extracted from ChatGPT.
