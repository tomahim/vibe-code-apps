Wolfram contrasts flexible human language with precise forms that a computer can execute. This is part of his proposed way to connect text with exact reasoning.

Wolfram describes computational language as a precise symbolic way to represent things and operations so they can be executed unambiguously. For example, a structured expression for a quantity of apples could support a calculation, whereas the ordinary sentence “I bought some apples” leaves details open. Human language is flexible and often useful precisely because it tolerates such vagueness. His proposal is to connect fluent language with precise computational representations where exact operations are needed. That is a proposed path forward, not a feature every ChatGPT answer already uses.
