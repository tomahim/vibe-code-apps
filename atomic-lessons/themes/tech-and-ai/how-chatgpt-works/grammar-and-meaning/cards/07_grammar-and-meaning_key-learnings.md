title: "3 key learnings"

**1. Syntax is a learnable regularity.** Training text contains repeated phrase and clause patterns, allowing a model to form many grammatical sentences and connect words across intervening material.

**2. Grammar, meaning, and truth are different checks.** “The blue idea ate breakfast” is structurally familiar but odd; “Paris is the capital of Germany” is smooth but false. Fluent form does not settle a factual claim.

**3. Exact structure can expose limits.** Long balanced parentheses have a simple counting algorithm. A model that handles short examples may still fail on longer nesting, so test precise tasks directly. The exact test reveals a limit that fluent writing can hide.
