The linked InstructGPT paper describes a historical human-feedback training method. It is an example, not a complete description of current ChatGPT training.

The InstructGPT research linked from the card describes a specific historical approach using demonstrations, comparisons, and reinforcement learning from human feedback. It helps explain why an instruction-following assistant differs from a raw completion model of similar size. It does not disclose the full training recipe of every later ChatGPT model. Read it for the sequence of ideas and the reported evaluations, while keeping its date and experimental setting in view. A method's success on one evaluation should not be generalized to every domain.
