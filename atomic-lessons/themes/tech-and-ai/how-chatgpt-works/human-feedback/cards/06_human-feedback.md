title: "Helpfulness is a separate goal"
archived: false
---
Predicting likely text and answering a user's request well are related but different objectives. Post-training tries to move behavior toward the latter.

Predicting the most likely continuation of “The answer is” can produce a plausible sentence, but the user may need a justified answer with a certain format and level of detail. Helpfulness asks whether the response advances that task. A useful assistant may ask for missing context, cite a reliable source, or show a calculation when the situation calls for it. Post-training can encourage those behaviors, while tools and evaluation can support them further. The objectives overlap, because language fluency helps, but they are not identical.
