title: "The limits of raw prediction"

---
A model trained only to continue text may imitate unhelpful or wandering passages. A useful assistant needs a more specific behavior target.

A raw model trained to continue text may treat “How do I fix this?” as the start of a forum thread, a joke, or an unrelated article. It has learned many writing patterns but not necessarily the assistant behavior a user wants. Post-training introduces examples and feedback about better responses. That can encourage direct answers, appropriate refusals, and clearer instruction following. It does not erase every mistake. The important distinction is between learning to imitate likely text and learning to respond usefully to a person in a conversation.
