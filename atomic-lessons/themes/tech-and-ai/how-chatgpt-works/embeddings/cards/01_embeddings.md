title: "What an embedding is"
archived: false
---
An embedding is an array of numbers used to represent an item. It lets a neural network work with text in numerical form.

Imagine giving the word “crocodile” a list of several hundred numbers. No one coordinate needs to mean “reptile”; the full pattern can place it near related uses of “alligator.” A network can compare and transform these lists mathematically, which it cannot do directly with the printed letters of a word. Embeddings are learned representations, not dictionary definitions. Their usefulness comes from the relationships they support in a task, such as predicting a missing word or the next token in a sentence.
