The essay's GPT-2 and GPT-3 dimensions are examples from its 2023 explanation. They should not be read as specifications for today's ChatGPT.

The essay names GPT-2 and GPT-3 dimensions to make an otherwise abstract architecture tangible. Those values describe the particular historical examples Wolfram discussed in 2023. They do not reveal the architecture or parameter count of a current ChatGPT model. When learning the mechanism, focus on the sequence of operations: tokens become vectors, positions matter, layers transform them, and output scores guide the next token. The exact numerical dimensions can vary widely without changing that broad teaching picture. Compare the mechanism, not the old parameter figures.
