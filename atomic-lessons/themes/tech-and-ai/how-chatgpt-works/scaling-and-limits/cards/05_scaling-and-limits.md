Matching long nested parentheses tests whether a model tracks exact structure. The essay uses it as an example of where approximate pattern learning may fail.

For the sequence “((()))” we can count openings and closings to verify balance. Short examples may be easy to recognize by pattern, but long nested strings require keeping track of exact depth at each point. The essay's toy network handles some cases and fails on longer ones, illustrating a gap between approximate pattern learning and exact rules. This does not prove that every transformer fails at every formal task. It demonstrates why carefully designed tests can reveal limits that fluent prose or simple examples conceal.
