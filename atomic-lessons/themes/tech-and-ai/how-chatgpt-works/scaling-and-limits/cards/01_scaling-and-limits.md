title: "What scale can improve"

---
Larger networks and more data can improve performance on many tasks. Size alone does not establish that every task is solved.

A larger network may represent more complex patterns, and more training examples may expose those patterns more clearly. The essay points out that this can improve writing-like tasks dramatically. But “better on many examples” is not the same claim as “can do every possible task.” A model might write a convincing explanation of an algorithm while failing to execute it exactly on an unusual input. Scale should be assessed with specific evaluations, especially for tasks that require precision rather than plausible language.
