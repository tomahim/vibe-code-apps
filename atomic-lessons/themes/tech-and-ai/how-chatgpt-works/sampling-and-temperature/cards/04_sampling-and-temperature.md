Temperature changes how strongly decoding favors high-scored tokens. Higher temperature generally allows more variety; it does not add knowledge.

Think of temperature as changing how sharp the candidate distribution is before selection. Lower settings concentrate probability on leading choices; higher settings spread more weight to alternatives. If the prompt is “What is 2 + 2?”, increasing temperature is not a way to discover better arithmetic. It may merely encourage more variable wording or mistakes. For an imaginative story, a wider range may be welcome. The essay's suggested value was an observation for its examples, not a setting that is best for all tasks or models.
