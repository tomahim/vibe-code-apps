Greedy decoding always takes the highest-scored option. In the essay's examples, this often makes longer passages repetitive or flat.

Consider repeatedly choosing the top-ranked word in a story. A locally safe word can be selected at every step, but the chain may settle into generic phrasing or repeat itself. Wolfram demonstrates this with older GPT examples in the essay; his particular output is an illustration, not a universal rule for every modern model. Greedy decoding is useful when consistency matters, but it can narrow the range of responses. It also does nothing to check whether the highest-ranked continuation is factually correct.
