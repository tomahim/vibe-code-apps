title: "Variety and repetition"

---
Too little variation can repeat patterns, while too much can make text wander. The useful balance depends on the task and model.

A poem that repeats the same phrase on every line may feel stuck, while a paragraph that suddenly changes characters and setting may feel incoherent. Decoding choices influence this balance, but the prompt and the trained model matter too. A legal summary, a brainstorming list, and a bedtime story have different tolerances for variation. It is sensible to lower randomness for a tightly constrained format and allow more for ideation, then read the result critically in either case.
