Inspecting every weight does not automatically reveal a short human explanation. The network's useful behavior and our understanding of it are separate questions.

Suppose a classifier correctly labels a cat photo. We could inspect all its intermediate numbers and weights, yet still struggle to answer which combination caused the final choice. An attractive heat map may highlight useful evidence, but it is only one view of the computation. The essay treats this as a scientific challenge: the network's behavior is observable, while a compact explanatory theory may be missing. The same caution applies when someone points to one attention head and claims it fully explains a sentence generated by ChatGPT.
