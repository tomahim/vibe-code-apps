title: "3 key learnings"

**1. Simple units can compose a complex function.** Each neuron combines weighted numerical inputs, a bias, and an activation. Many such operations across layers can distinguish patterns that one straight-line rule could not separate.

**2. Learned features need not have neat names.** An early image layer may show edge-like responses, while later features mix several properties. In language models, an internal value is rarely a readable label such as “past tense” on its own.

**3. Performance and explanation are separate.** A network may classify a cat correctly while its thousands of weights resist a short human account. Visualizations help investigate behavior but do not automatically prove why one output appeared.
