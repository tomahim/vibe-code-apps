title: "Features emerge across layers"

---
Early image layers may respond to edges or shapes, while later representations are harder to name. Language models also develop internal features that are difficult to interpret.

For an image, early internal patterns may resemble edges or color regions, which a person can recognize. Later patterns might mix many features and no longer look like a familiar object. A language network can similarly represent properties spread across many numerical dimensions rather than storing a labeled “topic” cell. If a model handles a sentence well, we may infer that useful features were learned, but it is risky to assign a precise human word to each internal value. Interpretability requires careful experiments, not just a visualization.
