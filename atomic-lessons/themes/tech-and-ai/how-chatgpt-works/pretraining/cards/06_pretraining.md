title: "Fluent can still be wrong"
archived: false
---
A model trained to continue text can produce a convincing false statement. Plausibility is a different test from factual verification.

Imagine asking for the year of an obscure event. A pretrained model may produce a confident date because it has learned the style of encyclopedia answers and associations with the topic. The date could still be wrong or unsupported. Next-token training rewards plausible continuations of observed text, not a live fact check against an authoritative source. When a claim matters, verify it with a source or a suitable tool. Fluency is evidence that the model can use language patterns; it is not evidence that every asserted fact was checked.
