title: "Why scale helps"
archived: false
---
More useful data and model capacity can improve predictions and generated text. The relationship is empirical and depends on training choices.

More varied, relevant examples can improve a model's ability to recognize recurring structures; more capacity can let it represent richer patterns. That helps explain why larger pretraining efforts often improved language generation. But quantity does not settle quality. Repeating unreliable text can reinforce mistakes, and greater capacity can still leave a model weak at a specialized task. The right evaluation asks what changed on held-out prompts: is the output more coherent, accurate, and useful for the intended setting? A bigger run is therefore a hypothesis to test, not a quality certificate.
