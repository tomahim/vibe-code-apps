Books, pages, and other text expose many examples of language use. The essay discusses their scale without making every source equally reliable.

Books and webpages contain many examples of grammar, facts, stories, mistakes, and conflicting opinions. Predicting tokens across them gives the model a broad signal about how people write. It also gives imperfect evidence about the world: a sentence appearing online is not automatically correct. The mixture and quality of training sources matter. Wolfram's 2023 essay emphasizes the amount of available text to explain why a large model has many examples, while leaving open exactly how the learned internal representations support such fluent continuations.
