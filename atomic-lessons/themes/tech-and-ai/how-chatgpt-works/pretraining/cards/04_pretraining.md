title: "What weights can retain"

Weights encode patterns learned across examples rather than a tidy database of cited facts. Specific remembered passages and broad patterns can both affect behavior.

Think of weights as a network of shared tendencies rather than a filing cabinet with one drawer per sentence. A pattern about plural subjects, a topic such as birds, and a common phrase may all affect the score of “nests.” Models can sometimes reproduce memorized passages, but memorization is neither a complete account of their behavior nor a reliable citation system. Asking “where did this answer come from?” therefore needs care: a generated claim may reflect many training influences, and the model may not know an exact source.
