title: "When prompting breaks down"
archived: false
---
A prompt may ask for a long exact procedure or a fact the model cannot verify. Clear instructions help, but do not guarantee correctness.

A clear prompt can specify “add these twelve numbers,” but the model may still make a carrying error if it answers from learned patterns rather than an exact procedure. Similarly, asking for a citation does not by itself ensure the cited page exists. Prompting helps express the task and constraints; it does not supply a verifier. For consequential work, combine a precise prompt with a calculator, a source lookup, or another checkable process. The boundary is the task's demand for evidence or exact steps, not merely the length of the instruction.
