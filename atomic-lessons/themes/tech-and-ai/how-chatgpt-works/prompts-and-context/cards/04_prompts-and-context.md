title: "Temporary use of information"

---
Information supplied in a prompt can guide the current answer. It is not automatically a permanent update to the model.

If you paste a temporary meeting agenda and ask for a summary, the model can use that agenda while it remains in the available context. That does not mean the agenda has been added to the model's general training data or stored permanently as a weight. Applications may separately keep conversation history or memories, but those are different mechanisms and depend on the product. For the lesson's core model picture, treat prompt information as input for the current computation. This helps explain why context limits and missing details affect answers.
