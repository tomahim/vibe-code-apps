title: "The need for earlier context"
archived: false
---
A later word may depend on a much earlier one. Looking only at an immediate neighbor misses some useful relationships.

In “The scientist who wrote the report said she would revise it,” the word “she” may relate to “scientist,” several words earlier. A model using only the immediately preceding word would miss that relationship. Attention provides a way for a token position to use information from earlier positions in the available sequence. It does not guarantee that the model always selects the correct referent, but it gives the architecture a route for long-range context to matter. This is a major difference from simple fixed-length n-gram counts.
