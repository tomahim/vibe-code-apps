A straight line may fit some observations but miss their shape. A more suitable curve can improve estimates between measured points.

Imagine points that curve upward when plotted against height. A straight line may pass near the middle points but systematically miss the ends. Adding a squared term could fit better, though a more flexible equation is not automatically the right one. We must compare predictions with data not used to choose the parameters. In language, an architecture with more adjustable weights may capture more patterns, yet the ability to fit training text does not by itself establish how well it will answer new prompts.
