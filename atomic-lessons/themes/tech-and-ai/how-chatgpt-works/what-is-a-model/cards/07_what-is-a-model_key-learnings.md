**1. A model predicts beyond a table.** Measurements from three floors do not directly answer a question about a height between them. A fitted rule supplies an estimate, just as a language model supplies predictions for unseen text contexts.

**2. Structure and parameters have different jobs.** Choosing a line is a structural decision; choosing its slope and intercept fits it to data. A transformer's broad architecture is selected before training adjusts its many numerical weights.

**3. Test the rule on new cases.** A curve can hug all known points and still behave badly between them. For an assistant, fluent training-like text is insufficient evidence of reliable factual answers or useful instruction following.
