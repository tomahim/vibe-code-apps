The plotted trajectories do not reveal a simple, established law of meaning. We should distinguish the visualization from a proven explanation.

The displayed paths and clusters are suggestive, but they do not reveal a universal coordinate system or an equation for meaning. Changing the projection may alter apparent shapes, and language itself has ambiguity, metaphor, and context shifts. To establish a semantic law, researchers would need clear definitions and predictions that hold beyond selected examples. Until then, treat the essay's geometric account as a hypothesis about why next-token prediction works so well. The established observation is the model's behavior; the compact theory remains open.
