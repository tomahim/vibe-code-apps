title: "Words in a feature space"

---
Numerical representations can place related uses of words near one another. A two-dimensional plot is only a projection of a larger space.

A word vector may have hundreds or thousands of coordinates, too many to draw directly. A two-dimensional plot compresses that information so nearby points can be shown on a page. If animal names cluster, the plot suggests the model learned a useful relationship. But projecting dimensions can distort distances and hide other relationships. It is better to say the visualization offers a window into learned structure than to claim it is a complete map of meaning. The axes may not even have simple names.
