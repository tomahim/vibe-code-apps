He asks whether meaningful paths follow discoverable constraints, like laws of motion. The essay presents this as an open research idea.

In physics, a law of motion can constrain where an object goes next. Wolfram asks whether there might be analogous constraints on how meaningful text moves through a representation space. For instance, a sentence about buying fruit may tend to continue along relationships involving quantities, shops, or eating. The essay's plots do not identify a simple formula for those trajectories. The value of the proposal is to separate a testable research idea from the already observed fact that models often generate coherent language.
