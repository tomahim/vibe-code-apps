title: 3 key learnings
archived: false
---


**1. Recursive self-improvement means progress compounds**: the system changes itself, and those changes shape how later improvements are made and kept.

**2. The harness is the practical RSI target**: cheaper and safer than editing weights or agent code, yet high-leverage, since the same model performs very differently under different harnesses.

**3. The open world poses two challenges**: no ground-truth referee to grade edits, and task-specific edits that do not travel, so the improvement process itself must become a source of learning.
