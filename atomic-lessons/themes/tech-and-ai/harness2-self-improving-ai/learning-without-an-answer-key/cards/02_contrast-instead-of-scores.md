The replacement for a score is the behavioral contrast. Run the current harness and a candidate on the same task under matched conditions, then compare what they did: which files were opened, which checks were run, what the deliverable contains. If the reference run never opened the configuration file and the candidate did, that difference is real evidence about the edit, obtained with zero knowledge of the correct final answer.

The Medium article offers the intuition through a simple comparison. Imagine two students taking the same open-book exam where you cannot grade the answers. You can still watch how they work. One never opens the textbook's relevant chapter; the other reads it, cross-checks two sources and shows their reasoning. You have learned something genuine about their methods without ever scoring the exam. Harness² systematizes exactly this kind of observation and uses it to steer every edit.
