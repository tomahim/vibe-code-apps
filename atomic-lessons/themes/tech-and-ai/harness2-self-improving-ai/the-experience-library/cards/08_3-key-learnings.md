title: 3 key learnings

---


**1. Harnessing-level recursion captures what refinement teaches**: after each task, reflection turns the improvement record into lessons, stored with the adopted harness in a per-domain library.

**2. The door policy is strict**: lessons enter only with execution-contrast support, and reuse is selective through Propose, never automatic inheritance of a tailored harness.

**3. The payoff is measured**: removing the library costs points, larger-budget libraries improve later refinement without probing, and experience even transfers across benchmarks.
