Each library entry bundles two things. First, the adopted harness itself: the full executable file tree that was selected for the task, available for direct reuse or adaptation of its components. Second, an EXPERIENCE.md recording the applicable situation, the lessons extracted, and the supporting evidence for each. An index lets later sessions find relevant entries on demand instead of stuffing everything into context.

The Medium article gives a concrete flavor of an entry. Context: debugging Python functions that process collections. Observation: the original execution failed to inspect empty-input behavior. Improvement: introduce explicit edge-case analysis before generating a patch. Lesson: when debugging collection-processing functions, inspect empty, singleton and boundary inputs. Short, checkable, and tied to evidence, that is the shape of a good entry.
