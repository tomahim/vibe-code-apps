Each library entry bundles two things. First, the adopted harness itself: the full executable file tree that was selected for the task, available for direct reuse or adaptation of its components. Second, an EXPERIENCE.md recording the applicable situation, the lessons extracted, and the supporting evidence for each. An index lets later sessions find relevant entries on demand instead of stuffing everything into context.

A made-up entry shows the flavor. Situation: summarizing multi-sheet budget workbooks. Observation: the base run never compared totals across sheets. Change tried: a guardrail to reconcile every total with its source sheet. Lesson: in budget workbooks, check cross-sheet totals before writing any summary. Short, checkable and tied to evidence, that is the shape of a good entry. This sample is an illustration for this lesson, not a quotation from the paper.
