**1. One contrastive step improved every model-harness configuration on three professional benchmarks**, with gains up to 11.5 points on WorkBuddy-Bench, 4.5 on JobBench and 11.8 points on LAB Partial.

**2. A weaker model with an improved harness can overtake a stronger model with its base harness**, and self-editing works: gains do not require a smarter external editor.

**3. Every design component is priced by ablation**: library, scoping and composition each cost points when removed, and the case study shows a 34.7-point recovery from four file edits.
