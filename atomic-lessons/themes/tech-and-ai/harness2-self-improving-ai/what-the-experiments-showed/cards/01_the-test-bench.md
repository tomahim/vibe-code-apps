The evaluation uses three benchmarks from distinct professional worlds. WorkBuddy-Bench covers office-style deliverables in three domains: Code, Web and Office, with 40, 35 and 25 test tasks. JobBench targets end-to-end professional job tasks with long executions, the kind of multi-hour work where procedures matter most. LAB is a legal-work benchmark from Harvey, scored by Partial, the per-criterion pass rate. Each main result is averaged over three runs with shuffled task orders.

Four configurations are tested, crossing two frozen executor models, GLM-5 and Gemini 3.5 Flash, with two off-the-shelf harnesses, OpenCode and Codex. The harness editor and selector are Claude Opus 4.8 running as an agentic session. This grid matters: it means gains cannot be written off as a trick that works on one model or one harness. The same improvement step is applied unchanged in every configuration.
