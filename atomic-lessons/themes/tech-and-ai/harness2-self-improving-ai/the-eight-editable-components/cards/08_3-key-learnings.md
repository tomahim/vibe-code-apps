title: 3 key learnings

**1. Four components are always-on text** (system prompt, guardrails, memory, tool guidance) and four activate on demand (skills, scripts, subagents, plugins); placement is the first design decision.

**2. Match the component to the weakness**: deterministic checking to scripts, repeatable workflows to skills, bounded side-quests to subagents, ignored critical rules to plugins, and only concise guidance to the prompt.

**3. The smallest effective change wins.** Every component carries a cost in triggering, maintenance, latency or invisibility, so Harness² lets execution evidence, not intuition, choose the fix.
