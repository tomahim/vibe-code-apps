title: 3 key learnings
archived: false
---


**1. Improvement starts with a reference execution**: watch how the harness behaves on the task, because procedure, not correctness, is what you can observe without an answer key.

**2. Two scoped proposals beat two generic ones**: the invariant candidate fixes cross-domain procedure, the domain candidate encodes verified facts and domain procedure.

**3. Probe gives behavioral contrasts, compose keeps only what they support**, and the successor may even be the unchanged harness when nothing earns adoption.
