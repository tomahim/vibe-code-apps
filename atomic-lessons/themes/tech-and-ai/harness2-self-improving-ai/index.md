How can an AI agent get better at its job without retraining the model behind it? This path explains Harness², a research framework from Northwestern University and Google Cloud AI Research that improves the system around the model: its instructions, tools, memory, skills and scripts. Every lesson is an original, plain-language explanation of the research paper [Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224) (Xu et al., October 2026); all results quoted here are the paper's own. Each lesson has seven teaching cards, a three-key-learnings recap, and four review checkpoints.
