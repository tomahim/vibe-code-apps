How can an AI agent get better at its job without retraining the model behind it? This path explains Harness², a research framework from Northwestern University and Google Cloud AI Research that improves the system around the model: its instructions, tools, memory, skills and scripts. Adapted, in plain language, from Dhanush Kumar's Medium article [Building Self-Improving AI Applications with Harness²](https://medium.com/@danushidk507/building-self-improving-ai-applications-with-harness%C2%B2-from-agent-execution-to-recursive-harness-9657306a8086) (November 2026) and the underlying research paper [Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224) (Xu et al., October 2026). Each lesson has seven teaching cards, a three-key-learnings recap, and four review checkpoints.
