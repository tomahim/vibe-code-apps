title: The assistant that keeps missing the point
archived: false
---


Picture a trip-planning assistant. You give a language model a flight search tool, a hotel list and a calendar, then ask for a three-day itinerary. On good days it feels like magic. On bad days it books a museum on its closing day, forgets that the train arrives after midnight, searches the same city three times, and hands back a confident plan that falls apart on arrival.

None of these slips are about raw intelligence. The same model can explain opening hours and time zones perfectly well when asked directly. What fails is everything around the reasoning: which checks the agent is told to run, how its tools are described, how much context it may spend, and what it remembers from the last trip it planned. Building the agent is the easy part. The hard part starts when it meets a situation its original setup never anticipated.
