This path follows the arc of the Medium article and the research paper beneath it. Lessons 2 and 3 define the harness and tour its eight editable components, from the system prompt to plugins. Lessons 4 to 7 explain the core machinery: recursive self-improvement, the Propose-Probe-Compose step, learning without an answer key, and the two ways to spend a refinement budget.

Lessons 8 to 10 zoom out. You will see how an experience library lets agents carry lessons across tasks, what the experiments on three professional benchmarks actually showed, and what it takes to run self-improving agents safely in the real world. By the end, the phrase "the model stays frozen while everything around it learns" should feel concrete rather than magical. [Further reading: the [Harness² paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224) and the [Medium article](https://medium.com/@danushidk507/building-self-improving-ai-applications-with-harness%C2%B2-from-agent-execution-to-recursive-harness-9657306a8086) this path vulgarizes.]
