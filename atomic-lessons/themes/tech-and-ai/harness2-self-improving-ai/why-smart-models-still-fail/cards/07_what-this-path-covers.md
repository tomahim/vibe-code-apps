This path follows the structure of the research paper beneath Harness². Lessons 2 and 3 define the harness and tour its eight editable components, from the system prompt to plugins. Lessons 4 to 7 explain the core machinery: recursive self-improvement, the Propose-Probe-Compose step, learning without an answer key, and the two ways to spend a refinement budget.

Lessons 8 to 10 zoom out. You will see how an experience library lets agents carry lessons across tasks, what the experiments on three professional benchmarks actually showed, and how to think about using self-improving agents with care. By the end, the phrase "the model stays frozen while everything around it learns" should feel concrete rather than magical. [Further reading: the research paper [Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224) by Xu et al., October 2026, on which every lesson in this path is based.]
