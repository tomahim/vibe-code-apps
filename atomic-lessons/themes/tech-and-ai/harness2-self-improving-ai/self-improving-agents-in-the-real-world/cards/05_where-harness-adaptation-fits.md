title: Where harness adaptation fits

The approach fits best where tasks come in families, execution leaves a visible trace, and there is enough volume to repay the effort of keeping lessons. The paper's own evidence comes from three kinds of work: office and professional tasks, legal tasks, and mixed office, web and code tasks. In all of them an agent repeats similar procedures, fails in observable ways, and can be rerun on the same problem to compare behavior.

It also helps to be precise about what is edited. Harness² changes the eight harness components, such as prompts, skills and scripts. It does not retrain the model, and it does not rewrite the surrounding application's code. Where a recurring failure can be seen, a targeted operational change can be proposed, its effect compared, and a reliable lesson kept, the loop applies. Outside that loop, ordinary engineering judgment still rules.
