Measured here, not a promise

The painting cache is bounded to five images. Nature lesson-patch mode separately retains its base and eight small cutouts after the first visit. The same decoded artwork is reused across ten growth states. Paintings have content-hashed URLs and a one-year private browser-cache policy on the production host. Decoded-image reuse is measured separately below. The browser may still evict cached files.

Growth is a deterministic spatial reveal between two fixed paintings, with ten distinct stops. Five groups have a painted sparse base. The other two groups and interiors use a computed dormant-foliage preview, so their plants change color and fullness, not geometry. These are not ten separately painted scenes. Nature lesson-patch mode instead uses one fixed sparse base and real transparent botanical pieces in three separate density regions. The shared slider drives fast, steady and late patch-density curves. The old oak trunk, bare branches and existing sparse scrub are permanent base scenery, not lesson growth. Only added canopy fragments and planting clusters change. It does not yet include authored sapling-to-mature stages, matching shadows or foreground occlusion layers. The moving overlay is a separate 2D canvas. This is a 2.5D design study, not a free-orbit 3D world.
