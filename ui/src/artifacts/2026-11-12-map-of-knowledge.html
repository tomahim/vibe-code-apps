Map of Knowledge

A navigation design study for Cultive (Little Grains). The whole map is the knowledge; every landform is a way in.

  • Islands are themes. Each ecosystem - forest, desert, volcano, atolls - is one theme, so a dozen themes share one map without crowding.
  • Shaped regions are families of related paths. No repeated hexagon grid: English paths live inside one English-shaped region, dive-physics paths in another shape. Every ecosystem gets its own geography.
  • Trails are learning paths, and the beads on them are lessons: done, current, locked.

A theme here holds 8-12 paths across its regions - room to grow past today's 6-7 tiles. Demo data only; nothing is stored.
