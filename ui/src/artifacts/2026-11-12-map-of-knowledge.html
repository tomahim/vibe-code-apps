Map of Knowledge

A prototype for a new Little Grains themes screen: the garden list becomes one generated world map in the Little Grains palette.

  • Every published theme is a territory. This demo uses the seven live themes - Language, Tech & AI, Sport, Life & Society, Energy, Diving N4 #1 and #2 - with their real paths and lesson counts.
  • Generated, not drawn. Territories are placed on a phyllotaxis spiral and shaped by seeded fractal coastlines; terrains rotate by position like the garden does today. Adding or archiving a theme just re-generates the map.
  • Trails are learning paths, beads are lessons: done, current, locked. Tap a territory to zoom into its paths.
