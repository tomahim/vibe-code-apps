Why your ears hurt at 10 meters

Pressure rises by roughly one atmosphere every ten meters of water. The air in your middle ear gets squeezed, and the soft tissue of the eardrum bends inward…

The fix is equalizing early and often: a gentle pinch-and-blow before you feel pain, not after.

The problem

You do not need more content. You need smaller grains.

The internet has no shortage of things to learn. What is missing is a format light enough to survive a commute, a lunch break, a queue at the bakery. Little Grains starts there: small enough to finish, connected enough to add up.

Lessons hang on paths, paths hang on themes, and the whole thing lives in one garden. Finished weeks of reading look like something.

How it works

Three moves, repeated

Pick a path

Browse themes and open a learning path, from diving physics to how data centers drink electricity. Each path is a short series of lessons.

Read and answer

Read cards at your pace, tap "read this card", and answer two checkpoint questions per lesson. Some you pick, some you type.

Watch it grow

Finished lessons water the garden, completed paths bloom with a celebration, and review rounds keep older beds from fading into autumn.

What makes it a game

Drops, carrots and badges

The reward system is simple on purpose: it celebrates finished work, not time spent staring.

💧

Drops

Earned for finished lessons and completed paths. They water the garden — the score is a place, not a number.

🥕

Carrots

Earned for questions answered, in lessons, practice quizzes and review rounds. Every honest attempt at recall counts.

🏅

Badges

Milestones with their own art: finishing paths, exploring themes, keeping up with review. Earned once, dated, yours.

⚔️

The weekly duel

Challenge a friend for the week on questions from shared reading. The leaderboard keeps score, Monday to Sunday.

Questions

Honest answers

When does Little Grains launch?
There is no date. It is a personal project in a private preview today, and it opens when it is ready — not before. The waitlist below exists so interested people hear about it the day that changes.
What will it cost?
Undecided. No pricing is being promised on this page, and joining the list commits you to nothing.
Which platforms?
The app is built mobile-first as an installable web app (PWA), so it works on phones without an app-store download. Other platforms may follow.
What makes it different from other learning apps?
The garden. Progress lives in a visible place that grows and seasons, review is woven into that place, and the weekly challenge turns learning into a friendly rivalry instead of a solitary streak.
Who is making it?
A small independent project, built in the open with a public build log planned. The content is human-reviewed before it ships.
