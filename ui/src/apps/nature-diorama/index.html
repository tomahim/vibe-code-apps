A layered clearing

This is a new Nature composition, not the original painting on a moving card. Ground, pond, trunks, mossy rocks and botanical cutouts occupy separate depths. A bounded camera slides and moves closer between three lesson areas.

The plants and rocks reuse real transparent artwork from the Nature interior study. Terrain, tree trunks, paths and water use simple geometry. Most foliage and mossy rocks are still flat cutouts: the camera stays within a narrow front-facing range, not a free orbit.

Use + / - on either device, pinch on the scenery on a phone, or use a mouse wheel. Only the camera changes; controls remain the same size. Drag gently to drift. Whole clearing resets the view. The shared slider demonstrates fast, steady and late added-planting density. Permanent trunks and existing scenery do not become younger. Matching shadows, authored growth stages and phone-hardware performance are not yet proven.

Demo stop only. No learning progress is saved.
