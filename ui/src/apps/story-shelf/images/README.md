# Original storybook art

The first four groups were generated on 30 October 2026, and the moon, tomb and plane groups on 31 October 2026, for The Story Room. These are AI-generated original illustrations, not licensed Wikimedia images, archival documents or accurate portraits of historical people. No third-party art assets are included and no external attribution license is imposed by these generated files. The UI labels the art as AI-generated and imagined. This statement does not assert exclusive copyright in AI-generated material.

Each group was made as a six-panel, 3 by 2 illustration sheet, then cropped in reading order and re-encoded as RGB WebP at quality 88. Files are bundled and never fetched from an image service at runtime. Total is approximately 3.3 MB.

Creative direction: richly detailed gouache and colored-pencil picture books on warm ivory; coherent characters and palettes; no lettering. Printing: amber/moss, fictional Mainz around 1450. Fossils: sea teal/terracotta, early nineteenth-century Dorset. Forest: moss/gold, botanical oak motifs. Mountains: alpine blue/rust, safe trail/shelter scenes. Each atlas and all its panels were directly visually reviewed. Illustrative limitations: the medieval town is not an exact reconstruction of Mainz; the workshop and Anning scenes are invented; the ancient sea is an artistic interpretation; snowflake close-ups combine scales poetically.

All sculpture geometry and cover typography were authored in the app. Three.js is the repository's existing dependency; no 3D models, reference-site assets or audio were downloaded.
