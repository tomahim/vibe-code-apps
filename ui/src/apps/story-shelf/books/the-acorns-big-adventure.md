# A very small parcel

An acorn dropped into the soft leaves below an oak. A squirrel picked it up, hurried across the woodland and tucked it into the earth. The squirrel was storing food. But this acorn would be left behind.

Inside it was the beginning of a tree, with a small supply of food to help it start. Not every acorn becomes an oak. This story follows one that finds the conditions it needs.

> Imagine a whole tree beginning inside one little acorn.

# The first tiny root

In damp soil, the acorn began to grow. A root pushed downward. Later a shoot reached up towards the light. The young plant needed water, air and suitable warmth to begin its journey.

Underground was busy, too. The root helped anchor the seedling and take in water. Above it, a small green shoot made its first appearance. A beginning could be quiet and still be extraordinary.

> Roots grow below. Shoots grow towards the light.
---page---
# A pocket of sunlight

Little leaves unfolded. With light, water and carbon dioxide from the air, green leaves can make sugars that help a plant grow. The seedling was starting to make its own food.

It could not walk away when the ground was dry or the shade was deep. Its place mattered. Rain, light, hungry animals and nearby plants would all help shape its future.

> Try turning the little tree to see its branches.

# A home with branches

Years passed in our story. The young oak grew taller, with stronger roots and spreading branches. Birds could perch above. Insects could visit leaves and bark. Fallen leaves became part of the woodland floor.

A tree is more than a tall plant in a picture. It is part of a place full of living things. Our oak shared the woodland with many neighbours, most too small for the squirrel to notice.

> How many different homes can one tree offer?

# The story starts again

Much later, the oak carried acorns of its own. Some were eaten. Some stayed on the ground. Some were carried away. And perhaps, in a little patch of suitable earth, another root would begin.

Our acorn's adventure is imagined, but the changes are real. Oak trees begin as seeds inside acorns. Each new tree needs the right conditions, a little luck and time. Big stories can start very small.

> Look for an oak leaf on your next walk. Leave wild places as you find them.
