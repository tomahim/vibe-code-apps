#### 1. CREATIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

- **Creative Twist / Re-imagining Angle:** Bookmarks rot: links die, pages get edited, the thing you saved is never the thing you find again. Amber preserves the moment itself - the full page, layout, images and all, frozen as a self-contained snapshot with its capture date and source sealed on it, like an insect in amber. Your collection is a chronological shelf of capsules you can open and replay exactly as they were, scrollable and self-contained. Over years the shelf becomes personal internet history: the announcement page on launch day, the menu of the restaurant before it closed, your project's first landing page. A "then and now" button puts the frozen capsule beside the live page, and the gap between them is the story.
- **Core Problem & Target User:** The web forgets. Content vanishes or mutates silently - studies get corrected, pages 404, prices and promises change - and screenshots are unsearchable, unverifiable clutter. Target user: researchers and journalists who need citable evidence, nostalgia keepers, and anyone who has ever said "I swear it said that last week."
- **Core Value Proposition:** A personal, beautiful, replayable archive of the web as you actually saw it - with provenance that makes a capsule evidence, not just a screenshot.

#### 2. USER STORIES

- **Primary User Story:** "As a keeper, I want to freeze any page in one tap with its date and source sealed in, so that I can always prove and revisit what I saw."
- **Secondary User Story:** "As a longtime user, I want to browse my capsules on a timeline and compare then versus now, so that my collection becomes a personal history of the web."

#### 4. DAY 1 POC EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

- **Minimal Technical Setup:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS. PoC bundles three demo pages (a product launch, a restaurant menu, a news announcement) each frozen at two dates; the player replays them offline with original styling; the shelf, search and then/now compare all run client-side.
- **Core 24-Hour Demo Flow:** Open the shelf, tap the restaurant menu capsule from "2 years ago", scroll the frozen page with its old prices, then hit then-and-now: the live page beside it, new name, new prices, one dish surviving. **Aha moment:** the comparison slider - watching the web's amnesia in one gesture, and realizing your shelf is immune to it.

#### 5. ADOPTION OPPORTUNITIES & DRIVERS

- **Instant Onboarding Hook:** "Save this page forever, exactly as it is" - the demo opens a capsule from a famous past product page and lets you replay it, instantly making the format tangible.
- **Growth Vector:** Then-and-now comparisons are natural shares ("the menu before it closed"), and every "the page was quietly edited" discourse moment sends people looking for exactly this.

#### 6. RISKS & MITIGATION

- **Content rights:** Frozen copies of others' pages have copyright constraints. Mitigation: personal-use framing, capsules private by default with no public republishing feature, and share exports limited to short excerpts with source attribution.
- **Provenance honesty:** A capsule is only evidence if it can't be quietly edited. Mitigation: captures are immutable once sealed, any user annotation is layered separately and visibly, and integrity hashing in Expansion 2 makes tampering detectable.
- **Storage growth:** Full-page snapshots are heavy. Mitigation: single-file deduplication of shared assets, retention controls, and a storage meter with clear tradeoffs between fidelity and size.
- **Interactive pages don't freeze well:** Apps and JS-heavy pages resist faithful capture. Mitigation: honest capture-quality indicator per capsule ("static copy - interactions not preserved"), never claiming fidelity the snapshot doesn't have.
