#### 1. CREATIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

- **Creative Twist / Re-imagining Angle:** Each week a boss spawns, a mountain monster whose hit points equal a vertical-gain target tuned to your recent training. Different workouts deal different damage: long runs are heavy slams, hill repeats are combo strikes, easy runs only chip. A boss that survives the week heals and returns angrier. Beat it and you earn a trophy and its loot (a title and a visual).
- **Core Problem & Target User:** Weekly volume goals are easy to ignore because they are abstract. Target user: a trail runner with a clear weekly structure who is motivated by a visible opponent.
- **Core Value Proposition:** Turn "run 1500 m D+ this week" into a fight you want to win by Sunday night.

#### 2. USER STORIES

- **Primary User Story:** "As a trail runner, I want a weekly boss whose health bar is my climbing target, so that I feel pulled to get my vertical done."
- **Secondary User Story:** "As a runner who skips sessions, I want the boss to escalate gently when I miss, so that I feel stakes without guilt."

#### 4. DAY 1 POC EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

- **Minimal Technical Setup:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS. Boss data in JSON, SVG or CSS sprites, damage formula in one pure function, localStorage for week state. Mobile-first layout.
- **Core 24-Hour Demo Flow:** Open the app, see this week's boss at full HP, log a 20 km with 900 m D+ and watch the boss lose 60 percent of its health with a shake effect. **Aha moment:** under 30 seconds the remaining HP shows exactly which run finishes it.

#### 5. ADOPTION OPPORTUNITIES & DRIVERS

- **Instant Onboarding Hook:** The first boss is pre-tuned from one question: how many metres do you climb in a normal week?
- **Growth Vector:** Boss kill cards for sharing in running groups, and co-op bosses with training partners.

#### 6. RISKS & MITIGATION

- **Scope Creep:** Game balance and art are endless. Mitigation: 3 bosses and one damage formula for the PoC.
- **Technical:** Weekly targets may push overtraining. Mitigation: cap target growth and add a recovery boss that rewards rest.
- **Retention/Engagement:** Weeks may feel repetitive. Mitigation: a boss roster, traits, and a season arc.
