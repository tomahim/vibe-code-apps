#### 1. CREATIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

- **Creative Twist / Re-imagining Angle:** A toast is the smallest high-stakes genre there is: ninety seconds, everyone watching, no slides, no second take. The Toast treats it as a craft with a shape. You pick the occasion and your relationship to the person, and the app scaffolds the classic arc (one story, one turn, one wish): a story only you could tell, the turn that says what it means, and the wish that raises the glass. Each beat has its own writing card with prompts and a word budget, so the toast physically cannot sprawl. Then rehearsal mode: a teleprompter at reading speed, a timer set to your target, and a playback page showing where you rushed. A "table test" checklist catches the classics (inside jokes nobody at the table shares, stories that embarrass the wrong person, an ending that forgets to name the person).
- **Core Problem & Target User:** Being asked to "say a few words" triggers weeks of dread, and the results split between rambling anecdotes and greeting-card filler - because nobody teaches the shape and nobody rehearses out loud. Target user: best men, maids of honor, colleagues at farewells, parents at birthdays - anyone handed a glass and a room.
- **Core Value Proposition:** From dread to done in one sitting: a proven three-beat structure, prompts that surface your best material, and a rehearsal loop that gets the toast out of your head and into your mouth before the room does.

#### 2. USER STORIES

- **Primary User Story:** "As an occasion speaker, I want a guided structure and per-beat prompts, so that I can write a ninety-second toast in one sitting instead of dreading it for weeks."
- **Secondary User Story:** "As a nervous speaker, I want to rehearse against a teleprompter and timer, so that the toast lands in time and in one piece on the day."

#### 4. DAY 1 POC EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

- **Minimal Technical Setup:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS. Occasion picker, three writing cards with prompts and live word counts, an assembled reading view, and the teleprompter (adjustable scroll speed, timer, big type). localStorage only; no backend, no accounts, no microphone on day 1. Works on phone, since rehearsal happens in kitchens and cars.
- **Core 24-Hour Demo Flow:** Pick "best friend, wedding" and the story card prompts you: "The moment you knew this person was for keeps." Write three sentences; the turn card reframes them; the wish card lands the glass. Then hit rehearse: the teleprompter scrolls and the timer shows 1:24. The "Aha!" moment: hearing your own assembled toast read back in ninety seconds and realizing it is already good.

#### 5. ADOPTION OPPORTUNITIES & DRIVERS

- **Instant Onboarding Hook:** The prompts do the hardest part (finding the story), so the first usable toast exists within fifteen minutes of opening the app.
- **Growth Vector:** Toasts are inherently social events - a successful one gets "how did you write that?" asked at the table, and wedding seasons return every year with new rooms.

#### 6. RISKS & MITIGATION

- **Authenticity risk:** Scaffolding could flatten toasts into formula. Mitigation: the structure is a floor, not a template - prompts push for specific, personal material, and the table test rewards stories only the speaker could tell.
- **Tone risk:** Eulogy and sensitive-occasion content demands care. Mitigation: sensitive occasions get their own restrained card sets and copy, and nothing auto-generates the words themselves.
- **Scope creep risk:** AI speechwriting, video coaching, event planning. Mitigation: the app scaffolds and rehearses; the words stay the user's, and occasion types expand by card sets, not by features.
