#### 1. CREATIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

- **Creative Twist / Re-imagining Angle:** A road-trip trivia game tied to the actual road. GPS detects the town or village you are driving through and unlocks a small question pack about it: its history, its weird specialty, its famous son or daughter, the legend of its name. The app reads questions aloud, passengers shout answers and claim points by tapping their corner of one shared phone. Passing a rival region during a derby weekend unlocks a bonus roast question. The driver plays safely: everything is audio, passengers handle the screen.
- **Core Problem & Target User:** Long drives with family or friends sag into separate phones after the first hour. Target user: a car full of people on a motorway who want a shared game that fits the journey instead of ignoring it.
- **Core Value Proposition:** The road itself deals the cards: every place you pass becomes ten minutes of shared play, and the trip home feels shorter.

#### 2. USER STORIES

- **Primary User Story:** "As a passenger on a long drive, I want a quiz that reacts to where we are, so that the journey itself becomes the game."
- **Secondary User Story:** "As a driver, I want to play by voice without touching anything, so that I stay safe and still get to compete."

#### 4. DAY 1 POC EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

- **Minimal Technical Setup:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS. Browser geolocation with coarse accuracy, the Web Speech API for reading questions, packs as JSON. No backend, no accounts.
- **Core 24-Hour Demo Flow:** Simulate a drive along one corridor (a GPS mock mode for testing), hear the first town pack unlock, claim answers by corner taps, finish with the scoreboard. **Aha moment:** the app speaks up by itself as you enter a town and nobody touched the phone.

#### 5. ADOPTION OPPORTUNITIES & DRIVERS

- **Instant Onboarding Hook:** The demo drive starts the audio within five seconds.
- **Growth Vector:** Word of mouth in the car itself, plus a shareable trip scorecard at arrival.

#### 6. RISKS & MITIGATION

- **Safety risk:** Any driver interaction is unacceptable. Mitigation: the driver mode is strictly audio, and the app locks interactions when no passenger session is claimed.
- **Content risk:** Authored facts must be right. Mitigation: each pack carries its sources and a review checklist, like the other learning apps in this collection.
- **Technical:** GPS in tunnels and low-signal stretches. Mitigation: packs pre-download, and missed unlocks can be claimed manually from the trip list.
