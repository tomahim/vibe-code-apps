#### 1. CREATIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

- **Creative Twist / Re-imagining Angle:** Each run is forged into a small procedural artifact: a vase, a totem, a medal that nobody designed, grown by an algorithm from the run itself. The elevation profile becomes the object's silhouette, pace variability carves ridges, heart rate zones set the glaze bands, distance sets size. Two identical efforts never mint the same relic twice. Your shelf fills with strange objects, each one secretly a recording of a morning in the mountains.
- **Core Problem & Target User:** Digital medals all look the same and mean nothing. Target user: a trail runner who loves objects and wants a trophy that actually encodes the run.
- **Core Value Proposition:** A growing cabinet of unique 3D relics, each one generated from and only from your run.

#### 2. USER STORIES

- **Primary User Story:** "As a runner, I want each run to mint a unique 3D artifact from its data, so that my efforts become objects I can collect."
- **Secondary User Story:** "As a collector, I want to read back what a relic's features mean, so that the object tells the run's story."

#### 3. SCOPE & ROADMAP (1-DAY POC VS 3-MONTH SCALE)

| Feature Name | Functional Description | Timeline Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core Mechanism: The forge | GPX or Strava export parsing, a lathe-style generator (profile to silhouette, variability to ridges), a 3D viewer with materials, and a cabinet shelf view. | Day 1 PoC |
| Expansion 1: Generators and export | Three generator families (vase, totem, crystal), STL export for 3D printing, a legend explaining each feature, local storage of the collection. | Month 1 MVP |
| Expansion 2: Live mint and gallery | Strava OAuth auto-mint after each run, monthly kiln (one best relic fired in gold), shareable turntable video, visiting a friend's cabinet. | Month 3 Polish |

#### 4. DAY 1 POC EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

- **Minimal Technical Setup:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Three Fiber with drei. Lathe geometry from the elevation profile, seeded noise for ridges, localStorage. No backend.
- **Core 24-Hour Demo Flow:** Drop a GPX file, watch the vase spin up out of the profile, and read the legend: "wide base: long climb; deep ridges: uneven pace". **Aha moment:** within 30 seconds you recognise your run in the shape.

#### 5. ADOPTION OPPORTUNITIES & DRIVERS

- **Instant Onboarding Hook:** Three sample relics sit in the cabinet on load, with their runs attached.
- **Growth Vector:** STL export leads to printed relics, and photos of printed objects advertise the app.

#### 6. RISKS & MITIGATION

- **Scope Creep:** Endless generator art direction. Mitigation: one polished generator in the PoC.
- **Technical:** STL export correctness. Mitigation: manifold lathe geometry and a tested exporter.
- **Retention/Engagement:** Cabinet fatigue. Mitigation: monthly kiln and generator unlocks.
