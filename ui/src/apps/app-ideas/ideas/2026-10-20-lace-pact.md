#### 1. CREATIVE CONCEPT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

- **Creative Twist / Re-imagining Angle:** A commitment-device game for runners. The evening before, you seal tomorrow's run as a pact: time window, minimum distance, and a small playful stake (a coffee owed, a photo of a bad hat, a chore swap). A friend is the pact keeper. Next day, a run in your data fulfils the pact and the seal breaks with a satisfying animation; a miss hands your keeper a stake card. The pact is the product, the run is the key.
- **Core Problem & Target User:** The hardest part of running 4 times a week is leaving the house, especially with a busy family schedule. Target user: a runner who keeps promises to others better than promises to himself.
- **Core Value Proposition:** A light social contract that turns "maybe tomorrow" into a plan with a witness.

#### 2. USER STORIES

- **Primary User Story:** "As a runner with a busy household, I want to pledge a time window for tomorrow's run, so that I plan it around my day and stick to it."
- **Secondary User Story:** "As a pact keeper, I want to see a simple fulfilled or missed result, so that I can cheer or collect the stake without chasing."

#### 3. SCOPE & ROADMAP (1-DAY POC VS 3-MONTH SCALE)

| Feature Name | Functional Description | Timeline Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |

#### 4. DAY 1 POC EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

- **Minimal Technical Setup:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS. A pact is a small JSON object saved in localStorage and encodable into a URL hash for the keeper link. CSS wax-seal animation. Mobile-first. No backend.
- **Core 24-Hour Demo Flow:** Create a pact in three taps, see the seal appear, press "I ran" with 12 km, and watch the seal crack and the streak increase. **Aha moment:** within 30 seconds the pact card feels like a promise you would be a bit embarrassed to break.

#### 5. ADOPTION OPPORTUNITIES & DRIVERS

- **Instant Onboarding Hook:** Preset stakes ("loser buys coffee") make the first pact a 10-second job.
- **Growth Vector:** Each pact sent to a keeper is an invitation to make their own.

#### 6. RISKS & MITIGATION

- **Scope Creep:** Money stakes and payments are a trap. Mitigation: playful non-monetary stakes only.
- **Technical:** Without a backend, honesty is self-reported. Mitigation: friend-as-witness model in the PoC and file verification later.
- **Retention/Engagement:** Pacts can feel like pressure. Mitigation: a rest-day pass and gentle copy, so a miss costs something small and funny.
